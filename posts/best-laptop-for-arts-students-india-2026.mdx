## Graphic Design Students

If you're in graphic design, visual communication, animation, or illustration, your laptop requirements are specific:

**Color accuracy** — Adobe Photoshop and Illustrator require a display with high sRGB or DCI-P3 coverage (95%+ sRGB minimum). Budget laptops often have 60–70% sRGB panels where your colors look muddy and prints don't match your screen.

**RAM** — 16GB minimum for Photoshop with large files open. 8GB is workable for basic use but frustrating for layer-heavy projects.

**GPU** — Photoshop and Illustrator primarily use CPU, but Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve, and After Effects benefit significantly from a discrete GPU.

**Storage** — Design projects eat storage. 512GB SSD minimum; consider external storage.

## For Humanities, Literature, Economics, Sociology Students

If your arts stream is humanities-based (BA in English, History, Political Science, Economics, Psychology) — your requirements are fundamentally simpler:

- Word processing and research (Google Docs, Word, Zotero)
- Video lectures (YouTube, LMS platforms)
- PDF reading and annotation
- Presentations (PowerPoint, Google Slides)

For this use case, a premium display and GPU are irrelevant. What matters: **battery life**, **keyboard quality**, **weight**, and **price**.
