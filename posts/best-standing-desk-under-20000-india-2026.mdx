## Setting Up Your Standing Desk Ergonomically

**Correct standing height:** Elbows at 90° when hands rest on keyboard. For a 170cm tall person: approximately 100–105cm standing desk height.

**Anti-fatigue mat:** Standing on hard floors for 1+ hour causes foot and lower back fatigue. A Kantek or Topo anti-fatigue mat (₹2,000–₅,000) significantly reduces this. Budget alternative: a thick bathroom anti-fatigue mat (₹500–₁,000).

**Monitor position:** Top of monitor at eye level when standing. May require a monitor arm (₹2,000–₃,500) to reach standing height without craning your neck.

**Recommendation:** Flexispot M2B converter for laptop/single monitor users on budget. Durian Space EZ for users who want a full electric desk under ₹20,000. Manual crank for reliability-first buyers.
