## Coffee Brewing Methods Under ₹3000

**French Press (₹800–₁,800):**
Coffee + hot water, steep 4 minutes, press plunger. Produces bold, rich coffee. No filters needed. Easiest manual method for beginners.

**Moka Pot (₹800–₁,500):**
Stovetop espresso — pressurised steam pushes water through fine ground coffee. Produces espresso-strength coffee (not identical to café espresso but similar concentration). Used throughout South India.

**Drip Coffee Maker (₹1,800–₃,000):**
Automatic — add water and ground coffee, press button. Machine brews into a carafe. Closest to filter coffee method familiar to South Indian households.

**AeroPress (₹3,000–₄,000):**
Slightly above ₹3,000 budget but worth mentioning — produces the cleanest, most café-quality coffee of any manual method. Highly recommended for serious coffee drinkers.

## 1. Borosil French Press — Best for Bold Coffee Lovers

**Price:** ₹1,099–₁,499

Borosil's French Press is the most popular home coffee maker among Indian coffee enthusiasts on a budget. The borosilicate glass construction handles boiling water safely (unlike thin glass alternatives that crack).

Using it:
1. Heat water to 92–95°C (30 seconds off boil)
2. Add 1 tablespoon coarse ground coffee per cup
3. Add hot water, stir, place lid (plunger up)
4. Wait 4 minutes
5. Press plunger slowly
6. Pour immediately (leaving it steeping makes coffee bitter)

Coffee used: Any ground coffee — Blue Tokai, Corridor Seven, Third Wave are good Indian brands available on Amazon.
