## GIF Earbud Shopping Tips

**Verify included accessories:** Premium earbud boxes contain charging cable, multiple ear tip sizes, and documentation. Suspiciously discounted GIF listings sometimes ship with fewer tips than claimed.

**Check Amazon Prime Day vs GIF:** Amazon India runs a separate Prime Day sale in July. If the earbud you want was discounted during July Prime Day, GIF may not offer the same model at a deeper price — the discount pattern alternates.

**Certified refurbished option:** Amazon Renewed (Certified Refurbished) earbuds are available at 30–50% off regular price year-round. During GIF, new + Renewed price difference may narrow — compare before choosing Renewed.
