## How to Choose an L Shaped Standing Desk

Measure the corner. An L-shaped desk usually needs a space of around 150 by 150 centimetres or more. Check the depth of each arm so that you have enough room for a monitor and keyboard.

Check the motor and frame. Dual motors and a wide steel frame handle the weight of an L-shaped top better. Ask about the weight capacity and the frame's stability at full height.

Decide left or right orientation. Some desks are reversible, and others are sold in a fixed layout, so match the orientation to your room.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Ignoring weight.** L-shaped tops are heavy.

**Buying without checking orientation.** Confirm left or right.

**Skipping the corner clearance.** Leave space to walk around.

**Overlooking assembly.** Plan for two people.

## What to Check on the Listing

Confirm the weight capacity, the height range, the motor count, the desktop dimensions and what is included. See whether delivery covers the whole package.

## Quick FAQ

**Is an L shape better than a straight desk?** It offers more surface area in a corner, but needs more space.

**Do I need dual motors?** Yes for heavy setups. Single motors suit lighter loads.

**How long does assembly take?** Often two hours or more with two people.

## Setting Up an L Shaped Desk

Put your main screen on the longer arm and keep secondary items on the short return. Keep the keyboard and mouse within easy reach, and use the return for paperwork or a second laptop. If you use a corner, leave clearance behind the desk for cables and for the desk's full height range.

Plan delivery and assembly. L-shaped tops are large and heavy, so measure doorways and stairwells, and allow two people and a couple of hours for assembly. Check that the floor is level to avoid wobble.

## Sizing the Desktop

Each arm of an L-shaped top is typically between 120 and 160 centimetres long and 60 to 70 centimetres deep. Measure your corner and leave room for the chair to move, then check that the frame's stated width range and weight capacity cover the top you choose. A frame rated below the weight of the desktop plus equipment will wobble or lift slowly.
