## RO vs UV vs UF: Which Purification Do You Need?

**RO (Reverse Osmosis):** Removes dissolved salts, heavy metals (lead, arsenic, fluoride), nitrates, and TDS. Required when input water TDS exceeds 200 ppm. Does not remove all bacteria (though reduces load significantly).

**UV (Ultraviolet):** Kills 99.99% of bacteria and viruses by disrupting their DNA. Does not remove dissolved salts or heavy metals. Best for microbiologically contaminated water with low TDS.

**UF (Ultrafiltration):** Removes bacteria and large particles without electricity. Works without power. Less effective than UV for viruses.

**RO+UV combination:** Covers both — dissolved contaminants AND microbiological contamination. The right choice for most Indian water conditions.

**When to use only UV (no RO):** If your input water TDS is below 150–200 ppm (mountain areas, some northern plains regions with clean aquifer water). RO unnecessarily removes beneficial minerals when TDS is already low.
