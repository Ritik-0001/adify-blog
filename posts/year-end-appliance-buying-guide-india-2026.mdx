At year end the smartest appliance buys are the ones you genuinely need within the next few months and can compare calmly, such as a refrigerator, a washing machine, a TV or a geyser, bought against a clear budget and after checking the live price on Amazon. For most households, high-ticket items from ₹15,000 and above deserve a few days of comparison rather than a rushed purchase.

This guide is researched, not hands-on tested, and it deliberately names no sale date or discount, since those change. Use it to build a shortlist now, then confirm the exact model number, warranty and installation terms before paying.

## 1. Refrigerators

Roughly ₹20,000–60,000 for good double-door models. A refrigerator runs all day, so energy rating and capacity matter more than extra features. Decide on size first, then compare star ratings and compressor warranties. A large model needs clearance around it and may draw more power.

## 2. Washing Machines

Roughly ₹20,000–50,000 for front-load and premium top-load models. Pick capacity by family size, check motor warranty and consider water and power use. Check that your balcony or utility area has the water inlet and drain a front-loader needs.

## How to Plan a Year-End Purchase

Set a total budget. List the appliances you need, rank them by urgency and allocate money to the top two or three.

Compare models, not prices alone. Check energy rating, warranty and service availability for each shortlisted model.

Track live prices. Prices on the same model can move, so check the live price over several days and decide on a number you are happy to pay.

## Energy Ratings and Running Costs

A lower purchase price can hide a higher running cost. Look at the star rating on refrigerators, ACs and washing machines, and compare the annual energy figures where the brand publishes them. A five-star appliance costs more upfront but usually costs less to run over the years you own it.

## Delivery and Installation Planning

Large appliances need delivery, unpacking and often installation by a technician. Check whether installation is included, how long the booking takes, and what extra charges apply for things like copper piping on ACs or a stand for a washing machine. Booking early avoids waiting at the busiest time.

## Who Should Skip Year-End Shopping

If your current refrigerator or washing machine still works well, a year-end purchase is mostly the pull of marketing. Replace appliances when they are failing or a repair bill approaches half the cost of a new unit, not because a calendar date is close. Likewise, skip buying an AC in winter unless the price is clearly lower and you have somewhere dry to store it.

## Who Should Skip Buying Now

If your current refrigerator, washing machine or geyser still works well, a year-end purchase is optional, and waiting costs nothing. Skip air conditioners entirely if summer is months away and your installation slot is not urgent, because the unit will only sit in its box. The only real reasons to buy now are a failing appliance, a move into a new home, or a price you have been tracking that you find fair, so make the decision from need, not from the calendar.

## Recommendation

Buy what you need now after a calm comparison, and hold off on appliances you will not use for months. Always check the live price and the warranty on each listing.
