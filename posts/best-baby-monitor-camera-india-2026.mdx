## Wi-Fi Phone Monitor vs Dedicated Monitor

**Wi-Fi + Phone (Tapo C210, Mi 360):**
- View from any phone, anywhere in house or world
- Shares Wi-Fi — may have latency if network is congested
- Requires phone to be charged and available
- Cheaper (₹2,000–₃,000)
- Can alert multiple family members simultaneously

**Dedicated Monitor (Motorola VM35):**
- Own private frequency — no Wi-Fi needed, no congestion
- Works in areas with no Wi-Fi or poor signal
- Dedicated screen always showing baby
- Works during internet outages
- Higher cost (₹7,000–₁₂,000)

## Indian Nursery Setup Tips

**Mounting height:** Place camera at cot head level or above, angled down at 45° for clear face view.

**Distance:** 1–2 metres from cot for close monitoring. Pan-tilt cameras cover the whole cot regardless.

**Wi-Fi strength:** Ensure strong Wi-Fi signal in nursery — Wi-Fi baby monitors lag badly on weak signals. Extend router reach with mesh node if needed.

**Privacy:** Disable cloud recording for baby room cameras — keep footage on local SD card only. No need for cloud backup of baby monitor footage.

**Temperature guidance:** Indian Academy of Pediatrics recommends keeping infant room at 24–26°C — Motorola's temperature display helps maintain this.

**Recommendation:** TP-Link Tapo C210 for most Indian parents — features are excellent at ₹2,500 and most parents already have a phone to monitor from. Motorola VM35 for joint families or homes with unreliable Wi-Fi where a dedicated monitor provides more reliability.
