## What Makes an Ergonomic Office Chair

**Lumbar support:** Adjustable lumbar cushion or built-in lumbar curve that matches the natural inward curve of the lower spine. This is the single most important feature for back pain prevention.

**Seat height adjustment:** Pneumatic height adjustment to set feet flat on floor, thighs parallel to ground. For Indian users (average height: 5'4"–5'8"), look for minimum seat height of 42–45cm.

**Armrest adjustability:** 3D armrests (height + lateral + forward/back) allow elbow support at desk height, reducing shoulder and neck strain.

**Breathable mesh back:** India's hot climate makes foam-backed chairs uncomfortable in summer. Mesh allows airflow and reduces sweating during long sessions.

**Recline mechanism:** Recline to 110–135° with recline tension adjustment. Brief recline breaks reduce spinal disc pressure.

## Chair Setup for Indian Users

**Seat height:** Set so both feet rest flat on floor with hips at 90–100° angle. Most Indians need seat height of 42–46cm.

**Lumbar position:** Adjust lumbar support to the widest part of your lower back (waist level), not mid-back.

**Monitor distance:** After setting chair, ensure monitor is at arm's length (50–70cm) and top of screen at or slightly below eye level.

**Take breaks:** Even the best ergonomic chair doesn't replace movement. Stand or walk for 5 minutes every 45–60 minutes.
