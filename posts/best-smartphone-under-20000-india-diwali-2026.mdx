## Diwali Buying Checklist for Smartphones

**Exchange offers:** Amazon and Flipkart offer ₹2,000–₁₀,000 exchange discount. Get old phone evaluated before sale — exchange values are pre-determined by device condition.

**Bank discounts:** SBI and HDFC typically offer 10% additional off on specific card types during Great Indian Festival.

**EMI:** No-cost EMI on 3–6 months is common on festival sales — check if EMI cost is built into the price (it usually is on no-cost EMI).

**After-sale support:** Check brand's service centre count in your city before buying. Realme and Redmi have good Tier 1 coverage; Samsung covers Tier 2–3 better.

**Recommendation:** Realme 13 Pro for camera-focused buyers, iQOO Z9 for gaming-focused buyers, Samsung Galaxy A35 for buyers who value long-term software support.
