## What Small Businesses Actually Need

**GST invoice printing:** High-volume monochrome — laser wins. Inkjet is slower and cost per page is higher for text-only printing.

**Marketing materials, proposals, brochures:** Colour — inkjet EcoTank wins for low cost per page. Laser colour printers cost significantly more to run.

**Scan and copy:** Most businesses need MFP (multifunction) — print, scan, copy at minimum.

**Remote printing:** Multiple staff printing from phones and laptops — Wi-Fi and cloud print support matter.

## 2. Epson EcoTank L3252 — Best for Colour Printing

**Price:** ₹13,000–₁₅,000

EcoTank uses refillable ink tanks instead of cartridges. Ink bottles (₹300–₄₀₀ per colour) print 4,500–7,500 pages depending on colour. Running cost: under ₹0.30 per page colour — dramatically lower than laser colour or cartridge inkjet.

Colour quality: Excellent for marketing materials, client proposals, and product catalogues.

Print speed: 10 pages per minute (colour) — slower than laser for text.

Best for: Design studios, travel agencies, real estate firms, restaurants printing menus — any business with regular colour printing needs.
