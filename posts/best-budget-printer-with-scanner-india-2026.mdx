## The Real Cost of Cheap Printers

Budget printers under ₹5,000 use cartridges that cost ₹500–₁,000 each and print 150–200 pages. This works out to ₹3–₅ per page — among the most expensive ways to print.

Epson EcoTank ink bottles (₹300–₄₀₀) print 4,500–7,500 pages — under ₹0.10 per page for black, under ₹0.30 per page for colour. The EcoTank costs more upfront but pays back within 2–3 ink refills.
