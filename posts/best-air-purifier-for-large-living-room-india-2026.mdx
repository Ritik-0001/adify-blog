## How to Choose for a Large Living Room

Start with room size. Measure length and width and check the rated coverage. For open plan areas, choose a unit rated for more than the floor area for a margin.

Look at the CADR and filter type. A true HEPA filter with a good CADR clears particles faster, and activated carbon helps with odours.

Consider running costs. Filters, electricity and noise at higher speeds all add up.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Undersizing.** A small unit in a big room does little.

**Blocking airflow.** Keep space around the purifier.

**Skipping filter replacement.** Old filters reduce performance.

## Quick FAQ

**How many purifiers do I need?** For an open plan area, one large unit or two smaller ones may work.

**Is noise a problem?** Higher speeds are louder. Check noise ratings.

**Do purifiers remove smells?** Only with activated carbon filters, and only partly.

## Placement and Running Costs

Place the purifier where air can flow freely, ideally a little away from walls and furniture, and not behind a sofa. In open-plan spaces, a central spot or near the main seating area works best. Keep windows and doors closed while it runs.

Budget for filters and electricity. HEPA and carbon filters usually need replacing every six to twelve months, depending on use and pollution, and the cost varies widely between brands. Running the unit on a low or auto setting most of the day is quieter and cheaper than blasting it on high.

## Real-World Scenarios

**An open-plan living and dining space.** Choose a unit rated well above the floor area and place it centrally, or use two smaller units at either end.

**A living room next to a busy road.** Prioritise a high CADR and a strong pre-filter, since dust load will be higher.

**A home with pets.** Look for washable pre-filters and strong airflow, and expect to clean the pre-filter more often.

**A home with allergies.** Choose true HEPA and check filter replacement availability.

## What to Check on the Listing

Confirm the CADR, the recommended room area, the filter type and the replacement cost. See whether the noise ratings are given for each speed, and check the warranty.
