## What to Check the Moment the Sale Opens

**Your pre-built Wishlist:** If you've added target products in the weeks before, check their sale pricing first — this is faster than re-searching from scratch and lets you compare against the price history you've already tracked.

**"Deal of the Day" and "Lightning Deals" sections:** These carry the steepest, most time-limited discounts, often on premium electronics, but stock is genuinely limited and can sell out in under an hour for popular items.

**Prime Early Access:** If you're a Prime member, the 24-48 hour early window before general public access is worth using specifically for competitive, fast-selling categories like flagship laptops and TVs.

## Verifying Genuine Discounts in Real Time

**Cross-check with Keepa or similar price-history tools:** A "40% off" badge on a product that was at that same price two weeks ago isn't a real sale discount — always verify against actual price history, not just the displayed strikethrough price.

**Compare against Flipkart simultaneously:** Running sales on both platforms mean prices can differ meaningfully for the same product — a quick tab-switch comparison before purchasing takes seconds.

**Read recent reviews for the specific listing:** Sometimes a heavily discounted listing is an older product revision or has different specifications than the current-generation model — verify you're buying what you think you're buying.

## Categories That Sell Out Fastest

Premium laptops (RTX 4070+ gaming laptops, MacBook Air/Pro), large-format OLED and Neo QLED TVs (65-inch+), and flagship smartphones consistently show the fastest stock depletion on their best-discounted configurations — if these are your target purchases, don't wait past the first day of the sale.

## Recommendation

Treat the sale's opening window as the priority period for high-value, high-demand purchases, and use the remaining sale days for less time-sensitive categories where stock and pricing tend to stay consistent throughout the full sale period.
