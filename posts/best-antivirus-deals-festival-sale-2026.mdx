## What Actually Matters When Choosing

**Number of devices covered**, not just the antivirus engine itself — most households need protection across 3-5 devices, making per-device pricing the real comparison point.

**Included VPN and identity protection** add real value if you'd otherwise pay for these separately, common in Norton and McAfee's higher-tier plans.

**Renewal pricing after year one.** Some plans jump significantly at renewal — check this before assuming the festival price repeats automatically next year.
