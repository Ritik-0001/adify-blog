## Wet Grinder vs Mixer Grinder for Batter

**Wet grinder wins for:**
- Better batter quality (cooler, fluffier ground batter ferments better)
- Large batch grinding (2–3 litres at once without stopping)
- Softer idlis and crispier dosas
- Less effort — add ingredients, switch on, return in 20 minutes

**Mixer wins for:**
- Small batches (1–2 cups batter)
- Homes where dosas are made 1–2 times a week only
- Space-constrained kitchens
- Non-South Indian cooking households

For families making idli-dosa 3+ times per week in quantities for 4+ people, a wet grinder pays for itself in batter quality and convenience within months.
