## Choosing Between Air and Pro

**Choose the MacBook Air M3** if your work is writing, browsing, coding in lighter languages, or general productivity — it handles all of this smoothly without the added cost of Pro-specific features.

**Choose the MacBook Pro M4** if you regularly edit video, work with large creative files, or run demanding development workloads where the better cooling and additional ports genuinely matter.

**Consider the previous-generation Air (M2)** if the price gap to M3 doesn't reflect a performance difference that matters for your actual workload — it remains a capable machine for most everyday use.
