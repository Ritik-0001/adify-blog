## ANC Earbuds vs ANC Headphones Under ₹5000

ANC headphones (JBL Tune 660NC, boAt Rockerz 550) outperform earbuds at this price for noise cancellation because:
- Larger earcup creates better physical isolation
- More room for microphones and ANC processor
- Over-ear seal blocks more ambient sound passively

Choose earbuds if:
- Portability and pocket size matter
- You want lighter weight (5–7g earbuds vs 200g+ headphones)
- You use them during workouts (sweat resistance is better in earbuds)
- You find over-ear headphones uncomfortable

Choose headphones if:
- Maximum ANC depth is the priority
- You use them primarily at a desk
- Comfort for 4+ hour sessions matters

**Recommendation:** Sony WF-C700N for the best ANC in a true wireless earbud under ₹5,000. JBL Tune 225NC for bass-forward listeners. Samsung Galaxy Buds FE for Galaxy phone users.
