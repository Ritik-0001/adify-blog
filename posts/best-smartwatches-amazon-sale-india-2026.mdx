## Smartwatch Deal Breakdown by Category

### Best for iPhone Users: Apple Watch SE 2

At GIF expected price of ₹21,990–₂₃,990, the Apple Watch SE 2 offers:
- Always-on heart rate monitoring
- Fall detection and crash detection
- Emergency SOS and international emergency calling
- Sleep tracking
- watchOS integration with iPhone (notifications, Apple Pay, etc.)

The SE 2 lacks some Series 10 features (ECG, blood oxygen, always-on display) but delivers the core Apple Watch health and safety functionality at a significantly lower price.

### Best for Samsung Galaxy Users: Galaxy Watch 6

At GIF expected price of ₹18,999–₂₁,999, the Galaxy Watch 6 offers:
- Advanced sleep coaching (Galaxy AI)
- Body Composition measurement (BIA sensor)
- 3nm processor (fastest Galaxy Watch chip)
- Full Samsung ecosystem integration

Requires Samsung Health app; works best with Galaxy smartphones.

### Best Budget: CMF Watch Pro 2 / Noise ColorFit

At GIF expected ₹2,000–₄,500, Indian-brand budget smartwatches deliver basic health tracking (step counter, heart rate, SpO2, sleep tracking) and notification display. For buyers who primarily want fitness tracking without smart features, these are adequate.
