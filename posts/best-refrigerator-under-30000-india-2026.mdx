## Buying Guide for Indian Refrigerators

**Size:** 250–300L for 2–4 person families is the sweet spot. Smaller = more frequent grocery trips; larger = wasted cooling energy.

**Inverter compressor:** Essential for Indian power fluctuations and electricity savings. Fixed-speed compressors are cheaper upfront but cost more in electricity — payback on inverter models is typically 2–3 years.

**Star rating:** 5-star refrigerators cost ₹1,500–₂,500 more per year less in electricity than 3-star. Over 10 years, this is ₹15,000–₂₅,000 saved.

**Warranty:** Compressor warranty matters most — compressor replacement is ₹8,000–₁₅,000. LG's 10-year compressor warranty is the industry leader.

**Diwali discount:** Refrigerators typically see ₹3,000–₆,000 off during festive sales. Exchange offers for old refrigerators can provide additional ₹2,000–₄,000.

**Recommendation:** Samsung 253L for buyers who want best brand reliability at this budget. LG 260L if a 10-year compressor warranty matters for long-term peace of mind.
