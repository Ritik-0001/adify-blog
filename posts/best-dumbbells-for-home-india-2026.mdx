## Types of Home Dumbbells in India

**Rubber Coated Hex Dumbbells (Recommended):**
- Cast iron core with rubber coating
- Hex shape prevents rolling, protects floors
- Accurate weight (±2%)
- Lifetime durability
- Price: ₹60–₁₀₀/kg

**Vinyl Sand-Filled Dumbbells (Avoid):**
- Sand filling inside vinyl casing
- Cheaper but weight inaccurate (±10–15%)
- Sand settles unevenly over time
- Casing cracks with regular use
- Price: ₹30–₅₀/kg

**Neoprene Dumbbells:**
- For light weights only (0.5–5kg)
- Used in yoga, rehabilitation, and aerobic exercise
- Good grip, colourful, comfortable
- Not suitable for serious strength training

**Adjustable Dumbbell Sets:**
- One pair, multiple weight settings
- Saves space vs multiple fixed pairs
- Selectorized (Bowflex, LifeFitness): expensive (₹30,000+)
- Pin-adjustable handle + plates: budget-friendly (₹2,000–₄,000)
