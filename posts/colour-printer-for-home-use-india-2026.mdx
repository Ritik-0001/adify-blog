## What Makes Color Printing Accurate

**Four or five-color ink systems** (cyan, magenta, yellow, black, and sometimes a second black or gray) produce more accurate color gradients than basic three-color systems found in some budget printers.

**Genuine ink tank technology** (not cartridge, even color cartridge) keeps per-page cost low enough that you won't hesitate before printing colorful content.
