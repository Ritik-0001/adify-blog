## Laser vs Inkjet: The Right Choice for India

**Cost per page:** Laser toner cartridges cost ₹1,500–₹3,000 but print 1,000–3,000 pages. Cost per page: ₹0.30–₹0.50. Inkjet cartridges at ₹500–₹700 print 150–300 pages: ₹1.50–₹3.00 per page. For 100+ pages per month, laser pays back within 3–6 months.

**Ink drying:** Inkjet printers that sit unused for 2+ weeks can clog their print heads — a common problem in India where home printers are used inconsistently. Laser printers have no this problem; toner doesn't dry out.

**Speed:** Laser printers print 20–28 pages per minute. Inkjet averages 8–15 pages per minute.

**Limitation:** Laser printers under ₹15,000 are monochrome (black and white only). Colour laser printers start at ₹25,000+.
