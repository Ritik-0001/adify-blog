## Epson EcoTank vs Canon MegaTank — Main Differences

Both brands use refillable tank systems. Key differences:

**Epson EcoTank:** More colour accuracy for documents and graphics. Wider product range (budget to professional). Ink bottles are slightly more widely available in India. Pigment-based black on some models (faster drying, smudge-resistant).

**Canon MegaTank (PIXMA G series):** Better photo colour reproduction on glossy paper. Warmer, more saturated colours. Canon's customer service is slightly more responsive in South India.

For most users the difference is minor — choose based on price and features rather than brand.
