## Storage vs Instant Geyser

**Storage Geyser:**
- Tank holds 10–25L of pre-heated water
- Heating time: 15–30 minutes (pre-heat before bath)
- Continuous hot water flow once heated
- Higher wattage (2000W) but thermostat cycling reduces running cost
- Takes wall space for tank mounting
- Best for: families, multiple showers

**Instant Geyser:**
- No storage tank — heats water as it flows through
- Hot water available in 30–60 seconds
- Limited flow rate: 3–6 litres/minute
- Higher wattage (3000–4500W) running continuously
- Compact — small wall box
- Best for: single users, kitchens, handwash
