## Front Load vs Top Load — India Comparison

| Factor | Front Load | Top Load |
|--------|-----------|----------|
| Wash quality | Superior | Good |
| Water usage | 35–40% less | Standard |
| Electricity | Less | More |
| Upfront cost | ₹28,000–₅₀,000+ | ₹15,000–₂₅,000 |
| Cycle time | 60–90 min | 40–50 min |
| Add mid-cycle | No | Yes |
| Maintenance | Higher | Lower |

**Choose front load if:** Budget extends to ₹30,000+, you have delicate fabrics (silk, wool, cottons), and water conservation matters.

**Choose top load if:** Budget is under ₹25,000, convenience matters (short cycles, mid-cycle additions), and maintenance simplicity is valued.

## Capacity Guide for Indian Families

- **2–3 people:** 6–7kg capacity
- **4–5 people:** 7–8kg capacity
- **6+ people:** 9–11kg or two wash cycles per day

Indian families tend to wash clothes less frequently but in larger loads. Size up by 1kg from your estimate — it's better to have capacity headroom.
