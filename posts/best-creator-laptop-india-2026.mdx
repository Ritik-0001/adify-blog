## What Makes a Laptop "Creator-Grade"

**Factory color calibration:** Pantone Validated or similar certification means the display ships accurate out of the box — critical for photographers and designers whose client deliverables depend on color trust.

**Wide color gamut:** 100% DCI-P3 or Adobe RGB coverage (versus the 60-70% sRGB of typical laptops) shows the full range colorists and photo editors need to see.

**GPU balance:** Creator laptops typically pair moderate GPUs (RTX 4060/4070) with strong CPUs and RAM — full gaming-tier GPUs (4080/4090) matter less than sustained multi-core CPU performance for most creative software.
