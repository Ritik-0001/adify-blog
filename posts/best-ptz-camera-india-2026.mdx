## PTZ vs Fixed Cameras — When to Use Each

**Use PTZ when:**
- Area is too large for one fixed camera to cover effectively
- You want to actively follow movement (auto-tracking or manual control)
- Budget is tight — one PTZ instead of three fixed cameras
- Outdoor area with large perimeter (parking, grounds)

**Use fixed cameras when:**
- Coverage area is defined and consistent (doorway, cash counter)
- Always-on recording needed (PTZ covering one area may miss another)
- Simpler installation and maintenance
- Multiple critical points need simultaneous recording

## PTZ Installation Notes for India

PTZ cameras require stable network connection and power. For PoE PTZ cameras, a PoE switch rated at 25W+ per port is required (many PTZ cameras draw more power than standard PoE cameras).

In areas with power fluctuations, use a UPS (₹2,000–₄,000) with the NVR/switch to avoid camera reboots during power cuts.
