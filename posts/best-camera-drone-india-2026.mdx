## DGCA Registration for Commercial Drone Use

All commercial drone use in India (weddings, real estate, events) requires:
1. Drone UIN (Unique Identification Number) from Digital Sky portal — even for Nano drones
2. Remote Pilot Certificate (RPC) for drones above 250g
3. Permission via NPNT (No Permission, No Takeoff) system for each flight in controlled areas

**Recommendation:** DJI Mini 4 Pro for enthusiasts and content creators — best image quality within Nano regulatory simplicity. DJI Air 3 for professionals who need dual cameras and are willing to handle DGCA licensing properly.
