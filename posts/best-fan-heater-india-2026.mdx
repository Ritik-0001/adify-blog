## Fan Heater Electricity Cost

A 2000W fan heater running for 2 hours costs: 2000W × 2h = 4 units × ₹7 = ₹28.

For daily winter use (2 hours/day, 4 months): ₹28 × 120 days = ₹3,360/season.

An oil-filled radiator running on thermostat (averaging 900W effective consumption) for 8 hours/night: 900W × 8h = 7.2 units × ₹7 = ₹50/night × 120 nights = ₹6,000/season.

In practice: fan heater for short bursts (morning routine, 1–2 hours) + OFR for overnight bedroom = most cost-effective combination.
