## Inkjet vs Laser: Which Under ₹20,000?

**Choose laser if:** You print primarily text documents (invoices, contracts, reports) at high volume, need fast print speed, don't need colour.

**Choose inkjet (EcoTank) if:** You need colour printing, lower volume (under 500 pages/month), and want the lowest long-term running cost for mixed colour and mono printing.
