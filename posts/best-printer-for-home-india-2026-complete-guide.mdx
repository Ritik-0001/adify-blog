## Ink Tank vs. Cartridge: The Decision That Matters Most

A ₹3,500 cartridge printer looks cheaper upfront than a ₹10,500 ink tank printer, but at 500 pages/month, the cartridge printer costs roughly ₹24,000/year in ink versus ₹900/year for the ink tank model — the "cheap" printer becomes dramatically more expensive within 6-8 months of regular use.

## What to Check Before Buying

**Monthly print volume:** Under 20 pages/month, a basic cartridge printer's higher per-page cost may not matter enough to justify the higher upfront ink tank cost. Above 50 pages/month, ink tank pays for itself within a year.

**Wireless printing:** Wi-Fi lets multiple household devices print without cable connections — worth prioritizing for families.

**All-in-one vs. print-only:** Scan and copy functions add convenience for households needing to digitize documents occasionally.
