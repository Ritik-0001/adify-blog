## How to Choose Which Appliance to Buy First

Start with the appliance that saves you the most daily effort or fails most often. An unreliable fridge is a food-safety issue, while a washing machine upgrade returns hours every week. A TV or a robot vacuum is a comfort upgrade rather than a necessity.

Check installation space before you fall for a model. Measure the door width, the ceiling height and the location of the water and power points. A fridge that does not fit through a door is a common regret.

Finally, compare running costs. Star ratings, inverter technology and water use affect your bills for a decade, so a slightly higher purchase price often pays back.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Buying on the headline price alone.** A low headline figure on a base variant can hide a smaller capacity or a lower star rating. Match the exact specifications before you compare.

**Ignoring installation.** Fridges, washers and dishwashers need space, plumbing and power points. Confirm free installation and whether the seller or the brand handles it.

**Forgetting the warranty details.** Look at the compressor or motor warranty separately from the product warranty, since they often differ.

## Quick FAQ

**Is Diwali a good time to buy appliances?** Festive sale periods are when many retailers and brands promote appliances, so prices can be attractive, but they vary by day and by seller. Watch the live price and buy when your shortlist model reaches a figure you are comfortable with.

**Should I pay extra for a higher star rating?** For appliances that run all day, such as a refrigerator, a better rating usually saves electricity over the years. For occasional-use items, the saving is smaller.

**Is it worth taking an extended warranty?** For large appliances, some buyers find it worthwhile. Read what it covers and compare the cost with the product price before deciding.

## Recommendation

For most families, replace the refrigerator or washing machine first, then upgrade the TV. Add a dishwasher or robot vacuum if your household has the space and the budget, and buy an air purifier if you live in a city with poor winter air.
