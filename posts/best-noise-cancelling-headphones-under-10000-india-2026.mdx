## Why ₹10,000 is a Meaningful ANC Threshold

ANC quality depends primarily on the DSP chip (noise cancellation processor) and the microphone system. Below ₹5,000, manufacturers use basic single-mic feedforward systems. The ₹7,000–₹10,000 range is where you start seeing:

- **Multi-microphone hybrid ANC** (feedforward + feedback): Significantly more effective at adapting to changing noise environments
- **Adaptive ANC**: Automatically adjusts cancellation depth based on ambient noise levels
- **Transparency mode**: Lets ambient sound through when needed (conversations, announcements)
- **Better microphone quality** for calls: Critical if you take calls on your commute

The Sony WH-CH720N and JBL Live 660NC are the two strongest options at this threshold.
