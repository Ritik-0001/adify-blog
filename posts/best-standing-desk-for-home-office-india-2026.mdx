## Size Considerations for Indian Apartments

Standard second bedroom in Indian 2-BHK: 3m × 3m to 3.5m × 3m.

**120cm × 60cm desktop:** Fits comfortably in most rooms. Enough space for laptop + external monitor + keyboard. Most popular choice.

**140cm × 70cm desktop:** Better for dual monitors or extensive paper-based work. Requires slightly more room and planning around furniture placement.

**160cm desktop:** Not recommended for standard Indian apartments unless you have a dedicated home office room.

**Check before buying:** Measure the wall where the desk will go. Allow 60cm depth for the desk + space behind for cables + 80cm working space in front.
