## Refrigerator Capacity Guide for Indian Families

| Family Size | Recommended Capacity |
|-------------|----------------------|
| 1–2 people (bachelor/couple) | 150–200 litres |
| 3–4 people | 200–300 litres |
| 4–6 people | 300–400 litres |
| Large joint family | 400–500+ litres |

**Single door vs double door:** Single door (direct cool) fridges are cheaper and more energy efficient for small capacities. Double door (frost free) fridges require no manual defrosting and offer better food organization with separate freezer compartment. Under ₹20,000, both types are available up to about 250–300 litres.

## Power Cut Performance — Important for India

During power cuts, refrigerators rely on insulation to maintain cold temperature. Indicators:
- Ice packs in freezer extend cold duration
- Don't open fridge frequently during power cuts
- Most Indian refrigerators maintain food-safe temperature for 2–6 hours without power (depends on insulation quality and how full the fridge is)

Godrej's Cool Balance claim of 3 hours is the longest in this category.
