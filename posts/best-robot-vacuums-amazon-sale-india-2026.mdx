## Indian Home Considerations for Robot Vacuums

**Tile and marble floors:** Most Indian homes have tile or marble — ideal for robot vacuums. Carpet navigation (common in Western home recommendations) is largely irrelevant.

**Pet hair:** Indian breeds (Indie dogs, Labradors common in flats) shed significantly. Choose 2500Pa+ suction for pet hair.

**Under-furniture clearance:** Traditional Indian home furniture has varying clearance. Measure the lowest piece — robot vacuums need 9–10cm clearance.

**Mop function:** Essential for Indian kitchens and dining areas where mopping is a daily ritual. Choose a model with mop function.

**Recommendation:** Mi Robot Vacuum-Mop 2 Pro for buyers entering the category under ₹15,000 at GIF. Roborock Q7 Max+ for buyers who want the best performance under ₹27,000.
