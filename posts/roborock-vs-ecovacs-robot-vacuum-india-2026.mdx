## Navigation and Mapping

Roborock robots typically use lidar with strong obstacle handling and multi-floor maps, and the brand has a reputation for consistent navigation. Ecovacs also uses lidar on many models and has its own mapping and obstacle recognition on higher-end versions.

In both cases, the model matters more than the brand. Entry-level models from either brand may map less precisely than mid-range ones.

## Suction and Cleaning

Both brands offer strong suction in mid-range models, measured in pascals on the listings. Higher numbers help with carpets and pet hair, but brush design and airflow also matter.

Check reviews for the specific model on your floor type, since real-world results depend on more than the number.

## Mopping

Roborock offers vibrating or rolling mop modules depending on the model. Ecovacs offers mop plates and, in higher models, dock-based washing and drying.

Robot mops wipe rather than scrub, so set expectations. The dock features can reduce manual cleaning.

## Docks and Automation

Ecovacs is often strong on self-emptying docks in the mid-range, which collect dust so you empty a bag every few weeks. Roborock offers docks on higher models such as the Q Revo, with washing and refilling.

Docks add cost and take floor space, but they reduce daily effort.

## App and Software

Roborock's app is widely regarded as polished, with clear room controls and schedules. Ecovacs's app is feature-rich with customisation options. Both support voice assistants.

## Price and Service

Prices overlap across mid-range models, typically ₹25,000–45,000. Check service coverage in your city, since spare parts such as brushes, filters and pads are ongoing needs.

## How to Choose Between Roborock and Ecovacs

Start with your floor type, budget and whether you want a self-emptying dock. Then compare specific models for navigation, suction and mop features. Read recent feedback on parts availability and after-sales support.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Buying on brand alone.** Model-level features vary widely.

**Ignoring consumable costs.** Filters, brushes and pads need replacing.

**Skipping the app check.** Make sure the app supports your phone and region.

## Quick FAQ

**Which has better navigation?** Roborock is generally strong, but it depends on the model.

**Which is better for pets?** Look at suction, brush design and dock features.

**Which is easier to maintain?** A self-emptying dock reduces manual emptying.

## Real-World Scenarios

**A small flat with mostly hard floors.** A mid-range model from either brand is enough. Prioritise navigation and a mop that suits tile.

**A larger home with multiple rooms.** Look for lidar mapping and multi-floor support so you can schedule zones and rooms.

**A home with pets.** Choose stronger suction and a tangle-resistant brush, and consider a self-emptying dock to reduce hair handling.

**A busy household that wants minimal effort.** A dock that empties dust and, at the higher end, washes the mop reduces manual work the most.

## Running Costs and Ownership

Filters, brushes and pads wear out, so check the price and availability of replacements. Look at warranty terms and service in your city, and keep the app updated for new features and bug fixes. Batteries also age, so plan for a possible replacement after a few years.

## Setting Up

Clear floors before the first run, set no-go zones in the app and let the robot map the house fully. Schedule cleaning for times when the house is empty, and empty the dustbin or dock regularly.

## What to Check on the Listing

Compare suction in pascals, battery life, dustbin and water tank size, and the navigation type. Confirm dock features, app compatibility and the warranty terms. Read recent reviews for real-world mapping accuracy and how the mop performs on your floor type.

## Noise, Battery and Everyday Use

Robot vacuums are audible but usually quieter than an upright vacuum, and most let you pick a quiet mode. Battery life varies by mode, with strong suction draining faster, so check whether the robot can recharge and resume, which suits larger homes.
