## Should You Pay for Antivirus at All?

For a single adult user who:
- Practices basic online hygiene (doesn't click unknown links, doesn't download pirated software)
- Keeps Windows updated
- Uses Chrome or Edge with SmartScreen enabled

Windows Defender is genuinely sufficient.

Pay for antivirus if:
- Family computer used by elderly or children who may click phishing links
- You do significant net banking and UPI transactions (Safe Banking feature is worth it)
- You connect USB drives from unknown sources regularly
- You want Hindi phone support when something goes wrong

**Recommendation:** Quick Heal AntiVirus Pro for most Indian paid antivirus buyers. Kaspersky Standard if you want maximum detection rates. Windows Defender for careful individual users who don't need extra features.
