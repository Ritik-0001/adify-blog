## Which ThinkPad Sub-Line to Choose

**Executives and frequent travelers wanting maximum portability:** ThinkPad X1 Carbon — lightest weight, most premium materials.

**Standard business/office use on a moderate budget:** ThinkPad T14 — same durability and typing experience, lower price.

**Engineers, architects needing certified GPU performance:** ThinkPad P1 — the only ThinkPad line with genuine workstation GPU options.

Every ThinkPad shares the same core DNA: exceptional keyboards, rigorous durability testing, and enterprise security features. The sub-line choice comes down to how much you value ultra-portability versus budget versus GPU-accelerated professional software needs.
