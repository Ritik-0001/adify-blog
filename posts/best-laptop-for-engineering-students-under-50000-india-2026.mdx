## Why ₹50,000 Is the Real Engineering Student Budget

At ₹40,000, you're mostly getting U-series CPUs with integrated graphics. At ₹50,000:

- **H-series CPUs** — Ryzen 5 6600H, Core i5-12500H — 45W TDP chips that sustain performance under load (compilation, simulation, rendering)
- **Entry-level discrete GPU** — RTX 3050 or 3050 Ti — required for SolidWorks 3D mode, ANSYS simulation, and CUDA-accelerated ML training
- **16GB RAM standard** — no compromise on multitasking
- **Better cooling** — thicker chassis, more heat pipes = sustained clock speeds

If you're in Mechanical, Civil, or Aerospace Engineering with CAD-heavy labs, ₹50,000 is where you should start your search, not ₹40,000.
