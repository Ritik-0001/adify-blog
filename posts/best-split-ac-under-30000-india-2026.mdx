## Choosing Room Size vs AC Tonnage

| Room Size | Recommended Tonnage |
|-----------|---------------------|
| Up to 100 sq ft (small bedroom) | 0.75 ton |
| 100–150 sq ft (standard bedroom) | 1 ton |
| 150–200 sq ft (large bedroom/living room) | 1.5 ton |
| 200–300 sq ft (large living room) | 2 ton |

**Important:** Undersizing causes AC to run continuously at maximum power (higher bill, less effective cooling). Oversizing causes frequent on/off cycling (less comfortable humidity control). Get the right tonnage for your room.
