## DGCA Drone Regulations India — Quick Overview

Under India's Drone Rules 2021 and subsequent amendments:

**Nano category (under 250g):**
- No pilot license required (Green zone flights)
- No registration required for recreational use
- Can fly without explicit ATC permission in most green zones
- Cannot fly over people, airports, or restricted areas

**Micro category (250g–2kg):**
- Remote Pilot Certificate (RPC) required
- Drone registration in Digital Sky portal required
- ATC permission required for all flights

**Key takeaway:** DJI Mini 3 (249g) stays in Nano category. Any drone 250g+ requires formal licensing and registration processes.

## India-Specific Buying Advice

**Buy from authorized channels:** DJI drones are sold through DJI's India website, Amazon, and authorized resellers (iStore, Imagine, etc.). Avoid grey market imports — warranty and repair support differ.

**Register on Digital Sky:** Even for Nano drones, registering at the Digital Sky portal (digitalskyapp.dgca.gov.in) is a good practice and required for commercial use.

**No-fly zones in India:** Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, and areas near airports have restricted airspace even for Nano drones. Use the Digital Sky Map app to verify before flying.

**GST on drones:** 18% GST applies. Factor this into your budget when comparing prices.

**Recommendation:** DJI Mini 3 is the sweet spot under ₹50,000 — Nano category compliance, excellent 4K video quality, and long flight time. If budget allows ₹60,000, the Mini 4 Pro's full obstacle sensing is worth the extra investment especially for beginners.
