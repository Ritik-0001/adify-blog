## When Laser Is Cheaper Than Inkjet

For businesses printing exclusively monochrome at very high volumes (3,000+ pages/month), a high-yield laser toner can match or beat EcoTank black cost per page while printing faster.

Example: Brother TN-2480 (₹4,500, 3,000 pages) = ₹1.50/page. EcoTank black = ₹0.07/page. EcoTank still wins, but laser has the speed advantage for volume.

**Colour laser is never cheaper than EcoTank** — colour laser toner sets cost ₹15,000–₂₅,000 for a fraction of the pages EcoTank ink bottles provide.

## Third-Party Ink Warning

Compatible/refill inks for EcoTank printers are available cheaply (₹100–₁₅₀ per bottle). They can work but risk:
- Print head clogging from inconsistent viscosity
- Colour accuracy issues
- Warranty voiding

For the small savings (₹150 vs ₹300 per bottle), original Epson/Canon ink is worth the premium. Running cost is already very low — save money on paper instead.

**Recommendation:** Epson EcoTank L3252 for wireless + cheap ink. Canon G2020 for the absolute lowest cost with USB-only connectivity. Either choice runs at a fraction of the cost of cartridge printers.
