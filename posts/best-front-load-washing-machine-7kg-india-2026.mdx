## How to Choose a 7 kg Front Load

A 7 kg drum suits two to four people, or roughly a load of clothes for a small family every day. If you regularly wash bulky items such as quilts, consider 8 kg instead.

Check the star rating and motor type. Inverter motors and direct drive designs tend to be quieter and more efficient, and the motor warranty is worth reading carefully.

Confirm installation. Front loaders need a level floor and a water inlet at the right height, and many brands include a demo. Ask about it before you order.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Skipping the base.** Front loaders vibrate during spin, so a solid floor or a stand matters for quiet operation.

**Ignoring hard water.** High TDS water leaves scale on the heater and drum. Choose a model with hard-water features and clean the machine regularly.

**Buying by spin speed alone.** A higher spin speed dries clothes faster but does not always mean better washing. Look at programmes and build quality.

## Quick FAQ

**Is 7 kg enough for a family of four?** It is workable, but 8 kg gives more room for bedding and towels.

**Front load or top load?** Front loaders use less water and are gentler on clothes, but they cost more and need a stable base.

**How long do they last?** With regular cleaning and stable water quality, many run for eight to ten years.
