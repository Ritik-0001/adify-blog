## Wattage Guide

**1000W:** Heats 15L water to 40–42°C in 8–10 minutes. For normal Indian winter mornings. Most budget models.

**1500W:** Heats 15L water to 40–42°C in 5–7 minutes. Faster. Better for cold December–January mornings when you can't wait. Slightly higher electricity cost per session.

**2000W:** Commercial grade. Heats water very fast. Not recommended for residential use — tripping domestic 16A circuits is a risk.

## Safe Immersion Rod Use

**Always unplug before removing from water:** The single most important safety rule. Touching water while the rod is plugged in causes electrocution.

**Don't use in overhead tank:** Immersion rods are for bucket use only — not designed for pressure tanks or geysers.

**Minimum water level:** Keep rod fully submerged while plugged in. Heating without water damages the element instantly.

**Hard water descaling:** Scale buildup from Indian hard water reduces heating efficiency and causes element failure. Soak in 1-part white vinegar + 2-parts water solution for 30 minutes monthly to dissolve limescale.

**Time check:** Set a phone timer when plugging in — overheat risk if left too long. Most modern rods have auto-cutout but timer is a backup.

**Recommendation:** Bajaj Majesty 1500W for most Indian buyers — reliable, ISI-marked, and available at every hardware store. Havells Instanio if you're in a hard-water city and want longer rod life.
