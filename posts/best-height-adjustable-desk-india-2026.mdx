## Single Motor vs Dual Motor: Why It Matters

**Single motor:** One motor drives both leg columns through a drive shaft. Works adequately for light loads (laptop + monitor, under 30kg). Motor strain increases at heavier loads; longevity concerns over 3–5 years of daily use.

**Dual motor:** Each leg column has its own motor. Stronger lift, smoother motion, handles heavy setups (multiple monitors, desktop PC, accessories, up to 70–80kg). Preferred for professional setups.

For Indian tech workers with a desktop or dual monitor setup, dual motor is the right choice. For laptop-only users, a quality single motor is adequate.

## Ergonomic Height Setup Guide

**Sitting height:** Elbows at 90° when hands rest on keyboard, feet flat on floor (or footrest), monitor top at eye level.

**Standing height:** Same — elbows at 90° for keyboard, monitor at or slightly below eye level.

For a 170cm tall user: Sitting height ~70–75cm, standing height ~100–110cm (varies with footwear).

**Anti-fatigue mat:** Essential accessory when standing — reduces foot and leg fatigue. Budget: ₹2,000–₄,000.

**Monitor arm:** Use a monitor arm (₹2,000–₄,000) rather than a fixed stand — allows quick monitor height adjustment as you switch between sitting and standing.

**Recommendation:** Flexispot E5 for users who will stand frequently (3+ height changes per day). Wipro Furniture for Indian corporate buyers needing local service. Manual crank if budget is under ₹12,000.
