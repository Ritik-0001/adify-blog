## Do You Need ISV Certification, or Will a Gaming Laptop Work?

**Professional engineers/architects working on client deliverables:** Get ISV-certified hardware. A crashed viewport mid-presentation or a rendering artifact in a client deliverable carries real professional cost.

**Students and hobbyists learning CAD:** A well-specced consumer laptop (RTX 4060/4070) runs the same software fine for learning purposes — certification matters far less when the stakes are a homework assignment, not a client contract.

## Software-Specific Considerations

**AutoCAD/Revit (Autodesk):** Officially certifies NVIDIA RTX A/Ada series across Dell, HP, and Lenovo workstation lines.

**SolidWorks:** Dassault Systèmes maintains its own certified hardware list — Dell Precision and HP ZBook consistently top it.

**Fusion 360:** More GPU-agnostic than desktop CAD tools; runs well on consumer RTX laptops, making the ASUS ProArt a reasonable choice for Fusion 360-heavy workflows.

**Recommendation:** Dell Precision 5680 for engineering firms and professionals needing full certification across multiple CAD platforms. HP ZBook Firefly 16 if portability to job sites matters. ASUS ProArt Studiobook 16 for freelancers wanting near-workstation performance at a lower price.
