## French Door vs Side-by-Side vs Top Freezer

**French door (₹55,000–₁,50,000):** Wide refrigerator at eye level (two doors swing open), freezer drawer at bottom. Best food accessibility, maximum fresh storage space. Most premium look.

**Side-by-side (₹45,000–₁,00,000):** Freezer and fridge side by side, full height. Good for families that use freezer heavily. Narrower shelves (less suited for wide Indian platters and large cooking vessels).

**Double door top freezer (₹15,000–₄₀,000):** Freezer on top, fridge below. Most common in India. Best value for money. Less convenient (must bend to access fresh food section).

**French door advantage in Indian context:** Wide shelves accommodate large pressure cookers, wide thalis, and heavy vessels. Eye-level fresh food access means you see everything and waste less.

## French Door Buying Checklist for India

**Measure first:** French door fridges are wide (80–90 cm). Verify your kitchen width, the path from main door to kitchen, and any corridor/doorway clearance.

**Water dispenser:** Requires permanent water connection (flexible hose) or manual refill. Check if your kitchen layout accommodates.

**Voltage stabilizer:** Most premium fridges include stabilizer-free operation. Verify range in specs.

**Installation:** Complex fridge models (water dispenser, ice maker) need professional installation beyond basic placement.

**After-sales:** Samsung and LG have good metro service. Verify service availability in your city.

**Recommendation:** Samsung RF57 670L for large families who entertain — triple cooling and SpaceMax give the most functional space. LG InstaView for smart-home integrated kitchens. Both are excellent choices in the ₹70,000–₉₅,000 range.
