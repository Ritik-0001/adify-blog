## How to Spend Your ₹10,000

Start with the relationship. Close friends and relatives can spend near the top of the range, while colleagues and acquaintances can choose items at the lower end.

Pick useful over trendy. Items that are used every day, such as cookware or a mixer grinder, are remembered longer than novelty gadgets.

Splitting the budget also works. A mid-range kettle and toaster plus a nice set of serving bowls often feels richer than one plain box at the same total.

## Making a Modest Gift Feel Bigger

Pair a cookware set with a handwritten recipe card, add a small box of gourmet tea to a kettle combo, or choose bedding in colours you know the couple likes. A short note on why you picked the item makes a simple gift memorable, and a gift receipt lets them exchange it without awkwardness.

## Who Should Skip Bedding as a Gift

Bedding is the riskiest item on this list for anyone who has not seen the couple's bedroom. Mattress size, colour scheme and fabric preferences are personal, and a set that does not fit is usually a wasted gift. If you are not close enough to know, choose cookware, a mixer grinder or an air fryer, which work in any kitchen.

## Who Should Skip This Budget

If the couple has publicly shared a registry, or you are close family expected to give something substantial, a gift under ₹10,000 may land as too modest, and pooling money with relatives for one larger item is the better move. Skip the air fryer if you know their kitchen is tiny or they already received one; a cookware set is the safer duplicate-proof choice. For colleagues and distant acquaintances, a single well-chosen item from this list is plenty, and adding more only makes the gift feel awkward.

## Recommendation

For most couples, an air fryer or a mixer grinder is the safest pick. Add a cookware set if you want to give a fuller kitchen start. Compare specifications on each listing and keep the invoice.
