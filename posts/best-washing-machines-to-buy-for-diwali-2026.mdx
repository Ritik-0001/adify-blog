## How to Choose a Washing Machine

Choose the type first. Front-loads use less water and treat clothes gently, but cost more and need a stable base. Top-loads are cheaper and easier to use for adding clothes mid-cycle, but use more water.

Pick capacity by household size: 6 to 7 kg for two to three people, 8 kg for four, and more for larger families. Check the service network, because a great machine with weak support is a poor purchase.

Check installation. Front-loaders need a level floor and a water inlet nearby. Confirm what the seller or brand includes.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Choosing capacity by price.** An undersized drum leads to overloading, which strains the motor and cleans poorly. Pick capacity for your household, not for the lowest price.

**Ignoring water pressure and hardness.** Front-loaders need steady water pressure, and hard water can leave residue on clothes and machines. Some brands offer hard-water features, so check the listing.

**Skipping the demo and installation.** Ask whether installation and a demonstration are included, and confirm the service centre nearest to you.

**Overlooking the base.** Front-loaders vibrate, so a level, stable floor or a stand is important for quiet operation.

## Quick FAQ

**Front-load or top-load?** Front-loads use less water and are generally gentler on clothes, but cost more. Top-loads are easier for adding clothes mid-cycle and are usually cheaper.

**Is a washer-dryer worth it?** It suits flats with no space to dry clothes, but it takes longer to dry and costs more. If you have a balcony or terrace, a separate washer is often enough.

**How long do washing machines last?** With regular cleaning and stable water quality, many last eight to ten years, but this varies by brand and use.
