## The One Rule for Student MacBooks: Never Buy 8GB RAM

Apple's base MacBook Air configuration ships with 8GB unified memory — this was adequate in 2020 but is now a real bottleneck. Modern web apps (Canvas, Google Docs with extensions, Notion), combined with typical student multitasking, push 8GB Macs into memory swapping, which causes visible lag. The 16GB upgrade costs roughly ₹15,000–₹20,000 more but transforms the day-to-day experience — treat this as mandatory, not optional.

## Do Students Need a MacBook Pro Instead?

Only if you're studying film editing, 3D animation, music production, or computer science with heavy compilation workloads. For business, humanities, most engineering, medicine, and general coursework, the MacBook Air's fanless design and battery life serve better than the Pro's extra power most students won't use daily.

**Recommendation:** MacBook Air M3 13-inch 16GB for most students. MacBook Air M2 16GB if budget is the primary constraint — the performance difference is marginal for coursework. Always prioritize the 16GB RAM upgrade over chip generation.
