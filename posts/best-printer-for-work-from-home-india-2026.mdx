## What WFH Workers Actually Need from a Printer

**Wi-Fi is non-negotiable:** WFH professionals switch between phone, laptop, and desktop. Wireless printing from any device on the home network is essential.

**Scan for signing documents:** Send-for-signature is common, but for forms, agreements, and legal documents, print-sign-scan is still frequently required. A flatbed scanner is essential.

**Compact size:** Home setups don't have dedicated server rooms. The printer needs to fit on a desk shelf or side table.

**Low running cost:** WFH printing is moderate volume (not enterprise scale) but still adds up. Cartridge printers at ₹4–₅/page cost more than they should for home use.

## WFH Setup Tips

**Place near your router:** Wi-Fi printing is faster and more reliable when the printer is within 5 metres of the router. Walls reduce signal.

**Use the manufacturer's app:** Epson iPrint and HP Smart app provide better mobile printing experience than generic AirPrint for advanced features.

**Keep spare ink/toner:** Order a replacement bottle/cartridge before the current one runs out — don't get caught needing to print a time-sensitive document with no ink.

**Recommendation:** Epson EcoTank L3252 for most WFH professionals — the colour capability, low running cost, and scanner make it the most versatile WFH printer. HP M136nw for pure document printing with faster output and lower upfront cost.
