## Brand-by-Brand Strengths

### HP — Best for Mono Laser Printing

HP dominates the laser printer category in India. The HP LaserJet MFP series (M136nw, M236sdw) are the bestselling office mono laser printers in the country with good reason — fast, reliable, and HP's service centers are in virtually every Indian city.

HP cartridges are widely available offline (Staples, local stationery shops, Amazon) — even in Tier 2/3 cities. This offline availability matters when you need a toner urgently.

HP's inkjet range (DeskJet, Smart Tank) is less impressive — expensive cartridges on DeskJet models and the Smart Tank running costs are slightly higher than equivalent Epson EcoTank.

**Best HP buy:** HP LaserJet MFP M236sdw (₹14,000–₁₆,000)

### Epson — Best for Low Cost Inkjet Printing

Epson pioneered the EcoTank concept in India and still leads on ink tank printers. The EcoTank L-series (L3252, L6490, L8050) provide the most cost-effective inkjet printing available — colour prints at under ₹0.30 per page versus ₹6–₈ for cartridge competitors.

Epson's print quality for documents and general colour printing is excellent. Photo quality on the standard EcoTank is good but not class-leading — that distinction goes to Canon.

Epson ink bottles (001 series) are broadly available across India, including on Amazon for home delivery.

**Best Epson buy:** Epson EcoTank L3252 (₹13,000–₁₅,000)

[Check Epson EcoTank on Amazon](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=epson+ecotank+l3252+india&tag=adifystore-21)

### Canon — Best for Photo Printing

Canon's PIXMA series consistently produces the most accurate, vivid photo prints among consumer inkjet printers. The colour management in Canon photo printers (PIXMA TS8370, Pro-200) is optimized for professional photo output.

Canon MegaTank (G-series) matches Epson EcoTank on running cost while offering slightly warmer colour tones that many users prefer for photos.

Canon's service network in South India (Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Kerala, Andhra) is particularly strong — often stronger than HP or Epson in those states.

**Best Canon buy:** Canon PIXMA MegaTank G3770 (₹12,000–₁₄,000) for running cost; Canon PIXMA TS8370 (₹18,000–₂₂,000) for photo quality

## Service Centre Coverage India

**HP:** Service centers in every state capital + most Tier 2 cities. Toll-free support. Widest coverage.

**Epson:** Good metro coverage. Tier 2/3 coverage is decent via authorized service partners. Ink bottles available at Amazon even where local service is limited.

**Canon:** Strong in South India and metros. Tier 2 coverage varies by state.

---

## Which to Avoid

**HP inkjet (DeskJet):** Expensive cartridges, poor value vs EcoTank alternatives.

**Generic/budget brands (Pantum, TSC):** Limited service, availability issues, inconsistent print quality.

**Recommendation:** Buy Epson EcoTank for home and light office colour use. Buy HP LaserJet for document-heavy offices. Buy Canon for photo printing or if you're in South India where Canon service is strongest.
