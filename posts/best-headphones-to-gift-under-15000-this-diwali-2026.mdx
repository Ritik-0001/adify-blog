## How to Choose Headphones to Gift

Pick between over-ear and earbuds. Over-ear headphones are generally more comfortable for long listening and offer stronger ANC; earbuds are more portable.

Check phone compatibility. AirPods work best with iPhone, while Sony, JBL and Soundcore work well on both platforms.

Look at battery life, ANC quality and codec support if the recipient has a phone that supports high-quality audio.

## Quick FAQ

**Is noise cancelling worth it?** For commuters and open offices, yes. It reduces constant background noise and makes music easier to enjoy at lower volume.

**Wired or wireless?** Wireless is convenient for daily use; wired can be useful for audio work, and some headphones support both.

**How long do headphones last?** With care, several years, though batteries wear over time.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Assuming all noise cancelling is equal.** Budget ANC reduces steady noise such as engines and fans but is less effective on voices and sudden sounds. Set expectations and compare reviews.

**Ignoring comfort.** Headphones are worn for hours. Weight, clamp force and ear cup size decide whether they get used. If you are unsure, choose a lighter model with softer cushions.

**Overlooking the codec and phone.** Some phones support higher-quality Bluetooth codecs, but many listeners will not hear the difference. Prioritise fit, battery and call quality.

**Forgetting the case and cable.** A hard case or pouch protects the gift, and a 3.5 mm cable can be useful on flights or for wired use.

## Where to Buy and What to Check

Buy from the brand's official store or an Amazon-fulfilled listing, since headphones are often affected by counterfeit or grey-market stock. Check that the box includes the warranty card, and register the product with the brand if it offers an app.

Read the return policy before you buy. Headphones are a personal fit item, and being able to swap them if they do not suit matters.

## Gift Presentation Tips

Headphones look good in a box, so keep the original packaging and add a small pouch or hard case. Pair the gift with a gift receipt tucked into the box in case the fit is not right. If the recipient uses a streaming service, a short note about a playlist you think they will like turns a gadget into a personal present.
