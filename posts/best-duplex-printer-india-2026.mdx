## Manual vs Automatic Duplex

**Manual duplex:** Printer prints one side, pauses, you manually flip pages and reload, printer prints the second side. Slow, error-prone, tiring for more than 10 pages.

**Automatic duplex:** Printer has an internal mechanism that flips pages automatically. Set "duplex" in print settings, press print, walk away. Properly formatted duplex output every time.

All duplex printers listed here are automatic.
