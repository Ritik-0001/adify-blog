## What Student Use Actually Requires

**Engineering / CS students:** Python, Java, C++, Android Studio, VS Code, MATLAB. Need 16GB RAM for running local servers and IDEs simultaneously. Fast SSD speeds up compilation.

**MBA / Commerce students:** Excel, PowerPoint, Google Workspace, Zoom. 8GB RAM is adequate, 16GB is better for longevity. Office tasks don't require a powerful CPU.

**Arts / Design students:** Photoshop, Illustrator, Premiere, Canva. Benefit from a good display (colour accuracy matters), 16GB RAM, and SSD for video editing.

**Medical / Science students:** Notes, PDFs, reference management (Mendeley, Zotero), Zoom. Light requirements — any 16GB laptop works.

**Battery life** is critical for students who move between classrooms, libraries, and canteens. 8+ hours real-world battery is the practical target.

## Student Laptop Buying Checklist

Before buying any student laptop:
- **16GB RAM** — don't buy 8GB at ₹50,000+; it will feel slow within 2 years
- **512GB SSD** — minimum for projects, apps, and media storage  
- **MIL-STD durability** — if listed, it indicates the laptop has passed drop and vibration tests
- **Warranty:** Check if on-site or carry-in service. On-site is better for students in tier-2 cities
- **USB-A ports:** Students still use pen drives and USB accessories; check for at least 1 USB-A port

**Recommendation:** IdeaPad Slim 5 Ryzen 7 for engineering and CS students (performance + battery). VivoBook 15 OLED for design and arts students (display quality). HP 14s for students in cities with HP service support.
