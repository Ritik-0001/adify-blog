## How to Choose a Gift Under ₹5,000

Think in three lanes: entertainment, convenience and personal use. Entertainment gifts such as a smart speaker, a streaming stick or headphones suit almost anyone who spends time at home. Convenience gifts, such as a blender or a fitness band, suit busy people. Personal-use gifts, such as a Bluetooth speaker, are best for one specific person.

Check the ecosystem. An Echo Dot is most useful in a home with Wi-Fi and an Amazon account; a Fire TV Stick needs an HDMI port on the television; a fitness band needs a smartphone to sync. A gift that arrives without the right setup can end up unused.

Keep receipts and warranty details handy. If the gift is a surprise, use Amazon's gift options so the price is hidden and returns stay simple. If you are unsure, an Amazon gift card can be a fallback, but a chosen gift usually feels more thoughtful.
