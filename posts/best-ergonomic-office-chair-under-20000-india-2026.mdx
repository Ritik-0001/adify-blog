## Indian Buyers' Checklist for This Budget

When buying at this price, verify these features are present:
- Adjustable lumbar (not just a fixed cushion)
- 4D or at minimum 2D (height) armrests
- Seat height range covers 42–52cm (for Indian height range)
- Reclining mechanism with tilt lock
- 2+ year warranty

Features that add cost without proportional benefit at this price: built-in massage function, RGB lighting, gaming chair aesthetics. Avoid gaming chairs under ₹20,000 — they prioritise looks over ergonomics.

**Recommendation:** Green Soul Beast Pro for most Indian buyers — comprehensive features, competitive price, and an established track record in the Indian market. Godrej Interio Optima if brand heritage and showroom experience matter.
