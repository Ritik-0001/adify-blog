## How to Choose a Phone to Gift

Choose the recipient's priorities first. Parents usually value a big, readable screen, reliable service and a simple interface. Students value performance, battery and cameras. A gift for someone using an old phone is best when the upgrade is obvious.

Check software support. Phones from Samsung, Google and OnePlus have publicly stated update policies, and longer support keeps a phone secure and useful for years.

Consider service and accessories. A wider service network is helpful if the phone needs a repair, and a case and screen protector are small add-ons that show care.

## Quick FAQ

**Should I buy the phone myself or give cash?** A chosen phone shows thought, but cash or a gift card avoids a mismatch if the recipient has strong preferences. For parents or relatives who ask for help, choosing is usually appreciated.

**How much storage is enough?** One hundred and twenty-eight gigabytes covers most users, but 256GB is more comfortable if the person takes many photos or videos.

**Is 5G worth having?** Yes, for a phone that will be used for several years. Coverage is spreading, and 5G support is standard in this price range.
