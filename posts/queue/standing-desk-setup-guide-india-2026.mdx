Setting up a standing desk correctly in India in 2026 means positioning your monitor at eye level in both sitting and standing modes, keeping elbows at roughly 90 degrees while typing, using an anti-fatigue mat, and gradually building up standing time rather than standing for hours immediately — skipping these steps causes new discomfort instead of the relief the desk is meant to provide.

Many new standing desk owners experience disappointing results within the first few weeks specifically because they set up the desk incorrectly or transition too aggressively, leading to foot and back discomfort that feels like the desk "doesn't work" when actually the setup or routine needs adjustment.

## Correct Monitor Positioning

**Eye level at the top third of the screen**, whether sitting or standing — a monitor arm makes maintaining this correct position across both heights practical, since a fixed monitor stand can only be correctly positioned for one height.

**Arm's length distance** from your eyes to the screen, roughly 50-70cm, regardless of sitting or standing position.

---

## Correct Keyboard and Elbow Position

**Elbows at roughly 90 degrees** when typing, in both sitting and standing modes — adjust desk height specifically to achieve this, not just to a "comfortable-looking" height.

**Wrists in a neutral, straight position** — avoid bending wrists upward or downward while typing.

---

## Building a Standing Routine Gradually

**Week 1-2:** Start with 30-45 minutes standing per hour, alternating with sitting.

**Week 3-4:** Gradually increase standing duration as your body adapts, using an anti-fatigue mat throughout.

**Ongoing:** Aim for a genuine mix of sitting and standing throughout the day rather than extremes of either — standing all day creates its own strain issues.

---

## Essential Supporting Equipment

**Anti-fatigue mat:** Genuinely necessary for comfortable extended standing on hard floors.

**Monitor arm:** Enables correct eye-level positioning across both sitting and standing heights.

## Recommendation

Treat the setup and routine as equally important as the desk purchase itself — correct monitor height, gradual standing time increases, and an anti-fatigue mat together determine whether your standing desk delivers genuine ergonomic benefit or creates new discomfort from improper use.
