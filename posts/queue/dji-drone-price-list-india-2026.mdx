## Choosing by Budget Tier

**Under ₹1 lakh:** DJI Mini 4 Pro or Air 3S — the Mini 4 Pro's sub-249g weight simplifies DGCA compliance, while the Air 3S offers stronger camera capability at a similar price.

**₹1 lakh and above:** Mavic 3 Classic for the best single-camera image quality using a larger 4/3-inch sensor.

**Professional production:** DJI Inspire 3 — dual-operator control, 8K RAW recording, interchangeable lenses for genuine cinema work.

---

## What Affects DJI Pricing in India

**Import duties:** DJI drones are imported, and pricing reflects India-specific duty structures that can shift with policy changes.

**Fly More Combo vs. standalone:** Combo packages (extra batteries, charging hub, carrying case) cost more upfront but deliver better value than buying accessories separately later.
