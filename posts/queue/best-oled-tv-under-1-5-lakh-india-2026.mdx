## What You're Trading Off vs. Flagship OLED

**Processor power:** LG's B-series uses a less powerful processor than the C-series or G-series, meaning slightly less sophisticated upscaling and motion processing for lower-quality source content.

**Peak brightness:** Entry-level OLED models generally have lower peak brightness than evo-enhanced flagship panels, mattering most for HDR highlight impact and bright-room viewing.

**What stays the same:** The fundamental OLED panel technology — true per-pixel blacks, infinite contrast ratio, and near-instant response time — is identical to flagship models, since this comes from the panel itself, not the processing electronics around it.
