## What Business Printing Specifically Needs

**Print speed:** Business printers should handle 20+ pages per minute to avoid queuing delays during busy periods.

**Network reliability:** Wired Ethernet connectivity is more dependable than Wi-Fi alone for consistent multi-user office printing.

**Total monthly duty cycle:** Verify the printer's rated maximum monthly volume comfortably exceeds your office's actual usage to avoid premature wear.
