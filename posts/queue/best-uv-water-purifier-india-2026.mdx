## UV vs. RO — When Is UV Enough?

**UV is sufficient when:**
- Your tap water TDS is below 200 ppm (test with a ₹200 TDS meter)
- Your water source is municipal (treated and chlorinated at source)
- Your primary concern is bacteria, viruses, and microorganisms
- You want to preserve natural minerals in water
- You don't want to waste water (RO wastes 3L per 1L purified)

**UV is NOT sufficient when:**
- TDS is above 200 ppm (hard water — UV does not reduce dissolved salts)
- Water contains heavy metals (arsenic, fluoride, nitrates)
- Borewell or groundwater source (typically high TDS)
- Water has visible turbidity (particulate matter)
