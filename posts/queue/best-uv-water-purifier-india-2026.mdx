## When UV is Sufficient (and When It's Not)

**UV is sufficient when:**
- Municipal treated tap water with TDS under 200
- Water is clear (not turbid) — UV doesn't work on cloudy water
- No heavy metals detected in local water
- No fluoride issues in your area

**UV is NOT sufficient when:**
- Borewell water (high TDS, minerals, salts)
- Water with visible colour, smell, or turbidity
- Areas with known lead, arsenic, or fluoride contamination
- Tanker water of unknown source

**How to check:** Get your water tested (₹200–₄₀₀ at local labs or buy TDS meter). If TDS is below 200 and there are no heavy metal concerns, UV + pre-filter is adequate.
