## Quick FAQ

**Do I have to buy on the day itself?** Practices vary between families. Many people buy in advance so the item is delivered in time and simply bring it into the home on the day.

**Are appliances acceptable as Dhanteras purchases?** Many families now include appliances, but customs differ. Follow your family's preference.

**How much should I spend?** There is no fixed amount. Choose what fits your budget and your household's needs.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Leaving it to the last minute.** Festive delivery slots fill up. If you want the item at home on the day, order early and plan for a possible delay.

**Buying only for the occasion.** A good festive purchase is something you will use for years. Choose quality over novelty, and skip items that will sit in a cupboard.

**Confusing price with quality.** A thicker base, a better lid and a longer warranty matter more than a shiny finish. Read the product details.

**Forgetting compatibility.** If you cook on induction, check that any new cookware is induction compatible before you buy.
