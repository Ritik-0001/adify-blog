## How Filterless Chimneys Work

A high-speed blower spins the air and oil outward so that oil droplets collect in a cup, while the cleaner air moves out through the duct. Because there is no mesh to clog, suction is meant to stay stable between cleans.

## How to Choose a Filterless Chimney

Look at the suction and the blower. A rating of around 1,200 cubic metres per hour or more suits heavy cooking, and a good motor warranty adds confidence.

Check the oil collector. A large, easy-to-remove cup makes cleaning simple, and a heat-resistant design is better.

Match the size to the hob. A 60 cm chimney suits a 2 or 3 burner hob, and a 90 cm model suits a 4-burner hob.

## Care and Servicing

Empty the oil collector after a week or two of heavy use, wipe the hood with a mild cleaner and have the unit serviced as the maker advises. Do not use harsh solvents on the glass or paint.

## Filterless Versus Baffle, Honestly

The Quick Pick saves you scrubbing, but it is not maintenance-free: the cup still needs emptying and the blower still needs servicing. A baffle chimney with auto-clean costs a little less and is easier to judge from specifications. If you cook oil-heavy food daily and hate cleaning mesh, filterless wins; if your frying is occasional, the baffle model is the safer and cheaper buy.

## Who Should Skip Filterless

If you rarely fry and cook mostly boiled or steamed food, a simple baffle chimney may be enough and cheaper. If your kitchen has a very long duct or many bends, check that the motor is strong enough, because a filterless design relies on a powerful blower to throw oil outward.

## Installation Tips

Keep the duct short and straight, use a wide duct pipe as the maker advises and mount the hood at the recommended height above the hob. Confirm the power socket and the wall strength, and ask the installer to demonstrate how to remove and clean the oil collector.

## Recommendation

For most households that want less cleaning, a filterless chimney from Elica or Faber is a sensible pick. Check the collector design and motor warranty on the exact model before you order.
