## What You Actually Need in a First Monitor

**Size:** 21.5" or 24". 21.5" fits any desk and any room. 24" is better if your desk is 120cm or wider. Anything above 24" is unnecessary for most beginners.

**Panel:** IPS. Better colour and viewing angles than TN for the same price. Ignore monitors that don't specify IPS — they're likely TN or older technology.

**Resolution:** Full HD (1920x1080). That's all you need. Don't pay more for "4K" or "2K" as a first monitor — your uses don't require it.

**Connectivity:** HDMI. Every laptop made after 2014 has HDMI. Make sure the monitor has at least one HDMI port.

**Refresh rate:** 60Hz or 75Hz. Don't pay for 144Hz for your first monitor unless you specifically know you'll play fast-paced video games.

## How to Connect Your Monitor

Most laptops connect via HDMI cable (usually not included with the monitor — buy a separate HDMI cable for ₹200–₃₀₀). Plug HDMI into your laptop, plug the other end into the monitor's HDMI port, power on the monitor, and your laptop will detect it automatically.

If your laptop doesn't have full HDMI (common on thin ultrabooks with USB-C only): buy a USB-C to HDMI adapter (₹500–₈₀₀) and connect that way.

Desktop PC users: connect via the HDMI or VGA port on your desktop's GPU or motherboard.
