## How to Choose a Massage Chair

Check the track and the rollers. An SL-track gives a longer path than an S-track, and more rollers can give a more varied massage.

Look at the recline and airbags. Zero-gravity recline lifts your legs above your heart, and airbags cover the arms, shoulders, hips and legs.

Try before you buy if possible. Comfort depends on your height and shape, so a store visit helps, and check the return policy if you buy online.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Buying on programs alone.** Comfort and fit come first.

**Ignoring space.** Reclined size is bigger.

**Skipping delivery planning.** Chairs are heavy.

**Using it too long.** Short sessions are enough.

## Real-World Scenarios

**A professional with a desk job.** A full-body chair with heat.

**Parents at home.** A chair with simple controls and good support.

**A small flat.** A compact chair that sits close to the wall.

## What to Check on the Listing

Confirm the track type, airbag count, recline, heat, user height range, dimensions, power rating and warranty. See whether delivery and installation are included.

## Using a Massage Chair Safely

Keep sessions to about fifteen to twenty minutes, start with a gentle intensity and avoid using it right after a heavy meal. Check with a doctor if you are pregnant, have heart or spine conditions, or wear a pacemaker.

## Quick FAQ

**Is a massage chair worth it?** Many people enjoy it for relaxation, though it is a big purchase.

**How long should I use it?** About fifteen to twenty minutes at a time.

**Do they need maintenance?** Occasional cleaning and a service check.

## What Different Price Bands Usually Offer

Chairs at the lower end of this range usually have a shorter roller track, basic airbag massage and a few programs. In the middle you tend to see longer SL-tracks, zero-gravity recline and heat. At the premium end, expect body scanning, foot rollers with more airbags, and finishes that look more like furniture. Decide which features you will use before paying for the rest.

## Delivery, Installation and Space

Massage chairs are heavy and bulky, often 70 to 100 kg in the carton, so check lift and door access. Many sellers include a technician for assembly. Leave space behind and in front of the chair for reclining, and keep the chair away from damp walls and direct sunlight.

## Final Checklist Before You Buy

Before you order, measure the room including the space the chair needs to recline, check lift and door access for a heavy carton and confirm whether assembly is included. Compare what the warranty covers on the frame, the rollers and the electronics, ask how service is arranged in your city and, if possible, try a similar chair in a store so that you know it fits your height and shape.
