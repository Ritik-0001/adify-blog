## How to Choose a Multi Gym

Check the stack weight. A 60 to 100 kg stack suits most beginners, while experienced lifters may want more.

Look at the frame. Thicker steel tubing and solid welds mean a stronger, safer machine, and the stated user weight gives a rough clue.

Think about space. Measure the room, including the height for lat pulldowns, and allow space to move around the machine. Leave at least a metre of clear room around the unit, and use a thick rubber floor mat to protect tiles and absorb noise.

## A Starter Routine on a Multi Gym

A simple three-day plan covers the whole body. On the first day do chest press, lat pulldown and shoulder press. On the second do leg press or leg extension, leg curl and seated row. On the third repeat the first day with slightly more weight. Do three sets of eight to twelve reps, rest about ninety seconds between sets and add a little weight when the last reps feel comfortable.

## Delivery and Assembly Reality

Multi gyms arrive in several heavy cartons, often 100 kg or more in total, so plan the route to the room and the number of people needed to carry them. Assembly can take a few hours, and some brands include installation. Tighten all bolts again after a week, and check the cables and pulleys for wear every few months.

## Cable, Pulley and Bolt Checks

A stack machine is only as safe as its cables, pulleys and pins. When the unit is new, watch how smoothly the weights rise and fall, and listen for rubbing sounds that suggest a misaligned guide rod. Every few months, look over the cable for fraying, confirm that the selector pin seats firmly and put a drop of light machine oil on the guide rods, following whatever the manual says.

## Who Should Skip a Multi Gym

If you want to lift heavy barbell movements such as squats and deadlifts, a stack machine will feel limiting, and a power rack with a bench is a better base. A multi gym is also a poor fit for a small bedroom with a low ceiling, since the lat pulldown needs vertical clearance. It suits beginners and families who want guided, safe movements without loading plates.
