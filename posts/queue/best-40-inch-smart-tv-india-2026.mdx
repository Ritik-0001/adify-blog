## Who a 40 inch TV suits

A 40 inch screen is about 88 cm wide, and a comfortable viewing distance is roughly 1.5 to 2.5 metres. It fits a bedroom, a guest room, a study or a small living room, and it works on a TV unit or wall mount. If your sofa is more than 3 metres away, a 43 or 50 inch makes more sense; many buyers find 43 inch only a little more expensive.

If it is for a child's room, check that the base is stable and that the TV can be wall mounted safely.

## Full HD or 4K at 40 inches

At 40 inches, the eye struggles to see the difference between 1080p and 4K from 2 metres and beyond. A 4K TV does bring benefits: HDR formats, a better processor and more future-proofing, and 4K streaming apps may look a bit better. But at this size, 4K models are usually priced higher, around ₹22,000-30,000, and offer limited visible improvement.

If you plan to connect a console or watch a lot of 4K streaming, choose a 4K model; for a bedroom with TV and OTT, full HD is fine. Avoid HD Ready (720p) at 40 inches since text and detail look soft.

## Smart platforms

Check the software. Google TV or Android TV offers the widest app support and Chromecast built in; Samsung's Tizen and LG's webOS are smooth and cover most services; some budget TVs use less common platforms with limited apps. Make sure Netflix, Prime Video, Disney+ Hotstar and YouTube are officially supported, and check remote features such as voice search.

Memory and storage matter: budget models with 1 GB RAM and 8 GB storage can lag over time. Look for at least 1.5 to 2 GB RAM if available. See the [Android TV 40 inch](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=40+inch+android+tv+full+hd&tag=adifystore-21) results for options.

## Ports and connectivity

You will want at least two HDMI ports, one of them ARC for a soundbar, two USB ports, Wi-Fi with dual-band support and Bluetooth for headphones or a speaker. If you plan to plug in a set-top box and a console, check for three HDMI ports. An optical audio out helps with older sound systems.

## Picture quality at this price

At roughly ₹15,000-25,000, you get decent colour, but contrast and brightness are modest because most use basic LED panels with edge lighting. Expect okay performance in a dim bedroom and reflections in a bright room. Choose a TV with a good processor and an anti-glare finish if the room gets sunlight.

Look for features like local dimming only on higher-end ones; for a bedroom, tune the picture mode to "movie" or "standard" and lower the backlight to reduce eye strain at night.

## Sound

Built-in speakers are usually 20 W, enough for a small room but thin on bass. A basic [soundbar for bedroom TV](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=soundbar+for+small+room+tv+bluetooth+hdmi+arc&tag=adifystore-21) improves dialogue significantly, though it is a separate cost; for this site's budget floor, treat it as an optional extra.

## Mounting

Check VESA mount size, often 200 x 200 mm at this size, and weight, around 6-8 kg. A [tv wall mount tilt](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=tv+wall+mount+tilt+32+to+55+inch&tag=adifystore-21) can be bought cheaply, but a proper installation matters. Use correct anchors for your wall.

Samsung and LG offer the strongest apps and picture processing in small sizes, with higher prices. Xiaomi, TCL, Acer and Kodak offer value, with Google TV and decent specs. Check warranty and service coverage in your city; a one-year comprehensive warranty is common, with an extra year on panels from some brands. ## Who should skip

Skip a 40 inch if your viewing distance is over 3 metres; go for 43 or 50. Skip HD Ready models. Skip if you want a console and 120 Hz gaming; choose a 4K model with HDMI 2.1 in a larger size. Skip very cheap unknown brands with weak support.
