The right AC purchase in India in 2026 comes down to five decisions in order of importance: correct tonnage for your room size, genuine 5-star BEE rating, 100% copper condenser, a reliable brand, and only then secondary features like smart connectivity or air purification. Most buyers focus on brand and features first — but an oversized or undersized AC, or one with an aluminum condenser, undermines every other good decision you make.

This guide walks through each decision in the order it should actually be made, since getting the fundamentals wrong (wrong tonnage especially) can't be fixed by choosing a better brand or paying more for features.

## Step 1: Get the Tonnage Right

| Room Size | Tonnage |
|-----------|---------|
| Up to 120 sq ft | 1 Ton |
| 120-180 sq ft | 1.5 Ton |
| 180-280 sq ft | 2 Ton |
| 280+ sq ft or open-plan | 2.5 Ton or dual-split |

**Adjust upward for:** rooms with heavy sun exposure (west/south-facing), top-floor rooms under direct roof heat, rooms with 4+ regular occupants, or kitchens/rooms with heat-generating appliances.

An undersized AC runs constantly at maximum capacity without ever reaching comfortable temperature — this is both less comfortable and less energy-efficient than correctly sizing from the start. An oversized AC cycles on/off frequently, causing temperature swings and unnecessary wear.

---

## Step 2: Insist on 5-Star BEE Rating

5-star rated inverter ACs use roughly 20-30% less electricity than 3-star rated units for the same cooling output. Given ACs run for years and electricity costs compound, the price premium for 5-star rating typically pays for itself within 2-3 years of regular use, then continues saving money for the rest of the AC's lifespan.

---

## Step 3: Verify 100% Copper Condenser

Copper conducts heat roughly 60% more efficiently than aluminum and resists corrosion significantly better — always check the specification sheet for "100% Copper Condenser," not ambiguous "copper" listings that might indicate hybrid aluminum-copper construction.

## Step 5: Consider Secondary Features Last

Only after nailing tonnage, star rating, condenser material, and brand should you weigh secondary features: self-cleaning technology, smart app control, air purification, or draft-free cooling. These matter for day-to-day comfort and convenience, but they don't compensate for getting the fundamentals wrong.

---

## Common Buying Mistakes to Avoid

**Buying based on price alone:** The cheapest 5-star AC on paper sometimes has a hybrid condenser or lower-quality compressor that fails within 3-4 years.

**Ignoring room-specific factors:** Sun exposure, ceiling height, and occupancy patterns should adjust your tonnage calculation beyond the basic square footage formula.

**Overlooking installation quality:** Even the best AC underperforms with poor installation (incorrect refrigerant charge, poor outdoor unit placement) — always use manufacturer-authorized installers.

**Recommendation:** Follow the five-step order above rather than starting with brand preference or feature wish-lists — a correctly-sized, 5-star, copper-condenser AC from any reputable brand will outperform a feature-rich but incorrectly-sized or lower-efficiency unit every time.
