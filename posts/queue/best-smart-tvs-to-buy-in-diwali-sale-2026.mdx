## How to Choose a Smart TV

Pick the size based on distance: 43 inches for 5 to 6 feet, 55 inches for 8 to 10 feet, and 65 inches for larger halls. Bigger is not always better in a small room.

Prioritise the panel and processor over gimmicks. A good 4K panel with decent HDR and a responsive platform matters more than added features. Check the number of HDMI ports and whether one supports ARC or eARC.

Budget for sound. Most slim TVs have thin built-in speakers, so a basic soundbar is often the best add-on.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Buying too big for the room.** A 65-inch TV in a small living room can feel overwhelming and expose flaws in the picture. Measure your seating distance first.

**Trusting marketing terms.** Labels like Ultra, Pro or Vision do not tell you the panel quality. Look at panel type, brightness, HDR support and refresh rate instead.

**Forgetting the sound.** Slim TVs rarely have strong speakers. Budget for a soundbar or plan for one later.

## Quick FAQ

**LED, QLED or OLED?** LED is the most affordable and works well in bright rooms. QLED offers stronger colour and brightness. OLED has the best contrast and is ideal for dark rooms and films, at a higher price.

**Do I need a 120Hz TV?** For console gaming at high frame rates, yes. For general viewing, a 60Hz panel is fine.

**Is Google TV better than Tizen or webOS?** Each platform has strengths. Google TV has a broad app ecosystem, Tizen is fast and clean, and webOS has a simple remote-driven interface. Pick the one whose interface you find easiest.
