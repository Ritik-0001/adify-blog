## Key Specifications for Portable Projectors

**Lumens (brightness):** The most important spec for Indian conditions. Indian rooms typically have ambient light from windows and ceiling lights. Minimum requirements:
- Dark room (blackout curtains): 200–400 ANSI lumens adequate
- Semi-dark room (evening, some ambient light): 500–700 ANSI lumens needed
- Brightly lit room (daytime): 1000+ ANSI lumens needed (most portable projectors can't achieve this)

**Resolution:** 1080p Full HD minimum for movies and streaming. 720p (HD) is acceptable for basic presentations and casual content but visibly lower quality than TV on large screens.

**Smart OS:** Android TV built-in means you can stream Netflix, YouTube, Hotstar, and Prime directly without an external device. Projectors without smart OS require a Fire Stick or Chromecast (extra ₹3,000–₄,000 and another power cable).

**Battery:** Battery-powered portable projectors (for outdoor/travel use without power sockets) typically last 1.5–3 hours — enough for a movie. Mains-powered portable projectors are brighter.
