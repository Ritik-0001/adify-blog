## Ink Tank vs Laser vs Basic Inkjet

**Choose an ink tank printer** if you print regularly and want the lowest cost per page over the printer's lifetime — the upfront cost is quickly recovered versus buying replacement cartridges.

**Choose a laser printer** if your primary use is high-volume black-and-white text documents, common in small home offices and study setups.

**Choose a basic inkjet** only for occasional, low-volume printing — cartridge costs make it the most expensive option per page over time.
