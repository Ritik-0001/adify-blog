## What Justifies Herman Miller's Price

**Three-size system:** Unlike most chairs' one-size-with-adjustments approach, Aeron comes in genuinely different proportioned sizes (A/B/C) — a meaningful fit advantage for petite or very tall users.

**12-year warranty:** Reflects genuine manufacturing confidence and results in a lower effective annual cost than the sticker price suggests for buyers who keep the chair its full lifespan.

**Decades of ergonomic research:** The PostureFit SL lumbar system and 8Z Pellicle mesh represent continuously refined engineering since 1994.

---

## What Domestic Alternatives Deliver at Lower Cost

**Featherlite Ergo Optima (₹45,000-₴8,000):** Dynamic lumbar support and breathable mesh construction replicating the core ergonomic principles at roughly a third of Aeron's price.

**Sunon Boostoffice Elite (₹15,000-₹18,000):** For buyers on a tighter budget, still delivers genuine dynamic lumbar support, just with less refined materials and a shorter warranty.

---

## Who Should Actually Buy Herman Miller

**Executives and long-tenure professionals** who can amortize the cost over a decade-plus of daily use, where the effective annual cost becomes reasonable.

**Buyers specifically needing the three-size tailored fit** — if standard one-size-with-adjustment chairs genuinely don't fit your body proportions well, this is a real functional advantage worth paying for.

---

## Recommendation

Herman Miller Aeron is worth it specifically for buyers who can comfortably afford the price and value the tailored sizing and warranty depth — for most buyers, a domestic alternative like Featherlite Ergo Optima delivers a genuinely comparable daily experience at a fraction of the cost, making it the more rational choice for most budgets.
