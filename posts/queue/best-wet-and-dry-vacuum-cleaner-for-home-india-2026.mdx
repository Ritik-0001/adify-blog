## How to Choose a Wet and Dry Vacuum

Match the tank size. A 15 to 20 litre tank suits most homes, and larger tanks suit garages and workshops.

Check suction and filters. Strong suction and a washable filter matter, and a foam filter is used for wet pickup.

Look at the accessories. A long hose, crevice tool, floor nozzle and blower port make the machine more useful.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Using the dry filter for wet pickup.** Switch to the correct filter.

**Overfilling the tank.** Empty it in time.

**Ignoring cable length.** Check reach.

**Not cleaning after use.** Rinse the tank and dry the filter.

## Real-World Scenarios

**A home with a terrace and balcony.** A 20 litre wet and dry vacuum.

**A household with pets.** A model with good filtration and tools.

**A small workshop.** A stainless steel model with a strong motor.

## What to Check on the Listing

Confirm the tank size and material, the motor power, the filter types, the cable length, the accessories and the warranty. See whether spares are readily available.

## Safety and Care

Do not vacuum flammable liquids or hot embers, switch off the machine before emptying and keep the motor head dry. Rinse the tank and let the filter dry fully before storing.

## Quick FAQ

**Can it replace a home vacuum?** For floors and spills, yes, but it is heavier.

**Is it noisy?** Often louder than home vacuums.

**Can it pick up water?** Yes, with the correct filter.

## Using It for Spills and Outdoor Cleaning

A wet and dry vacuum is useful for water spills after a leak, cleaning terraces and balconies after the monsoon, picking up leaves and clearing dust from car interiors. Always check that the correct filter is fitted for wet or dry use, and empty the tank promptly.

## Storage and Maintenance

Rinse the tank and let it dry fully after wet use, wash or replace filters as advised and store the hose and attachments neatly. Keep the cable dry and untangled, and avoid leaving the machine in a damp corner.

## Warranty and Service

Compare the motor and whole unit warranty, and check availability of spares like filters, hoses and nozzles. Keep the invoice and service number handy.
