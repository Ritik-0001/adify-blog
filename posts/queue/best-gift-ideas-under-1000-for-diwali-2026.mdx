## How to Choose a Gift Under ₹1,000

At this budget, presentation and reliability matter more than features. Start with the recipient: a colleague or neighbour wants something neutral, a close friend can take a slightly more personal pick, and children usually prefer chocolates or small toys over practical items.

Next, think about what happens after Diwali. Sweets and dry fruits are enjoyed within days, while a flask, glass set or power bank stays useful for years. If you are giving several gifts, mix one consumable with one lasting item so the present does not disappear in a week.

Finally, plan for delivery. Festive demand pushes delivery times out, and gift boxes can arrive dented if the packaging is thin. Order at least a week ahead, choose Amazon-fulfilled listings where possible, and glance at recent reviews for packaging complaints.

If you have one gift to buy and no idea what the person likes, go with the Happilo dry fruit box or the Milton flask. For a younger recipient, the power bank is the more modern pick. For relatives you see once a year, the Borosil glass set or the Cadbury box is enough.
