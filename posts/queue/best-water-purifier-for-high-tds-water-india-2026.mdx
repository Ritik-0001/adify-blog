## How to Choose for High TDS Water

Test your TDS. Water below about 300 TDS is often fine with UF or UV. Between 300 and 500, RO with TDS control is a good option. Above 500, RO is generally needed.

Look at the membrane and pre-filters. A quality RO membrane and a sediment or carbon pre-filter protect the system and extend life. Ask about the membrane rating and replacement cost.

Manage the reject water. RO wastes water, so consider using it for cleaning or plants, or look at models with lower wastage.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Using a UV-only purifier on high TDS water.** It does not reduce TDS.

**Skipping pre-filters.** They protect the membrane.

**Delaying membrane replacement.** Performance drops.

**Ignoring hardness.** Hard water scales the membrane.

## Real-World Scenarios

**A home on borewell water with 1,000+ TDS.** RO with a strong membrane and pre-filter.

**A home with 300 to 500 TDS.** RO with TDS control.

**A coastal home with salty water.** Choose RO designed for high salinity.

## Quick FAQ

**Is RO safe for drinking?** It removes contaminants and some minerals, so a TDS controller helps.

**What is a safe TDS?** Often 50 to 150 after treatment is considered comfortable, but follow health guidance.

**How often should I service?** Every six to twelve months.

## Installation and Servicing

Installation needs a water inlet, a drain and a power point. Ask the brand about the annual maintenance contract, which usually covers filter and membrane changes and a set number of visits. In high-TDS areas, membranes work harder, so servicing on schedule is essential to keep taste and safety consistent.

## Reject Water and Reuse

RO purifiers reject a large share of the input water, especially with high-TDS supply. Collect the reject water in a bucket and reuse it for mopping, watering plants or washing vehicles, and consider models that recover more water.

## Testing Your Water

A simple handheld TDS meter costs little and helps you check the purifier's output. Test the input water, then test the purified water after installation and every few months. A rising output reading suggests the membrane is wearing out. TDS is only one measure, so ask a lab for a full test if you suspect contamination.
