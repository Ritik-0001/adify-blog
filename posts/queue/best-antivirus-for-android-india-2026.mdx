## Android Threats in India: What to Actually Worry About

**Sideloaded APKs:** Most Android malware in India comes through APK files shared on WhatsApp, Telegram, and dubious download sites — fake versions of popular apps (Jio, PhonePe, CRED, banking apps). Enable "Scan apps from unknown sources" in Google Play Protect settings.

**Phishing via SMS/WhatsApp:** Fake links that mimic bank websites, UPI payment requests, and government portals. No antivirus fully stops users from entering credentials on a convincing fake website. Training matters more than software here.

**OTP-stealing malware:** Malicious apps that request SMS permissions to intercept OTPs. Play Store has removed thousands of such apps, but they circulate through unofficial channels.

**Banking Trojans:** Overlay attacks that display a fake UPI interface on top of your real banking app. Rare but sophisticated. Detected by Bitdefender's banking protection in Android.

## Android Security Best Practices (More Important Than Any App)

1. **Only install apps from Play Store** — eliminate 90% of Android malware risk by not sideloading APK files
2. **Keep Android updated** — monthly security patches from Google/Samsung/OPPO address known vulnerabilities
3. **Review app permissions** — banking, keyboard, and accessibility apps requesting camera/microphone access they don't need are suspicious
4. **Verify OTP requests** — no legitimate bank or UPI app will ask you to share an OTP via a callback or link
5. **Enable Google Play Protect** — make sure it's active

**Recommendation:** Google Play Protect + security best practices for most users. Bitdefender Mobile Security for users who sideload apps, travel internationally, or want comprehensive phishing protection.
