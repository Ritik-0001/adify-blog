## How to Choose an Ergonomic Chair for Home

Start with your work pattern. If you sit for more than six hours a day, invest in a chair with adjustable lumbar support and seat depth. If you alternate between sitting and standing, pick a chair that is easy to move and adjust.

Check the fit. Your feet should rest flat on the floor, your knees should be at about 90 degrees and your forearms should be parallel to the desk. Chairs with a wide seat height range suit more users.

Think about the room. A home office often shares space, so check the chair's footprint, the wheels for your floor type and the noise of the gas lift.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Buying the cheapest chair.** A chair that does not adjust properly can cause discomfort over time.

**Ignoring the desk height.** A chair cannot fix a desk that is too high or low.

**Skipping the warranty check.** Confirm what the warranty covers, especially the gas lift.

**Sitting without breaks.** Stand and stretch regularly.

## What to Check on the Listing

Confirm the weight limit, the adjustments, the warranty and the assembly terms. Read reviews about mesh sagging and armrest durability after several months.

## Quick FAQ

**Is a headrest necessary?** It helps when you lean back, but is optional.

**How long should a chair last?** Five to eight years is common.

**Can a chair cure back pain?** No. It supports comfort, but persistent pain needs medical advice.
