## How to Choose a Robot Vacuum With Mop

Check your floors. Robot vacuums work best on hard floors and low-pile carpets, and mop modules suit tile and laminate.

Look at navigation. Lidar-based models map the house and cover rooms methodically, while gyroscope models are cheaper but less precise.

Think about maintenance. Self-emptying docks reduce manual work but add cost, and mop pads need washing.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Expecting a mop to scrub.** Robot mops wipe rather than scrub stains.

**Ignoring cables and small items.** Pick up cords and toys to avoid getting stuck.

**Not setting no-go zones.** Use the app to block off areas.

## Quick FAQ

**Are robot vacuums good for Indian homes?** They work well on hard floors and daily dust, but heavy dirt may need a manual vacuum.

**Do I need a self-emptying dock?** It reduces work but adds cost.

**Can it climb thresholds?** Many can handle low thresholds, but check specifications.

## Setting Up a Robot Vacuum in an Indian Home

Before the first run, pick up cables, shoes and small objects, and lift curtains and rug fringes that can tangle the brush. Use the app to draw no-go zones around pet bowls, floor-level electrical points and delicate items.

Indian homes often have a mix of tile, marble and rugs, so choose a model that handles thresholds well and allows carpet detection to lift the mop. Run the robot when the house is empty for the first few weeks so you can see where it struggles.

## Running Costs and Ownership

Plan for consumables. Filters, side brushes, main brushes and mop pads wear out and need replacing, and dock bags cost extra on self-emptying models. Check that spare parts are available in India before you buy.

## What to Check on the Listing

Look at the suction rating in pascals, the battery capacity, the dustbin size and the water tank size. Confirm the navigation type, whether multi-floor mapping is supported, and that the app works with your phone. Check warranty terms and whether spare brushes and pads are sold in India.

## Pets, Rugs and Thresholds

If you have pets, look for a tangle-resistant main brush and a large dustbin, and expect to clean the brush regularly. Rugs with long fringes can snag a robot, and dark or shiny surfaces can confuse cliff sensors on some models. Check the stated climbing height for thresholds, since Indian homes often have raised door sills.
