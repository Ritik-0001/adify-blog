## ANC in Earbuds vs Headphones Under ₹3000

Earbuds face a harder ANC challenge than headphones — smaller earcup means fewer microphones, less physical isolation, and less space for the ANC processor.

The passive isolation from the ear tip seal is actually a significant part of the total noise reduction in earbuds. Earbuds with good seal (silicone ear tips that fit your canal) often block as much noise passively as the active ANC adds on top.

Key insight: if the ear tips don't create a tight seal in your ear canal, ANC will be noticeably less effective. Prioritise fit and seal when using ANC earbuds.

## How to Maximise ANC in Budget Earbuds

1. **Get the right ear tip size**: The seal is as important as the ANC. Try all three included sizes; the right tip should feel snug without being uncomfortable.

2. **Use comply foam tips**: Aftermarket foam ear tips (₹300–₹500 on Amazon) improve passive seal dramatically, boosting effective noise reduction.

3. **Enable ANC mode correctly**: Some earbuds have multiple ANC levels. Test the strongest setting for commute environments and lighter modes for office use.

4. **Transparency mode**: Use it when you need to hear announcements or conversations — tap the bud rather than removing earbuds, preserving battery.
