## How to Choose a Top Load Washer

Pick capacity for your household: 7 kg for two to three people, 8 kg for four, and larger for joint families. A big drum helps with bedding and reduces the number of loads.

Check the motor type and star rating. An inverter motor is quieter and more efficient than a standard one, and a higher star rating reduces electricity use.

Consider water. Top loaders use more water than front loaders, so check the water use claims and pick a model with load sensing to adjust water levels.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Ignoring the drum type.** A drum with a good agitator or pulsator design cleans more evenly. Read the description and reviews.

**Forgetting the space.** Top loaders need clearance for the lid to open. Check that it fits under any shelf.

**Buying an older model without checking the warranty.** Confirm that the warranty runs from the date of purchase.

## Quick FAQ

**Is a top load washer good for Indian clothes?** Yes, especially for sarees and heavy cotton, and it is easier for adding clothes mid-cycle.

**How long do they last?** With regular cleaning, many last eight to ten years.

**Do I need a stand?** A stand can help with height and stability, but many top loaders work on a flat floor.

## Running Costs and Maintenance

Top loaders are simple to run, but lint traps and drum edges collect residue. Clean the lint filter regularly, run a drum-clean cycle every month or two and use detergents made for automatic machines. Avoid overloading, especially with heavy items such as blankets, which can unbalance the drum.
