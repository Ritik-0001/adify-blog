## What Matters for Online Classes

**Webcam:** Most laptops still ship with 720p 30fps webcams in 2026. Full HD (1080p) webcams are genuinely better for class participation — your face looks sharper, eye contact reads better, and professors can see you clearly. Some laptops in this category offer 1080p webcams; it's worth checking.

**Microphone:** Dual-array microphones (built into laptops) cancel background noise better than single-mic setups. In Indian households with TVs, fans, and ambient noise, noise cancellation in the mic pickup matters.

**Battery life:** Online classes from 9 AM to 5 PM with a break — that's 7 hours minimum. India has variable power supply; being tethered to a charger in a room with frequent outages is a real problem. Aim for 7+ hours.

**Display size and quality:** 14–15.6 inches is the sweet spot. FHD (1920×1080) is necessary — anything lower causes eye strain during long video sessions.

**Thermal stability:** Some budget laptops throttle under sustained video playback + browser multitasking. Check reviews for thermal performance, not just spec sheets.

## Internet Speed Matters More Than Laptop

A common online class issue in India: poor video call performance blamed on the laptop, when the real problem is Wi-Fi signal or ISP bandwidth during peak hours (9 AM–6 PM).

Test your internet before buying a better laptop. If your connection speed on speedtest.net during class hours drops below 5 Mbps, no laptop upgrade will fix the video pixelation.
