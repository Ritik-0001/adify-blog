## What Makes an RO Purifier Suitable for Hard Water?

**RO membrane capacity:** Standard RO membranes handle TDS up to 1,000–1,500 ppm effectively. For TDS above 1,500 ppm (very hard water), high-capacity membranes (2,000 ppm rated) are required. AO Smith's Z8 uses a high-capacity membrane.

**Water recovery ratio:** Standard RO: 25% recovery (3L wasted per 1L purified). High-efficiency RO: 33–40% recovery. For hard water regions where water is already scarce (Rajasthan, Gujarat), high recovery ratio reduces water wastage.

**Pre-filter importance:** Hard water with high sediment loads (common in borewell water) chokes RO pre-filters faster. High-capacity pre-sediment filters (5–10 micron) protect the expensive RO membrane and extend membrane life.

## Borewell Water Special Considerations

Borewell water (groundwater) in India often contains:
- Very high TDS (500–2000 ppm)
- Nitrates (agricultural runoff)
- Fluoride (geological leaching)
- Iron (oxidized from pipes/ground)

All of these require RO purification. For iron-contaminated water, an iron pre-filter (₹2,000–₃,000 add-on) before the RO protects the membrane from iron fouling.
