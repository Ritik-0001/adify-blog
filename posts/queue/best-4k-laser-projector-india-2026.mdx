## How to Choose a Laser Projector

Think about throw distance. Ultra short throw projectors sit just below the screen, which suits living rooms, while standard models need a longer distance and ceiling or table mounting.

Look at brightness and the screen. For daytime viewing, aim for high brightness and an ALR screen, and calibrate for the lighting you actually have.

Plan sound and sources. Built-in speakers help, but a soundbar or surround system gives better results, and check the HDMI and eARC support.

## Installation Notes

Use a professional installer for ultra short throw systems, since alignment and screen mounting are delicate, and plan power and HDMI cables to the projector location. Keep air vents clear.

## Who Should Skip This

If your wall is uneven, your room is small or you simply want the best picture per rupee, a large 4K TV will serve you better than a laser projector costing over ₹1.5 lakh once a screen and installation are added. Projectors also demand a tidy cable plan and some care with alignment. They reward people who value a very large image above everything else, not casual viewers.

## Recommendation

For living rooms, an ultra short throw laser projector from Samsung, LG or Hisense with an ALR screen is the full solution, and XGIMI or BenQ suit media rooms. Compare specifications, screen requirements and warranty on each listing.
