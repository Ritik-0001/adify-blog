## What the dock actually does

A full station typically handles four jobs: it empties the robot's dust bin into a bag, refills the robot's water tank, washes the mop pads in a tray with scrubbing ribs, and dries them with warm air. Cheaper docks only empty dust, which is a different product.

Drying matters in Indian humidity: a wet mop left in a closed dock grows a sour smell within days. Hot-air drying is the feature that prevents it.

## Water tanks, and the weekly chore

Docks use a clean-water tank and a dirty-water tank, usually 3 to 5 litres each. A medium home uses the clean tank in several runs, and you empty the dirty one often. If you have hard water, which is common across north and west India, scale builds up in pumps and nozzles, so use filtered or softened water.

Some premium models can connect to a water supply and drain, but this needs plumbing and is not realistic in most flats.

## Maintenance you still have to do

Even a smart dock needs attention. Empty the dirty-water tank after each use. Rinse the washing tray and scraper weekly. Wash or replace the dust bag, clean the main brush of hair, and wipe sensors. Descale occasionally.

Replacement parts such as mops, brushes and filters are an ongoing cost. Check availability before buying. See [robot vacuum replacement mop pads](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=robot+vacuum+replacement+mop+pads&tag=adifystore-21).

## Floors and rugs

Mop-washing docks work best on tile, marble and sealed wood. Carpets and rugs need mop-lifting, and loose rugs with fringes can tangle brushes. Some models can be set to avoid carpets. Check the maximum threshold climb, as Indian homes often have raised door sills.

## Noise and space

Docks make noise during drying and emptying. Place them in a hall or corridor rather than a bedroom, with a few feet of clearance in front and a power outlet.

## Who should skip a self-washing dock

Skip it if your home is small, you have mostly carpets, or you rarely mop. A simpler robot with a manual mop tank will cost far less. Also skip if your water is extremely hard and you will not filter it.
