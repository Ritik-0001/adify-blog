## How to Choose a Solar Water Heater

Size by family. A rough guide is about 25 to 30 litres per person per day, so a family of four needs about 100 to 150 litres per day.

Check your roof and water. You need a shaded-free roof facing the sun and a strong roof structure, and hard water favours flat plate collectors and regular maintenance.

Look at warranty and installer. A long tank warranty and a reliable local installer matter as much as the brand.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Choosing too small.** You will run out of hot water.

**Ignoring shade.** Trees and buildings reduce output.

**Skipping maintenance.** Clean collectors and check pipes.

**Using an unqualified installer.** Poor plumbing causes leaks.

## Real-World Scenarios

**A family of four in a house with a roof.** A 100 to 150 litre system.

**A joint family.** A 200 litre or larger system.

**A flat with shared roof.** Check society rules and space.

## What to Check on the Listing

Confirm the capacity, the system type, the tank material and warranty, the backup element, the installation terms and the service availability. See the roof area needed.

## Maintenance Basics

Clean the collectors every few months, check pipes and insulation and flush the system as advised if you have hard water. A yearly service extends the life of the system.

## Quick FAQ

**Does it work on cloudy days?** Output drops, so use backup.

**Is it worth the cost?** For heavy use in sunny regions, often yes over years.

**Do I need approvals?** Check your society and local rules.

## Roof Space and Structure

A typical home system needs a few square metres of shade-free roof space facing the sun, plus a stable base and a path for pipes to the bathrooms. Check that the roof can bear the weight of a filled tank, and plan pipe insulation to reduce heat loss on the way down.

## Hard Water and Maintenance

Hard water can scale collector tubes and tanks over time, so choose a system that suits your water and plan periodic descaling or flushing. A yearly check by the installer extends life and keeps performance steady, and a good local installer is as important as the brand.

## Final Checklist Before You Buy

Before you order, confirm the capacity against your family size and hot water use, check installation needs such as space, plumbing, electrical load and roof access and ask who installs and services the system. Compare the warranty on the tank and on the heating or compressor parts, and ask for a written quote that lists everything included.
