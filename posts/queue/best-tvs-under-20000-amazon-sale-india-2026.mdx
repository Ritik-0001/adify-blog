## What to Expect from a ₹20,000 4K TV

**Good:**
- 4K resolution (3840×2160) — genuinely sharp for HD streaming content
- Built-in WiFi for OTT — Netflix, Prime Video, Hotstar accessible without a streaming stick
- Dolby Audio — improved TV speaker performance vs non-Dolby panels
- Android TV / MIUI TV — Google Assistant, Chromecast built-in

**Limitations (compared to premium TVs):**
- Panel brightness: 250–350 nits (premium: 500–1000 nits). Harder to watch in daylight
- Response time and refresh rate: 60Hz, 10–15ms response. Fine for movies; not ideal for gaming
- Panel uniformity: Some uniformity issues (clouding, flashlighting) common in budget panels
- App selection: Some apps may not be optimised for the specific launcher

## GIF TV Purchase Tips Under ₹20,000

**43-inch is the sweet spot:** 40-inch at ₹15,000 is underpowered; 50-inch at ₹24,000 is better value for the bedroom. 43-inch for bedroom or smaller drawing room hits the right balance.

**Check HDMI 2.0 vs HDMI 1.4:** Budget TVs include HDMI 1.4 (4K/30fps) rather than HDMI 2.0 (4K/60fps). For gaming consoles (PS5, Xbox), this matters — HDMI 1.4 limits to 4K/30fps.

**OTT subscription check:** If you're buying for streaming, verify your existing subscriptions (Netflix, Prime, Hotstar) work on the TV's OS — Android TV and MIUI TV support all major Indian OTT platforms.
