Roughly ₹25,000–45,000. A 43 inch TV suits a viewing distance of around 4.5 to 6 feet and works well in bedrooms and small living rooms.

Why it works: fits small spaces. The trade-off is that large rooms feel underserved.

## 5. A Simple Distance Rule

As a guide for 4K TVs, sit about 1.2 to 1.6 times the screen diagonal away. For a 55 inch TV, that is about 5.5 to 7.3 feet, and for a 65 inch TV about 6.5 to 8.7 feet. If you like a more immersive view, sit closer.

Why it works: easy to apply. The trade-off is that preferences differ.

## How to Choose Your TV Size

Measure your seating distance. Use a tape from the sofa to the wall and apply the distance rule, then check available wall or stand width.

Think about content. Movies and sports benefit from larger screens, while casual TV and news are fine on smaller ones.

Consider budget and room. A good 55 inch TV usually beats a cheap 65 inch one for picture quality, so do not stretch to a bigger size at the cost of quality.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Buying too small.** Many regret not going one size up.

**Ignoring wall or stand width.** Measure first.

**Choosing size over quality.** A poor large TV disappoints.

**Skipping mounting plans.** Check the wall strength.

## Real-World Scenarios

**A bedroom 6 feet from the bed.** A 43 or 50 inch TV.

**A living room with a sofa 8 feet away.** A 55 to 65 inch TV, with 65 inch if you like a bigger picture.

**A media room 11 feet from the screen.** A 75 inch TV.

## What to Check on the Listing

Confirm the screen size, the resolution, the panel type, the HDMI ports and refresh rate, the sound, the warranty and the dimensions with stand. See whether wall mounting is included.

## Mounting and Placement

Mount the TV with the centre of the screen at eye level when seated, use a bracket rated for the weight and size and keep cables tidy. Avoid placing the TV opposite a bright window.

## Warranty and Service

Compare warranty on the panel and the whole TV, and check service availability in your city. Keep the invoice and serial number.

## Quick FAQ

**Is bigger always better?** Not if your room is small.

**Is 4K necessary at 43 inch?** It is common, and helps with sharpness.

**Do I need a soundbar?** It improves sound on large TVs.
