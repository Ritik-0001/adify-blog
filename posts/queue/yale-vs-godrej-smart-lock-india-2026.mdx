## Who they are

Yale is a global lock brand with a strong digital range in India: fingerprint, PIN, RFID card, app and key backup, with several form factors. Godrej is an Indian giant in locks and security, with its Advantis and Catus smart lock ranges, plus huge name recognition.

## Range and lock types

Yale offers a spread: mortise-based smart locks for modern doors, rim locks for older doors, and retrofit options that sit on an existing deadbolt. This breadth is a real advantage if your door is not standard.

Godrej concentrates on digital locks for main doors, often with multiple unlocking modes, including fingerprint, PIN, card, and app on connected models. It has options for both new installations and replacements.

## Features and app

Yale's higher-end locks offer app control, temporary guest codes, access logs, and in some models integration with voice assistants. The ecosystem is more mature for remote access.

Godrej offers solid core features, with an app on connected models, and its focus is reliable locking rather than smart-home integration. Fewer models integrate with assistants.

## Service and installation

This is where Godrej shines. It has a large service network in India, and many buyers find it easier to get a technician quickly, including in smaller cities.

Yale has an installation network too, but coverage varies by region. Check before buying that a technician can visit your area, because incorrect installation is the leading cause of smart lock problems.

## Security and emergency access

Both brands include a mechanical key or emergency power access. Check how to power the lock if batteries die: many have a 9V battery terminal outside. Check also that fingerprint sensors are capacitive rather than optical, since they resist spoofing better and handle dry or damp fingers well.

Battery life varies: expect several months on four AA cells, depending on use. Keep a spare set of batteries, and never rely on the lock without a backup key.

## Door and install reality

Smart locks need a door thick enough for the body, and a proper mortise cut-out. Heavy teak doors in older Indian homes may need modification. Some locks need drilling that cannot be undone. ## Who should skip a smart lock

Skip a smart lock if your door is warped, your building has frequent power fluctuation without backup, or if you rarely have guests and trust metal keys. A good mechanical lock costs a fraction.

## Recommendation

Choose Yale if you want more form factors, a polished app and remote access. Choose Godrej if service availability and brand trust matter most, especially outside the metros. Whichever you pick, confirm door compatibility and a local installer before ordering.
