## How to Choose a Treadmill Under ₹1 Lakh

Look at the continuous motor rating, not just the peak HP. A machine rated for a higher continuous HP will run cooler and last longer under regular use.

Check the belt size and the maximum user weight. A wider belt, such as 45 cm, feels safer when running, and a higher user weight rating usually means a stronger frame. Taller runners should also look at belt length.

Look at incline and cushioning. Auto incline adds variety, and a good deck cushion protects your knees.

## Delivery, Assembly and Space Planning

Treadmills in this band are heavy, often 70 to 100 kg in the carton, so check lift access, door widths and the number of delivery staff. Many brands offer free installation by a technician, and some require you to book it. Leave at least two metres of clear space behind the machine and keep it away from curtains and loose cables. A flat, firm floor reduces vibration and extends the life of the motor.

## Belt and Motor Upkeep

Lubricate the belt as the manual advises, keep the deck clear of dust, and put a mat under the machine. A few minutes of upkeep every month protects the motor you paid extra for.

## Who Should Skip the Top of This Range

If two people in the house mainly walk, a treadmill near the ₹1 lakh ceiling buys strength you will not use. A mid-range model with a decent continuous HP and a solid warranty costs far less and covers brisk walking and light jogging well. Stretch toward the top of the band only if someone runs most days or the machine will be shared by several runners.

## Who Should Skip a Treadmill at This Price

If you mainly want to walk for half an hour a day and your flat is small, a ₹60,000 to ₹1,00,000 treadmill is more machine than you need, and a walking pad or a good folding model costs far less. These heavier treadmills also need delivery access, floor strength and a corner that stays permanently occupied. One honest trade-off between a Sparnod-class heavy build and a PowerMax-class mid build: the heavier machine feels steadier at running speeds but is harder to move and fold.

## Recommendation

For most buyers, a PowerMax or Sparnod treadmill with a strong continuous motor rating is a good starting point. Compare belt size, user weight and warranty on the listing.
