## How to Choose a Treadmill Under ₹1 Lakh

Look at the continuous motor rating, not just the peak HP. A machine rated for a higher continuous HP will run cooler and last longer under regular use.

Check the belt size and the maximum user weight. A wider belt, such as 45 cm, feels safer when running, and a higher user weight rating usually means a stronger frame.

Look at incline and cushioning. Auto incline adds variety, and a good deck cushion protects your knees.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Chasing peak HP numbers.** Check continuous HP and the warranty.

**Ignoring the belt length.** Taller runners need a longer belt.

**Skipping installation.** Check whether the brand offers it.

**Underestimating space.** Measure the room and the fold height.

## Real-World Scenarios

**A couple who both run.** A sturdier machine with a 3 to 4 HP motor.

**A family with mixed use.** A model with preset programs and auto incline.

**A beginner.** A mid-range machine with a decent warranty.

## What to Check on the Listing

Confirm the continuous HP, the maximum speed, the belt dimensions, the maximum user weight and the warranty on the motor and frame. See whether installation and assembly are included, and read reviews for noise and after-sales service in your city.

## Maintenance Basics

Lubricate the belt as the manual advises, keep the deck clear of dust, and place a mat under the treadmill. A little maintenance extends the life of a motor and protects your floor.

## Quick FAQ

**Is a treadmill under ₹1 lakh good enough for running?** Yes, models in this band are built for regular running.

**Do I need auto incline?** It adds variety but is not essential.

**How long do they last?** Many last for years with care.

## Delivery, Assembly and Space Planning

Treadmills in this band are heavy, often 70 to 100 kg in the carton, so check lift access, door widths and the number of delivery staff. Many brands offer free installation by a technician, and some require you to book it. Leave at least two metres of clear space behind the machine and keep it away from curtains and loose cables. A flat, firm floor reduces vibration and extends the life of the motor.
