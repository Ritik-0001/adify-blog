## Room Size Guide for AC Tonnage

**Under 80 sq ft (small studio room, box room):** 0.75 ton  
**80–130 sq ft (standard single bedroom, study room):** 1 ton  
**130–200 sq ft (master bedroom, large bedroom):** 1.5 ton  
**200+ sq ft (living room, large rooms):** 1.5–2 ton

**Ceiling height matters too:** These calculations assume 9–10 ft ceiling. Higher ceilings (10–12 ft) need 10–15% more tonnage.

**Sun exposure:** South-facing rooms with large windows need ~10% more capacity than north-facing rooms.

## Features That Matter for Small Room ACs

**Sleep mode:** Gradually adjusts temperature after you fall asleep — prevents over-cooling that disturbs sleep in small rooms.

**Auto-clean:** Prevents mold and bacteria growth in evaporator coil — especially important in humid regions (Mumbai, Kerala, Bengal coast).

**Quiet operation:** Small rooms mean the indoor unit is close to where you sleep. Look for under 26 dB noise level.

**R32 refrigerant:** More eco-friendly than R22, easier to refill in India (widely available).

---

## Window AC vs Split AC for Small Rooms

Window ACs (₹15,000–₂₅,000) are cheaper and easier to install than split ACs. For a rented room where you'll move in 1–2 years, a window AC might make more economic sense.

Split AC advantages: quieter (compressor is outside), better cooling distribution, more efficient inverter options, better resale value.

**Recommendation:** 1 ton 5-star inverter split AC from LG, Panasonic, or Daikin for most small bedrooms. Measure your room before buying — getting the right tonnage matters more than brand selection for small rooms.
