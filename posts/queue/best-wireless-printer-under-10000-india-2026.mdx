## WiFi Direct vs Network WiFi Printing

**Network WiFi (WiFi to router):** Printer connects to your home WiFi router. Any device on the same network can print. Best for fixed setups where printer stays in one place.

**WiFi Direct:** Printer creates its own WiFi hotspot. Connect directly from your phone without a router. Best for hostel rooms, temporary setups, or printing from a phone without home WiFi.

**AirPrint (iOS):** Apple's native protocol. iPhone and iPad users — no app needed. Connect to the same WiFi and print directly from any app with a share button.

**Mopria (Android):** Google's native Android printing. Recent Android phones (9+) can print directly without an app if printer supports Mopria.

HP Smart app works for all setups and is the recommended method for most Indian users.

## Should You Choose Ink Tank or Cartridge?

**Choose ink tank (Smart Tank 580, EcoTank L3252) if:**
- You print 100+ pages per month
- You want colour printing with low running cost
- You plan to use the printer for 3+ years

**Choose cartridge (DeskJet 2331) if:**
- You print less than 50 pages per month
- You need the lowest upfront cost
- You only occasionally need colour prints

**Recommendation:** HP Smart Tank 580 for most buyers — wireless, lowest running cost, HP ecosystem. Epson EcoTank L3252 if photo quality matters more. Canon G3020 for vibrant colour output.
