## What pets add to the air

Three things matter: loose hair, which is large and clogs the intake; dander, tiny skin flakes that float and trigger allergies; and odour, from litter boxes, wet fur and accidents. A purifier handles them in stages: a pre-filter for hair, HEPA for dander, and carbon for smells.

## Pre-filters, hair and dander

A washable pre-filter catches hair and larger dust and extends HEPA filter life. Vacuum or rinse it every week if you have shedding dogs or cats. Without one, hair coats the HEPA layer and cuts airflow.

True HEPA, rated H13 or at least 99.97 percent at 0.3 micron, captures dander. Avoid unclear terms like HEPA-type.

## Carbon for odour

Litter box smell and wet dog odour are gases, not particles. Only activated carbon removes them, and thin carbon sheets saturate within months. Look for a thicker carbon layer or granules. If odour is your main concern, a purifier is a supplement to cleaning, not a replacement.

## CADR and room size

CADR, the clean air delivery rate, tells you how much air is cleaned per hour. For a 150 to 200 sq ft room, a CADR of 300 to 400 m3 per hour is a reasonable target. Pet owners should size up, since hair and dander levels are higher. Place the purifier away from walls and near where the pet sleeps.

## Noise and filter cost

Check the noise level at low and high speed, with 25 to 35 dB at low and around 50 to 60 dB at high. Filters often cost roughly ₹2,500-8,000 a set, replaced every 6 to 12 months. Pet homes may need earlier changes. Add the cost over three years before comparing models. ## Who should skip a purifier

If your main problem is hair on furniture, a good vacuum or a pet-hair roller solves it, not a purifier. Allergy sufferers should also talk to a doctor.

## Placement for pet homes

Put the purifier where the animal spends most of its time, with at least 30 cm of clear space around the intake. Keep it off the floor if a dog can lick or knock it, and away from the litter tray so the carbon is not saturated by the strongest odour source. Close the door of a bedroom if the purifier is meant for it.

## Recommendation

Choose a purifier with a washable pre-filter, True HEPA and thick carbon, sized for your room, and budget for filter replacement. Clean the pre-filter weekly.
