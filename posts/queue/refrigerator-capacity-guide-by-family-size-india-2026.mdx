## How to Choose a Capacity

Start with family size. A rough guide is about 50 to 60 litres per person, plus extra if you shop in bulk or entertain often.

Think about your habits. If you buy groceries weekly and store leftovers, add some margin, and if you shop daily, you can go smaller.

Check the freezer. If you store a lot of frozen items, look for a larger freezer or a convertible model.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Buying too small.** It forces extra shopping trips.

**Buying too large.** It wastes space and electricity.

**Ignoring dimensions.** Measure the space and door swing.

**Skipping the star rating.** It affects running cost.

## Real-World Scenarios

**A couple in a 1BHK.** A 200 to 250 litre double-door.

**A family of four.** A 300 litre model.

**A joint family.** A 400 litre or larger model.

## What to Check on the Listing

Confirm the capacity, the freezer capacity, the compressor warranty, the star rating, the dimensions and the installation terms. See whether the shelves and bins suit your storage.

## Using Space Well

Keep the fridge about three quarters full for good airflow, store cooked and raw food separately and use door shelves for items that handle temperature changes. Clean shelves monthly.

## Quick FAQ

**Is 250 litres enough for four?** For frequent shoppers, yes.

**Does bigger use more power?** Usually, but inverter models help.

**Do I need a freezer-heavy model?** Only if you stock frozen food.

## Matching Dimensions and Space

Capacity is only part of the story. Measure the width, depth and height of the space and leave clearance for ventilation and door swing. Some taller models fit narrow spaces better than wide ones, and measuring the path from the door to the kitchen avoids delivery problems.

## Energy Use and Star Ratings

A higher star rating means lower running cost, and inverter compressors help hold temperature steadily. For a refrigerator that runs all day for ten years or more, running costs matter as much as the purchase price. Compare ratings and warranties on the models you are considering.

## Warranty and Service

Check the compressor warranty and the whole product warranty, and ask about service in your city. Keep the invoice and warranty details together.

## Final Checklist Before You Buy

Before you order, measure the space, the doorway, the lift and the path to the room, check water and drain points or ventilation clearance and confirm whether delivery and installation are included. Compare the warranty on the motor or compressor with the warranty on the whole product, check the energy rating and keep the invoice and installation record.

## Recommendation

For most families, 250 to 350 litres is the sweet spot. Go larger if you shop in bulk or have a joint family. Compare specifications and warranty on each listing.
