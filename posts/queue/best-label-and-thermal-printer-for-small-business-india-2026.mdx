## How thermal printing saves money

A thermal printer has no ink, toner or cartridge. A heated print head darkens special paper. Direct thermal printing uses heat-sensitive labels, so there is no ribbon; thermal transfer uses a wax or resin ribbon and prints more durable labels. For shipping labels that live for a week, direct thermal is cheaper and simpler. For barcode labels on product packaging, warehouse shelves or items exposed to heat, sunlight or rubbing, thermal transfer lasts longer.

## Cost per label: the number that matters

A roll of 500 labels in 100 by 150 mm typically costs several hundred rupees, so a single label works out to roughly ₹0.8 to ₹1.5 depending on quality and volume. That compares to ₹3 or more per sheet on an inkjet with A4 stickers. If you ship 50 parcels a day, the printer pays back in a few months. Buy labels in cartons for better pricing, and avoid very cheap rolls, which smear and fade.

## What to check before you buy

Check the driver: you want Windows and ideally macOS support, plus the ability to print from Chrome or your courier portal with a custom paper size. Check the resolution: 203 dpi is fine for shipping labels and standard barcodes, while 300 dpi suits small text and tiny QR codes. Check the interface: USB is enough for most, with Ethernet or Wi-Fi useful for shared use. Check the core size so rolls fit the printer.

## Barcodes and print quality

For GS1 or retail barcodes, the printer must be capable of crisp, high-contrast bars, which is why quality heads matter. A smudged barcode fails scanning at a warehouse and can bring chargebacks. Test a sample print on your actual labels and scan it with a [barcode scanner](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=barcode+scanner+USB+1D+2D&tag=adifystore-21). ## Care, storage and common failures

Thermal print heads wear out and attract dust. Clean them with isopropyl alcohol swabs and avoid touching them. Store labels away from heat and sunlight, because direct thermal paper darkens when warm and the print fades after months. Avoid very cheap rolls with rough adhesives that jam sensors. Keep a spare [thermal printer cleaning kit](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=thermal+printer+cleaning+swab+isopropyl&tag=adifystore-21) in the packing area.

## Who should skip a thermal label printer

Skip it if you ship fewer than ten parcels a week, since the printing cost of a normal inkjet on plain paper cut and taped is cheaper than a ₹15,000 printer. Skip it if your courier supplies pre-printed labels. Skip very cheap unbranded ₹6,000 printers if you need dependable daily printing.
