## Important Considerations for Corporate Gifting

**Check client gift value policies:** Many larger organizations, especially in finance and government-adjacent sectors, have strict limits (often ₹5,000 or less) on gifts employees can accept — verify before selecting a gift value.

**Avoid overly personal items** regardless of budget — corporate gifts should remain professionally appropriate.

**Consider dietary and cultural sensitivities** for gift hampers containing food items, particularly for diverse client bases.
