Choose Bose if comfort and noise cancelling are your priorities, Sony if you want the most complete feature set for the money, and Sennheiser if you value natural sound and very long battery life. Their flagship over-ear headphones all cost roughly ₹25,000 to ₹40,000 in India, so the decision is about character rather than price.

This is a researched brand comparison based on product lines and reviewer consensus, not hands-on testing. Check which generation is being sold before ordering, because all three brands keep older models on sale beside the current flagship, and an older pair at a similar price is a worse deal.

*Prices below are approximate and can change during sales, so check the live price on Amazon before you buy.*

## Bose: quiet and comfortable

Bose's QuietComfort line (including the QuietComfort Ultra Headphones) is known for the best passive comfort and some of the strongest low-frequency noise cancelling. The sound is smooth and slightly warm, with an app for EQ. Weight is low and the clamp is gentle, so they suit long flights and office days.

The trade-offs are fewer codec options than Sony on some models and a relatively simple app. Battery life is around 20 to 24 hours. Choose Bose if you wear headphones for hours or want a quiet cabin on a long train ride. [Browse Bose headphones](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=Bose+QuietComfort+headphones&tag=adifystore-21).

## Sony: the feature leader

Sony's WH-1000XM models combine strong noise cancelling with an extensive app, adaptive sound control, multipoint Bluetooth, LDAC for high-resolution audio on Android and quick charge. The XM line sits at roughly ₹25,000-35,000 depending on generation. The sound is clean and adjustable, though some find the default tuning bass-forward. The foldable design on some generations travels well, while others use a non-folding case.

Sony is the best default for anyone who wants strong all-round performance and many controls. [Search Sony headphones](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=Sony+noise+cancelling+headphones&tag=adifystore-21).

## Sennheiser: natural sound and endurance

Sennheiser's Momentum line, such as the Momentum 4 Wireless, is known for a refined, natural sound and very long battery life, quoted at up to around 60 hours. Noise cancelling is good but generally a step behind the best from Bose and Sony in loud environments. Its app includes an EQ and sound personalisation, and it supports aptX Adaptive on Android. Prices are roughly ₹25,000-35,000.

Sennheiser suits listeners who care about music and want a pair that lasts through a long trip without charging. [Browse Sennheiser Momentum](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=Sennheiser+Momentum+4+Wireless&tag=adifystore-21).

## ANC versus fidelity

If you commute on a noisy local train or fly often, noise cancelling matters most and Bose or Sony lead. If you listen to music in quiet places at home, sound quality matters more and Sennheiser is a good choice. Wireless fidelity is limited by Bluetooth, so a high-quality wired headphone remains better for critical listening, though you give up convenience.

## Comfort and weight

Bose is typically lightest and most relaxed. Sony sits in between, with cushioned pads and moderate clamp. Sennheiser's pads are comfortable but slightly firmer. If you have a larger head or wear glasses, try them at a store because clamp force affects pressure on the temples.

## Calls

All three offer decent microphones. Sony and Bose handle background noise slightly better on calls. If you work from noisy cafes, favour those two.

## Apps and ecosystems

Sony's app is the most customisable. Bose's is simpler. Sennheiser's Smart Control app is balanced between the two. None locks you into a phone brand, which makes all three good choices for Android and iPhone users alike.

## Protecting the pair

A hard travel case helps if your headphone case is soft. [Browse headphone hard cases](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=headphone+hard+travel+case&tag=adifystore-21) for daily commutes.

## Service and warranty

All three brands operate in India with authorised retailers. Buy from an official brand store or an authorised seller on Amazon, and keep the invoice, because grey-market pairs can lack warranty.

## Who should pick which

- **Pick Bose** if comfort and silence matter above all.
- **Pick Sony** if you want the most features and flexibility, plus strong value.
- **Pick Sennheiser** if you listen critically and travel long distances without charging.
- **Skip all three** if you want in-ear convenience; premium earbuds suit gym and commute better. [See noise cancelling earbuds](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=premium+noise+cancelling+earbuds&tag=adifystore-21).

## Recommendation

For a single pick, Sony offers the most complete package. For marathon wear and travel quiet, go with Bose. For natural sound and exceptional battery life, go with Sennheiser. Confirm the generation, warranty and return window before ordering.
