## How to Choose a Memory Foam Mattress

Start with your sleeping position. Back sleepers usually prefer medium-firm to firm, side sleepers need more cushioning at the shoulders and hips, and stomach sleepers do best on firmer surfaces.

Check thickness and layers. Six inches is a minimum for comfort, and eight inches or more gives better depth and support, especially for heavier sleepers. Read what each layer does rather than relying on the total thickness.

Look at heat and airflow. Memory foam holds warmth, so look for gel infusion, open-cell foam or a grid design if you sleep hot. A breathable cover also helps.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Ignoring the trial period.** A home trial lets you test the mattress properly. Read the return and pickup terms.

**Buying the wrong size.** Queen size varies slightly between brands. Measure your bed frame and compare dimensions.

**Overlooking the base.** A slatted or solid base with proper support keeps the mattress in shape.

**Choosing softness over support.** Very soft mattresses can lead to back discomfort for some people.

## What to Check on the Listing

Confirm the exact dimensions in inches, the thickness, the density of foam layers and the warranty. See whether the cover is removable and washable, and read recent reviews for sagging or odour complaints in the first months.

## Quick FAQ

**How long does a memory foam mattress last?** Many last seven to ten years with care, though quality varies.

**Do I need to flip it?** Most foam mattresses are one-sided and do not need flipping, but rotating them occasionally can help even out wear.

**Will it smell?** New foam can have a mild odour that fades after airing for a day or two.
