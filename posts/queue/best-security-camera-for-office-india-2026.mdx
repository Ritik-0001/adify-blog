## Office Camera Positioning

**Reception:** Wide-angle camera covering entry, visitor seating, reception desk — captures all visitors entering.

**Server room:** Camera covering the door — records all access. Critical for IT security audits.

**Exit doors:** Cover all emergency exits and stairwells. Compliance requirement for many corporate buildings.

**Parking/loading area:** Outdoor PTZ or fixed camera covering vehicle access.

**Open work areas:** Ceiling-mounted wide-angle dome cameras — 1 camera per 30–40 sq ft adequately covers open plan offices.

**Conference rooms:** Optional — requires explicit disclosure to employees (HR and legal review recommended).

## Compliance and Data Considerations

**Employee notification:** Indian law does not specifically require it, but HR best practices mandate disclosure in offer letters/employee handbooks that CCTV operates in workplace areas.

**Data retention:** Most insurers require 30-day retention. Financial and regulated industries may require 90 days.

**Access controls:** Limit who can view footage — not all managers should have DVR access. Hikvision NVR supports multi-user roles.

**Recommendation:** Hikvision IP NVR for medium and large offices — superior resolution and enterprise features justify the higher cost. CP Plus for offices in Tier 2/3 cities with local installation requirements. TP-Link VIGI for small offices wanting simpler setup.
