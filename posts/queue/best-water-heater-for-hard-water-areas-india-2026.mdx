## What hard water does to a geyser

Hard water carries dissolved calcium and magnesium. When heated, these precipitate as scale. In a geyser, scale coats the heating element, which then works harder, overheats and burns out. Scale also collects at the bottom of the tank and acts as insulation, increasing electricity use and slowing heating.

Delhi NCR, Rajasthan, Gujarat, Haryana, parts of Punjab, Hyderabad and Chennai borewell areas are well-known for hard or high-TDS water. If your kettle fills with white crust within weeks, treat your water as hard.

## Tank materials

### 1. Glass-lined or vitreous enamel tanks

Mild steel with a glass-like inner coating, the standard in good storage geysers. It resists corrosion, but scale still builds up. These work with a good anode.

### 2. Stainless steel tanks

A stainless steel inner tank resists corrosion without needing a sacrificial anode as much, though poor welds can still leak. Many premium models use 304-grade steel. Check [stainless steel tank water heater](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=stainless+steel+tank+water+heater&tag=adifystore-21).

### 3. Copper or special-alloy elements

An element coated with titanium or made of Incoloy alloy resists scale better than a plain copper element. This one detail often decides how long a geyser lasts in a hard-water home.

## The magnesium anode

Most storage geysers have a magnesium anode rod inside the tank. It corrodes in place of the tank walls. In hard water, the anode wears out faster, often every two to three years. When it is gone, the tank starts to rust. Ask whether the anode is replaceable and whether the brand sells it. See [geyser magnesium anode rod](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=geyser+magnesium+anode+rod&tag=adifystore-21) listings to see if spares are easy to find.

## Descaling routine

- Turn off power and drain the tank once or twice a year in hard water.
- Flush using the drain valve or by loosening the inlet, until the water runs clear.
- Remove the element and soak it in a vinegar or descaling solution, if the manual allows, so scale loosens.
- Replace the anode when it is mostly consumed.

Many service centres charge a visit fee for this. Doing it yearly is far cheaper than replacing a geyser after four years. Search [geyser descaler](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=geyser+descaler&tag=adifystore-21) for descaling chemicals and read the safety instructions.

## Treat the water as well as the heater

A whole-house water softener, or a salt-based or resin softener installed on the main inlet, protects the geyser, taps and washing machine together. It costs far more than a geyser, but in severe hard water areas it saves money across the entire plumbing system. A cartridge-based scale inhibitor is a smaller option. Compare [water softener for home](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=water+softener+for+home&tag=adifystore-21) if you have very hard borewell water.

## Instant versus storage

Instant geysers have tiny heating chambers that clog quickly with scale and can fail abruptly. In hard water, a storage model with a bigger element surface and a descaling access port tends to last longer. If you must use an instant one, add a scale filter before it. ## Service and warranty questions

Ask before you buy: how long is the warranty on the tank versus the element? A five- to seven-year tank warranty with a one-year element warranty is common; check the terms for hard-water exclusions, since some brands void coverage if you do not descale. Make sure there is a service centre in your city that stocks anodes and elements.

## Who should skip premium models

If your water is only moderately hard and you add a pre-filter, a mid-range geyser with a good element is enough. Spending beyond ₹30,000 on a geyser is rarely about scale resistance; it is more about design and capacity.
