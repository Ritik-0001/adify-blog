## What Defines "Premium" Tier

**Self-washing, self-drying mop system:** Eliminates the mildew odor problem that affects mop pads left damp in a dock, and removes the manual mop-cleaning chore entirely.

**Extendable side brush/arm:** Physically extends to sweep debris from corners and wall edges that a purely circular robot body geometrically cannot reach.

**Advanced AI obstacle avoidance:** Uses onboard cameras and machine learning to identify and navigate around cables, small objects, and pet waste — preventing the "robot smeared something across the floor" failure mode of less sophisticated obstacle detection.

**Auto-empty AND auto-refill:** Top-tier docks handle dust emptying, clean water refilling, and dirty water draining, approaching true "set and forget" operation for weeks at a time.
