## What Matters When Choosing an OTT Streaming Device

**App support:** All Indian OTT apps should be available without sideloading. Fire TV and Android TV both have good Indian app support.

**Remote:** A physical voice remote makes it usable for all family members — not just tech-savvy users. Chromecast without Google TV requires phone control.

**Processor:** Heavier OTT apps (Hotstar, JioCinema during IPL) can lag on underpowered devices. Choose a device with at least 2GB RAM.

**Wi-Fi:** Wi-Fi 5 (802.11ac) minimum. Wi-Fi 6/6E future-proofs for faster ISP speeds.

**Resolution:** 4K streaming future-proofs the setup even if current TV is FHD — content is moving to 4K.
