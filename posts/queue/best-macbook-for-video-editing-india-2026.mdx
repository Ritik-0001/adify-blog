## What Actually Matters for Video Editing Performance

**GPU cores:** More GPU cores mean faster timeline scrubbing, effects rendering, and export times. This matters more than CPU core count for most editing software.

**Unified memory (RAM):** 4K/6K footage with multiple tracks, effects, and color grades consumes memory fast. 24GB is the practical minimum for serious 4K work; 16GB will bottleneck on complex timelines.

**ProRes hardware acceleration:** Apple Silicon chips include dedicated media engines that accelerate ProRes and H.264/H.265 encoding/decoding — this is why MacBooks often outperform Windows laptops with objectively higher specs on video-specific tasks.

**Storage:** 1TB minimum for active project files; use external drives for archival footage.
