## ANC Reality Check: What ₹5000 Buys

Budget ANC headphones use a single-microphone feedforward system — one mic outside the earcup picks up ambient noise and the DSP generates an inverse signal. This works well for:
- Consistent low-frequency noise (AC, fan, engine hum)
- Traffic drone during commutes
- Office background noise (keyboards, distant conversations)

It works poorly for:
- Sharp, sudden sounds (announcements, doors slamming)
- Close conversations and high-pitched voices
- Irregular noise patterns

Premium ANC (Sony XM5, Bose QC45 at ₹25,000–₹35,000) uses multi-microphone hybrid feedforward + feedback systems that adapt to noise profiles in real time — significantly more effective. At ₹5,000, you're getting a simplified version of this.
