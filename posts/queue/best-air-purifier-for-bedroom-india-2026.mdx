## Bedroom-Specific Requirements

**Noise level:** At night, any sound above 40dB is noticeable. Look for sleep/night mode under 35dB. 30–33dB is ideal — comparable to a library or quiet office.

**CADR for bedroom:** A 15m² bedroom (standard Indian apartment room) needs ~150 m³/hour minimum CADR for 5 air changes per hour. 200–300 CADR covers most Indian bedrooms comfortably.

**Size:** Bedroom placement is typically beside the bed or on a dresser. Compact units (under 30cm footprint) fit without disrupting the room.

**Night mode:** Dims the LED display indicator. Small detail, but a bright blue LED in a dark room is genuinely disruptive.

## Bedroom Placement Tips

**Position near breathing zone:** Place on bedside table or 1–2 metres from the head of the bed — not across the room.

**Avoid pointing airflow at face:** Air purifiers move a significant volume of air. Direct airflow causes dry throat and eyes. Angle slightly away from the bed.

**Run on auto overnight:** Auto mode is quieter than manual high speed and adjusts to actual air quality. Set before sleeping and let it run.

**Keep bedroom door closed:** A sealed room allows the purifier to clean air more efficiently — keeps outdoor pollution from diluting the purified air.
