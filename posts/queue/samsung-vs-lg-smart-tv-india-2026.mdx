## Panel Technology

Samsung pushes QLED across its mid and upper ranges, which gives strong brightness and colour, and offers OLED in its premium models. LG focuses on OLED at the top and offers LED and QNED models at lower prices.

For bright rooms, Samsung's QLED shines. For dim rooms and films, LG's OLED offers deeper blacks.

## Smart Platform

Samsung uses Tizen, which is fast and clean, with strong integration for Samsung phones and appliances. LG uses webOS, which offers a simple, pointer-driven interface and quick access to apps.

Both cover the major streaming apps, so choose the interface you prefer.

## Gaming

Both offer 120Hz, VRR and HDMI 2.1 on higher models. LG's OLED range is popular with gamers thanks to four HDMI 2.1 ports. Samsung's high-end models also offer strong gaming features and a game bar.

Check the specifications of the exact model.

## Sound

Both provide decent built-in sound at mid-range, and both benefit from a soundbar. Samsung's Q-Symphony can pair the TV speakers with a compatible Samsung soundbar.

## Price and Range

Prices overlap at most sizes. LG's LED range is competitively priced, and Samsung's QLED range often offers strong value during sales. OLED models cost more from both brands.

## Service and Availability

Both have wide service networks. Check the nearest service centre and the warranty terms.

## How to Choose Between Samsung and LG

Start with the room and budget. If you have a bright room, look at QLED. If you want OLED, LG has more options at different prices.

Read recent reviews on the exact model, especially for panel uniformity, HDR performance and software.

## Real-World Scenarios

**A bright living room.** A Samsung QLED is a strong fit.

**A dark home theatre.** An LG OLED suits this better.

**A console gamer.** Compare HDMI 2.1 ports and features.

**A budget buyer.** Compare entry-level LED models from both.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Comparing different tiers.** Compare like for like.

**Ignoring ports.** Check HDMI specifications.

**Skipping the sound.** Plan for a soundbar.

## Quick FAQ

**Which is brighter?** Samsung's QLED models are typically brighter.

**Which has deeper blacks?** LG's OLED models do.

**Which is better for gaming?** Both offer strong options.

## What to Check on the Listing

Compare the panel type, the refresh rate, the number of HDMI 2.1 ports and the operating system. Look at the warranty length and what it covers on the panel, and check whether installation is included. Read recent reviews for reports of panel uniformity, software bugs and after-sales support.

## Software and Ecosystem

Samsung's Tizen integrates with SmartThings, which can control compatible Samsung appliances and smart home devices. LG's webOS supports Google Assistant, Alexa and Apple AirPlay, and offers a quick, simple home screen. If you already own a Samsung phone or appliances, Samsung's ecosystem adds convenience, and LG is more neutral.

## Setup and Long-Term Use

Both brands provide software updates for several years. After setup, spend time in the picture settings, choose a cinema or filmmaker mode and calibrate brightness for your room. A soundbar is the most effective upgrade for either brand.

## Sports, Films and Streaming

For cricket and sports, brightness and motion handling matter, and Samsung's QLED sets do well in bright rooms. For films in a dark room, LG's OLED offers deeper blacks. For everyday streaming, both platforms carry all the major apps, so pick the interface you find quicker to navigate.

## Smart Home and Extras

Samsung's SmartThings can control compatible appliances, lights and speakers from the TV. LG's ThinQ does something similar for LG appliances. If you already own products from one brand, staying in the ecosystem adds convenience, though it is rarely a reason on its own to buy a TV.

## Warranty and After-Sales

Read what the warranty covers, especially the panel, and ask whether installation and a demo are included. Keep the invoice and register the product with the brand if it offers an app, since it helps with service requests.

## Recommendation

If you want a bright, value-focused TV, start with Samsung. If you want OLED or webOS, consider LG. Match the model to your room, budget and use.
