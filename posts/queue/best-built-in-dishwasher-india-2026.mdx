## How to Choose a Built-in Dishwasher

Check capacity and racks. 12 to 14 place settings suit most families, and flexible racks hold large Indian vessels and cookers.

Look at the wash programmes. An intensive or heavy-duty programme with higher temperature handles oil and turmeric stains better.

Plan plumbing and power. You need a water inlet, a drain and a power socket near the cabinet, and a good installer will check them.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Skipping a plumbing check.** Plan the points.

**Buying for place settings alone.** Rack flexibility matters.

**Ignoring noise.** Check the claim.

**Not pre-scraping.** Remove large food waste.

## Real-World Scenarios

**A family of four.** A 12 place setting model.

**A joint family.** A 14 place setting model.

**A small kitchen.** A slimline 45 cm model.

## What to Check on the Listing

Confirm the capacity, the cutout dimensions, the programmes, the noise claim, the water use, the warranty and the installation terms. See whether the installation is included.

## Planning the Installation

Plan the cabinet cutout, the water and drain lines and the power socket before ordering, and keep the dishwasher near the sink. Use a water softener or salt if your water is hard, as the maker advises.

## Quick FAQ

**Do dishwashers clean Indian utensils?** Models with intensive programmes handle them well.

**How much water do they use?** Often less than hand washing, depending on the model.

**Do they need special detergent?** Use dishwasher detergent and rinse aid.

## Loading Indian Utensils

Tilt pans and kadais so water reaches the inside, keep steel plates upright in the rack and place bowls face down. Large cookers and tawas often need a hand wash or a lower rack with the tines folded. Scrape off large food waste and use the intensive programme for oily or turmeric-stained items.

## Water, Salt and Detergent

If your water is hard, fill the salt compartment as the maker advises, use a proper dishwasher detergent and add rinse aid for spotless glass. Clean the filter weekly and run an empty hot cycle with a cleaning agent now and then to prevent smells.

## Warranty and Service

Dishwashers have pumps, valves and electronics, so check the warranty on the whole unit and on key parts, and whether service is available in your city. Ask the seller who handles installation, because incorrect plumbing can cause leaks and may affect warranty claims.
