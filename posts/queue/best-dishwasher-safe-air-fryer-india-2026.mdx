## How to Choose an Easy-Clean Air Fryer

Check which parts are dishwasher safe. The basket and tray are often safe, while the heating element and housing are not. Read the manual and the listing carefully.

Look at the coating and the shape. Nonstick or ceramic surfaces resist sticking, and simple shapes with few corners are easier to clean.

Think about how you will clean it. If you prefer hand washing, a wide basket and a smooth surface help. A dishwasher only helps if it fits the basket.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Using abrasive scrubbers.** They damage the coating.

**Putting the whole unit in the dishwasher.** Only removable parts.

**Skipping drying.** Dry before storing.

**Ignoring odours.** Wash after fatty foods.

## Real-World Scenarios

**A busy household.** A removable basket saves daily effort.

**A family that cooks marinated food.** Cleaning matters more.

**A small kitchen.** Check basket size for your dishwasher.

## Quick FAQ

**Is dishwasher safe always best?** It saves effort, but many makers still recommend hand washing for the coating.

**Can I use dishwasher detergent?** Follow the manual.

**How long do coatings last?** With care, several years.

## Cleaning Routine That Extends Life

Let the air fryer cool before cleaning, then wash the basket and tray with warm soapy water and a soft sponge, or in the dishwasher if the manual allows it. Wipe the heating element area with a damp cloth occasionally to remove grease. Avoid metal utensils that scratch nonstick coatings, and use silicone or wooden tools.

## Which Foods Make the Most Mess

Marinated meats, cheese-topped items and battered foods cause the most residue. Lining the basket with perforated parchment paper made for air fryers reduces sticking and keeps cleaning to a minimum. Do not use regular parchment paper without perforations, since it can block airflow.

## Basket Size and Dishwasher Fit

Before you count on the dishwasher, measure your dishwasher's rack and compare it with the air fryer's basket, since large XL baskets may not fit flat. If the basket is too big, wash it by hand and use the dishwasher only for smaller parts such as trays and dividers. Also check whether the dishwasher's heated dry cycle is safe for the coating.
