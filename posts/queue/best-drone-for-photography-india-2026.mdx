## Camera Specs — What Matters for Aerial Photography

**Sensor size:** Larger sensors capture more light, especially in low light (golden hour, dusk). The 1/1.3-inch sensor in the Mini 4 Pro and Air 3 is the best available in non-professional consumer drones under ₹2,00,000.

**Aperture:** ƒ/1.7 on the Mini 4 Pro (wider than most drone cameras). Wider aperture = more light = better low-light shots without increasing ISO noise. At sunrise and sunset — the golden hours for landscape photography — ƒ/1.7 produces cleaner images.

**RAW capture:** 10-bit RAW (DNG) allows extensive post-processing without quality loss. For serious photographers who process images in Lightroom or Capture One, RAW capture is essential. JPEG-only drones (common in budget models) are limiting for professional-level post-processing.

**Obstacle sensing:** Omnidirectional obstacle sensing on the Mini 4 Pro prevents crashes when focusing on composition rather than navigation — particularly important during concentrated photography sessions.
