## What Makes a Printer Durable

**Print head type:** Fixed print heads (built into the printer, not the cartridge) last significantly longer than cartridge-integrated heads. Epson and Canon's dedicated print heads are designed for tens of thousands of pages. HP cartridge printers replace the print head every time you replace the cartridge — this protects the printer but makes cartridges expensive.

**Ink system:** Ink-tank printers have simpler ink delivery systems than cartridge printers — no cartridge contact points, fewer seals to fail. Less failure-prone over 4–6 years.

**Duty cycle:** Manufacturers rate printers by monthly duty cycle — the maximum recommended pages per month. A printer rated for 1,000 pages/month will outlast one rated for 200 pages/month even at identical actual use.

**Heat management:** Laser printers run hot — fuser assemblies wear out after 20,000–50,000 pages. Inkjet printers generate less heat but dry ink in nozzles if unused for months.

## Printer Maintenance for Longer Life (India Conditions)

**Dust covers:** Indian dust levels are high. A simple cloth dust cover over the printer when not in use prevents paper feed roller contamination — a common cause of paper jams after 2–3 years.

**Regular use:** Print at least one page per week, even if you don't need to. This prevents nozzle drying in inkjet printers. For laser printers, occasional use is fine.

**Genuine ink:** Non-genuine inks can damage print head nozzles. The ₹200–₃₀₀ saving on third-party ink bottles is not worth a ₹2,000–₃,000 print head repair.

**Power stability:** Connect printers through a basic surge protector. Power surges (common in Indian electrical systems) damage electronic control boards. A ₹500 surge protector extends printer life.

**Recommendation:** Epson L3252 for color home printing — the EcoTank system's durability advantage over cartridge printers is real and well-documented by Indian users. Brother HL-L2321D for dedicated black-and-white document printing with the highest reliability ceiling.
