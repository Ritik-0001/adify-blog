Roughly ₹5,000–12,000. A 4 to 6 litre air fryer lets a couple cook snacks and many meals with little oil. It is a popular gift because it is easy to use and fits small kitchens, though capacity should fit the size of their household and it does take up counter space.

## How to Choose a Wedding Gift

Match the budget and the relationship. Close family may choose a premium appliance, while friends and colleagues can pick a useful kitchen item at a lower price.

Think about the couple's home. A small flat suits compact appliances, while a larger home suits a bigger air fryer or an oven.

Avoid duplicates. Weddings often bring several of the same item, so a quiet check with the family helps.

## Timing and Delivery

Send gifts a few days before the wedding if you can, or deliver to the couple's home after the festivities, when there is time to unpack and set things up. Large appliances are better shipped to the new home than to the venue, and a quick message to a family member avoids a missed delivery. Share the invoice so the couple can claim the warranty without hassle.

## Pooling Money for One Bigger Gift

Siblings and cousins can pool money for a single premium appliance such as a convection oven or a coffee machine, which usually feels more generous than several small gifts. If you give more than one item, pair a small appliance with something personal, such as a recipe book or a set of good spices.

## Who Should Skip Appliance Gifts

If the couple is moving into a rented flat or about to relocate abroad, bulky appliances become a burden, and cash or a gift card is kinder. Skip a coffee machine when you are not sure they drink coffee; it is the item most likely to sit unopened. An honest trade-off between the two safest picks: the air fryer suits people who want quick weeknight meals, while the mixer grinder suits people who cook traditional Indian food daily, so choose by their cooking habits rather than by what looks more impressive.

## Recommendation

For a safe wedding gift, choose an air fryer or a mixer grinder, and for a premium option, consider a coffee machine or a convection oven. Confirm the specifications on the listing and keep the invoice.
