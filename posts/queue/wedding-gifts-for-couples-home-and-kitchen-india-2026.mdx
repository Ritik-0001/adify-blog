## How to Choose a Wedding Gift

Match the budget and the relationship. Close family may choose a premium appliance, while friends and colleagues can pick a useful kitchen item at a lower price.

Think about the couple's home. A small flat suits compact appliances, while a larger home suits a bigger air fryer or an oven.

Avoid duplicates. Weddings often bring several of the same item, so a quiet check with the family helps.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Gifting something fragile or bulky without delivery planning.** Plan delivery to the right address.

**Choosing a very niche gadget.** Pick something useful for most homes.

**Ignoring the warranty.** Include the invoice and the warranty card.

**Forgetting a gift note.** A short message makes a simple gift feel personal.

## Real-World Scenarios

**A close friend with a modest budget.** An air fryer or a cookware set.

**A cousin setting up a new flat.** A mixer grinder and a microwave oven.

**A colleague.** A coffee machine or a good dinner set.

## Quick FAQ

**Should I send gifts to the venue or home?** Delivery to the home is usually easier.

**Is cash better?** Many families prefer cash, but a thoughtful gift can be remembered for years.

**Should I buy a gift card?** It is an option if you are unsure what they need.

## Gifting Etiquette and Timing

Send gifts a few days before the wedding if you can, or deliver to the couple's home after the festivities, when there is time to unpack and set things up. Large appliances are better shipped to the new home than to the venue, and a quick message to a family member avoids a missed delivery. Add a short handwritten note, and share the invoice so the couple can claim the warranty without hassle.

## What to Check on the Listing

Look at the seller rating, the return window and the warranty terms. For electrical items, confirm the voltage and plug type suit Indian homes, check the capacity against the couple's kitchen, and see whether installation is included. If the gift is fragile, read reviews for packaging quality, since a damaged gift on arrival spoils the moment.

## Combining Gifts for a Fuller Set

If you want to give more than one item, bundle a small appliance with something personal, such as a recipe book, a set of good spices or a framed photo. Siblings and cousins can also pool money for a single premium appliance such as a convection oven or a coffee machine, which usually feels more generous than several small gifts.

## Recommendation

For a safe wedding gift, choose an air fryer or a mixer grinder, and for a premium option, consider a coffee machine or a convection oven. Confirm the specifications on the listing and keep the invoice.
