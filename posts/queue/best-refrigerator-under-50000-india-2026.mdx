## Why Convertible Freezer Technology Matters

Traditional refrigerators fix freezer and fridge space permanently — convertible models let you shift the balance (e.g., converting freezer to fridge mode) based on seasonal needs: less freezer space needed in winter, more fridge space needed during festival cooking and entertaining, or vice versa when stocking up on frozen items during hot summer months.
