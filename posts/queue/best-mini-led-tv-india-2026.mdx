## Why Mini-LED Matters

**Traditional LED TVs:** Typically use dozens to a few hundred local dimming zones — large zones mean less precise control, causing "blooming" (light bleeding around bright objects on dark backgrounds).

**Mini-LED TVs:** Use thousands of much smaller LEDs organized into hundreds or thousands of independently controlled zones — dramatically reduces blooming and achieves near-OLED contrast precision.

**The brightness advantage:** Unlike OLED (which self-limits brightness to prevent burn-in and manage power), Mini-LED backlighting can push significantly higher peak brightness — better for HDR highlight impact and bright-room viewing.

## Mini-LED vs. OLED: Which to Choose

**Choose Mini-LED if:** Your room has significant ambient light, you want maximum brightness for HDR impact, or you're concerned about OLED burn-in risk with static content (news tickers, gaming HUDs).

**Choose OLED if:** You primarily watch in controlled/dark lighting, absolute best contrast and black levels matter most, and you don't display static content for extended periods.
