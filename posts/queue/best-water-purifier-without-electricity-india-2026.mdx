## When Non-Electric Purifiers Work (and When They Don't)

**Works for:**
- Municipal water with TDS under 200 ppm (check with TDS meter)
- Water where primary concern is bacteria and biological contamination
- Households in frequent power outage areas where electric purifiers are unreliable
- Rental homes where installing a wall-mounted electric purifier is impractical
- Small households (1–2 people) with low water consumption

**Does NOT work for:**
- Borewell or groundwater (high TDS)
- Hard water (TDS above 200 ppm) — UF cannot reduce dissolved minerals
- Water with heavy metal contamination (arsenic, fluoride)
- Water with chemical contamination
