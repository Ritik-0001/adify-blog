## How to Choose a Built-in Hob

Look at the burners. Brass burners hold up well and spread heat evenly, while a mix of sizes lets you cook different dishes.

Check the safety features. A flame failure device cuts the gas if the flame goes out, and auto ignition is convenient.

Measure the cutout. Check the cutout size against your counter, and make sure the model suits your gas connection and cylinder or piped gas.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Ignoring the cutout size.** Measure carefully.

**Choosing thin glass.** Look for toughened glass.

**Skipping flame failure safety.** Prefer it.

**Using the wrong cookware.** Large pans can overheat the glass edges.

## Real-World Scenarios

**A modular kitchen.** A built-in hob with a matching chimney.

**A family that cooks daily.** A hob with brass burners and a wok burner.

**A rental home.** A freestanding stove, as built-ins need a cutout.

## What to Check on the Listing

Confirm the glass thickness, the burner material and sizes, the ignition type, the flame failure device, the cutout size and the warranty. See whether installation is included.

## Care for Glass Hobs

Wipe spills quickly, avoid dragging heavy pans across the glass and clean with a soft cloth and a mild cleaner. Let the glass cool before cleaning, and do not drop heavy items on it.

## Quick FAQ

**Are glass hobs safe?** Toughened glass is designed for kitchen use, with careful handling.

**Do I need a built-in?** Only if you have a counter cutout planned.

**Brass or aluminium burners?** Brass lasts longer.

## LPG vs PNG Compatibility

Most hobs are sold set for LPG, and some can be converted to piped natural gas with a different nozzle kit. If your building has piped gas, check that the model supports it and who handles the conversion. Using the wrong nozzles is unsafe, so always involve an authorised technician.

## Installation Planning

Measure the counter cutout, check clearance to the wall and chimney and make sure the hob sits level. Keep the gas hose short and approved, and use a certified installer. Test for leaks after installation with soap water, and keep the flame failure device clean.

## Matching the Hob With a Chimney

Choose a chimney that is at least as wide as the hob, ideally a 90 cm model for a 4-burner layout, and mount it at the maker's recommended height. A mismatched pair leaves smoke escaping around the edges. Many brands sell hobs and chimneys as matched sets with a joint warranty.

## Final Checklist Before You Buy

Before you order, measure the counter cutout or wall space and compare it with the dimensions on the listing, ask your carpenter and electrician to confirm clearances, power points and gas or water connections, and check who handles installation. Read the warranty terms on motors and electronics, keep the invoice and manual, and confirm that spare parts and service exist in your city.
