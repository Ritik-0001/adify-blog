## Service Network Comparison

**Aquaguard (Eureka Forbes):** Most extensive water purifier service network in India. Eureka Forbes has been selling in Indian homes since the 1980s through direct sales force — the largest technician network of any purifier brand. Service available in Tier 2, Tier 3 cities, and semi-urban areas where Kent's network is thinner.

**Kent:** Strong service in major metros (Delhi, Mumbai, Bangalore, Hyderabad, Chennai, Pune). Service infrastructure in Tier 2 cities is adequate but less extensive than Aquaguard. Kent offers 1-year free service — no cost first-year AMC.

---

## AMC (Annual Maintenance Contract) Cost Comparison

Water purifier AMC covers: annual servicing, filter inspection, membrane check, and emergency call-outs.

**Kent AMC:** ₹1,800–₂,500/year after the free first year. Includes filter replacements during service visit.

**Aquaguard AMC:** ₹2,500–₃,500/year. Higher cost but includes more service visits and Aquaguard's service guarantee.

Over 5 years, Kent's lower AMC saves ₹3,500–₅,500 in service costs compared to Aquaguard.

## Hardware Quality

Both brands use similar quality RO membranes (Dow Filmtec and Vontron are common sources). Kent's additional purification stages (UF stage as third-line filter) provide an additional safety margin. Aquaguard's newer models include "Active Copper" and mineral-enhancement features.

**Build quality:** Comparable. Kent uses more plastic (cost reduction), Aquaguard slightly better fit-and-finish on premium models.

## When to Choose Each

**Choose Kent if:**
- You live in a metro or major city with good Kent service
- Storage capacity matters (15L Kent vs. 7L Aquaguard standard)
- Budget is the primary constraint (Kent has more features per rupee)
- You want first-year service included without extra cost

**Choose Aquaguard if:**
- You live in a Tier 2/3 city or town where Aquaguard's network is stronger
- You prioritise service responsiveness over hardware specs
- You want Eureka Forbes's brand reliability and legacy

**Recommendation:** Kent Grand Plus (₹14,000–₁₆,000) for most Indian metro buyers — better hardware value. Aquaguard Enhance (₹12,000–₁₅,000) for buyers in cities where Eureka Forbes service is demonstrably more accessible than Kent.
