## What to Buy During Great Indian Festival

### Best Discounts (Buy During GIF)

**TVs:** 20–30% off. 55-inch Sony/Samsung/LG that sells for ₹60,000 MRP typically reaches ₹45,000–₅₀,000. Exchange adds ₹3,000–₅,000.

**Earbuds and Headphones:** 30–50% off. The single best category. boAt Airdopes, Nothing Ear (a), JBL TWS drop to half MRP during flash deals.

**Smartwatches:** 30–40% off. Noise, Fire-Boltt, and boAt smartwatches reach their lowest annual prices.

**Small Appliances:** 20–30% off. Air fryers, mixer grinders, induction cooktops. Indian brands (Pigeon, Prestige, Philips India) see significant GIF discounts.

### Moderate Discounts (Also Worth Buying)

**Smartphones:** 10–20% off + bank discount + exchange. Net discount can reach 25–30% on select models.

**Laptops:** 10–20% off. Best for established models that have been available for 6+ months.

**Refrigerators and Washing Machines:** 15–20% off. Often combined with exchange for additional savings on large appliances.

### Limited Discounts (Wait or Shop Elsewhere)

**Apple products:** Minimal discounts on Apple — typically 5% off only. Apple sales tend to be on older models, not current flagship.

**High-end cameras:** Discounts are small. Better to buy after thorough research independent of sale timing.

## Bank Discount Strategy

**HDFC Bank (Debit + Credit):** Amazon's primary banking partner for GIF — typically 10% off (up to ₹2,500 off) on all HDFC transactions. Apply at checkout.

**SBI Debit Card:** Usually 5–10% off on select transactions during GIF.

**ICICI, Axis:** Also participate in rotating offers. Check Amazon's 'Bank Offers' tab at checkout.

**EMI:** No-cost EMI on 3–6 months is widely available during GIF. For purchases above ₹20,000, no-cost EMI on HDFC/ICICI is offered.

## GIF vs Big Billion Days

Amazon and Flipkart run concurrently to compete for the same buyers. Strategy:
- Electronics: Amazon generally has better deals on major brands (Sony, Samsung, Apple)
- Fashion and lifestyle: Flipkart typically has edge
- Indian brands (Noise, boAt, realme): Check both — exclusive deals vary

**Same-day comparison:** Open both sites on the same day. Some brands go exclusive to one platform.

**Recommendation:** Track prices 30 days before GIF starts (typically late September). Set alerts on CamelCamelCamel for your target products. The best deals appear in the first 24–48 hours of GIF.
