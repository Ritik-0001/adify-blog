## Why the Dual-Camera System Matters

Most drones require either physically flying closer for tighter shots (disturbing wildlife, risking obstacles) or accepting digital zoom's quality loss. The Air 3S's dedicated telephoto camera captures genuine optical medium-zoom footage without either compromise — a wide establishing shot and a compressed telephoto shot from the same position, in the same flight.
