## What Students Need in a Monitor

**Size:** 21–24 inches is the student sweet spot. 21.5" fits most desks; 24" gives more screen real estate for split-screen work.

**Resolution:** 1080p (Full HD) is mandatory. 720p text is noticeably blurry at typical desk distances.

**Panel type:** IPS over TN. IPS panels have better colour accuracy and wider viewing angles — important when studying with notes or a textbook beside the monitor.

**Eye care:** Flicker-free backlight and blue light filter modes reduce eye strain during 6+ hour study sessions.

**Refresh rate:** 60Hz is sufficient for study use. 75Hz+ is a bonus but not a priority.
