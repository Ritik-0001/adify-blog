## How to Choose a Capacity

Count your laundry. A rough guide is about 1 kg per person per day for light laundry, so a family of four needs around 4 kg a day, and a machine of 7 to 8 kg handles a day or two of washing.

Think about bedding. If you wash quilts, curtains or bedsheets often, choose a larger drum.

Consider space and budget. Larger machines cost more, use more water and need more room.

## Loading Tips

Load the drum loosely, leaving a hand's width at the top for front-loaders, and balance heavy items across the load. Use the right detergent quantity and run bedding on its own program.

## Matching the Size to Your Space

A larger drum means a larger machine, so measure the width, depth and height of the space, including the door swing for front-loaders. Check the water inlet and drain positions, and allow space behind for hoses. Many flats have limited balconies or utility spaces, so planning helps avoid surprises on delivery day.

## Motor Cover Grows With Size

Larger drums put more load on the motor and bearings, so the motor warranty deserves a closer look as you move up in capacity. Check that the brand has service in your city, and keep the invoice and warranty card for claims.

## Who Should Skip This

If you live alone and wash a small load once a week, a 7 kg or larger machine will mostly run half-empty and waste water and detergent, so a compact 6 kg model or even a good semi-automatic fits better. Households whose washing is handled by a laundry service or a building facility can skip buying a large machine altogether.

## Recommendation

For most families, 7 to 8 kg is the best balance, and larger families should consider 9 to 10 kg. Measure your space and check the motor warranty on the exact model before you order.
