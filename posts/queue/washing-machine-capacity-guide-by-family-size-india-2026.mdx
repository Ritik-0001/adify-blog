## How to Choose a Capacity

Count your laundry. A rough guide is about 1 kg per person per day for light laundry, so a family of four needs around 4 kg a day, and a machine of 7 to 8 kg handles a day or two of washing.

Think about bedding. If you wash quilts, curtains or bedsheets often, choose a larger drum.

Consider space and budget. Larger machines cost more, use more water and need more room.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Buying too small.** You will run extra loads.

**Overloading the drum.** It reduces cleaning quality.

**Ignoring spin speed.** It affects drying time.

**Skipping installation planning.** Check water and drain.

## Real-World Scenarios

**A couple in a flat.** A 7 kg front-loader.

**A family of four.** An 8 kg inverter machine.

**A joint family.** A 10 kg or larger machine.

## What to Check on the Listing

Confirm the capacity, the spin speed, the energy rating, the motor warranty, the installation terms and the model number. See the drum dimensions if you wash bulky items.

## Loading Tips

Load the drum loosely, leaving a hand's width at the top for front-loaders, and balance heavy items across the load. Use the right detergent quantity and run bedding on its own program.

## Quick FAQ

**Is 7 kg enough for four people?** For light laundry, yes, but 8 kg is more comfortable.

**Is a bigger drum wasteful?** Only if you run half loads often.

**Does capacity affect cleaning?** Overloading does.

## Matching the Size to Your Space

A larger drum means a larger machine, so measure the width, depth and height of the space, including the door swing for front-loaders. Check the water inlet and drain positions, and allow space behind for hoses. Many flats have limited balconies or utility spaces, so planning helps avoid surprises on delivery day.

## Warranty and Service

Compare the motor and whole machine warranty, and check that the brand has service in your city. A longer motor warranty is a useful sign of confidence, and keeping the invoice and warranty card helps with claims.

## Recommendation

For most families, 7 to 8 kg is the best balance, and larger families should consider 9 to 10 kg. Compare specifications and warranty on each listing.
