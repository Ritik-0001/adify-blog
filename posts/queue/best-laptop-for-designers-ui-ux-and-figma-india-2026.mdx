## Why the screen decides this purchase

UI work is judged on pixels and colour. A designer needs a panel with sRGB at minimum and ideally Display P3, plus enough resolution to see 1x and 2x layouts. A 14-inch 2.8K or 3K display at 16:10 shows more of a long artboard than a 16:9 one. Brightness around 400 nits helps with daytime work, but matte or glossy finish is a personal call: glossy looks richer, matte tolerates office lights.

## RAM and the GPU question

Figma runs in the browser or desktop app and can chew through memory once files grow to hundreds of frames with libraries linked. Sixteen gigabytes handles normal product files; 32 GB helps if you also run Photoshop, Illustrator, Chrome and prototypes in parallel. A dedicated GPU is not required for UI design. Motion designers and 3D artists need one; most UI designers just need a stable integrated graphics chip and a good display. Do not pay a gaming-laptop premium for graphics you will not use.

## Screen size: 14, 15 or 16 inches?

Fourteen inches is the commuting sweet spot at under 1.6 kg. Fifteen or sixteen inches gives a comfortable canvas but pushes weight past 1.8 kg and often needs a bigger bag. Many designers pick 14 inches and add an external monitor on the desk. A [27-inch 4K monitor](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=27+inch+4K+IPS+monitor+sRGB&tag=adifystore-21) and a [USB-C hub](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=USB+C+hub+HDMI+multiport+adapter&tag=adifystore-21) transform a 14-inch laptop into a proper workstation.

## Colour accuracy on a tight budget

You do not need a ₹1,50,000 laptop to get decent colour. A mid-range model with a verified 100 percent sRGB IPS screen at around ₹70,000 to ₹80,000 is good for UI work, which targets screens rather than print. If you do print or brand packaging, add a [colour calibration device](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=Calibrite+display+calibrator&tag=adifystore-21), because laptop screens drift over time and print houses expect consistent proofs.

## Input devices that help more than specs

A good [Bluetooth mouse with a smooth scroll wheel](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=wireless+mouse+ergonomic+precision&tag=adifystore-21) makes Figma zooming easier than any trackpad, and a [graphics tablet](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=Wacom+Intuos+pen+tablet&tag=adifystore-21) helps if you sketch icons or illustrations.

## Who should skip a premium laptop

Skip a top-end model if you design only in browser-based Figma on a modest scale, since a clean 16 GB Ryzen or Core i5 laptop at around ₹65,000 will do. Skip gaming laptops for the weight, fan noise and poor colour. Skip 8 GB RAM models for any professional workflow.
