## The Honest Limitation of Sub-₹1,000 Headphones

At under ₹1,000, every headphone compromises somewhere:
- Build quality: plastic that feels lightweight and fragile
- Driver quality: acceptable but not accurate
- Comfort: earcups compress against ears, uncomfortable after 1 hour
- Mic quality: sufficient for online calls, not for recording

If headphones are for 2–4 hours of daily use over a year or more, consider stretching to ₹1,500–₁,800 (Sony MDR-ZX310) — the build quality and comfort improvement is significant at ₹600–₈₀₀ more.
