## Brand Background

Sihoo is a Chinese ergonomic furniture brand with a global presence and a range that includes the M57, M18 and Doro C300. Green Soul is an Indian brand focused on gaming and office chairs, with series such as Beast and Jupiter.

The difference in background shows up in service: Green Soul's local presence can make support and parts easier to access in India, while Sihoo relies on its Indian distributors and Amazon fulfilment.

## Adjustments and Ergonomics

Sihoo chairs typically offer adjustable lumbar support, headrest and multi-dimensional armrests, and the Doro C300 adds adaptive lumbar support. Green Soul's Beast and Jupiter chairs offer adjustable lumbar and headrests, with some models offering wide recline.

Compare the exact adjustments on the models you are considering, since both brands have entry-level and premium versions.

## Build and Materials

Both use mesh backs and metal or reinforced bases. Sihoo often emphasises the mesh quality and structure, and Green Soul emphasises frame strength and recline. Read recent reviews about long-term mesh tension and armrest durability.

## Comfort and Fit

Fit depends on your body and desk. Check seat depth, seat height range, weight limit and the headrest position. Some users find Sihoo's seat firm, while others prefer it for support. Green Soul's chairs are also firm to medium.

## Service and Warranty

Green Soul, as an Indian brand, may offer easier service coordination in some cities. Sihoo offers warranty through its sellers and distributors. Read the warranty terms on each listing and note who handles claims.

## Price and Value

Prices overlap: Sihoo M57 around ₹13,000–18,000 and Green Soul Beast around ₹12,000–17,000, with premium models higher for both. Compare features at the same price rather than brand alone.

## How to Choose Between Sihoo and Green Soul

Start with the fit and adjustments you need. If you want adaptive lumbar or extensive adjustment, look at Sihoo. If you value local service, compare Green Soul's models.

Read recent reviews on the exact model. Long-term reviews show mesh sagging and armrest wear better than specifications.

## Real-World Scenarios

**A work-from-home professional sitting eight hours a day.** Prioritise adjustable lumbar, seat depth and armrests. The Sihoo M57 and the Green Soul Jupiter Pro both suit this, and the Doro C300 adds adaptive support for a higher price.

**A student with a small room.** A compact chair such as the Sihoo M18 or a Green Soul Beast fits better and costs less.

**A taller or heavier user.** Check the weight limit, seat width and headrest position, and choose a model with a higher weight rating.

**A user who wants service nearby.** Look at the warranty terms and how claims are handled in your city before you decide on brand.
