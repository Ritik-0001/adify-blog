## Inverter vs Fixed Speed — Settle This First

Every AC worth buying in India in 2026 is inverter-type. Fixed speed ACs (the older, cheaper kind) turn the compressor fully on when cooling and fully off when it reaches temperature — cycling repeatedly. Inverter ACs vary the compressor speed continuously, maintaining temperature without cycling. The result:

- **30–40% lower electricity bills** over fixed speed
- **Quieter operation** — no on/off compressor clunking
- **Faster initial cooling** — inverter compressors run at 100%+ capacity briefly when first switched on, then settle
- **Longer compressor life** — no repeated start/stop stress

The star rating on inverter ACs (BEE rating) is calculated differently from fixed-speed ratings. A 5-star inverter AC is significantly more efficient than a 5-star fixed-speed one.

## What 1.5 Ton Covers

1.5 ton is appropriate for:
- Rooms of 120–180 sq ft in plains India
- Rooms of 100–150 sq ft in Rajasthan/UP (extreme heat zones)
- Standard 10×12 to 12×15 ft Indian bedrooms

Undersizing (putting 1 ton in a 150 sq ft room) causes the AC to run at full load constantly, burning electricity and wearing out the compressor faster. Oversizing (1.8 ton in a 100 sq ft room) leads to frequent cycling and humidity control problems — the room cools before humidity is removed, leaving a clammy feeling.

## The Installation Cost Reality

Every AC review ignores this: **installation adds ₹3,000–₹6,000** that brands don't advertise.

Standard installation (up to 3 metres of copper pipe, basic mounting) is ₹3,000–₹4,000. Additional pipe length (₹200–₹300/metre), stabiliser (₹2,000–₹3,500 if your voltage is unstable), drilling charges for concrete walls (₹500–₹1,000 extra) — budget ₹4,000–₹7,000 over the sticker price.

Buy from Amazon/Flipkart during sale: installation is included or subsidised. Buy from local dealer: negotiate installation in the price.
