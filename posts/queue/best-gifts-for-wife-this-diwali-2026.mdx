## How to Choose for Your Wife

Listen for hints. If she has mentioned her old watch stopped working, or that she never finds time to read, you already have your answer. A gift based on something she said matters more than an expensive gift chosen at random.

Avoid appliances unless she has asked for them, because a mixer or a vacuum can feel like a household purchase rather than a present. Choose accessories, personal tech or self-care items that are clearly for her.

Presentation counts. Ask for gift wrapping where available, add a note, and if possible plan a small moment for handing it over. The way the gift arrives is part of what she will remember.

## Quick FAQ

**Is an expensive gift necessary?** No. A thoughtful, well-chosen gift at ₹2,000 can mean more than a costly one that ignores her taste. Use the price only as an upper limit, not as a measure of effort.

**Which gift works if I have no clue what she likes?** A classic watch, a Kindle or a good pair of earbuds fits most tastes. If she wears jewellery, a watch in a matching tone is a very safe option.

**Should I choose Apple or Android accessories?** Match her phone. AirPods and Apple Watch work best with iPhone, while Android users are better served by earbuds and watches from Samsung, OnePlus or Amazfit.
