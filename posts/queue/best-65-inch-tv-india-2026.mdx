## OLED vs. QLED at 65 Inches

**OLED (LG C4):** Per-pixel lighting delivers true blacks and infinite contrast ratio — unmatched for dark scenes and movie watching in controlled lighting. Peak brightness, while improved with evo technology, still trails top QLED models in very bright rooms.

**QLED/Neo QLED (Samsung QN90D):** Mini-LED backlighting delivers significantly higher peak brightness, making it better suited for bright rooms with significant window light or daytime viewing. Black levels are excellent but not quite OLED's absolute per-pixel control.
