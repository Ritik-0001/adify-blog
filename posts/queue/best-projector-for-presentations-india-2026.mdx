## What Matters for Presentation Projectors

**Brightness (ANSI Lumens):** For a room that cannot be fully darkened, 3000 lumens minimum. 3500–4000 lumens for large conference rooms or classrooms with windows.

**Resolution:** Full HD (1920×1080) is now the standard. WXGA (1280×800) is acceptable for text-heavy slides but looks soft for photos or videos.

**Throw Ratio:** Short-throw projectors (ratio under 1.0) project large images from close to the screen — useful in tight conference rooms where the projector cannot be placed far back.

**Connectivity:** HDMI (for laptops and modern devices), VGA (still needed for older office PCs), and USB-A (for plug-and-play presentations without a laptop) are the essential ports for office use.
