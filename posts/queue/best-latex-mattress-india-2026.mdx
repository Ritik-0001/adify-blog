## Natural, blended or synthetic

Natural latex comes from the sap of rubber trees, and India has a real rubber industry in Kerala and Tamil Nadu, which is why several domestic brands lead here. Synthetic latex is a petroleum-based styrene-butadiene copolymer; it is cheaper, feels similar at first, but generally wears quicker. Blended latex usually mixes the two in a stated ratio, such as roughly 70 percent natural.

A fully natural mattress is the most durable and the most expensive. A blend is a sensible middle at roughly ₹20,000-40,000 in queen. Anything cheaper that claims "100 percent latex" without a certification or composition detail deserves suspicion. Try a quick [latex foam mattress](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=latex+foam+mattress+orthopedic&tag=adifystore-21) search to see how wildly the claims vary.

## Dunlop vs Talalay

Dunlop latex is poured and baked, so it comes out slightly denser at the bottom and gives a firmer, more supportive feel. It is the common choice in India and tends to be cheaper. Talalay is vacuum-processed and flash-frozen, giving a more even, softer and bouncier surface, and is usually priced higher.

For most people, a Dunlop core with a thin Talalay top layer is the sweet spot, but you will see this combination mostly in premium ranges above ₹40,000. If you simply want something firm that holds the lower back, a pure Dunlop mattress will do the job well.

## Firmness and your body

Latex firmness is measured differently from foam, so brand descriptions matter. In practice: a medium-firm 6-inch latex block suits most adults between 55 and 85 kg. Side sleepers who want a little more shoulder give should look for a softer top layer or a pin-core perforation pattern with zoned softness near the shoulders.

Heavier sleepers above about 90 kg should look for 8 inches or more with a firm base layer, because softer thin latex can bottom out. Latex does not "hug" the way memory foam does; if you love the sinking sensation, you may find it too responsive at first. Give it three to four weeks to settle in.

## Weight, handling and bed bases

A queen-size natural latex mattress can weigh 35 to 60 kg, noticeably more than foam. Plan for two people during delivery and for stairs. The bed base must be solid or slatted with gaps of not more than around 7 cm, otherwise the latex can sag into the gaps and shorten life.

Because latex is heavy and springy, it is a bad fit for a lightweight folding bed or a hostel-style cot frame. If you also need a topper for guests, a [latex mattress topper](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=latex+mattress+topper+queen&tag=adifystore-21) is a way to try latex before committing to a full mattress, though toppers do not replace a worn core.

## Climate, humidity and care

Latex is a breathable material, especially with perforations, so it works well in hot weather. In coastal cities with persistent humidity, the risk is not heat but moisture trapped between the mattress and a solid plywood base; air the bed monthly and use a breathable cover. Keep it out of direct sunlight, because UV breaks down rubber and makes it brittle; shade-air it instead.

Natural latex often has a mild rubbery smell for the first one or two weeks. That is normal and fades, but if you are sensitive, let the mattress air out in a ventilated room before you sleep on it. Latex allergy is real, so anyone who reacts to gloves or balloons should avoid it completely.

## Brand and warranty reality

Domestic brands such as Kurl-on, Duroflex and Sleepwell have latex ranges, and direct-to-consumer brands sell blended latex too; some imported brands cater to the luxury end. Because lineups change, check the product specification for latex content, thickness and cover material rather than relying on a name. Warranty lengths of 10 years are common on paper, but read what counts as a defect: most cover sagging beyond about 25 mm and not normal softening.

## Who should skip latex

Skip it if you have a latex allergy, if you want the deep slow-sink feel of memory foam, or if your budget for the whole mattress is under about ₹15,000, where quality latex is rare. Skip also if your bed base is a thin plywood box with no ventilation holes, as moisture control becomes difficult. Finally, skip if you move often, since the weight makes shifting house a real task.
