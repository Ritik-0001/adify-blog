## Noise Cancelling

Both offer top-tier active noise cancelling. Bose is widely regarded as slightly stronger, especially on low, steady sounds such as aircraft engines. Sony's cancelling is very close and handles a broad range of noise well.

In everyday use, the difference is small, and both make commutes and flights noticeably quieter.

## Comfort

Bose is known for plush cushions and a gentle clamp, making it comfortable for long wear. Sony is lighter and comfortable too, though some people find the headband less plush.

If you wear headphones many hours a day, the Bose comfort advantage can matter.

## Sound Quality

Sony offers a balanced sound with customisable equaliser options and wide codec support, including LDAC for compatible Android phones. Bose offers a smooth, warm sound with spatial audio features.

Both sound excellent for most listeners. Audio preferences are personal, so read reviews describing the tuning.

## Calls and Microphones

Both have strong microphones for calls and video meetings, using multiple mics and noise reduction. Both handle wind and background noise well.

## Battery and Charging

Both offer roughly 24 to 30 hours of battery with noise cancelling on, according to the brands, and quick charging that gives several hours from a short top-up.

## Design and Portability

Sony's XM5 has a headband that does not fold flat, so the case is larger. Bose's Ultra folds more compactly on some variants. Consider your bag size.

## Price and Value

Sony typically costs less than Bose, and older Sony models such as the XM4 offer strong value. Bose costs more, which reflects its comfort and cancelling reputation.

## How to Choose Between Sony and Bose

Start with comfort and budget. If you want the most comfortable, quietest option and can pay for it, choose Bose. If you want value, features and codec support, choose Sony.

## Real-World Scenarios

**A frequent flyer.** Bose's cancelling and comfort are strong, and Sony is close.

**An Android user who cares about codecs.** Sony's LDAC support is an advantage.

**A remote worker on calls.** Both are good, so choose on comfort.

**A budget-conscious buyer.** Sony XM5 or the older XM4 offers better value.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Buying from unauthorised sellers.** Counterfeits exist.

**Ignoring fit.** Try if possible.

**Expecting perfect silence.** ANC reduces noise, not eliminates it.

## Quick FAQ

**Which has better ANC?** Bose is slightly stronger, and Sony is very close.

**Which is more comfortable?** Bose for many users.

**Which is better value?** Sony.

## App and Features

Sony's Headphones Connect app offers a detailed equaliser, adaptive sound control and multipoint options. Bose's app is simpler, with a focus on modes such as quiet, aware and immersion. If you enjoy tweaking, Sony's app gives more control, and if you prefer set-and-forget, Bose is easier.

## Long-Term Ownership

Both brands offer replacement ear cushions, and batteries in wireless headphones typically last several years before capacity drops. Buy from an authorised seller so warranty claims are honoured, and look at what the warranty covers, since battery and cushion wear are often treated separately.

## Spatial Audio and Extra Features

Bose's Ultra models offer immersive audio modes that simulate a wider soundstage, which some listeners love for films and music and others switch off. Sony offers 360 Reality Audio and adaptive sound control that changes noise cancelling by location. Both feel gimmicky for some people, so treat them as extras rather than deciding factors.

## Using Them on Flights and Commutes

Both work well on aircraft and trains, and both have transparency modes that let you hear announcements without removing the headphones. For long flights, comfort, battery and a case that fits your bag matter more than small differences in noise cancelling.

## Recommendation

If you want the best balance of price, features and performance, choose the Sony WH-1000XM5. If comfort and top-tier cancelling are the priority, choose the Bose QuietComfort Ultra.
