## How to Choose for a 1BHK

Start with where you spend time. Cool the bedroom first, since sleeping comfort matters most, and add a hall AC later.

Check the floor and sun. A top-floor flat or a west-facing wall raises the load and may call for the next size up.

Keep running costs in mind. An inverter with a good star rating helps if you use the AC through the night.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Buying oversized.** It can cool unevenly.

**Ignoring installation charges.** Copper piping and mounting add cost.

**Skipping society rules.** Check outdoor unit placement.

**Not servicing.** Clean filters monthly.

## Real-World Scenarios

**A single professional in a 1BHK.** One 1 ton AC in the bedroom.

**A couple working from home.** A 1.5 ton in the hall and a 1 ton in the bedroom.

**A rented 1BHK.** A single AC with transferable warranty.

## What to Check on the Listing

Confirm the capacity, the star rating, the inverter type, the compressor warranty, the condenser coil and the installation terms. See whether basic installation is included.

## Placement and Installation Tips

Mount the indoor unit on a wall that allows air to flow across the room, keep the outdoor unit in a shaded, ventilated spot and use a drain that does not drip on neighbours. A neat installation reduces noise and protects the wall.

## Quick FAQ

**Is 1 ton enough for a 1BHK bedroom?** Usually, yes.

**Do I need two ACs?** Not necessarily; start with one.

**Is 5-star worth it?** If you run it many hours a day.

## Electricity Costs in a 1BHK

An inverter AC in a bedroom running through the night can be a major part of your monthly bill, so a good star rating and a sensible thermostat setting help. Use a fan with the AC, close curtains in the afternoon and keep filters clean to reduce running costs.

## Warranty and Service

Compare the compressor, PCB and unit warranty and check that the brand services your city. If you rent, ask whether the warranty is transferable, and keep the invoice and installation details in case you move.

## Final Checklist Before You Buy

Before you order, confirm the capacity against your room size and sun exposure, check the star rating and the warranty on the compressor and PCB, and ask what installation includes, such as piping length, a stand and drain work. Check society rules for the outdoor unit, confirm the model number on the listing and plan a service visit before the next summer.
