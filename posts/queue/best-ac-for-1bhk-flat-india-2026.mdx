## How to Choose for a 1BHK

Start with where you spend time. Cool the bedroom first, since sleeping comfort matters most, and add a hall AC later.

Check the floor and sun. A top-floor flat or a west-facing wall raises the load and may call for the next size up.

Keep running costs in mind. An inverter with a good star rating helps if you use the AC through the night.

## Placement and Installation Tips

Mount the indoor unit on a wall that allows air to flow across the room, keep the outdoor unit in a shaded, ventilated spot and use a drain that does not drip on neighbours. A neat installation reduces noise and protects the wall.

## Night-Time Running Cost

An inverter AC in a bedroom running through the night can be a major part of your monthly bill. Pair it with a ceiling fan, draw the curtains before the afternoon sun hits and wash the filters regularly so the compressor works less. These habits matter more in a small flat, where one AC does most of the work.

## Renting a 1BHK

If you rent, ask whether the landlord allows an outdoor unit and who pays for installation and removal. Keep the invoice and installation details, and check whether the warranty can be transferred or moved with you, because a tenant often changes flats before the cover ends.

## Who Should Skip This

If you are a single occupant who mostly sleeps at home on a low floor with a shaded bedroom, a 1.5 ton unit for the hall is likely unnecessary, and a ceiling fan with one 1 ton AC will do. Likewise, if your landlord will not permit an outdoor unit, a split AC is the wrong buy, and a window model is worth considering instead.

## Recommendation

For most 1BHK flats, a 1 ton inverter split for the bedroom is the best first AC, with a 1.5 ton unit for the hall later. Check outdoor unit rules in your building and the installation terms before you order.
