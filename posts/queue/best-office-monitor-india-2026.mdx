## Office Monitor Requirements — WFH India 2026

**Screen size:** 24 inches minimum for office work. Side-by-side application use requires the screen width.

**IPS panel:** For video calls — IPS panels reproduce your meeting participant's skin tones accurately. TN panels skew colors, making faces look unnatural.

**Multiple inputs:** Dual HDMI or HDMI + USB-C allows connecting work laptop and personal PC to the same monitor.

**Ergonomic stand:** Height-adjustable, tilt, and swivel allows proper monitor positioning to avoid neck strain over 8-hour workdays.

**Eye care:** 8+ hours of daily use requires flicker-free and blue light filter.
