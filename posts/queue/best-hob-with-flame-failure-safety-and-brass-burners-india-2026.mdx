## What a flame failure device does

A flame failure device, often called FFD or FSD, uses a thermocouple, a small probe sitting in the flame. While the flame heats it, a tiny current holds a gas valve open. If milk boils over, wind blows the flame out or the flame dies, the probe cools within seconds and the valve shuts, so raw gas stops flowing.

This matters in Indian homes where boiling milk, tea and rice water regularly douse flames. Without FFD, an unlit burner can leak gas silently into a closed kitchen. It does not replace a gas detector or a safe cylinder or PNG connection, but it reduces a real risk.

## How to check FFD on a listing

- Look for the words "flame failure" for every burner, not just "safety".
- Check product photos for a small metal probe next to each burner cap.
- Read the manual or Q and A section for the cut-off time, usually a few seconds.
- Ask whether the thermocouple is replaceable, since it wears out in a few years.

Cheaper hobs may add FFD only on the large burner. Be sure you know which ones are covered.

## Brass vs aluminium burners

Burners are the part that takes the most heat and wear.

### 1. Brass burners

Brass resists corrosion from acidic spills and handles repeated heating well. Good brass burner caps keep a steady blue flame and are easy to clean with a brush. They add to the price but generally last longer. See [brass burner gas hob](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=brass+burner+gas+hob&tag=adifystore-21).

### 2. Aluminium burners

Cheaper and lighter, they may pit, warp and develop uneven flames sooner, especially with daily heavy frying. Not unsafe, but they often need replacing sooner.

### 3. Stainless steel caps

Some hobs use steel for caps with brass for the body. Check that the burner ports (the small holes) are cleanly drilled, since rough holes cause yellow flames and soot.

## Triple-ring and thermal efficiency

A triple-ring burner has three concentric flame rings, spreading heat across a wide pan and often giving higher thermal efficiency. For Indian cooking, such as large kadhai frying, a triple-ring big burner is valuable. ## Other safety details worth checking

- Toughened glass of at least 6 mm if you choose a glass top
- Sturdy pan supports that do not wobble
- Auto-ignition that sparks reliably without needing a lighter
- An easy gas shut-off valve placed where you can reach it
- A drip tray or sealed burner design that prevents boil-over from reaching the gas line

Pair the hob with a proper chimney to remove heat and fumes, and consider a [gas leak alarm for kitchen](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=gas+leak+alarm+for+kitchen&tag=adifystore-21).

## Maintenance that keeps safety working

Clean burner ports with a needle or brush every few weeks so flames stay even. Wipe the thermocouple tip with a dry cloth, because grease and carbon coating it can delay the cut-off or cause the flame to cut out when you release the knob. If a burner needs you to hold the knob longer than usual, the probe may need replacing.

Replace the gas hose roughly every two to five years, and use a clip rated for your fuel type.

## Who should skip premium safety models

If you are buying for a rented flat and plan to move soon, a good mid-range hob with FFD on all burners is enough; there is no need to pay for designer finishes. But do not skip FFD to save a couple of thousand rupees, especially if elderly people or children use the kitchen.
