## What Humid Climates Specifically Need

**Corrosion-resistant outdoor units:** Salt air (coastal cities) and general humidity accelerate outdoor unit coil corrosion — anti-corrosive coatings (Voltas Blue Fin, similar treatments from other brands) genuinely extend outdoor unit lifespan in these conditions.

**Effective dehumidification mode:** Look for ACs with a dedicated "Dry Mode" that removes moisture without over-cooling the room — genuinely more comfortable in humid conditions than temperature reduction alone.

**Mold-resistant indoor components:** Self-cleaning technologies (Daikin's Dew Clean, Hitachi's Frost Wash) matter more in humid regions where indoor unit mold growth happens faster.
