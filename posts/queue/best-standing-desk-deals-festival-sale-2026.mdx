## What to Check Before Buying

**Single motor vs dual motor:** Dual motor desks are noticeably more stable at full height and under load — worth the price difference if you'll use standing mode regularly.

**Weight capacity and desktop size:** Confirm the desk supports your monitor setup, not just a laptop — dual-monitor arms add meaningful load at the front edge.

**Memory height presets:** A genuinely useful feature if multiple people share the desk, or if you switch between sitting and standing several times a day.
