## How to Choose Under ₹1 Lakh

Be realistic about features. Under ₹1 lakh you will usually trade a long SL-track and body scanning for a basic roller, heat and airbags, so choose based on comfort.

Check the size and weight limits. Make sure the chair fits your height and the room, including when reclined.

Look at warranty and service. A good warranty and local service are valuable, since massage chairs have moving parts.

## Getting the Most From a Value Chair

Use short sessions, start with a gentle intensity and use heat for tight muscles. Keep the chair clean and cover it if it sits in a sunny room. If you find a feature uncomfortable, adjust the program rather than forcing it.

## What You Give Up Under ₹1 Lakh

Below this price you should expect a shorter roller track, fewer programs, simpler airbags and plainer upholstery. You can still get heat and a decent full-body massage. If you want body scanning and long tracks, you may need to stretch the budget, so decide whether those features matter before you buy.

## Aftercare

Keep the invoice and warranty details, wipe the upholstery gently and avoid dragging the chair across tiles. A short check of the moving parts before the warranty ends is wise, because motor and roller faults tend to show up with regular use.

## Who Should Skip This

If you are 6 feet or taller, or heavier than the chair's stated weight limit, an entry model with a short track may never line up with your shoulders and lower back. In that case a stretch to a longer-track chair, or a good ergonomic recliner, will serve you better than a cheap full-body chair you stop using.

## Recommendation

For most buyers under ₹1 lakh, an entry full-body chair with heat and airbags from Robotouch or JSB is a sensible choice. Compare the warranty terms and height range line by line before ordering.
