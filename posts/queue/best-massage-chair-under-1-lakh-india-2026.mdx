## How to Choose Under ₹1 Lakh

Be realistic about features. Under ₹1 lakh you will usually trade a long SL-track and body scanning for a basic roller, heat and airbags, so choose based on comfort.

Check the size and weight limits. Make sure the chair fits your height and the room, including when reclined.

Look at warranty and service. A good warranty and local service are valuable, since massage chairs have moving parts.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Expecting premium features.** Compare against your budget.

**Skipping a trial.** Try one in person if you can.

**Ignoring warranty terms.** Read what is covered.

**Overlooking delivery.** Large boxes need access and help.

## Real-World Scenarios

**A first-time buyer.** An entry full-body chair with heat.

**A home with limited space.** A space-saving recliner.

**A gift for parents.** A simple chair with easy controls.

## What to Check on the Listing

Confirm the track type, the airbag count, the heat, the user height range, the dimensions and the warranty. See whether delivery and installation are included.

## Getting the Most From a Value Chair

Use short sessions, start with a gentle intensity and use heat for tight muscles. Keep the chair clean and cover it if it sits in a sunny room. If you find a feature uncomfortable, adjust the program rather than forcing it.

## Quick FAQ

**Is a ₹1 lakh chair good?** It can be a good entry point.

**Is it better than a massager?** It covers more of the body.

**Is it safe for elders?** Often yes, but check with a doctor.

## What You Give Up Under ₹1 Lakh

Below this price you should expect a shorter roller track, fewer programs, simpler airbags and plainer upholstery. You can still get heat and a decent full-body massage. If you want body scanning and long tracks, you may need to stretch the budget, so decide whether those features matter before you buy.

## Delivery and Aftercare

Check lift access and the number of delivery staff, and ask whether assembly is included. Keep the invoice and warranty details, wipe the upholstery gently and avoid pulling the chair across tiles. A short check of moving parts before the warranty ends is wise.

## Final Checklist Before You Buy

Before you order, measure the room including the space the chair needs to recline, check lift and door access for a heavy carton and confirm whether assembly is included. Compare what the warranty covers on the frame, the rollers and the electronics, ask how service is arranged in your city and, if possible, try a similar chair in a store so that you know it fits your height and shape.
