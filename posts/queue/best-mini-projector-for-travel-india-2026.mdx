## Travel Projector Requirements

**Portability:** Under 500g and smaller than a 1L water bottle for jacket-pocket portability. Under 1kg for bag portability (can share bag space with laptop and accessories).

**Battery:** Minimum 2 hours for a full movie. 3+ hours for back-to-back use without finding a power outlet.

**Android TV / Smart OS:** Travel means unknown screen environments. Android TV built-in means you can stream Netflix, YouTube, Hotstar without connecting to an unknown smart TV or finding a specific app.

**Brightness:** 200–400 ANSI lumens is realistic for travel projectors. Dark hotel room (curtains closed, lights off): 200 lumens adequate for 80–100 inch projection. Semi-dark room: 300+ lumens needed.
