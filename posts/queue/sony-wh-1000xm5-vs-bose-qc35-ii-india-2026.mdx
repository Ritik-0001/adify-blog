## The Key Differences

### ANC Performance

**Sony WH-1000XM5:** 8 microphones (4 on each earcup), QN1 HD + QN2 HD dual-chip processing. Adaptive ANC adjusts in real time to ambient noise profiles. In airplane cabin: engine noise reduced to near-inaudible. In metro: wheel noise and announcements reduced significantly.

**Bose QC35 II:** Dual microphones, Bose TriPort ANC. ANC is consistent and effective — Bose's ANC philosophy is smooth, non-fatiguing noise cancellation. Not as deep as XM5 in absolute terms but many users prefer its character (less ear pressure sensation).

**Winner:** Sony XM5 for absolute ANC depth. Bose QC35 II for users who find deep ANC creates ear pressure discomfort.

### Sound Quality

**Sony XM5:** LDAC codec support (up to 990kbps wireless audio — 3× Bluetooth standard quality). DSEE Extreme upscaling. Clear, detailed, slightly warm signature. Better instrument separation and soundstage.

**Bose QC35 II:** SBC and AAC only (no LDAC/aptX HD). Sound signature: smooth, balanced, slightly recessed bass. Good for vocal music and podcasts; less impressive for complex, layered music.

**Winner:** Sony XM5, significantly — LDAC codec and better drivers deliver noticeably better audio quality.

### Call Quality

**Sony XM5:** 4 beamforming microphones for calls, Precise Voice Pickup technology. Call quality is excellent.

**Bose QC35 II:** 2 microphones specifically tuned for call clarity. Bose's call quality reputation is well-earned — voice reproduction is clear and natural even in noisy environments.

**Winner:** Close. Sony XM5 is objectively better by measurement; many users subjectively prefer Bose's call sound character.

### Comfort

**Sony XM5:** 250g. Softer, larger ear cushions. 180-degree flat fold (not as compact as QC35 II). No swivel on ear cups.

**Bose QC35 II:** 235g. Lighter. Swivel ear cups fold into a compact case. Many users find QC35 II's round ear cups more comfortable for glasses wearers.

**Winner:** Draw — preference-dependent. Bose is lighter; Sony has softer cushioning.

### Battery

**Sony XM5:** 30 hours ANC on. 3 minutes charge = 3 hours playback.
**Bose QC35 II:** 20 hours ANC on. 15 minutes charge = 2.5 hours.

**Winner:** Sony XM5 — 10 more hours is significant for travel.

### Connectivity

**Sony XM5:** Bluetooth 5.2, multipoint (2 devices), LDAC, AAC, SBC.
**Bose QC35 II:** Bluetooth 4.1, multipoint (2 devices), NFC, SBC, AAC. Also has 2.5mm wired cable.

**Winner:** Sony XM5 — newer Bluetooth standard and LDAC codec.
