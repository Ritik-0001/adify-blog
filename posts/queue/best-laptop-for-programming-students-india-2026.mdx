## What CS Engineering Students Actually Use

**Programming languages used in Indian BE/BTech CS:**
- Year 1: C, C++ (lab practicals)
- Year 2: Java (OOP, data structures), Python (data science basics)
- Year 3–4: Web development (HTML/CSS/JS, React), databases (MySQL), Android (Kotlin or Java)

**Tools commonly required:**
- VS Code or Eclipse (Java)
- XAMPP or Node.js (web development)
- MySQL or PostgreSQL
- Android Studio (Kotlin projects require 16GB RAM to run emulators)
- Jupyter Notebook (Python data science)

Android Studio with running emulators is the most RAM-intensive common student use case — it requires approximately 8–10GB RAM just for the emulator, leaving little for the OS and other apps on an 8GB machine.

## College Hostel Considerations

**Portability:** 15.6" laptops weigh 1.8–2.1kg. Carrying to classes, hostels, and labs daily is manageable. 17" laptops are too heavy for this use pattern.

**Durability:** Plastic chassis laptops (VivoBook, IdeaPad) are adequate for careful students. Budget a ₹800–₁,500 laptop sleeve/bag.

**Charger:** All three laptops use standard barrel connectors or USB-C charging. USB-C charging allows using a phone power bank as emergency laptop power — useful for fieldwork/exams.

**Recommendation:** ASUS VivoBook 15 Ryzen 5 7530U 16GB (₹35,000–₃₈,000) for most Indian CS/IT engineering students. HP Laptop 15s for students in cities without ASUS/Lenovo service centres. Avoid 8GB RAM laptops regardless of brand — the 8GB limitation becomes apparent within the first semester of CS coursework.
