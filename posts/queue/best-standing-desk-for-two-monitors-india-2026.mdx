## How to Choose a Standing Desk for Two Monitors

Pick the width and depth. A 150 to 160 cm width suits two 24 to 27 inch monitors, and 70 to 80 cm depth helps with viewing distance.

Check the frame and load. A dual-motor frame with a higher load rating stays steadier with two monitors and an arm, and look for anti-collision and memory presets.

Plan your ergonomics. Place monitors at or just below eye level, about an arm's length away, and alternate sitting and standing through the day.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Choosing a narrow top.** Two monitors need width.

**Ignoring load limits.** Add up all weights.

**Skipping cable planning.** Allow slack for height changes.

**Using a thin top with a monitor arm.** Check clamp limits.

## Real-World Scenarios

**A developer with two 27 inch monitors.** A 160 cm top on a dual-motor frame.

**A designer with a laptop and an external monitor.** A 140 to 150 cm top.

**A shared home office.** A wide top with memory presets.

## What to Check on the Listing

Confirm the frame model, the load capacity, the lift range, the motor count, the tabletop size and material, the warranty and what is in the box. See whether assembly is included.

## Setting Up Your Desk

Set the standing height so that your elbows are at about ninety degrees, place both monitors in an arc and keep the keyboard and mouse close. Use an anti-fatigue mat if you stand for long periods.

## Warranty and Service

Compare warranty on the frame, motors and controls, and check service availability. Keep the invoice and serial number.
