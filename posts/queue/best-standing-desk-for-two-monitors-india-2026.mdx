## How to Choose a Standing Desk for Two Monitors

Pick the width and depth. A 150 to 160 cm width suits two 24 to 27 inch monitors, and 70 to 80 cm depth helps with viewing distance.

Check the frame and load. A dual-motor frame with a higher load rating stays steadier with two monitors and an arm, and look for anti-collision and memory presets.

Plan your ergonomics. Place monitors at or just below eye level, about an arm's length away, and alternate sitting and standing through the day.

## Setting Up Your Desk

Set the standing height so that your elbows are at about ninety degrees, place both monitors in an arc and keep the keyboard and mouse close. Use an anti-fatigue mat if you stand for long periods.

## Who Should Skip This

If your room is narrow or your wall has a window sill or socket exactly where a 150 to 160 cm desk would sit, a wide dual-monitor desk may not fit at all, so measure first. Also skip the premium frame if you only use one laptop screen and sit most of the day. A smaller height-adjustable desk or a simple riser costs far less for that pattern of work.
