## What ₹60,000 Should Deliver

**Continuous Duty (CHP) motor rating:** Look for continuous horsepower rating (not just peak HP) of at least 2.5-3 CHP for regular running use — peak HP numbers alone can be misleading marketing.

**Belt size:** 20-inch width minimum for comfortable running stride; 22+ inches preferred for taller users (6ft+).

**Max user weight and incline:** Verify max weight capacity comfortably exceeds heaviest household user, and auto-incline (versus manual) adds workout variety without stopping the treadmill.
