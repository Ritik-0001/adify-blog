## Measure before you shop

A comfortable seated workstation needs a desk about 50-60 cm deep, 100 cm wide at minimum, and around 90 cm behind the chair to roll back and stand. In a 7 x 8 foot room, that means placing the desk against the shorter wall so you face the wall, or putting it beside a window in an alcove. Sketch the floor, mark the door swing and keep a clear path of at least 60 cm.

Sunlight matters too: do not put the screen facing a window, and keep it perpendicular to the light, or add curtains to cut glare.

## Desk choice: depth and shape

Pick a narrow rectangular desk, not a corner unit, unless the room is truly L-shaped. A depth of 55 cm suits a laptop, a 24 inch monitor on an arm and a keyboard. Wall-mounted fold-down desks free floor space but limit monitor height and are less stable; they suit part-time use. A floating shelf desk is another option, though it needs strong wall anchors.

Choose a light-coloured top, which makes a small room feel larger, and rounded edges, which are safer in tight spaces. A [folding desk wall mounted](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=wall+mounted+folding+desk+study&tag=adifystore-21) works if the room doubles as a guest room.

## The chair that tucks under

In small rooms, armrests that flip up or lower to desk height allow the chair to slide fully beneath the desk and save 30 cm. Look for a compact chair with a narrow seat, adjustable lumbar and a mesh back, and avoid very large executive chairs. A [compact ergonomic chair with flip-up armrests](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=ergonomic+chair+flip+up+armrest+compact&tag=adifystore-21) can be the difference between a usable and a cramped room.

Spend here first: you will sit in it for hours, and it is the one item you cannot make up for with accessories.

## Vertical storage

Use the wall, not the floor. Wall shelves above the desk, a pegboard for cables and supplies, and a slim bookcase under 30 cm deep keep clutter off the work surface. A [wall mounted shelf](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=floating+wall+shelves+set+of+3&tag=adifystore-21) set can hold books and a printer; check the weight rating and use proper anchors for your wall type.

A monitor arm clears the desk surface by lifting the screen off it, giving room for a notebook. See [monitor arm single](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=monitor+arm+single+desk+clamp&tag=adifystore-21) for clamp-on options that need a desk edge of at least 1.5 cm thickness.

## Monitor and laptop height

In a small room, the common error is working on a laptop on a low desk. Put the laptop on a stand or run an external monitor with the top edge at eye level, around an arm's length away. Use an external keyboard and mouse. This setup lets you sit properly and also reduces eye strain in a room with limited light.

## Lighting in a small room

Overhead lights in compact rooms can feel harsh and cause glare. Combine a soft ceiling light with a task lamp aimed from the side and a strip of bias lighting behind the monitor. Use warm light, around 3000-4000K, for evening, and cooler light, around 5000K, in the daytime. A [LED desk lamp dimmable](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=led+desk+lamp+dimmable+touch+eye+care&tag=adifystore-21) with a clamp saves desk space.

## Cables, power and Wi-Fi

Put a power strip on the wall or under the desk and tie cables along the desk leg. Check that your router signal reaches the room, since a spare room at the far end of the flat may need a mesh point. Also add a surge protector, especially if you use a desktop.

## Sound and comfort

Small rooms echo. A rug, curtains and a bookshelf all dampen sound. If the room gets hot, a small fan or an AC with a timer matters more than any desk accessory; a hot room ruins focus quickly.

## Who should skip a dedicated room

If the spare room is under about 6 x 7 feet, a wall-mounted fold-down desk or a corner of the living room may be a better answer, since a full desk plus chair leaves no space to move.

## Recommendation

Compact 110 cm desk, slim mesh chair with flip-up armrests, monitor on an arm, wall shelves, and layered lighting. Buy the chair and monitor arm first, measure everything twice, and keep the floor clear.
