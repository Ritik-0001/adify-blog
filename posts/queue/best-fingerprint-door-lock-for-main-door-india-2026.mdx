## How to Choose a Fingerprint Lock for a Main Door

Check door and frame. Measure the door thickness, check whether the door is wood or metal and choose a lock body that fits.

Look at security features. A solid metal build, a tamper alarm and a mechanical key backup improve security and resilience.

Think about the environment. Dust, humidity and sun can wear sensors, so choose a lock with a good reputation and warranty.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Choosing the wrong lock body.** Check the door type.

**Ignoring weather exposure.** Choose sealed locks.

**Using dirty fingers.** Clean sensors can read better.

**Skipping professional fitting.** Poor fitting weakens security.

## Real-World Scenarios

**An apartment main door.** A mortise fingerprint lock.

**A villa gate door exposed to rain.** A weather-protected lock.

**A metal door.** A rim lock designed for metal doors.

## What to Check on the Listing

Confirm the lock type, the door thickness, the unlocking methods, the build material, the weather protection, the warranty and the installation terms. See whether the lock has a mechanical key.

## Daily Use and Care

Register several fingers per user, keep the sensor clean and dry and replace batteries when the alert appears. Test the key backup from time to time.

## Quick FAQ

**Do fingerprint locks work with dirty or wet fingers?** Accuracy can drop, so keep a PIN or key.

**Are they safe?** Reputable brands are secure.

**Can I install them myself?** Professional fitting is advised.

## Security Habits That Matter

A strong lock is only part of home security. Use a deadbolt where possible, keep the key backup in a safe place, remove old fingerprints when people leave and avoid using easily guessed PINs. Add a video door phone or camera if you want to see who is at the door.

## Installation and Warranty

Ask the seller whether professional installation is included and what the warranty covers, especially for electronics exposed to weather. Keep the invoice and installation details, and book a yearly check of the lock and latch.

## Choosing Between Fingerprint, PIN and Card

Fingerprint is fastest for daily use, but a PIN is useful for visitors and for times when hands are wet or dusty. Cards or tags suit domestic help. A good lock offers all of these so that you are never locked out because one method fails, and the mechanical key remains the last resort.
