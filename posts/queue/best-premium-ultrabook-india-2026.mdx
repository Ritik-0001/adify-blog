## What Defines "Premium" in an Ultrabook

**Chassis material:** CNC-machined aluminum or magnesium alloy, not plastic — affects both feel and long-term durability.

**Display bezels:** Thin bezels maximize screen-to-body ratio, a key visual and practical premium indicator.

**Keyboard and trackpad quality:** Precise key travel, minimal flex, and glass trackpads distinguish premium ultrabooks from budget alternatives with similar specs.

**Weight-to-performance ratio:** Premium ultrabooks deliver strong performance despite thin, light chassis designs — a genuine engineering achievement, not just marketing.
