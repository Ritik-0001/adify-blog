## Brand Comparison Overview

### Atomberg — Best BLDC Brand
India's pioneer in consumer BLDC ceiling fans. Founded 2012. Patented BLDC motor technology. Product range: Renesa, Ikano, Efficio, and smart IoT models.

**Strengths:** Industry-leading energy efficiency (28W). Remote control standard across models. India-focused BLDC engineering. Strong brand recognition in urban markets.

**Weaknesses:** Smaller physical service network than Crompton or Havells in Tier 3 cities. Limited design variety — functional aesthetic, not decorative.

**Best for:** Buyers who prioritise energy savings and remote control.

### Crompton Greaves — Best All-Around Brand
India's largest ceiling fan manufacturer by production volume. Over 100 years of electrical manufacturing. Service centres in most Indian cities and towns.

**Strengths:** Most extensive service network in India. Wide price range (₹800–₅,000+). Both standard and BLDC models (Energion HS). Reliable motor quality across price points.

**Weaknesses:** BLDC models (Energion series) entered the market later than Atomberg — less accumulated reliability data.

**Best for:** Buyers who want wide choice and easy service access, especially in smaller cities.

### Havells — Best Premium Quality Brand
Premium Indian electrical brand with LED lighting, switchgear, and fans. Products priced 10–20% above Crompton equivalents.

**Strengths:** Best build quality and materials at equivalent prices. Widest design range — from functional to decorative and designer fans. Havells Leganza (₹1,800–₂,200) is the most value-efficient premium standard fan.

**Weaknesses:** Higher price than Crompton or Orient for equivalent specifications.

**Best for:** Buyers who want premium build quality and won't compromise on design aesthetics.

### Orient Electric — Best Value Brand
Strong position in South and East India. Competitive pricing versus Crompton. Aeroslim BLDC range (₹2,800–₃,500) is the most affordable BLDC from a major established brand.

**Strengths:** Aeroslim BLDC is priced below Atomberg/Crompton BLDC. Wide range including 1400mm large-room fans. Good value across standard and BLDC.

**Best for:** Value-conscious buyers who want BLDC at the lowest major-brand price.

### Usha International — Trusted Budget-Mid Brand
Long-established Indian brand. Good quality at mid-range prices. Strongest in North and West India.

**Best for:** Buyers in regions with good Usha service coverage who want mid-range quality.
