## Choosing the Right Tablet for Your Use Case

**Choose iPad** if you want the smoothest long-term software updates and plan to use accessories like Apple Pencil or a keyboard case for note-taking or light creative work.

**Choose Galaxy Tab S9 FE** if you're already in the Samsung/Android ecosystem and want S Pen note-taking without buying into Apple's accessory pricing.
