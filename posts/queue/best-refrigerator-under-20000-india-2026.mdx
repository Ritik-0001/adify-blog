## How to Choose a Refrigerator Under ₹20,000

**Capacity:** 150L serves 1–2 people. 190–200L serves 2–3 people. 215–235L serves 3–4 people. Single-door refrigerators above 235L are rare under ₹20,000.

**Energy Star Rating:** Indian BEE star ratings from 1 to 5 stars. More stars = less electricity consumed. A 1-star 190L fridge consumes approximately 250–280 units/year. A 5-star equivalent consumes 100–130 units/year — saving ₹1,500–₂,000 annually at ₹8–₁₀/unit Indian electricity rates.

**Inverter vs. Non-Inverter Compressor:** Inverter compressors vary speed based on cooling load (more efficient). Non-inverter compressors run at fixed speed (start-stop operation). Inverter compressors cost ₹2,000–₃,000 more but last longer and run quieter.

**Direct Cool vs. Frost Free:** Direct cool (single door): efficient, requires manual defrosting. Frost free (double door): automatic defrosting, no icing, costs 30–40% more to run.
