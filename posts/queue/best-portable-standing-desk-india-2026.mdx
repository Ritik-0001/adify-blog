## Types of Portable Standing Desks

**Desk converters (X-lift style):** Sit on top of your existing desk. Scissor-lift mechanism raises the entire surface. Some include separate keyboard tray. Most stable portable option but bulky.

**Z-stand / simple risers:** Fixed-height wooden or bamboo platforms. Economical. Set them up, remove them. No adjustment while working.

**Monitor arms + keyboard tray combination:** Keep monitor at standing height, separate keyboard tray at a different height. Most ergonomically adjustable but most complex.

**Laptop stands:** Elevate just the laptop screen. For single-laptop setups, pair with external keyboard and mouse.
