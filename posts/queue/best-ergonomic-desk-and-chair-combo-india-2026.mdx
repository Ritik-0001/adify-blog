## Why Both Matter Together

**Position variation:** Alternating sitting and standing throughout the day reduces the health risks associated with prolonged static sitting, regardless of chair quality.

**Proper seated support:** When you are seated, dynamic lumbar support prevents the slouching and back strain that undermines even a great standing desk's benefits during seated hours.
