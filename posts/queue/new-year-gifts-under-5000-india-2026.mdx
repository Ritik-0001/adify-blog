## A gift that fits the date

New Year is about resolutions: getting fit, organising life, learning something or simply enjoying more of it. A good gift supports one of those aims without lecturing. For friends, add a little fun; for family, favour comfort and routine.

### 1. Fitness band or smart ring alternative

A fitness band tracks steps, sleep and heart rate, with a week or more of battery, roughly ₹2,000 to ₹4,500. Cheaper bands have rough accuracy, but for motivation they work. Check compatibility with their phone app. Browse [Xiaomi Smart Band](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=Xiaomi+Smart+Band&tag=adifystore-21).

### 2. Yoga mat and resistance bands

A quality yoga mat with a strap, roughly ₹800 to ₹2,500, and a set of resistance bands, roughly ₹500 to ₹1,500, suits home workouts. Choose 6 mm thickness for joint comfort. See [yoga mat with bag](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=yoga+mat+6mm+with+bag&tag=adifystore-21).

A 1 litre steel bottle with time markings, roughly ₹500 to ₹2,000, encourages hydration. Check that the cap is leakproof. Compare [time marker water bottle](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=time+marker+water+bottle+steel&tag=adifystore-21).

## Making a budget gift feel special

Wrap with care and include a handwritten note with a wish for the year. Add a small treat, such as chocolates or tea. Personal notes beat price. A [gift hamper basket](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=gift+hamper+basket+empty+with+filler&tag=adifystore-21) lets you assemble a custom hamper for under ₹3,000.

## Matching gifts to people

For fitness-minded friends, a band or yoga set. For organised friends, a planner. For parents, a throw or an insulated flask. For siblings and cousins, a speaker or board game. When uncertain, go for consumables and comfort.

## Gifts to avoid

Avoid cheap unbranded electronics, strongly scented items for people you do not know well, and gifts that need a particular size. Avoid gifts that imply a criticism, such as weight loss items, unless asked.

## Who should skip a gadget

Skip gadgets for people who dislike technology, and give a hamper instead. Skip any gift needing setup that the receiver cannot manage.
