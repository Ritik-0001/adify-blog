## What Changed in 2026

RTX 40-series laptops have matured. The thermal management issues that plagued first-gen RTX 40 laptops (severe throttling when GPU and CPU are both under load simultaneously) are largely fixed in current models with updated BIOS and better vapour chamber designs.

The big win: DLSS 3.5 (Frame Generation) in RTX 4060/4070 laptops makes demanding games like Cyberpunk 2077 and Alan Wake 2 genuinely playable at high settings on a budget GPU. A ₹90,000 laptop with an RTX 4060 + DLSS 3.5 plays like a desktop RTX 4070 in supported titles.

## Should You Get RTX 4060 or RTX 4070?

In laptops, the RTX 4060 and RTX 4070 are both 8GB GDDR6. The 4070 has more CUDA cores and a wider memory bus — in practice, about 20–30% more gaming performance.

At ₹1 lakh, you're buying RTX 4060. RTX 4070 laptop versions start at ₹1.1–₹1.2 lakh. If your budget can stretch: the Lenovo Legion Pro 5 Gen 9 with RTX 4070 at ₹1.1 lakh is a meaningful upgrade for 1440p gaming.

If ₹1 lakh is firm: the RTX 4060 + DLSS 3.5 at 1080p is excellent. You're not leaving performance on the table at 1080p resolution.

## Thermal Reality Check for India

Gaming laptops run hot. In Indian summers (35–45°C ambient), a laptop that maintains 85°C CPU temp in a 20°C room will hit 95°C+ in an Indian summer. At 100°C, the CPU throttles — performance drops significantly.

**What to do:** Game in an air-conditioned room if possible. Use a laptop cooling pad (adds 5–8°C improvement). In the ASUS Armoury Crate or Lenovo Vantage software, set fan profile to Turbo when gaming — the fans are loud but temps stay manageable.

Most Indian gaming cafés replace thermal paste annually. For home use, replacing paste every 2–3 years with Thermal Grizzly Kryonaut (₹1,500) keeps your laptop performing like new.
