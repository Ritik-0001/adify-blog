## What ₹15,000–₹20,000 ANC Buys

At this price, you get:
- **Sony QN1 HD or equivalent dual-chip ANC** — genuinely silences consistent noise sources (airplane cabin, AC hum, traffic)
- **Adaptive ANC** with environment detection — auto-adjusts cancellation depth
- **Transparency mode** — clear, natural ambient sound passthrough
- **Multipoint pairing** — stay connected to phone and laptop simultaneously
- **Speak-to-chat** (Sony XM4): Auto-pauses music when you speak, resumes when you stop

These features separate ₹15,000+ ANC headphones from budget alternatives.
