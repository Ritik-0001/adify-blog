## Setting Up a Desktop Security Camera in India

**Placement tips:**
- Corner of room for widest coverage angle
- At height of 1.5–2 metres for best face-level capture
- Away from windows (backlight causes underexposed footage)

**Power:** USB-C or micro-USB power adapter included. Requires a power outlet nearby — no battery option for desktop cameras.

**Privacy considerations:** Under Indian law (IT Act 2000 and subsequent amendments), recording in private spaces without consent can be illegal. Desktop cameras in your own home for security purposes are fine — inform household members the camera is active.

**Storage:** Use a Class 10 microSD card (₹400–₈₀₀ for 64–128GB) for local recording without cloud subscription fees.

**Recommendation:** TP-Link Tapo C210 for the best balance of features, reliability, and price. Mi 360 for the most budget-conscious choice. Tapo C225 for premium colour night vision.
