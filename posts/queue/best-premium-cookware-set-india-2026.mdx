## What premium cookware actually means

At the premium tier, the difference is not paint or packaging. It is metal thickness, how the layers are bonded, handle riveting, and whether the base stays flat after hundreds of heat cycles. A premium kadhai survives temperature shocks and does not warp into a wobble on an induction hob.

Look for weights and thickness in the listing, such as a base of around 2.5 to 3 mm, rather than adjectives. If a brand does not say, assume it is thin.

## 1. Meyer, the safe all-rounder

Meyer's stainless and hard-anodised sets are widely stocked in India and priced roughly ₹10,000-20,000 for bundles. Their sets typically include a mix of pots, pans and lids, and the brand has a reasonable service reputation. The trade-off is that you pay for the name, and stainless variants will need patience to cook eggs and delicate fish without sticking. ## 3. Hawkins tri-ply and hard-anodised pieces

Hawkins is known for pressure cookers, and its tri-ply and hard-anodised pots are built to the same sturdy standard. Individual pieces cost more per item than sets from other brands, and the range is small, so you may end up buying a few pieces separately. ## 4. Cast iron and enamelled cast iron

Brands such as Lodge are imported, while Le Creuset sits at the extreme premium end, with single pieces costing more than a full tri-ply set. Cast iron holds heat for dosas, searing and slow cooking, and it lasts generations. It is heavy, needs seasoning, and takes time to heat up and cool down. If you cannot lift a full 28 cm skillet comfortably with one hand, choose a smaller size. ## 5. Ceramic and non-stick premium pans

Tefal, Greenpan and Prestige offer ceramic or premium non-stick pans. These are good for low-oil cooking and eggs. They are not for high-heat tadka or metal spatulas, and the coating degrades over two to four years even with careful use. ## Induction and gas compatibility

Test any pan with a magnet before buying: if it sticks to the base, it works on induction. Stainless tri-ply with a magnetic outer layer works on induction and on gas. Pure aluminium and copper without a steel base do not. For gas, a thick base prevents hot spots, but a thin-based pan on a high flame will scorch your dal at the edges.

## Care, in practice

Stainless does not need seasoning but stains easily when overheated. Cook on medium heat, add food once the pan is hot, and soak burnt residue instead of scrubbing with steel wool. Hard water leaves white scale, which a spoon of vinegar removes.

Cast iron should be wiped dry after washing and rubbed with a thin film of oil. Non-stick must never go on a high flame empty, and should be washed by hand.

## Who should skip a premium set

If you cook only simple one-pot meals, a pressure cooker, a kadhai and a tawa may cover everything for less. People who share a kitchen with others who scrub with steel wool will damage premium cookware quickly. If your kitchen is small, a 10-piece set will cost more space than it is worth. Buy individual pieces you will use.
