## What "Copilot+ PC" actually guarantees

Microsoft set a hardware floor: an NPU delivering at least 40 TOPS, 16 GB of RAM and 256 GB of storage. Meeting it unlocks features such as Recall, Live Captions with translation, Cocreator in Paint, and on-device Windows Studio Effects for video calls. That is it. The badge does not promise better gaming, faster Excel or smoother video editing, since those depend on the CPU and GPU, not the NPU.

The honest picture: most of the NPU features are conveniences. Live captions, background blur on calls and image generation in Paint are pleasant, yet few buyers will notice them after the first week. The real win of the chips behind these laptops is efficiency.

## Three chip families, three different trade-offs

Snapdragon X Elite and X Plus use an ARM design that gives class-leading battery life and silent running, but some x86 apps run through emulation and a few drivers, anti-cheat tools and older software do not work. Intel's Core Ultra 200V (Lunar Lake) laptops give full x86 compatibility with strong battery life and are the safest pick for people who rely on legacy software. AMD's Ryzen AI 300 laptops offer the best integrated graphics and multi-core power of the three, with battery life a notch behind.

## ARM compatibility: the question that decides everything

Windows on ARM now runs mainstream software well: Chrome, Edge, Office, Zoom, Teams, Photoshop and VS Code all have native builds. The risks are in niche territory. Tally, depending on your version, and some banking or government client software may rely on old drivers or Java components. Printers and scanners from smaller brands sometimes lack ARM drivers. Games with kernel-level anti-cheat often refuse to run. If you work in finance, CAD, or a company with locked-down legacy tools, ask IT before buying or choose an Intel or AMD model instead.

## Battery life: marketing versus Indian reality

Manufacturers quote 20 hours or more of video playback. Real office use with Chrome tabs, a Teams call and brightness at 60 percent is more like 10 to 14 hours on the efficient ARM chips and 8 to 12 on Intel Lunar Lake. That is still excellent. ## Who should skip a Copilot+ PC

Skip it if you game, because integrated graphics and ARM compatibility make it a poor choice. Skip it if your work depends on old Windows software. Skip it if privacy bothers you: Recall stores periodic screenshots locally, and you may want to switch it off. And skip it if a standard laptop with 16 GB RAM costs ₹20,000 less and you will not use the AI tools.
