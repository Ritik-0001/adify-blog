## How to Choose an XL Air Fryer

Think about capacity. A 4 litre basket suits two to three people, and 6 litres or more suits families. Dual baskets add flexibility if you cook different foods together.

Look at the basket and cleaning. A nonstick, dishwasher-safe basket saves effort, and a removable divider helps with mixed foods.

Check controls and power. Digital panels with presets are easier for beginners, and higher wattage heats faster.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Overfilling.** Air needs to circulate.

**Using too much oil.** A light spray is enough.

**Skipping preheat.** Preheat for best results.

**Ignoring cleaning.** Clean after each use.

## Real-World Scenarios

**A family of four.** A 6 litre basket handles a meal.

**A couple who cooks two items.** A dual basket helps.

**A beginner.** Choose presets and a simple interface.

## What to Check on the Listing

Confirm the capacity, wattage, basket material, dishwasher-safe parts and warranty. See whether accessories such as racks are included.

## Quick FAQ

**Is an air fryer healthy?** It uses less oil than deep frying.

**Can I bake in it?** Some models allow baking.

**How long do they last?** Three to five years or more.

## Cooking Tips for Indian Food

Pat vegetables and paneer dry before cooking, and use a light coating of oil for a crisp finish. For kebabs and tikkas, marinate well and shake the basket halfway through. Preheat for a few minutes, and avoid stacking food so hot air can circulate. Batch cooking works best when you cook in two rounds rather than overfilling once.

## Worth Paying for XL Capacity?

If you regularly cook for four or more, an XL basket saves time by avoiding multiple batches. For one or two people, a 4 litre model is usually enough and takes less space. Dual-basket models add flexibility but take up the most counter space, so measure your kitchen first.

## Running Costs and Safety

Air fryers draw between about 1,400 and 2,000 watts while heating, so use a socket rated for the load and avoid extension boards. Even so, cooking times are short, and electricity costs per meal are modest. Keep the appliance a few centimetres from walls so that hot air can escape, and never leave it running unattended.
