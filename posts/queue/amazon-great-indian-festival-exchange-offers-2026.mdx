## How Exchange Valuation Works

**Brand and model:** Premium brands (Apple, Samsung, Sony) typically retain higher exchange value than budget brands at comparable age and condition.

**Age:** Exchange value depreciates significantly year-over-year — a 1-year-old device retains substantially more value than a 3-4-year-old equivalent.

**Working condition:** Fully functional devices with no visible damage receive maximum eligible credit; damaged screens or non-functional buttons reduce valuation significantly.

---

## Maximizing Exchange Value by Category

**Laptops:** Back up and factory-reset before evaluation. Include the original charger if possible for slightly higher credit.

**Smartphones:** Ensure the screen has no cracks and all buttons/cameras function — even minor cosmetic damage can meaningfully reduce valuation.

**TVs and appliances:** Confirm the unit powers on and operates normally during valuation assessment — appliance exchange programs typically require a working-condition check.

## Exchange vs. Independent Resale

**Choose Amazon exchange for convenience** — the credit applies instantly toward your new purchase without separate listing or buyer coordination.

**Choose independent resale (Cashify, OLX) for maximum value** — particularly for premium devices in excellent condition, independent resale sometimes yields 10-20% more than manufacturer exchange programs.

**Quick comparison tip:** Get an independent resale estimate before committing to exchange — if the difference is minor, exchange convenience wins; if substantial, the extra effort may be worthwhile.
