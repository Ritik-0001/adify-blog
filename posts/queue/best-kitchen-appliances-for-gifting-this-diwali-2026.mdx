## How to Choose a Kitchen Appliance Gift

Ask or observe what the recipient already uses. A cook who complains about a slow mixer will love an upgrade, while a household with limited counter space may not welcome a large appliance.

Check capacity and size. An air fryer that is too small frustrates a large family, and one that is too big crowds a small kitchen.

Look at service and warranty. Popular brands like Philips, Preethi and Hawkins have wide service networks, which matters for a gift that will be used daily.

## Quick FAQ

**Is a kitchen appliance a good gift for a wife or mother?** It can be, if she has asked for it or clearly needs it. Otherwise pair it with a personal gift so she does not feel the present is for the household.

**Air fryer or OTG?** An air fryer is faster and easier for daily snacks. An OTG suits baking and grilling in larger batches.

**How do I pick a mixer grinder?** Look at motor wattage, jar quality and the warranty. Multiple jars for wet and dry grinding make it more versatile.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Gifting a big appliance without asking.** A large oven or mixer can take over a small counter. If you are not sure about space, choose a compact item or ask a family member.

**Assuming everyone cooks the same way.** A household that cooks mostly on the stove may not use an air fryer, while a busy working couple may love one. Match the gift to the way the recipient actually cooks.

**Ignoring cleaning effort.** Appliances with complicated parts get used less. Look for dishwasher-safe or easy-wipe components.

**Skipping the warranty check.** Confirm the warranty length and service centre coverage in the recipient's city.
