## Choose by what he does, not what the occasion suggests

Many husbands claim to want nothing, and then use a gift for years if it fits their routine. Think of his commute, his hobbies, his wardrobe and what he keeps complaining about. A rushed gift that does not fit his habits is worse than a modest one aimed at his life.

## Make it personal

A handwritten letter, a photo collage or a planned evening beats any object. Cook his favourite meal, arrange a drive or book a short getaway. Add a [personalised mug or keychain](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=personalised+gift+for+husband+photo&tag=adifystore-21) to carry a memory. Plan the day around something he enjoys, not what social media suggests.

## Gifts to avoid

Avoid clothes without knowing sizes, and avoid gifts that hint at self-improvement such as diet products. Avoid unbranded electronics and gifts that duplicate something he just bought.

## Who should skip a big gift

Skip an expensive gift if he prefers experiences or if finances are tight; thoughtfulness matters more. Skip surprises if he dislikes them: ask a friend of his for a hint.
