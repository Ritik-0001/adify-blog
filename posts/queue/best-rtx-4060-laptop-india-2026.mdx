## RTX 4060 vs. RTX 4070: Is the Upgrade Worth It?

The RTX 4070 (roughly ₹20,000-30,000 more) delivers about 25-35% better raw performance and meaningfully better 1440p capability. If you're gaming primarily at 1080p, the RTX 4060 is genuinely sufficient — the RTX 4070 becomes worthwhile specifically for 1440p gaming or wanting extra headroom for the next 3-4 years of increasingly demanding titles.
