## Key Questions Before Buying

**Is your TDS above 300?** If yes, RO is necessary. Test with a ₹200–400 TDS meter before buying.

**How many people in the family?** A 5L tank is adequate for 2–3 people. For 4–6 people, pay slightly more for a 7–10L tank or you'll run dry during peak morning usage.

**What's your water supply schedule?** Many Indian cities have intermittent supply (1–2 hours/day). A larger tank compensates for this — fills during supply, provides water throughout the day.

**Recommendation:** Havells Fab for most Indian families under a ₹10,000 budget — established brand, decent warranty, nationwide service. Pureit Classic if local filter cartridge availability matters more.
