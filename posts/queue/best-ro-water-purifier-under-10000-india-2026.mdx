## What You Get Under ₹10,000

**Purification stages:** 5–7 stages (pre-sediment filter, activated carbon, RO membrane, UV lamp, post-carbon filter). This covers all required stages for complete hard water purification.

**Storage tank:** 5–7L typical at this price. Adequate for 2–3 person households with average consumption.

**TDS reduction:** All RO purifiers reduce TDS effectively. A mineralizer or TDS controller is more important than the TDS reduction itself — adding minerals back up to 50–150 ppm range.

**Purification rate:** 8–10L per hour typical at this price. Fills a 7L tank in 40–50 minutes.

**What's missing vs. premium:** Larger tanks (10–15L), faster purification (20L/hr), higher-end UV lamps, smarter filter-change alerts.

## Water Wastage in RO Purifiers

Standard RO membranes waste 3L for every 1L purified (25% recovery). Under ₹10,000, most RO purifiers are around 25–33% recovery. To reduce water wastage: install a rejected water tap that routes waste water to a bucket for garden/bathroom use — this converts "wasted" water into usable grey water and improves effective RO efficiency.
