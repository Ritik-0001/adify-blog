**Camera:** 1/1.3-inch CMOS sensor. 48MP stills. 4K/60fps video. 4K/100fps slow motion. 10-bit D-Log M colour profile for post-production grading. ƒ/1.7 aperture (one of the widest in consumer drones — better low-light performance).

**Obstacle avoidance:** Omnidirectional — forward, backward, left, right, upward, and downward. Stops before obstacles in all directions. Previous DJI Mini models had no sideways or upward obstacle sensing.

**Transmission:** O4 video transmission. Up to 20km range. 1080p/60fps live view with low latency.

**Weight:** 249g — exactly under the 250g DGCA nano threshold.

## DGCA Regulations for Drones in India

India's DGCA (Directorate General of Civil Aviation) classifies drones by weight:
- Nano (under 250g): Minimal restrictions. No DGCA registration required. Flying allowed without UIN (Unique Identification Number) in many conditions.
- Micro (250g to 2kg): DGCA registration required. Pilot certificate required for some operations.

The Mini 4 Pro's 249g weight is specifically engineered to stay below the 250g threshold. For Indian buyers, this means significantly less regulatory overhead.

**Still restricted even for nano drones:**
- No flying over populated areas
- No flying near airports (5km restriction)
- No flying above 60m altitude without specific permission
- Military/government zone restrictions apply
