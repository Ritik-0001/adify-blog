## How to Set Up Price Tracking

**Install Keepa (browser extension) or a similar Amazon India price tracker.** Once installed, it overlays a price history graph directly on product pages.

**Add your shortlisted products to tracking 2-3 weeks before the expected sale date.** This gives you enough historical data to spot any pre-sale price manipulation.

**Set price-drop alerts** if the tool supports them, so you're notified the moment your target product's price actually drops during the sale.

---

## Reading a Price History Graph Correctly

**A genuine discount** shows a price that's been relatively stable for weeks, then drops meaningfully during the sale window.

**A fake or inflated discount** shows a price spike shortly (days) before the sale starts, followed by a "discount" back down to roughly the same level it was before the spike — this is the classic "regular price" manipulation pattern.

**Seasonal fluctuation** is normal for some categories (AC prices naturally vary by season) — don't mistake this for sale-specific discounting.

## What to Do With Tracking Data

**Confirm before buying** that the current GIF price is genuinely lower than the stable price you observed in the weeks prior — not just lower than an artificially spiked reference point.

**Compare the same tracked data across Flipkart** if the product is available on both platforms, to see which offers the genuinely better sale-period price.
