## How to Use This List Effectively

**Match the category to your actual need first.** It's easy to get pulled toward the loudest "deal of the day" banner instead of the category you actually came to shop.

**Cross-check discount depth against your target price, not the displayed percentage.** A 50% off badge on an inflated base price is worse than a genuine 20% off a fair one.

**Don't spread your budget too thin across categories.** One well-researched high-value purchase (a laptop, a TV, a major appliance) usually delivers more total savings than several smaller unplanned ones.
