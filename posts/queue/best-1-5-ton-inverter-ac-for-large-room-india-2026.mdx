## How to Choose a 1.5 Ton AC for a Large Room

Check the room size and sun exposure. A 1.5 ton AC suits rooms up to about 150 to 180 square feet, and top-floor rooms or those with west-facing walls may need more capacity.

Look at star rating, inverter type and copper condenser. A 5-star rating saves electricity over years, and a copper condenser typically lasts longer.

Plan for installation and service. Installation quality affects performance, so confirm what is included and the service network in your city.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Undersizing.** A weak AC in a large room runs constantly and struggles.

**Ignoring installation charges.** Copper piping and stand costs can add to the price.

**Skipping servicing.** Clean filters regularly.

## Quick FAQ

**1.5 ton or 2 ton?** Choose 2 ton for rooms above about 180 square feet or with heavy sun exposure.

**Is 5-star worth it?** For heavy daily use, the electricity savings can justify the higher price.

**How long does an AC last?** Ten years or more with maintenance.

## Installation and Running Costs

Installation is part of the purchase. Ask what the seller or brand includes, since copper piping beyond the standard length, an outdoor stand and drain piping often cost extra. A good installer keeps the pipe run short and the outdoor unit in a shaded, ventilated spot.

Running costs depend on the star rating, room conditions and usage. A 5-star inverter model consumes noticeably less electricity than a 3-star one in heavy use. Set the temperature to around 24 to 26 degrees and use the sleep mode at night to save power. Clean the filters every few weeks.

## What to Check on the Listing

Confirm the tonnage, star rating, condenser material and whether the price includes installation. Check the compressor, PCB and product warranty separately, and note the ISEER rating rather than only the star label. Read recent buyer feedback for installation experience and service response.
