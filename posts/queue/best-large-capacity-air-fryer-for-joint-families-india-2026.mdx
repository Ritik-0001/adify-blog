## Why size matters for a big family

A 4 litre basket handles roughly 500 g of fries, enough for two people. For six to eight family members, you would run three or four rounds, and the first batch goes cold. A larger machine cooks in one or two rounds. But bigger is not automatically better, because capacity depends on layout and airflow, not only litres.

## Basket style, 8 to 10 litres

Large basket fryers have a single deep drawer, usually 8 to 10 litres, from brands such as Philips (XXL range), Cosori, Ninja and some Indian brands.

- Good: simple to use, easy to clean, compact for the capacity, good for fries, wings and nuggets.
- Weakness: food stacks deep, so you must shake it for even crisping, and layering reduces crunch.

Basket models work best for items you toss, such as potato wedges, vegetables and chicken pieces. Search [8 litre air fryer basket](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=8+litre+air+fryer+basket&tag=adifystore-21).

## Oven style with trays, 10 to 15 litres

These look like a mini oven with a glass door and two or three racks, sometimes with a rotisserie. Food lies in a single layer on each tray, so crisping is more even and you can cook several items together.

- Good: more surface area, visible cooking, supports baking, roasting and dehydrating.
- Weakness: larger footprint, longer preheat, trays need more washing, and some feel less powerful than a basket model.

For joint families cooking tikka, rotis, roasted vegetables and cutlets in volume, oven style often makes more sense. Browse [oven style air fryer 12 litre](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=oven+style+air+fryer+12+litre&tag=adifystore-21).

## Power and wiring

Large units can draw 1,700 to 2,000 watts or more. In older homes, a shared kitchen socket may be overloaded if a kettle, mixer and air fryer run together. Use a 16 amp socket and check your MCB. Do not use thin extension cords.

## Counter space and storage

A 10 to 12 litre unit is big, often 35 to 45 cm wide and tall, and weighs 6 kg or more. It also needs clearance of 10 to 15 cm behind and above for hot air release. Joint family kitchens are often busy and tight, so decide in advance where it will live. If it must be stored away, you may rarely use it.

## Cleaning

Large baskets and trays are bulkier to wash, and may not fit in a small sink or dishwasher. Look for non-stick coated parts, removable crumb trays and a design without crevices. Reusable liners help reduce scrubbing. Search [air fryer tray replacement](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=air+fryer+tray+replacement&tag=adifystore-21).

## Cooking examples for big families

- Basket: a kilo of frozen fries or a double batch of chicken wings in about 20 minutes
- Oven style: two trays of cutlets and a rack of roasted vegetables at once
- Rotisserie: a whole chicken or a large paneer block on a spit, if the model supports it

Do not overload, since air needs room to circulate. Even in large machines, a single layer crisps best.

## Honest trade-off

For very large families, a large air fryer still does not replace a full oven or a stove. Rotis, large rice quantities and gravies are not air fryer territory. Think of it as a crisping and roasting assistant, not a primary cooker.

## Who should skip a large model

- Small families, since a 4 to 6 litre model is cheaper and faster to preheat
- Kitchens without a spare 16 amp socket
- People who mainly reheat small portions
