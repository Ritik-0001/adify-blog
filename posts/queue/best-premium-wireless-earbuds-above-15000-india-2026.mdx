## How to Choose Premium Earbuds

Check the fit. Ear tips and shape matter for noise isolation and comfort. Try the included tips and use the fit test in the app if available.

Look at ANC, calls and battery. Premium earbuds offer six to eight hours per charge, with the case providing several more.

Consider the ecosystem. AirPods work best with iPhone, Galaxy Buds with Galaxy phones, and Sony, Bose and Sennheiser are more neutral.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Using the wrong tips.** Try all sizes.

**Buying from unauthorised sellers.** Counterfeits exist.

**Ignoring ecosystem limits.** Check compatibility.

**Expecting silence.** ANC reduces noise.

## Fit, Comfort and Sound

Fit is everything with earbuds. A good seal improves both noise cancelling and bass, so try the medium ear tips first, then adjust. Foam tips seal better for some people, and wing tips help earbuds stay secure during workouts. Spend a few days with any pair before judging.

## Battery, Cases and Charging

Expect five to eight hours of use per charge and several more from the case. Wireless charging and quick charging are common on premium pairs. Batteries in small earbuds age, so buy from an authorised seller and check the warranty terms.

## Calls and Multipoint

Good microphones and multipoint pairing, which connects to two devices at once, make earbuds useful for work. Check whether the model supports multipoint and whether it works with your devices.

## Water and Sweat Resistance

If you use earbuds for workouts or in rainy weather, check the IP rating. Ratings such as IPX4 handle sweat and light rain, and IPX7 handles brief submersion. Wipe the earbuds after workouts and dry the charging contacts, since moisture in the case can cause charging problems over time.
