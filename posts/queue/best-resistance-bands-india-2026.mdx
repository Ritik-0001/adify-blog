## Types of Resistance Bands

**Loop Bands (Mini Bands):** Short circular bands (20–35cm). Used primarily for hip activation, lateral walks, glute work, and banded compound exercises. Come in packs of 5 with different resistance levels.

**Therapy Bands (Flat Ribbon Bands):** Long flat bands (1–2m). More versatile — can wrap, loop, or anchor. Used in physical therapy, yoga, and upper body exercises. Often sold by the metre or in pre-cut lengths.

**Tube Bands with Handles:** Cylindrical tube with plastic handles. Mimic dumbbell cable exercises. Good for chest press, curls, and rows. The handles make exercise easier but tubes can snap at the connection point.

**Pull-Up Resistance Bands (Heavy Bands):** Wide, thick loop bands (180cm circumference). Used to assist pull-ups and dips (band takes some bodyweight), stretch overhead, and add resistance to barbell movements. Resistance: 10–80kg depending on width.

## Band Care and Longevity

Natural latex bands last 6–24 months depending on use intensity. Extend their life by:
- Avoid direct sunlight storage (UV degrades latex)
- Don't use with bare metal edges (chalk, barbell knurling tears bands)
- Inspect for micro-tears before each use — snapping bands cause injuries

When a band snaps (they will eventually), replace it. Band sets cost ₹500–₁,000 — budget for annual replacement.
