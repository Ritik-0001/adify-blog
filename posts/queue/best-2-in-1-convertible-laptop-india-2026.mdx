## What you give up for the flip

A convertible has a screen that folds back, so the display needs a stiff, expensive hinge and a touch digitiser layer. That costs weight and money. Expect 1.4 to 1.7 kg for a 14-inch model, which is more than a comparable clamshell. Tablet mode on a laptop this size is awkward for long reading, because the keyboard is still pressing on the underside. People buy convertibles for tent mode, stand mode, and flat writing with a pen, not as a replacement for a real tablet.

## Pen support matters more than the spec sheet suggests

Not every touchscreen supports a pen, and not every pen works on every screen. There are two common systems. Wacom AES and Microsoft Pen Protocol (MPP) use batteries and different tips, and a pen from the wrong system simply will not register. USI is a third standard used on some Chromebooks. Before buying a separate stylus, confirm the exact protocol in the laptop's specs. Pressure levels of 4,096 are common and plenty for note-taking, while serious illustration benefits from 8,192 levels and tilt support. A [stylus pen for touchscreen laptops](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=active+stylus+pen+for+touchscreen+laptop&tag=adifystore-21) is worth buying only after you have confirmed that match.

## The hinge: where cheap convertibles fail

A convertible gets flexed hundreds of times a year, so look for steel hinges, a screen that stays put without wobbling when you tap it, and a laptop that does not creak when you pick it up by one corner. Cheaper plastic builds can develop a loose screen within two years. Check whether accidental damage cover is offered for the hinge, since normal warranty may exclude physical damage. A [laptop sleeve with a stylus loop](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=laptop+sleeve+14+inch+padded&tag=adifystore-21) protects the glass edges, and a [screen guard for touch laptops](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=matte+screen+protector+laptop+14+inch&tag=adifystore-21) gives a paper-like feel for writing.

## Who should skip a convertible

Skip it if you type for most of your day, because the keyboard and trackpad usually get compromised for the slim hinge. Skip it if you want a tablet to read in bed: a dedicated iPad or Android tablet is lighter by half a kilo. Skip it if you edit video with a heavy GPU load, since thin convertibles throttle. And skip it if your budget is under ₹55,000, where convertibles tend to use weak screens and slow storage.
