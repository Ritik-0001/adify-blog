## What Actually Matters in Premium Ergonomic Chairs

**Dynamic lumbar support:** Adjusts and moves with your spine's natural curve as you shift position, rather than a fixed pad — this is Aeron's signature feature that alternatives specifically try to replicate.

**4D or multi-directional armrests:** Height, width, depth, and angle adjustment accommodates different desk heights and typing postures precisely.

**Breathable mesh material:** Distributes weight evenly and allows airflow, preventing the heat buildup that solid foam cushioning causes during long sitting sessions.

**Weight-activated recline tension:** Automatically calibrates recline resistance to your body weight rather than requiring manual tension adjustment.
