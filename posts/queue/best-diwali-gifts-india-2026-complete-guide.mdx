## Gift Ideas by Budget Tier

**Under ₹1,500:** Premium chocolates, dry fruit gift boxes, small home decor items, basic wired earphones.

**₹1,500-₴,000:** boAt/Noise earbuds, budget smartwatches, quality candle/diffuser sets, air fryers.

**₹5,000-₹15,000:** Mid-range headphones, fitness smartwatches, premium kitchen appliances, smart home devices.

**₹15,000+:** Premium electronics, high-end kitchen appliances, quality furniture pieces — reserved for closest family relationships.

## Gift Ideas by Recipient

**Parents:** Practical home appliances (water purifier, air fryer) or comfort items (quality blankets, massage devices) — items showing you understand their daily needs.

**Colleagues:** ₹1,500-₴,000 range — premium chocolates, desk accessories, or branded drinkware that's thoughtful without being overly personal.

**Close friends:** ₹2,000-₹8,000 — tech accessories, experience-based gifts, or items matching their known hobbies.

**Household staff:** Cash bonuses remain the most practical and appreciated gift, sometimes paired with sweets or clothing.

## Timing Your Diwali Shopping

Start shopping 2-3 weeks before Diwali to avoid the last-minute price surge and stock shortages that affect popular gift categories every year, and to take advantage of any pre-Diwali sale pricing on Amazon.
