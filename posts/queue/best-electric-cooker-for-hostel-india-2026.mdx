## Hostel Cooking Constraints

**Power socket:** Indian hostel rooms typically have 5A (standard) power sockets. Appliances above 750–800W require a 15A socket (larger three-pin socket, or adapter). Most hostel rooms don't have 15A sockets readily accessible. Verify your appliance's wattage — anything under 750W uses standard 5A sockets.

**Hostel rules:** Many Indian college hostels prohibit cooking appliances in rooms (fire safety). Check hostel rules before purchasing. Electric cookers drawing over 500W may trip room circuit breakers.

**Space:** Hostel rooms have limited surface and storage space. Compact appliances (diameter under 25cm) fit on shelves and are easier to hide for hostels with restrictions.

## Realistic Hostel Cooking Advice

A hostel multi-cooker allows: boiled eggs, instant noodles (Maggi), rice, dal (pre-soaked), poha, upma, and reheated curries from home. It does not allow: deep frying, making roti/chapati, or anything requiring a high-heat wok.

For actual cooking meals in hostel: buy simple pre-cooked pouches (MTR ready meals, ₹50–₁₀₀) that need only heating — faster and lower hostel risk than raw cooking.
