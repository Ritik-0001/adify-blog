## How to Choose for a Family of Four

Eight kilograms is the standard recommendation for four people, giving room for bedding and towels. Seven kilograms works if you wash daily, but it can feel tight.

Decide front load or top load. Front loaders are gentler and use less water, while top loaders are cheaper and simpler.

Think about water and service. Hard water calls for a model with suitable features, and service coverage matters as much as the machine itself.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Buying too small.** A 6 kg drum forces multiple loads and strains the motor when overfilled.

**Ignoring the warranty.** Read what the motor and the product warranties cover.

**Not planning for installation.** Check the water inlet, drain and floor strength.

## Quick FAQ

**Front or top load for a family of four?** Front load is gentler and more water-efficient, and top load is cheaper and simpler.

**How often should I clean the machine?** A monthly tub clean cycle helps prevent odours.

**Is a higher spin speed better?** It helps clothes dry faster, but it can wear fabrics.

## Real-World Use for a Family of Four

Four people typically generate seven to ten kilograms of laundry every two days, so one 8 kg load daily or every other day is a common pattern. A larger drum reduces the number of cycles, which saves time and water. Households with young children or heavy daily use may prefer a bigger drum or a machine with a quick-wash cycle for small loads.

Think about laundry habits too. If you wash many delicate items or sarees, a front loader's gentler action helps. If you prefer adding items mid-cycle or drying in a small space, a top loader is more convenient.

## Buying Tips

Check delivery and installation slots, since many brands schedule a demo within a day or two of delivery. Ask about the hose length, the stand and the water inlet requirements. Keep the invoice and warranty card safe, and register the product with the brand if it offers an app.

## Recommendation

For most families of four, an 8 kg front load like the LG or IFB is a strong long-term buy. If budget matters, the Samsung or Whirlpool top loaders offer good value.
