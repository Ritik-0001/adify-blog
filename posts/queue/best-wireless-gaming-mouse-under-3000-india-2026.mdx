## LIGHTSPEED vs. Regular Bluetooth vs. Budget 2.4GHz

**Logitech LIGHTSPEED:** 1ms latency. 2.4GHz proprietary wireless. Best in class at any price.

**Budget 2.4GHz wireless (non-LIGHTSPEED):** 4–8ms latency. Most wireless gaming mice under ₹2,000 use this protocol. The 4–8ms additional input delay is perceptible in fast FPS games.

**Bluetooth:** Not suitable for gaming. 15–30ms latency, connection instability.

The G304's LIGHTSPEED protocol justifies its price premium over budget wireless alternatives — you're paying for wireless performance that doesn't disadvantage you in competitive play.
