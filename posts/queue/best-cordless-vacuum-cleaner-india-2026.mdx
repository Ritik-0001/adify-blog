## How to Choose a Cordless Vacuum

Check battery and modes. A removable battery lets you swap and extend cleaning time, and many models give around 30 to 60 minutes in low power but much less in boost mode.

Look at suction and filtration. Strong suction and a sealed filter system help with dust and allergies, which matters in Indian cities.

Think about weight and attachments. Lighter models are easier on stairs, and attachments for crevices, furniture and cars add value.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Expecting full-home cleaning on one charge.** Check run time.

**Ignoring bin size.** Small bins fill quickly.

**Skipping filter care.** Wash or replace filters as advised.

**Choosing the cheapest.** Suction and battery may disappoint.

## Real-World Scenarios

**A small flat with tile floors.** A lightweight cordless vacuum.

**A home with pets.** A model with an anti-tangle brush.

**A family with allergies.** A model with sealed filtration.

## What to Check on the Listing

Confirm the suction rating, the battery capacity and run time, the bin size, the filter type, the attachments and the warranty. See whether spare batteries are available in India.

## Battery and Care Tips

Charge the battery fully before first use, avoid running it to zero often and store it away from heat. Empty the bin after each use and clean filters on schedule.

## Quick FAQ

**Can it replace a corded vacuum?** For small to medium homes, often yes.

**How long does the battery last?** Depends on power mode.

**Are spare batteries available?** Check by model.

## Cleaning Indian Floors

Indian homes often have a mix of tiles, marble, carpets and rugs, with dust that settles quickly. A cordless vacuum with a soft roller head works well on tiles, and a motorised brush bar helps on rugs. Daily quick passes keep dust from building up, which is easier than a weekly deep clean.

## Battery Life and Replacement

Batteries wear out over time, so check whether the brand sells replacement batteries in India and at what approximate cost. Avoid leaving the vacuum on charge for days, and store it in a cool place. Using lower power modes extends run time.

## Warranty and Service

Compare the warranty on the motor, the battery and the whole unit, and check that the brand has service in your city. Keep the invoice and the model number for claims.

## Final Checklist Before You Buy

Before you order, check the weight, the bin or tank size, the battery or cable length and the attachments in the box, and read the warranty on the motor and battery separately. Confirm that filters, brushes and spare batteries are available in India, and look at reviews from homes similar to yours, with similar floors, pets and dust levels.
