## How to Choose a Business Laptop

Focus on durability and support. Business laptops usually offer longer warranties and on-site service options. Check the warranty type and response time.

Look at security and ports. A fingerprint reader, TPM, webcam shutter and a full set of ports, including HDMI and USB-A, reduce the need for dongles.

Consider display and weight. A bright, anti-glare full-HD screen is easier on the eyes, and a weight around 1.4 to 1.6 kilograms suits daily carrying.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Choosing consumer specs.** Look at warranty and support.

**Ignoring RAM.** Aim for 16GB.

**Skipping the display check.** Confirm brightness and resolution.

**Overlooking the keyboard.** Test if possible.

## Real-World Scenarios

**A consultant who travels.** A lightweight ThinkPad or EliteBook is a fit.

**An office employee.** A ProBook or Latitude works.

**A freelancer.** A ThinkPad E14 balances price and durability.

## Quick FAQ

**Are business laptops better than consumer laptops?** They focus on durability and support, not style.

**Do I need a Windows Pro licence?** It helps with security features.

**How long do they last?** Five years or more.

## Warranty and On-Site Service

Business buyers often care more about uptime than benchmarks. Check whether the laptop includes on-site service or only carry-in repair, and whether accidental damage cover is available. A three-year warranty with next-business-day service can be worth more than a faster processor if your work depends on the laptop.

## Security and Manageability

Look for a TPM chip, a fingerprint reader or IR camera for Windows Hello, and a privacy shutter. If your company uses device management, confirm that the laptop supports the required software. Buy a Windows Pro edition if you need BitLocker and domain features.

## Docking and Connectivity

Many professionals connect a monitor, keyboard and mouse at a desk, so check for USB-C or Thunderbolt with display output and power delivery. A single-cable dock keeps the desk clean and charges the laptop, and Wi-Fi 6 support helps in busy offices. If you travel, look for 4G or 5G options on select models.
