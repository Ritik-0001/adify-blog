## What to Check Before Buying

**Plumbed vs. internal water tank:** Plumbed dispensers connect to your home's water line for continuous supply; internal tank models need manual refilling every few days — plumbed is more convenient but requires proper installation access to a water line.

**Built-in filtration:** Look for models with internal water filters (replaced every 6 months) that improve dispensed water taste and quality beyond your tap water's baseline.

**Ice maker type:** Cubed ice makers are standard; some premium models offer crushed ice options as well.
