## Price Tier Breakdown

**₹6,500-₸,500 (Budget TN):** Basic TN panels, narrower viewing angles, faster response but weaker color accuracy — acceptable for basic office use.

**₹8,000-₹10,000 (Standard IPS):** Dell SE2422H, LG 24MP400 — genuine IPS panels with accurate color and wide viewing angles, ideal for study and general use.

**₹11,000-₹14,000 (144Hz Gaming IPS):** Higher refresh rates for competitive gaming while retaining IPS color quality.

**₹15,000-₹18,000+ (Premium 144Hz+):** Enhanced color gamut, HDR support, and higher refresh rates (165Hz+) for serious gaming or content creation.

---

## What Actually Changes as Price Increases

**Panel technology:** TN to IPS is the biggest quality jump for color accuracy and viewing angles.

**Refresh rate:** 60Hz standard, jumping to 75Hz, 144Hz, or 165Hz at higher tiers for smoother motion.

**Color gamut coverage:** Higher tiers cover more of the sRGB or even DCI-P3 color space, mattering for creative work.

---

## Recommendation

For general study and office use, budget toward ₹8,000-₹10,000 for genuine IPS quality. For gaming specifically, ₹11,000-₹14,000 gets you 144Hz refresh without sacrificing panel quality — avoid the sub-₹7,000 TN tier unless budget is an absolute hard constraint.
