## Start with the technology, not the brand

The real decision is ink tank versus laser. Ink tank printers use refillable bottles of liquid ink, giving cheap colour pages and good photos, but nozzles can clog if the printer sits idle for weeks. Laser printers use toner powder, print crisp text quickly, and sit unused for months without drying. Mono laser pages are cheap; colour laser printers cost far more. Once you know the technology, brand choices narrow considerably.

## Canon: strengths and weaknesses

Canon's PIXMA G-series, such as the G2010, G3010 and G4010 families, are among the most common ink tank printers in India, with prices roughly ₹14,000 to ₹22,000. They have broad retail and service presence, bottles are easy to find and photo quality is good. Wi-Fi models are available. The trade-offs are that cheaper models are slower, the ink tank design needs careful refilling, and lasers like Canon's imageCLASS or LBP are less common at retail. See [Canon PIXMA G3010](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=Canon+PIXMA+G3010+ink+tank&tag=adifystore-21).

## Brother: strengths and weaknesses

Brother's INKvestment tank printers such as the DCP-T series, roughly ₹15,000 to ₹22,000, offer sturdy builds and generous in-box ink, while its mono laser printers like the HL-L2321D and DCP-L2541DW, roughly ₹12,000 to ₹22,000, are known for toner efficiency and reliability. Duplex and Wi-Fi come on higher trims. The weakness is a thinner service network than Canon's in smaller towns, so check before buying. ## 1. Home use with colour: Canon G-series usually wins

For school projects, photos and the occasional document, Canon's G-series balance cost and availability, and refill bottles are cheap. Brother's tank printers are competitive, with some models offering faster text printing, so shop price and Wi-Fi features. A regular printer user should pick whichever has a reachable service centre.

## 2. Office use with mono documents: Brother laser often wins

A mono laser printer from Brother prints fast, handles invoices and contracts well and does not clog. A single toner cartridge lasts for roughly 1,000 to 3,000 pages depending on the model, and costs per page are typically lower than inkjet for text. Canon's lasers are good too but are less visible in the market. See [Canon imageCLASS laser printer](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=Canon+imageCLASS+laser+printer&tag=adifystore-21).

## 3. Photo printing: Canon pulls ahead

If photos matter, Canon's colour reproduction and its range of photo inkjets outclass most competing tank printers. For gallery-quality prints, you would look beyond ink tank models to dedicated photo printers. See [Canon photo printer](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=Canon+photo+printer+home&tag=adifystore-21).

## Running costs in real life

An ink tank printer costs roughly ₹0.3 to ₹1 per black page and ₹1 to ₹3 per colour page when you refill with genuine bottles, which is far lower than cartridge inkjets. A mono laser's toner costs roughly ₹0.7 to ₹1.5 per page. Third-party ink is cheaper but can void warranty or clog heads. Buy genuine [Canon ink bottles](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=Canon+GI+790+ink+bottle&tag=adifystore-21) or [Brother toner cartridges](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=Brother+TN+2365+toner+cartridge&tag=adifystore-21) matched to your model, and print a test page weekly to keep ink tank heads clear.

## Service and after-sales

Canon has a wide authorised service network across Indian cities and many small towns. Brother also has service centres in major cities, but fewer outside metros. Both handle warranty claims through a toll-free number. Before buying, search for a service centre near you. If you live outside a metro, the brand with the nearer centre is the better choice.

## Who should skip each

Skip an ink tank printer if you print rarely: nozzles clog after weeks of idle time. Skip a laser printer if you want colour photos. Skip entry-level all-in-one models under ₹10,000, which have poor refill economics.

## Recommendation

Choose Canon for a family colour ink tank printer, and Brother for a compact mono laser or a reliable tank printer in a city with a service centre.
