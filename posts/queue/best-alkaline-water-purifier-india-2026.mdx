## How to Choose an Alkaline Purifier

Start with water safety. Choose RO plus UV or UF based on your water source and TDS, since a purifier that does not treat your water properly is not worth the extras.

Check the claim. Look for the stated pH range, the mineral cartridge life and replacement cost, and be cautious about large health claims.

Look at service and cost. Mineral cartridges add running costs, so check replacement prices and availability.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Paying extra for claims.** Read the evidence and specs.

**Skipping TDS testing.** Know your water.

**Ignoring cartridge costs.** Include them in the budget.

**Not changing cartridges on time.** Follow the schedule.

## Real-World Scenarios

**A family that wants minerals added back.** An RO plus UV purifier with mineral addition.

## Getting the Most From Your Purifier

## Warranty and Service

## Quick FAQ

**Is alkaline water healthier?** The evidence is debated, so do not rely on claims.

**Does RO remove minerals?** Yes, and TDS control adds some back.

**Are alkaline cartridges costly?** They add running costs.

## Reading Marketing Claims Carefully

Terms such as alkaline, mineral and ionised are used loosely in marketing. Look for a stated pH range, the specific minerals added and an independent test or certification if one is mentioned. Avoid sellers who promise medical benefits. If a feature is not clearly explained in the specifications, treat it as a marketing line rather than a reason to pay more.

## Final Checklist Before You Buy

Before you order, test your water TDS, check the tank size and purification rate against your daily use and confirm the technology suits your water source. Ask for the annual service or AMC terms in writing, check that filters and membranes are available in your city and keep the invoice and installation record.
