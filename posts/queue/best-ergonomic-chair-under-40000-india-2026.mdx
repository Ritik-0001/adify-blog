## What "Genuine Ergonomic" Means at This Price

**Dynamic (not fixed) lumbar support:** The lumbar pad should move and adjust as you shift position — fixed foam pads marketed as "ergonomic" without this dynamic quality provide meaningfully less real support.

**Breathable mesh, not just cushioned foam:** Proper mesh construction with weight-distributing tension prevents pressure points and heat buildup that solid foam cushions cause over hours of sitting.

**Multi-directional armrests:** Height, width, and depth adjustment (not just height alone) accommodate different desk setups and typing postures correctly.
