## What a trading setup actually stresses

Charting platforms like TradingView in a browser, Zerodha Kite, Upstox Pro, Angel One's SmartAPI terminals and desktop tools such as Amibroker or MetaTrader do not need much computing power. What hurts is memory (twenty Chrome tabs, five chart windows, a news feed, WhatsApp Web) and display handling. A weak integrated GPU driving three screens through a poor dock will cause flicker, lag on tick data and dropped connections. Eight gigabytes of RAM is a false economy; 16 GB is the working minimum.

## Count the outputs before anything else

Two external monitors is the easy case. A laptop with one HDMI port plus one USB-C port with DisplayPort output handles it directly. Three external monitors almost always requires a Thunderbolt or USB4 dock, or a laptop with HDMI plus two USB-C DisplayPort ports. Beware of "DisplayLink" docks: they work through software compression, can add a slight lag and sometimes fail after Windows updates. A true Thunderbolt dock is more reliable for trading, where a frozen chart at the wrong moment costs real money.

## The dock matters as much as the laptop

A single Thunderbolt dock powers the laptop, drives two or three monitors, and gives you wired Ethernet, which is better than Wi-Fi for order latency. Buy a dock from the same brand as your laptop when possible, since firmware compatibility is smoother. A [Thunderbolt 4 dock](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=Thunderbolt+4+dock+dual+4K&tag=adifystore-21) costs roughly ₹15,000 to ₹25,000. Pair it with matching [24-inch IPS monitors](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=24+inch+IPS+monitor+1080p+HDMI&tag=adifystore-21), keeping all screens the same size and resolution so the windows do not jump when you drag a chart across.

## Cooling, power and uptime

Trading laptops tend to run for ten hours a day, plugged in, with the lid often closed. Choose a model with decent ventilation, and think about battery wear: some business laptops let you cap charging at 80 percent in the BIOS, which noticeably extends battery health. Add a small [UPS for the router and laptop](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=UPS+600VA+for+router+laptop&tag=adifystore-21) because a ten-second power cut during an open position is the thing traders regret not insuring against. A dual-band router with a wired LAN link is the real latency fix.

## Who should skip a dedicated trading laptop

Skip it if you trade with a single monitor and a phone, since a ₹40,000 laptop is enough. Skip gaming laptops unless you want GPU power for other work: their loud fans and high power draw are unnecessary for charting. And skip very cheap Celeron or Pentium models, which stall with multiple browser windows.
