## How to choose when the budget is tight

A budget gift lands well when it solves a small daily annoyance. Think about what the person uses every morning, carries in a bag or looks at on a desk. Avoid decorative items that gather dust and cheap gadgets with unknown brands that fail in a month. A branded ₹2,000 item beats a no-name ₹4,000 one.

A compact waterproof speaker from JBL, roughly ₹2,500 to ₹4,500, delivers surprisingly big sound for balcony parties and bathroom playlists. Battery lasts around 8 to 12 hours. It will not match a home theatre, but it is the sort of gift people take everywhere. Compare [JBL Go speaker](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=JBL+Go+4+Bluetooth+speaker&tag=adifystore-21).

## Packing a budget gift so it feels bigger

Combine two small items, such as a speaker with a pouch or a flask with tea, and wrap it in a nice paper with a note. A [gift hamper box](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=gift+hamper+box+with+ribbon&tag=adifystore-21) and a handwritten card add value for a few hundred rupees. People remember thought, not price.

## Gifts to avoid

Avoid very cheap smartwatches and powerbanks with unclear capacity claims, which often fail. Avoid decorative items without a clear use. Avoid perfumes without knowing the person's taste. Avoid anything where size or colour matters, like clothing, unless you know it well.

## Who should skip a gadget gift

Skip gadgets for people who struggle with technology; choose a comfort item or a hamper. Skip anything that needs a subscription or a specific phone unless you are sure.
