Two people sharing a room get the best results from either one long desk of 180 cm or more or two separate compact desks placed back to back or at right angles, with noise-cancelling headsets and a dedicated power strip each, for roughly ₹60,000 to ₹1 lakh including chairs.

## Two desks or one long one

One long desk, sometimes called a bench desk, saves space and cost: a 180-200 cm top with a 60-70 cm depth lets two people sit side by side. The drawback is that you share the surface, which means movements, calls and cable clutter are closer to each other, and the monitor placement may force an uncomfortable angle.

Two separate desks provide privacy and independent height settings, which helps if one of you is much taller. Placing them back to back gives each a view of a wall; at right angles, they can form an L in a corner. Back-to-back layouts need a room at least 3 metres wide for chairs to roll out, so measure.

## Layout in a typical Indian bedroom

Many Indian flats have rooms of about 10 x 12 feet, with a bed taking one wall. The usual answer: place the desk on the wall opposite the bed so the work area is out of sight when you rest, or use a partition, curtain or bookcase to separate it. A [room divider shelf](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=room+divider+bookshelf+open+shelf&tag=adifystore-21) can double as storage and a visual boundary.

Face desks towards walls, not windows, to avoid screen glare, and keep chairs a clear 90 cm apart so you do not bump each other.

## Noise: the real issue

Two people on calls in one room is the biggest annoyance. Solutions: noise-cancelling headsets with a good microphone, a soft rug, wall panels or heavy curtains to cut echo, and scheduled call times if possible. Look for headsets with microphone noise reduction, since the person on the other end can hear your partner's voice otherwise. See [noise cancelling headset with mic](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=noise+cancelling+headset+microphone+usb+office&tag=adifystore-21).

If your calls clash often, a small [acoustic foam panels](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=acoustic+foam+panels+wall+soundproofing&tag=adifystore-21) set on the nearest wall can reduce echo, though they do not soundproof between you.

## Power and cables

Two laptops, two monitors, chargers and phones can overload a single socket. Run a separate surge-protected power strip for each desk and avoid chaining. Cable trays under each desk and a spiral wrap keep the floor clear. If you have a single wall socket, a four-way extension from a power board and a [surge protector power strip](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=surge+protector+power+strip+6+socket&tag=adifystore-21) is safer than a cheap multi-plug.

For internet, a good router positioned centrally or a mesh unit avoids both of you competing on one weak signal. If you can run it, wired ethernet for one person frees Wi-Fi bandwidth.

## Chairs and ergonomics

Two good chairs matter more than any desk. Each person should have a chair adjustable to their body, ideally ones with seat depth and lumbar adjustment. Two chairs of similar footprint also tuck under the desk more neatly. A pair of [ergonomic office chairs](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=ergonomic+office+chair+mesh+adjustable&tag=adifystore-21) in the ₹12,000-20,000 range is a realistic target.

If you have different heights, sit-stand desks or at least adjustable monitor arms help. If budget is tight, put money into the chair, then a [monitor arm dual](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=monitor+arm+dual+desk+mount&tag=adifystore-21) to reclaim desk space.

## Lighting for two

Overhead lights alone cause glare. Give each person a small desk lamp with dimming and warm and cool settings, and set one light so it does not shine in the other person's eyes. Use blackout curtains for the afternoon sun if your desks face a window.

## Who should skip this layout

If one of you has frequent long meetings and the other needs deep quiet, a shared room may not work, and a co-working space for one person can be cheaper than outfitting a separate study. If the room is smaller than about 9 x 10 feet, consider a foldable wall-mounted desk for one person instead.

## Recommendation

For most couples: a 180-200 cm bench desk with two chairs, separate power strips, headsets and a shelf divider. For people with different heights or conflicting calls: two desks, back to back, with panels between them. Measure the room first, then buy chairs before the desk.
