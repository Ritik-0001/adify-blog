## How combo units work

A combo has two systems in one body. The purifier pulls air through filters. The humidifier adds moisture by one of two methods: evaporative, where a wet wick or filter is blown with air, or ultrasonic, where a vibrating plate creates a fine mist. Evaporative designs are usually better in combos because they do not spray mineral dust into a purifier's own intake.

Run both together and you get filtered, slightly humidified air. In practice, combos usually have smaller tanks and weaker humidifiers than a standalone unit.

## Water tank and maintenance

The humidifier side is where combos need more care. Empty and rinse the tank daily, and descale weekly in hard-water areas. Standing water grows bacteria and mould, which the unit can then blow into the room. Wipe the wick and replace it on schedule, usually every 3 to 6 months.

Hard water leaves white scale and, with ultrasonic units, white dust. Use filtered or demineralised water when possible. [Descaler for humidifiers](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=humidifier+descaler+citric+acid&tag=adifystore-21).

## Trade-offs

Combos save space and cost one plug point. But a fault in one function can take the whole unit offline. Tanks are often small, 2 to 4 litres, so you refill often. Filter and wick costs add up. Humidifier output is usually enough for a bedroom of 150 to 250 sq ft, not a large living room.

Humidity can also reduce the efficiency of some filters if the wet air reaches them, so check the design.

## Humidity settings

Aim for 40 to 50 percent relative humidity. Above 60 percent encourages mould and dust mites. A basic hygrometer shows the reading. Use a combo with an auto humidity sensor or buy a separate meter. [Digital hygrometer](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=digital+hygrometer+thermometer+room&tag=adifystore-21).

## Running cost reality

Besides electricity, count a HEPA filter set each year and a wick or humidifier cartridge every few months. On a Philips or Sharp combo that can be several thousand rupees a year, and Dyson's sealed filters cost more still. Add that to the purchase price before comparing a combo with two separate machines.

## Who should skip a combo

If you live in a humid coastal city, you do not need a humidifier at all, and a purifier alone is enough. If you do not want a daily cleaning routine, skip the humidifier. If your room is larger than 250 sq ft, two separate machines serve better. People with allergies or asthma should consult a doctor before adding a humidifier.

## Recommendation

For a dry winter bedroom, a mid-priced combo with an evaporative humidifier and an easy-to-clean tank is a good fit. Otherwise buy a purifier and a humidifier separately. Clean the tank daily.
