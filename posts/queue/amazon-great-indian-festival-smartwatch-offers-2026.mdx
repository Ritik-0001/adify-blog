## What to Check Before Buying

**Health sensor accuracy:** Premium tiers (Apple Watch, Galaxy Watch) have clinically-validated ECG and blood oxygen sensors; budget trackers' health metrics are directional estimates, not medical-grade readings — set expectations accordingly.

**Cellular vs. GPS-only models:** Cellular variants cost more but let the watch function independently of your phone — worth the premium for fitness-focused buyers who run or cycle without their phone.

**Battery life claims:** Premium smartwatches typically last 18-36 hours; budget fitness trackers often claim 7-14 days — verify which trade-off matters more for your actual usage pattern.
