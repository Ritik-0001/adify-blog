Newlyweds should start with a refrigerator, a washing machine, a water purifier, a geyser and a few kitchen basics, then add comfort items like an AC or a TV as the budget allows. A sensible first set of big appliances can cost roughly ₹60,000 to ₹1,50,000 depending on size and brand, and you can spread purchases over a few months.

This checklist is researched, not hands-on tested, and it assumes a couple furnishing a first home. What changes the list most is whether you rent or own, so settle that before you spend, then confirm the delivery and installation terms on each large item.

## 1. Refrigerator

Roughly ₹20,000–40,000. A 250 to 350 litre double-door refrigerator suits a couple and leaves room for guests. Choose a good energy rating and a long compressor warranty, and expect it to be the appliance that runs the longest every day.

## 2. Washing Machine

## How to Plan Your First Purchases

Start with needs, not wants. A refrigerator, washing machine, purifier and geyser come first, and a TV or AC can follow.

Check space and wiring. Measure doorways, kitchen counters and balcony space, and ask an electrician about the load before ordering heavy appliances.

Stage the spending. Buying over a few months lets you compare live prices and avoid overspending.

## Setting a First-Year Budget

List the items in order of priority, add approximate prices and decide how much you can spend before the wedding and in the first year. Leave a margin for installation, stabilisers, stands and annual servicing. A phased plan keeps debt away and lets you choose better models.

## If You Are Renting

Think about portability. Compact, freestanding appliances move easily, while built-in items stay behind. Check the landlord's rules on drilling, and keep the installation paperwork so that you can reinstall in the next home.

## Who Should Skip Premium Everything

Resist buying the top version of every appliance. A mid-range refrigerator or washing machine from a trusted brand serves most couples for a decade, and the money saved can go toward an AC or a better mattress. Dishwashers and robot vacuums are nice extras, but neither is a first-year need unless you cook daily or have a large floor area.

## Small Extras That Add Up

Big appliances rarely arrive alone in the bill. A refrigerator and an AC may want a voltage stabiliser in areas with unsteady supply, a washing machine may need a stand or a water inlet filter, and a geyser needs a proper pressure valve and wiring. Ask each seller what is included in the box, and keep a small line in the budget for these items so the total does not surprise you.

## Recommendation

Start with a refrigerator, a washing machine, a water purifier and a geyser, then add kitchen items and comfort appliances. Always check the live price and warranty on each listing.
