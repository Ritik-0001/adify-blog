## How to Choose a TV for PS5

Look for HDMI 2.1 support. The PS5 outputs 4K at 120Hz over HDMI 2.1, so a TV with a full-bandwidth port unlocks the console's best modes.

Check for VRR and ALLM. Variable refresh rate reduces tearing, and auto low latency mode switches to game mode automatically.

Check input lag. Lower is better, and reviews often report figures.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Assuming every HDMI port is 2.1.** Check which ports support it.

**Ignoring 120Hz support.** Some 4K TVs are limited to 60Hz.

**Not using game mode.** Enable it.

**Skipping the cable.** Use the supplied HDMI 2.1 cable.

## What to Check on the Listing

Confirm the refresh rate, HDMI 2.1 ports, VRR support, input lag and warranty. See whether installation and a wall mount are included.

## Real-World Scenarios

**A serious console gamer with a bigger budget.** An LG C-series OLED offers HDMI 2.1 on all ports, and pairs well with a PS5 and a second console or a PC.

**A gamer who plays in a bright room.** A bright QLED or Mini-LED may suit better than OLED, since it fights reflections more easily.

**A casual gamer on a budget.** A 120Hz QLED from Hisense or TCL gives the features that matter at a lower price.

**A household that also watches films.** OLED's contrast helps with films, so it can serve both use cases.

## Console Setup Tips

Connect the PS5 to an HDMI 2.1 port, and enable 120Hz output and VRR in the PS5 display settings. Turn on the TV's game mode to reduce input lag, and use the supplied HDMI cable or a certified ultra-high-speed cable. Check that HDR is calibrated using the PS5 setup screen.

## Gaming Features Explained

VRR, or variable refresh rate, matches the TV's refresh rate to the game's frame rate, which reduces tearing and stutter. ALLM, or auto low latency mode, switches the TV into game mode when it detects a console. HDR gaming needs a TV with good peak brightness, and Dolby Vision gaming is supported on some Xbox consoles but not on PS5, which uses HDR10. Look for a TV that supports the features you will use and has low input lag, ideally around 10 milliseconds or less in game mode.
