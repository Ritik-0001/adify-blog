## How to Choose a Camera Gimbal

Start with your camera and lens weight. Add the weight of the body, the lens and accessories like a mic, then choose a gimbal whose payload comfortably exceeds it.

Check compatibility. Brands publish lists of tested camera and lens combinations, which show whether balancing and control cables work.

Think about size and battery. A smaller gimbal travels easily, and a longer battery life helps with long shoots.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Overloading the gimbal.** Stay below the limit.

**Skipping balancing.** Balance carefully before powering on.

**Ignoring lens changes.** Rebalance after changing lenses.

**Not updating firmware.** It improves performance.

## Real-World Scenarios

**A travel vlogger with a light camera.** A DJI RS 3 Mini or Zhiyun Crane M3.

**A wedding videographer with a heavier setup.** A DJI RS 4.

**A hybrid shooter.** A DJI RS 4 Mini.

## What to Check on the Listing

Confirm the payload, the camera compatibility list, the battery life, the warranty, the seller and what is in the box. See whether a quick-release plate is included.

## Getting Smooth Footage

Balance the camera properly, use a walking technique with bent knees, pick the right follow mode and practise before important shoots. Keep batteries charged and carry the right cables.

## Warranty and Service

Check that the warranty is valid in India and that the seller is authorised. Keep the invoice and the serial number safe.

## Quick FAQ

**Do I need a gimbal if my camera has stabilisation?** It helps with larger movements.

**Is a heavier gimbal better?** Only if your camera is heavy.

**Is balancing hard?** It takes a few minutes with practice.

## Balancing Basics

Mount the camera so that its centre of gravity sits on the gimbal's axes, then adjust the tilt, roll and pan arms until the camera stays level when you let go. Newer gimbals have auto-lock and quick-release plates that speed this up. Rebalance after changing lenses or adding a microphone, and re-run any automatic calibration when the manual suggests.
