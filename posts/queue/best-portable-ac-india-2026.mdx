The best portable AC in India is the Voltas Portable AC (1 ton, ₹25,000–₃₂,000) or LG portable unit — but the honest recommendation first: portable ACs are significantly less efficient than split ACs, cost more per BTU of cooling, and are louder; they make sense only for renters who genuinely cannot drill walls for a split AC installation or need cooling in multiple rooms.

Portable ACs cool less effectively than split ACs of the same rated tonnage because they exhaust hot air through a hose that runs through a window/wall gap — this gap also lets some warm air back in, reducing net cooling efficiency. Expect a portable AC to cool about 20–30% less effectively than a split AC with the same tonnage rating.

## When a Portable AC Makes Sense in India

**Rented apartment, no permission to drill walls:** Portable AC is the only option (besides a window AC, which requires a window opening of specific dimensions).

**Need to cool 2–3 different rooms:** One portable unit can serve multiple rooms by moving it (though moving a 30+ kg unit is not convenient).

**Temporary cooling:** Event venue, temporary office, short-term rental.

**Heritage or listed buildings:** Where structural modifications aren't permitted.

**When it doesn't make sense:** If you own the property and can install a split AC, always choose the split inverter — it's quieter, more efficient, and will cost thousands less in electricity annually.

## Honest Portable AC Limitations in India

**Electricity cost:** Portable ACs typically have 2–3 star efficiency — significantly worse than 5-star split inverter ACs. For 8 hours daily use over 5 summer months, you'll pay ₹3,000–₅,000 more in electricity compared to an equivalent split inverter.

**Noise:** 48–55 dB indoor noise. A split AC's indoor unit is 19–28 dB. Difference is clearly audible.

**Cooling effectiveness:** In Indian summers (42°C+), portable ACs struggle to cool beyond 5–7°C below ambient in poorly insulated rooms.

**Weight:** 25–35 kg. "Portable" is relative — moving between rooms requires significant effort.

## Better Alternatives to Portable AC

**Window AC (₹18,000–₂₅,000):** If you have a window of the right size, a window AC is significantly better than portable — quieter, more efficient, and cheaper. Most Indian windows accommodate standard window AC dimensions.

**Mini-split with no-drill brackets:** Some apartments allow mounting split AC outdoor units on balcony railings with special brackets, avoiding drilling the building exterior.

**Recommendation:** If at all possible, choose a window AC over a portable AC — better cooling, lower electricity cost, less noise. Portable AC only if window AC is also impossible.
