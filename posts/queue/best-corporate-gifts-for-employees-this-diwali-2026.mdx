## How to Choose Corporate Gifts

Start with headcount and budget per person, then work backwards. A gift that costs ₹800 per employee across 100 people is an ₹80,000 order, so confirm stock and delivery before committing to a specific product.

Keep gifts uniform. Different values for different grades can create friction, so a single gift for everyone, with an optional upgrade for long-service staff, is usually the fairest approach.

Plan logistics early. Festive delivery slots fill quickly, so place bulk orders well ahead of Diwali, ask about GST invoicing for company accounts, and check whether items can be delivered to multiple office locations. Keep a small buffer of extra units for damaged or late-joining employees.

## Quick FAQ

**Can we get a GST invoice for a bulk purchase?** Amazon Business is designed for organisational buying and supports GST invoicing for eligible purchases. Check the current terms and eligibility on the listing or in your Amazon Business account before you order.

**How far ahead should we order?** For anything above a few dozen units, order at least two to three weeks before Diwali. Festive demand pushes delivery times out, and seller stock can run low on popular items.

**Should gifts be identical for everyone?** Usually yes. A uniform gift avoids comparison, and you can recognise long-serving staff with an additional token rather than a different main gift.

**What about remote employees?** Gift cards or a courier to individual addresses work best. Collect delivery addresses early and allow extra days for smaller towns.
