## What young people actually want

Teens and early twenties value things that fit their routines: music on the commute, late-night gaming, study sessions, and looking after fitness. They notice brands and compatibility, and they dislike gifts that look like parental lessons, such as a self-help book. Aim for gear they can show friends.

Sony's WH-CH720N or JBL Tune series, roughly ₹4,000 to ₹10,000, give 30 hours or more of battery, comfortable fit and some noise cancelling on certain models. They suit students in noisy hostels. Cheaper options exist but often sound harsh. Browse [Sony WH-CH720N](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=Sony+WH-CH720N+headphones&tag=adifystore-21).

### 2. True wireless earbuds

OnePlus Buds, Nothing Ear or Samsung Galaxy Buds, roughly ₹3,000 to ₹12,000, work for commuting and sport. Check compatibility with their phone for extra features. See [wireless earbuds](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=wireless+earbuds+ANC&tag=adifystore-21).

An Xbox wireless controller, roughly ₹5,000 to ₹6,500, works on PC, Android and Xbox. For PC gamers, a gaming mouse from Logitech or Razer, roughly ₹2,000 to ₹6,000, makes a visible difference. A gaming headset with a microphone, roughly ₹3,000 to ₹8,000, helps teammates hear them. A handheld gaming PC is far more expensive and best left to adults with existing libraries. Check [gaming headset](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=gaming+headset+with+microphone&tag=adifystore-21).

## Setting boundaries kindly

A gift is also a chance for a conversation about screen time. A fitness band or a study tablet can pair with a shared plan about sleep and homework. Avoid giving a gaming console to a teen who struggles with exams unless the rules are clear. For young adults, respect their taste and ask a friend to confirm before spending.

## Gifts to avoid

Avoid cheap knock-off earbuds, unbranded smartwatches and any tech that locks into a platform the person does not use. Avoid giving the wrong console games without checking age ratings.

## Who should skip a big tech gift

Skip an expensive gift if the person already owns a good version, or if the family has a plan to buy it for exams or college. Skip a phone as a gift unless you are certain of the model.
