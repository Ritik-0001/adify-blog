## What Programmers Need in a Monitor

**Size:** Minimum 22 inches. 24 inches recommended for comfortable multi-pane IDE use.

**Resolution:** 1080p at 24" or QHD (2560x1440) at 27" for more vertical code lines visible.

**Panel:** IPS over TN — code editors are viewed from slightly off-axis at a desk, and IPS maintains clarity at wider angles.

**Refresh rate:** 60–75Hz is entirely adequate for programming. Don't pay a premium for 144Hz+ for coding work.

**Eye care:** Blue light filter and flicker-free backlight — developers typically spend 8–10 hours daily in front of monitors.

**Ports:** HDMI is essential. USB-C pass-through from laptop is a useful feature at higher budgets.
