## Home Office Monitor Priorities

**Size:** 24–27 inches. Below 24" is cramped for multi-window work; above 27" is difficult to use without moving your head constantly.

**Resolution:** 1080p at 24" is acceptable; 1440p (QHD) at 27" is significantly better for productivity-heavy work.

**Panel:** IPS mandatory. TN panels produce inaccurate flesh tones in video calls and color shift from off-axis.

**Connectivity:** HDMI for laptop; DisplayPort or USB-C for docking; dual inputs if connecting two devices.

**Ergonomics:** Height adjustment + tilt + swivel is the ideal stand. Prevents neck and shoulder strain across 8-hour workdays.

**Eye care:** Flicker-free and blue light mode for extended daily use.
