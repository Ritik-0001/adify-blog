## Wall-Mounted Standing Desk Option

For extreme space constraints (less than 80cm of available wall width), wall-mounted folding desks (₹4,000–₈,000) fold flat against the wall when not in use. They cannot be standing-height adjustable but can be installed at standing height as a fixed-height workspace. Useful in bedrooms where the desk is only used for specific tasks.

## Cable Management in Compact Setups

Compact desks in small apartments are often near walls with limited cable routing options. A compact cable management tray (₹400–₇₀₀) under the tabletop keeps power cables from the floor — important in electric desks whose cables move as height changes.
