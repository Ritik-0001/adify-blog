## Do You Actually Need 10kg?

**Joint families (8+ members):** 10kg reduces the number of weekly loads meaningfully compared to 7-9kg machines.

**Households regularly washing heavy comforters or winter bedding:** 10kg drums accommodate these bulky items without overloading.

**Guest-accommodating households (frequent extended family visits):** Occasional large laundry volumes are handled without splitting into multiple loads.

**Standard families of 4-5:** 7-9kg is typically sufficient — 10kg may mean running partial loads inefficiently for typical weekly laundry.
