## What steam does that a normal oven cannot

A convection oven dries the surface of food. A combi-steam oven adds water vapour, which is why it can revive day-old parathas, bake bread with a proper crust, steam fish or momos without a pot, and reheat dal-chawal so it tastes closer to fresh. For an Indian household that cooks daily and reheats a lot, that reheating job is the real everyday use, more than the glamorous baking.

There are two designs. A pure steam oven only steams, usually at 100 degrees Celsius or a little above, and costs less. A combi-steam oven mixes steam with hot-air baking, grill or convection modes. If you also want to bake, buy the combi type. Pure steamers are a niche purchase that mostly suits people who already own a separate oven.

## The water tank and Indian hard water

Most built-in steam ovens in this segment use a refillable tank, often around 1 litre or a little more, and a 30 to 40 minute steaming session can use most of it. Plumbed-in water connections exist on premium models but are rare in Indian flats.

Hard water is the real running problem. Tap water with high TDS leaves limescale on the steam generator, and descaling is a monthly or two-monthly job in many cities. Use filtered RO water where you can, run the descale programme when the oven prompts, and keep descaling tablets or citric-acid solution recommended by the brand on hand. Ignoring this is the most common reason steam ovens develop faults after the first year. [Descaling products for steam ovens](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=oven+descaling+tablets&tag=adifystore-21).

## Power, niche and installation

Many built-in ovens draw around 2.5 to 3 kW at peak, so ask your electrician for a dedicated 16 A line with its own MCB, not a shared socket from the chimney point. Keep the ventilation gaps the manual specifies, which are normally at the back and top of the niche.

Check the door swing and handle clearance against the adjacent drawer, and confirm the height from the floor. Putting a steam oven at eye level is more comfortable and safer with hot trays than putting it under the hob. ## Who should skip a built-in steam oven

If you mostly bake cakes and microwave leftovers, a good convection microwave at ₹15,000 to 25,000 does most of what you need, and a freestanding countertop steam oven gives you steaming without a niche. A built-in unit also commits you to a wall cut-out, so it makes little sense for rented homes. If your kitchen has no spare niche and your budget is under ₹60,000, a combination of an air fryer and a pressure cooker is the more honest answer.
