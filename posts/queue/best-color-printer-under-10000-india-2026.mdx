## Why Ink Tank Wins Under ₹10,000

A cartridge color printer at ₹4,000–₹5,000 sounds like a bargain. But the math turns against you quickly:

A standard HP or Canon ink cartridge set (black + color) for an entry-level printer costs ₹800–₹1,200 and prints about 150–200 color pages. That's ₹5–₹8 per color page. If you print 100 color pages per month, you're spending ₹6,000–₹9,600 per year on ink alone.

The Epson L3252 ink set costs ₹600–₹700 and prints 4,500 color pages. That's ₹0.15 per color page. 100 pages/month = ₹180/year in ink.

The ₹4,000–₹5,000 premium for the Epson L3252 pays itself back within the first 2–3 months of regular use.
