## How to Choose a Premium Laptop

Decide on the operating system. macOS offers a polished, efficient experience, and Windows gives more choice and compatibility with specific software.

Check the display. Premium laptops often offer OLED or high-resolution IPS screens. Look at brightness, colour coverage and refresh rate.

Look at build, battery and warranty. Aluminium or magnesium builds, all-day battery and a strong warranty justify the higher price.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Paying for specs you will not use.** Match the laptop to your work.

**Ignoring RAM on a MacBook.** Choose 16GB.

**Overlooking ports.** Check what you need.

**Skipping accidental damage cover.** Consider it for premium machines.

## Real-World Scenarios

**A creative professional.** MacBook Air M3 or a Zenbook OLED.

**A business traveller.** A thin, light laptop with long battery life.

**A Samsung phone user.** A Galaxy Book integrates well.

## What to Check on the Listing

Confirm the processor, RAM, storage, display and warranty. See whether accidental damage cover is offered.

## Quick FAQ

**Is a premium laptop worth it?** For daily heavy use, build and display quality matter.

**MacBook or Windows?** Choose based on software and preference.

**How long do they last?** Five years or more.

## What Premium Buys You

At this price you are paying for build quality, display, speakers, battery life and a better keyboard and trackpad, more than raw performance. A ₹1,20,000 laptop and a ₹70,000 laptop can feel similar for browsing and office work, but the premium model is more pleasant to use for eight hours a day, and typically lasts longer.

## Extended Cover and Accessories

Consider accidental damage cover, a good sleeve and a compact GaN charger. If you work at a desk, a monitor and a USB-C dock turn a thin laptop into a full workstation, and the total cost is worth including in your budget.
