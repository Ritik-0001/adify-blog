## How to Choose a Gift Under ₹10,000

Start with the relationship. Close friends and relatives can spend near the top of the range, while colleagues and acquaintances can choose items at the lower end.

Pick useful over trendy. Items that are used every day, such as cookware or a mixer grinder, are remembered longer than novelty gadgets.

Check delivery and returns. Choose a seller with easy returns and ship to the couple's home, with a gift message.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Buying a duplicate.** Ask a family member what they have.

**Ignoring colour and size.** Choose neutral options for bedding.

**Forgetting the invoice.** Include a gift receipt for exchange.

**Buying unknown brands.** Choose reliable makers.

## Real-World Scenarios

**A friend at a tight budget.** A cookware set or a mixer grinder.

**A relative who wants something modern.** An air fryer.

**A colleague.** A dinner set or an electric kettle combo.

## Quick FAQ

**Is a gift under ₹10,000 enough?** Yes, a thoughtful practical gift is appreciated.

## Personalising a Budget Gift

A gift under ₹10,000 feels bigger when you add a personal touch. Pair a cookware set with a handwritten recipe card, add a small box of gourmet tea to a kettle combo, or choose bedding in colours you know the couple likes. A short note about why you chose the gift makes even a simple item memorable.

## What to Check on the Listing

Read the seller rating and the return window, since wedding gifts sometimes need to be exchanged. Confirm the number of pieces in a cookware or dinner set, the material and whether it is induction compatible, and check the warranty on electrical items. Reviews often mention packaging, which matters for glassware and crockery.

## Delivery and Timing Tips

Order at least a week before the wedding so that delays do not leave you empty-handed, and choose delivery to the couple's home or a trusted family member. If you are sending gifts from another city, mark the parcel as a gift and add a message on the order, so it arrives ready to be opened.

## Recommendation

For most couples, an air fryer or a mixer grinder is the safest pick. Add a cookware set if you want to give a fuller kitchen start. Compare specifications on each listing and keep the invoice.
