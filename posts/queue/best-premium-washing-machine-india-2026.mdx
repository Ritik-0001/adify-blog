## What Justifies Premium Pricing

**i-DOS automatic detergent dosing:** Sensors detect load weight and fabric type, automatically dispensing the precise detergent amount needed — eliminates both under-dosing (poor cleaning) and over-dosing (wasted detergent, fabric residue).

**Superior vibration and noise control:** Premium build materials and engineering reduce operational noise and vibration significantly compared to mainstream models — genuinely noticeable in apartments with thin walls.

**Longer, more thorough warranty coverage:** Premium models typically include more comprehensive parts and labor coverage beyond just the motor warranty most brands offer.

**Smart app integration depth:** Beyond basic remote start/stop, premium smart features include detailed cycle customization, maintenance alerts, and detergent/fabric softener level monitoring.
