## What Justifies Premium Soundbar Pricing

**Genuine height channels:** Premium soundbars include actual upward-firing drivers (not just processing simulation) that bounce sound off your ceiling to create real overhead audio positioning for Dolby Atmos content.

**Room calibration technology:** Sophisticated microphone-based tuning adjusts audio output to compensate for your specific room's size, furniture, and acoustic properties — genuinely improves sound accuracy beyond generic factory tuning.

**Driver count and quality:** More individual drivers, each precisely tuned for specific frequency ranges, deliver more detailed and accurate sound reproduction than fewer, larger generic drivers.
