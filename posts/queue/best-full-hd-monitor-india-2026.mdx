## Full HD Monitor Size Guide — India 2026

| Size | Resolution | Pixel Density | Best For |
|------|-----------|---------------|----------|
| 19.5" | 1920x1080 | 113 PPI | Tight desk space, minimum budget |
| 21.5" | 1920x1080 | 102 PPI | Standard home use |
| 24" | 1920x1080 | 94 PPI | Sweet spot for productivity |
| 27" | 1920x1080 | 82 PPI | Large screen, slightly soft text |

At 27 inches, 1080p pixel density (82 PPI) starts to show slight softness in text. For productivity work with lots of reading, 24" is the better choice at 1080p. For gaming and video content, 27" 1080p is acceptable.

## Full HD vs QHD — Is It Worth Upgrading?

QHD (2560x1440) at 27 inches gives 109 PPI — significantly sharper than 1080p. In India, QHD 27" monitors start at ₹14,000–₁₈,000. Worth upgrading if:
- You read and write code (more lines visible)
- Your GPU supports QHD output (all modern discrete GPUs and integrated M-series Mac chips do)
- Budget allows ₹15,000+

At ₹10,000–₁₂,000, Full HD remains the best choice — the QHD monitors at this price are from less reliable brands with worse panels.
