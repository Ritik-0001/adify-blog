## What College Students Need

**Battery life:** College schedules span 6–8 hours. A 4-hour battery means lugging a charger everywhere. Minimum 8 hours, ideally 12.

**Weight:** 1.5–1.8kg is the college sweet spot. Above 2kg becomes genuinely tiring to carry on campus.

**16GB RAM:** Multiple browser tabs (20–30), Zoom, Google Docs, Canva, and occasional YouTube simultaneously requires 16GB. 8GB is limiting.

**Display:** 15.6" is ideal balance — large enough for spreadsheets and code, not too large to carry.
