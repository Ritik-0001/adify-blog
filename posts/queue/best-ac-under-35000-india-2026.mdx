## AC Buying Guide for Indian Homes

**Capacity formula for Indian rooms:**
- Under 100 sq ft: 0.75 Ton
- 100–150 sq ft: 1.0 Ton
- 150–200 sq ft: 1.5 Ton
- 200–250 sq ft: 2.0 Ton

Add 0.25 Ton for west/southwest-facing rooms that receive afternoon sun.

**Star rating:** 5-star is always worth the premium in India — energy savings pay back the price difference in 2–3 years.

**Installation:** AC installation cost is separate from purchase — ₹1,500–₂,500 including copper pipe run up to 3 metres. Additional ₹200–₃₀₀/metre for longer pipe runs.

**Diwali discount:** ACs are unusual in the festive season — typically more aggressive discounts on remaining stock before winter. Exchange offers of ₹3,000–₅,000 on old ACs may apply.

**Recommendation:** LG PS-Q19YNZF for most Indian buyers — best combination of energy efficiency, AI features, and 10-year compressor warranty at the most competitive price. Daikin if long-term reliability over 15–20 years is the priority.
