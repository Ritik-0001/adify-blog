## What Actually Matters for a Student Phone

**Battery capacity over camera megapixels.** A 5,000mAh+ battery matters more day-to-day than an extra 10MP on the camera sensor for most student use cases.

**Storage headroom.** 128GB should be the realistic minimum given app sizes, lecture recordings, and photos accumulating over a full academic year.

**Software update commitment.** Samsung and Motorola generally commit to longer update cycles than budget-focused rivals, worth factoring in if you want the phone to last through your full degree.
