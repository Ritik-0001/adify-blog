## How to Choose a Double Door Refrigerator

Pick capacity by family size: 200 to 250 litres for three people, 250 to 300 for four to five. A fridge that is too large wastes power and space.

Look at compressor type and warranty. Inverter compressors adjust speed and tend to be quieter and more efficient, and it is worth reading the compressor warranty terms.

Check shelf layout and dimensions. Vegetable drawer size, door bins and shelf height matter in daily use, and the fridge must fit your space with clearance for ventilation.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Ignoring ventilation.** A fridge crammed into a niche cannot release heat and runs harder.

**Overlooking the doorway.** Measure the path from the entrance to the kitchen.

**Buying on star rating alone.** Also look at the warranty, service network and shelf layout.

## Quick FAQ

**How many stars should I choose?** Three stars is common at this price. Higher ratings can save power over time.

**Is a convertible freezer useful?** It helps during festivals or when you need extra fridge space.

**How long do fridges last?** Ten years or more is common with proper care.

## Running Costs and Ownership

A 3-star inverter double door typically consumes a moderate amount of electricity, and the difference between 3-star and 4- or 5-star models shows up on the annual bill rather than the price tag. Keep the fridge away from direct heat, leave clearance at the back, and avoid keeping the door open for long. Defrosting is rarely needed on frost-free models, but the gasket and shelves should be wiped regularly.

Check the warranty on both the product and the compressor, keep the invoice, and register the appliance with the brand if it offers an app. Service response times in your city are worth checking before you buy.

## What to Check on the Listing

Look at the usable capacity of the fridge and freezer separately, the number of shelves and whether the top shelf is tall enough for your bottles. Check the cooling type, star rating and the compressor warranty, and see whether the price includes delivery and installation.
