## Front Load vs Top Load — Which for India?

**Front load washing machines:**
- Better wash quality (tumble action vs agitator)
- Lower water consumption (30–40% less water per cycle)
- More energy efficient
- Gentler on fabrics — better for silk, wool, Indian synthetic fabrics
- Higher upfront cost
- Longer cycle time (45–90 mins vs 30–45 min top load)
- Door at front — must bend to load/unload

**Top load washing machines:**
- Lower upfront cost
- Faster cycle time
- Can add clothes mid-cycle
- Easier to load/unload standing
- Higher water consumption
- Slightly harder on fabrics than front load

**For Indian households:** Front load is generally recommended for the water savings (significant in cities with water supply issues), fabric care (Indian fabrics often include delicate silks, handlooms), and long-term electricity savings.
