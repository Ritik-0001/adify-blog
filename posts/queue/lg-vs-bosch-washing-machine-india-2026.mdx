## How to Choose Between LG and Bosch

Start with your budget. LG has more choices at lower and middle prices, while Bosch concentrates on mid to premium models.

Think about priorities. If you want features and wide service coverage, LG is a strong choice, and if you want build and quiet motors, Bosch is worth a look.

Compare specifications. Check capacity, spin speed, motor warranty and energy rating on the exact models.

## Setting It Up

Place the machine on a level, firm floor, remove the transit bolts as per the manual and leave the door ajar after use to prevent odours. Clean the filter and detergent drawer regularly.

## Water and Electricity Use

Front-loaders from both brands use less water than most top-loaders, and star ratings give a rough idea of energy use. In areas with limited water, this matters over years. Ask whether the model has a hot wash option, since heating water adds to power use, and check if it suits your supply.

## Dealing With Hard Water

Many Indian cities have hard water that leaves scale on drums and heaters. Look for models with a hard water or drum clean program, use a descaling agent occasionally and consider a water softener if scaling is severe. Good service access helps if parts need replacing.

## Who Should Skip This

If your home is on a top floor without a lift and the machine has to be carried up narrow stairs, the heavier premium front-loaders from either brand are hard to deliver and position, so check access first. And if you wash mainly small, light loads and are on a tight budget, a simpler semi-automatic or top-loader is far cheaper to buy and fix.

## Recommendation

If you want a wide range and features, shortlist LG. If you want durable, quiet running, shortlist Bosch. Check the motor warranty and installation terms on the exact model before ordering.
