## Installation and Care

Place the machine on a level, firm floor, remove the transit bolts as per the manual and leave the door ajar after use to prevent odours. Clean the filter and detergent drawer regularly.

## Water and Electricity Use

Front-loaders from both brands use less water than most top-loaders, and star ratings give a rough idea of energy use. In areas with limited water, this matters over years. Ask whether the model has a hot wash option, since heating water adds to power use, and check if it suits your supply.

## Dealing With Hard Water

Many Indian cities have hard water that leaves scale on drums and heaters. Look for models with a hard water or drum clean program, use a descaling agent occasionally and consider a water softener if scaling is severe. Good service access helps if parts need replacing.
