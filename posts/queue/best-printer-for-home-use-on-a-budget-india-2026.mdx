## Tips for Printing on a Budget

**Print draft mode:** Most inkjet printers have a "draft" or "economy" mode that uses 30–50% less ink at the cost of slightly lighter output. For internal notes and drafts, draft mode is unnoticeable.

**Print in black and white when color isn't needed:** Set your default to "grayscale" in printer settings. Color ink is more expensive than black — use it only when color actually adds value.

**Paper:** Don't skimp on paper. Cheap thin paper causes more paper jams, requires printer maintenance more often, and ink bleeds through lightweight paper. Standard 70–75 GSM paper (JK Copier, ITC, Bilt) is adequate and costs ₹180–₂₂₀ per 500-sheet ream.
