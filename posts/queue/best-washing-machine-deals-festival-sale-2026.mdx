## Front-Load vs Top-Load: What to Consider

**Choose front-load** if you care about fabric longevity, lower water consumption, and plan to stack a dryer on top in a compact space.

**Choose top-load** if upfront cost and simple, faster wash cycles matter more than the marginal fabric-care benefits of front-load washing.

**Match capacity to household size** — 6-7kg suits 2-3 people, while families of 4-5 should look at 7.5-8kg capacity to avoid overloading and reduced wash quality.
