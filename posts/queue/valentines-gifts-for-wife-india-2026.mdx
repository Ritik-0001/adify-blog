## Start with her, not the occasion

The strongest gifts answer a specific need or taste: something she mentioned wanting, something that saves her time or something that celebrates a shared memory. A mid-priced item chosen with care beats an expensive generic one. If she values practicality, a useful gift with a heartfelt note works. If she loves surprise, add a small romantic element.

## Experiences and small touches

A dinner you cook, a weekend away, a spa day or tickets to a concert matter as much as an object. Pair any gift with fresh flowers and a handwritten card. Take over a task she dislikes for a week. An [open-when letters set](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=love+letters+gift+box+for+wife&tag=adifystore-21) is a sweet touch.

## Gifts to avoid

Avoid household appliances as the only gift unless she asked. Avoid weight-related products. Avoid gifts that are about your convenience. Avoid last-minute generic hampers.

## Who should skip big-ticket gifts

Skip expensive gifts if they strain the budget; a sincere effort matters more. Skip jewellery if you do not know her taste.
