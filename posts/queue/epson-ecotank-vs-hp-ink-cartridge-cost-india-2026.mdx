## Why HP Still Sells Cartridge Printers

Despite the obvious running cost disadvantage, HP cartridge printers sell because:

**Lower upfront price:** ₹3,499–₅,500 vs ₹8,500 looks like a bigger saving than it is.

**Printer market economics:** HP makes profit on ink cartridges, not the printer itself. The low printer price is a loss leader or thin-margin product; cartridges are high-margin.

**Infrequent use looks cheaper:** If you print fewer than 10 pages per month, cartridges cost ₹70–₹90/month vs ₹1–₹2/month — a small absolute difference. EcoTank doesn't pay off at very low volumes.

---

## When HP Cartridges Are Actually Better

**You print very rarely (under 10 pages/month):** Ink-tank printers can clog if unused for months. At very low volumes, a cartridge printer with regular nozzle-cleaning cycles may be more reliable.

**You only need black and white:** HP LaserJet printers (toner-based, not cartridges) at ₹8,000–₁₂,000 beat EcoTank on per-page black-and-white costs at high volumes.

**You travel frequently with the printer:** Cartridge printers have fewer ink-spill concerns when moving.
