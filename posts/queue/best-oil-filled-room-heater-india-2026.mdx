## Why Oil-Filled Radiators are Best for Indian Winters

**Safety:** Surface stays below 80°C. Curtains, bedding, and toys touching the surface won't catch fire. No exposed heating elements.

**Air quality:** Doesn't burn oxygen or dry the air like fan heaters. Better for asthma, dry skin, and nasal sensitivity common in North Indian winters.

**Silence:** No fan noise — critical for bedrooms. The oil circulates silently by convection.

**Efficiency:** Thermostat cycles the heater on/off to maintain set temperature — uses less electricity over a full night than the rated wattage suggests.

## Fin Count Matters

OFR effectiveness depends on total oil volume and fin surface area:

- **7 fins:** Small rooms under 100 sq ft
- **9 fins:** Rooms 100–130 sq ft
- **11 fins:** Standard Indian rooms 130–180 sq ft
- **13 fins:** Large rooms or open-plan spaces

For a standard Indian bedroom (12x12 feet = 144 sq ft), an 11-fin OFR is correctly sized.
