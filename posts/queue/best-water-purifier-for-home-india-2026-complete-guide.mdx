## Step 1: Test Your Water's TDS Level

**TDS under 200 ppm:** UV-only purification is sufficient — RO would unnecessarily strip beneficial minerals.

**TDS 200-500 ppm:** RO+UV combination is recommended for genuine safety and taste improvement.

**TDS above 500 ppm:** RO+UV+UF is essential — common in North India, Rajasthan, Gujarat, and Haryana bore well or hard water regions.
