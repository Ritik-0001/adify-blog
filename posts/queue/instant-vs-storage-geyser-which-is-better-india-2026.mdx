## 1. Storage Geysers: Best for Bathrooms

Roughly ₹15,000–30,000 for premium models depending on capacity and brand, while basic units cost less. A storage geyser heats a tank and holds the water hot, so several showers can happen without waiting. Thermostats cut power once the set temperature is reached.

Why it works: steady hot water for families. The trade-off is a heating wait at the start, standing heat loss and a larger wall footprint.

## 2. Instant Geysers: Best for Kitchens and Basins

Usually below ₹15,000, so instant geysers sit under this guide's premium focus and suit kitchens and basins. An instant geyser heats water as it flows, usually in a 1 to 3 litre chamber. It is compact and gives hot water in seconds, which suits washing utensils or a basin.

Why it works: quick, small and low standby loss. The trade-off is a limited flow rate, and it is not meant for a full bathroom shower for most models.

## How to Choose Between Instant and Storage

Start with usage. For bathing, storage is almost always the answer, because a shower needs several litres per minute. For a sink, an instant unit saves you from heating a whole tank.

Check electrical capacity. Instant geysers draw high power, often around 3,000 watts, so wiring and the MCB must support it. Storage geysers draw about 2,000 watts for longer.

Look at running cost. A well-insulated storage geyser holds heat for hours, while an instant geyser only draws power while you use it. For short, frequent draws instant units can be cheaper, and for long baths storage units usually win.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Using an instant geyser for a full bath.** Flow rate and wattage usually make this frustrating.

**Ignoring wiring.** Overloaded wires are a safety risk.

**Skipping an installer visit.** Correct pressure valves and earthing matter.

**Leaving a storage geyser on all day.** Use the thermostat and a timer.

## Real-World Scenarios

**A family of four in a flat.** A 25 litre storage geyser in each bathroom.

**A small kitchen with a hand-washing sink.** A small instant geyser under the counter.

**A rental home with limited wall strength.** A compact storage geyser or an instant unit.

## Quick FAQ

**Which saves more electricity?** It depends on use. For short draws, instant units can; for long baths, a well-insulated tank usually does.

**Which lasts longer?** Both can last years with maintenance; the tank anode on storage geysers needs checking.

**Can I use an instant geyser in a bathroom?** For a basin, yes; for a shower, check wattage and flow.
