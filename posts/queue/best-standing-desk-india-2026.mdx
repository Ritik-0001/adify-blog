## Why Use a Standing Desk?

Research from the British Journal of Sports Medicine shows alternating sitting and standing every 30 minutes reduces lower back discomfort by 35% in office workers. Indian tech professionals, who average 9–10 hours at desk daily, report higher benefit rates.

Standing desks do not burn significant calories — that's a common myth. The benefit is postural variety: different muscle groups activate when standing, reducing the static load on the lumbar spine that causes chronic back pain.

## Manual vs Electric Standing Desks

**Manual (crank or pneumatic):** ₹8,000–₁₅,000. You crank a handle to adjust height. Inconvenient — most users stop adjusting and leave it at one height. Not recommended unless budget is very tight.

**Electric:** ₹18,000 upwards. Press a button, desk moves. Convenience is what makes the standing desk useful — if adjustment is friction-filled, you won't alternate.

**Buy electric.** The price premium over manual is worth it because it removes the friction from using the desk as intended.
