## Certified Range vs. Real-World Range

Manufacturer-certified IDC (Indian Driving Cycle) range figures are measured under controlled, ideal conditions — real-world range is typically 20-30% lower, affected by:

- **Riding style:** Aggressive acceleration and higher speeds reduce range meaningfully.
- **Payload:** Rider weight and any additional passenger/cargo reduces range.
- **Terrain:** Uphill riding and stop-start traffic both increase energy consumption.
- **Weather:** Extreme heat or cold affects battery efficiency.

Always plan around realistic range estimates (roughly 70-80% of certified figures) rather than the marketing number when evaluating whether a scooter suits your commute.
