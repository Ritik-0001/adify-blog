## How to Choose a Soundbar Under ₹25,000

Check the ports. HDMI eARC gives the best connection to modern TVs, and an optical input is a useful fallback for older sets.

Look at the subwoofer. A wireless subwoofer adds real bass, and the size and power matter more than the headline wattage.

Think about your room. Smaller rooms suit a simple 2.1 setup, and larger rooms benefit from extra channels or rear speakers.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Buying on watt numbers.** Reviews of sound matter more.

**Ignoring TV compatibility.** Check eARC and ARC support.

**Placing the soundbar in a cabinet.** It muffles sound.

**Skipping the remote setup.** Pair it with the TV remote.

## Real-World Scenarios

**A 43 to 55 inch TV in a bedroom.** A 2.1 soundbar with a subwoofer.

**A family movie room.** A 5.1 or Atmos soundbar.

**A Samsung or LG TV owner.** A same-brand soundbar for easier control.

## What to Check on the Listing

Confirm the channels, the subwoofer type, the HDMI ports and eARC, the supported audio formats, the warranty and what is in the box. See whether wall-mount brackets are included.

## Setting Up for Better Sound

Place the soundbar just below the TV at ear height, keep the subwoofer near a wall or corner for stronger bass and use the dialogue or night mode if you watch late. Run the room calibration if the model has one.

## Warranty and Service

Check the warranty length and whether service is on site. Keep the invoice and the model number for claims.

## Quick FAQ

**Is a soundbar better than TV speakers?** Yes, for dialogue and bass.

**Do I need Dolby Atmos?** Not necessarily at this budget.

**Can I connect it by Bluetooth?** Most models support it.

## Wall Mounting and Cable Tips

Many soundbars include a wall-mount bracket, and mounting directly below the TV keeps the sound aligned with the picture. Use a short HDMI cable for eARC, keep the subwoofer cable-free by plugging it into a nearby socket and tidy the wires with clips. If you place the bar on a stand, make sure it does not block the TV's remote sensor.
