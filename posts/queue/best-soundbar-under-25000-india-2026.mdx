## How to Choose a Soundbar Under ₹25,000

Check the ports. HDMI eARC gives the best connection to modern TVs, and an optical input is a useful fallback for older sets.

Look at the subwoofer. A wireless subwoofer adds real bass, and the size and power matter more than the headline wattage.

Think about your room. Smaller rooms suit a simple 2.1 setup, and larger rooms benefit from extra channels or rear speakers.

## Placement, Mounting and Setup

Place the soundbar just below the TV at ear height, and avoid pushing it inside a cabinet, which muffles the sound. Keep the subwoofer near a wall or corner for stronger bass, and use a dialogue or night mode if you watch late. Many soundbars include a wall-mount bracket, and mounting directly below the TV keeps the sound aligned with the picture. If you use a stand, make sure the bar does not block the TV's remote sensor.

## Pairing With Your TV

A same-brand soundbar often works with the TV remote and unlocks extras, which is why Samsung and LG owners lean toward their own brand. Use a short HDMI cable for eARC, tidy the wires with clips and run the room calibration if the model has one. Check the warranty length and whether service is on site.

## Who Should Skip This

If your living room is small and you watch mostly news and soaps, the TV's built-in speakers with dialogue enhancement may be enough, and a bar adds clutter without much benefit. If you want real surround from rear speakers or a large room, the upper end of this budget may feel limited and a full home theatre could suit you better.

## Recommendation

For most rooms, a 2.1 or 3.1 soundbar with a wireless subwoofer from Sony, Samsung or LG is a strong upgrade under ₹25,000.
