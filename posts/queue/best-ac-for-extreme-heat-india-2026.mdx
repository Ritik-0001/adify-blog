## Why Standard ACs Struggle in Extreme Heat

As outdoor ambient temperature rises, the AC compressor must work against a larger temperature differential to achieve the same indoor cooling — beyond certain thresholds, standard compressors experience reduced efficiency, longer cooling times, and in extreme cases, thermal protection shutoffs that temporarily halt cooling entirely. Tropical-rated ACs use components (compressor lubricants, refrigerant formulations, electronics cooling) specifically engineered to avoid these limitations.

## Additional Tips for Extreme Heat Regions

**Ensure proper outdoor unit placement:** Direct sun exposure on the outdoor unit reduces efficiency — shade or a protective cover (that doesn't block airflow) helps maintain performance during peak heat.

**Consider slightly oversizing capacity:** In regions regularly exceeding 45°C, sizing up one tonnage level from standard recommendations provides headroom that prevents units from running constantly at maximum capacity.
