## How to Choose a Treadmill Under ₹40,000

Check the belt size. A belt of at least 42 cm by 120 cm is comfortable for most people when jogging, while narrower belts are fine for walking.

Look at the motor and warranty. A continuous rating of 1.5 to 2 HP suits regular jogging, and a motor warranty of a few years gives peace of mind.

Check the weight limit. Choose a machine with a weight rating well above your own for a stronger frame.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Buying on peak HP.** Check continuous HP.

**Ignoring the belt size.** Taller users need longer belts.

**Skipping reviews on service.** Read city-specific feedback.

**Using it without a mat.** A mat reduces noise.

## Real-World Scenarios

**A beginner who walks daily.** A simple folding model.

**A couple who jogs.** A model with a wider belt and a better motor.

**A small flat.** A compact, quiet model with a mat.

## What to Check on the Listing

## Treadmill Safety Basics

Always clip the safety key to your clothing before starting, keep the belt clear of loose items and use shoes with a good grip. Begin at a slow speed, hold the handrails while stepping on and avoid jumping on at high speed. Keep children and pets away from a running treadmill, and unplug the machine when it is not in use for long periods.

## Making the Most of a Mid-Range Treadmill

Mix steady walks with short interval blocks, such as one minute of jogging and two minutes of walking, and gradually shift the ratio as your fitness improves. Track your weekly total rather than a single session, and use the preset programs on your console to avoid boredom. Consistency matters more than intensity in the first few months.

## Budget Tips for This Price Band

Set aside a little extra for a thick rubber mat and for a stabiliser if your home has voltage swings. Compare the warranty on the motor separately from the warranty on parts, and prefer a seller that arranges installation. Buying a slightly more expensive model with a longer belt and a better motor warranty usually costs less over several years than replacing a weak machine.
