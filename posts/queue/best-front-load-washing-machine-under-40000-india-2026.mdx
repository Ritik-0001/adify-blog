## Why Front Load Over Top Load

**Wash quality:** Front loaders tumble clothes through a smaller amount of water repeatedly, providing more thorough mechanical cleaning than top loaders' agitator or pulsator-based washing.

**Water and detergent efficiency:** Front loaders typically use 40-50% less water per cycle than comparable-capacity top loaders.

**Fabric care:** Gentler tumbling action causes less wear on clothes over time compared to top-load agitators.

**Trade-offs:** Longer cycle times (60-120 minutes vs. 30-45 for top load) and typically higher price for comparable capacity.
