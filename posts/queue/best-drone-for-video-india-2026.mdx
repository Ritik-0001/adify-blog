## Video Specifications That Matter for Aerial Content

**4K/60fps:** Provides smooth 4K motion at 60 frames per second. Can be slowed down 2.5x for 24fps slow motion playback. Essential for wedding, sports, and action aerial content.

**4K/100fps:** Slow motion at 4K resolution — slows down 4x for cinematic 24fps playback. Available on Mini 4 Pro. Rare at this price point.

**10-bit D-Log M:** Log colour profile records wider dynamic range than standard video — preserves highlight detail (bright sky) and shadow detail (dark foreground) simultaneously. Enables professional colour grading in DaVinci Resolve or Premiere Pro. 8-bit video (standard) limits colour grading range significantly.

**Bitrate:** Higher bitrate = more data per frame = more detail. Mini 4 Pro records at 150Mbps maximum — good for colour grading. Compare to DJI Mini 3 at 100Mbps.

**Stabilisation:** 3-axis gimbal on all DJI drones provides smooth footage. EIS (Electronic Image Stabilisation) supplements gimbal for high-wind conditions.
