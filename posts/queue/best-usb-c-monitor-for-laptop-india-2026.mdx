## How to Choose a USB-C Monitor

Check the USB-C port on your laptop. It must support DisplayPort alternate mode or Thunderbolt for video, and some laptops charge but cannot output video over USB-C.

Look at power delivery. A monitor with 65 to 96 watts power delivery can charge most laptops, and a higher wattage suits larger laptops.

Think about the hub and resolution. A built-in USB hub with ports for a keyboard and mouse simplifies the desk, and 4K at 27 inches is sharp for text and photos.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Assuming every USB-C port outputs video.** Check the laptop specs.

**Ignoring power delivery.** Match your laptop's charger.

**Using a poor cable.** Use the supplied or a certified cable.

**Skipping colour needs.** Check coverage for creative work.

## Real-World Scenarios

**A MacBook or Windows laptop user at a desk.** A 27 inch 4K USB-C monitor.

**A creative professional.** A colour-accurate USB-C monitor.

**A traveller.** A portable USB-C monitor.

## What to Check on the Listing

Confirm the screen size, the resolution, the panel type, the USB-C power delivery, the ports, the stand adjustment and the warranty. See whether a USB-C cable is included.

## Setting Up a One-Cable Desk

Plug the monitor into power, connect the USB-C cable to the laptop and plug the keyboard, mouse and other peripherals into the monitor's hub. Set the display scaling for comfortable text and adjust the stand to eye level.

## Warranty and Service

Check the warranty length, the dead pixel policy and service availability. Keep the invoice and serial number.

## Quick FAQ

**Will it charge my laptop?** If the power delivery is high enough.

**Do I need 4K?** Helpful at 27 inch for sharpness.

**Are hubs reliable?** Good ones are.

## Cables, Docks and Compatibility

Use the cable supplied with the monitor or a certified USB-C cable rated for video and power, because cheap cables can limit resolution or charging. If your laptop has only one USB-C port, remember that the monitor will use it for video, power and data together. Check whether your laptop supports DisplayPort over USB-C, since some budget laptops do not.

## Final Checklist Before You Buy

Before you order, check that your laptop or PC has the right ports for the resolution and refresh rate you want, measure your desk for the width and stand depth and confirm the cable included. Read the warranty and the dead pixel policy, and look at reviews for colour accuracy and brightness if you do creative work.
