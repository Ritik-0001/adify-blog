## Olympic versus standard

Olympic means sleeves of 50 mm diameter, taking plates with a 50 mm hole. Standard bars have 25 or 28 mm sleeves and take smaller plates. Olympic bars rotate better, carry more weight and let you grow, so for a serious setup, buy Olympic. Do not mix: Olympic plates do not fit standard bars.

## 1. Men's 20 kg Olympic bar

The standard bar is 2.2 m long, weighs 20 kg and has a shaft diameter of 28 mm. Look for a listed capacity of 450 kg or higher and a tensile strength of 150,000 PSI or above. Indian-made bars are usually priced roughly ₹8,000-20,000. Cheaper bars may have poor knurling and loose sleeves. ## Plate types compared

### Cast iron plates

Cheapest, often roughly ₹100-150 per kg, thick for their weight and loud when dropped. They rust without paint or coating and damage floors. Good for a garage with a platform, not for a bedroom.

### Rubber-coated plates

Iron plates with a rubber layer. Roughly ₹150-250 per kg. Quieter and kinder to floors, but not meant for dropping from overhead. [Rubber coated Olympic plates](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=rubber+coated+olympic+weight+plates&tag=adifystore-21).

### Bumper plates

Solid rubber discs made for dropping, essential for Olympic lifts. Thicker per kg, and costlier, around ₹250-400 per kg. They are overkill if you only squat, bench and deadlift. [Olympic bumper plates](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=olympic+bumper+plates+set&tag=adifystore-21).

## How much weight to buy

For most beginners, a 20 kg bar plus 60 to 100 kg of plates is enough for a year or more. A typical starter set includes two each of 20 kg, 10 kg, 5 kg and 2.5 kg, plus a pair of 1.25 kg for small increases. Add collars. Spring collars are cheap but loose, while lock-jaw collars hold better. [Barbell collars lock jaw](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=barbell+collars+lock+jaw&tag=adifystore-21).

## What else you need

A barbell is not useful alone. You need a rack or squat stand, a bench and a floor mat or platform. Budget at least another ₹15,000-30,000 for a sturdy rack. Without a rack, do not squat or bench heavy, because a failed rep without safety arms is dangerous.

## Floor, noise and neighbours

A dropped plate can crack tiles. Use a rubber floor or a plywood platform covered with mats. In flats, avoid dropping the bar and lower under control. Heavy loads on an upper floor are usually fine but spread the weight with a thick mat.

## Who should skip Olympic equipment

If you train with moderate weights and want variety, adjustable dumbbells are far simpler and safer without a spotter. If your budget is under ₹25,000 in total, start with dumbbells and a bench, and add a barbell later.

## Knurling and sleeve spin

Two small details separate a good bar from a bad one. Medium knurling grips the palm without tearing skin during high-rep deadlifts, while aggressive knurling can shred hands. And the sleeves should spin freely with bushings or bearings, which matters most for cleans and snatches and less for squats and presses.
