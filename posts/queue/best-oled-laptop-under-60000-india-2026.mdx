## Why OLED Matters on a Laptop

**Contrast:** OLED can turn individual pixels off completely — true blacks. IPS panels have a backlight that bleeds through even dark pixels (black crush, grey blacks). The difference is immediately obvious side-by-side.

**Colour accuracy:** OLED panels at this price point come factory-calibrated with 100% DCI-P3 coverage. IPS panels under ₹60,000 typically cover 60–72% DCI-P3. For photo/video work, this is significant.

**Eye strain:** OLED's per-pixel lighting reduces the haloing effect around text that IPS backlights create. Lower brightness settings on OLED are more comfortable for night use.

**HDR:** True HDR requires per-pixel control. OLED delivers genuine HDR; most IPS "HDR" certifications are marketing.

**Trade-offs:** OLED has lower peak brightness than top IPS panels (harder to use outdoors). Burn-in risk with static elements over very long periods (less of a concern for laptops than phones). Slightly shorter battery life (each lit pixel consumes power).
