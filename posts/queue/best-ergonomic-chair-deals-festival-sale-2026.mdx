## What Adjustability Actually Matters

**Adjustable lumbar support (height and depth)** is the single most important feature — it should match your specific lower-back curve, not just add generic padding.

**Seat depth adjustment** matters if you're notably taller or shorter than average — a fixed seat depth that's wrong for your height undermines the rest of the ergonomic design.

**4D armrests** (height, width, depth, angle) reduce shoulder and wrist strain far more than fixed or 2D armrests, especially during long typing sessions.
