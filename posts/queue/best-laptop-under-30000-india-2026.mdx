## What to Avoid Under ₹30,000

**Intel Celeron/Pentium:** Outdated processors from 2015–2018 still sold at budget prices. Avoid — they struggle with modern Windows and Chrome simultaneously.

**4GB RAM:** Technically functional but practically frustrating. 4GB on modern Windows 11 means constant swapping — the OS itself uses 2.5GB idle.

**HDD (spinning hard disk):** Old hard disks make Windows feel broken — 5-minute boot times. SSD is mandatory. Check spec sheet carefully.
