## Should You Buy 22-Inch or 24-Inch?

The price gap between 22-inch and 24-inch has narrowed — the LG 24MK400H (24") costs ₹9,500–₁₁,500 versus LG 22MK400H (22") at ₹7,000–₈,500. That's ₹2,500–₃,000 for 2.5 inches of additional screen space — generally worth paying.

**Choose 22 inches if:** Desk space is limited (below 50cm wide free space), or budget is firm below ₹8,000.

**Choose 24 inches if:** Budget allows ₹9,500–₁₁,500 and desk accommodates it — the larger screen is more useful for everyday productivity.

**Recommendation:** LG 22MK400H for most 22-inch buyers — IPS panel and Reader Mode at ₹7,000–₈,500 is exceptional value. If budget allows ₹9,500+, step up to the LG 24MK400H instead — the extra screen space is worth ₹2,500.
