## What Functions Do You Actually Need?

**Print + Scan + Copy (Basic MFP):** Adequate for most home and small office needs. Available from ₹9,000.

**+ ADF (Automatic Document Feeder):** Essential for offices that regularly scan multi-page documents (contracts, invoices, reports). Without ADF you manually place each page on the flatbed.

**+ Fax:** Still required for communication with government offices, courts, banks, and hospitals in India. Less needed in tech companies.

**+ Duplex (automatic double-sided):** Paper cost savings. Important for offices printing 500+ pages/month.
