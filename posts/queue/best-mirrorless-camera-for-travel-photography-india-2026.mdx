## How to Choose a Travel Camera

Think about size and weight. A smaller camera gets used more, and a light kit with one zoom is easier to carry on long days.

Look at battery and weather. Good battery life and some weather sealing help on trips, and a spare battery and a USB charger add flexibility.

Consider video and stabilisation. In-body stabilisation helps with handheld shots in low light and with video, which suits travel diaries.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Packing too many lenses.** One or two are enough.

**Ignoring battery spares.** Carry at least one.

**Skipping a memory card plan.** Bring fast cards and backups.

**Buying from unauthorised sellers.** Check warranty.

## Real-World Scenarios

**A family traveller.** A Canon EOS R10 or Sony a6700 with a zoom.

**A creator who also films.** A Fujifilm X-S20 or Sony a6700.

**A low-light and street shooter.** A Sony a7C II or a fixed-lens compact.

## What to Check on the Listing

Confirm the model, the kit lens, the warranty, the seller and what is in the box. See whether a memory card, battery and charger are included.

## Packing and Protecting Your Camera

Use a padded sling or a small camera bag, a silica gel pack for humid climates and a lens cloth. Carry a UV or clear filter for dust, and back up photos each evening to a phone, laptop or drive.

## Warranty and Service

Confirm the warranty is valid in India and that service centres exist in your city. Keep the invoice and serial numbers for claims.

## Quick FAQ

**APS-C or full-frame for travel?** APS-C is lighter, and full-frame offers better low light.

**Do I need weather sealing?** Helpful but not essential.

**Is a mirrorless better than a phone?** For zoom, low light and control, yes.
