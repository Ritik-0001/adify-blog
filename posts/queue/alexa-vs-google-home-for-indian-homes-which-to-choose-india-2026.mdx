## Languages: the Indian question

Alexa understands Hindi and Hinglish, and supports a handful of Indian languages with ongoing additions. Google's assistant has traditionally covered a wider range of Indian languages, including Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Marathi, Gujarati, Kannada and Malayalam, and handles code-mixing well.

If your household speaks mainly a regional language, check which assistant covers it for the commands you use, such as controlling lights, playing music and setting alarms. Language support changes, so verify on the current support page.

## Device support in India

Alexa has strong support from Indian brands: Wipro, Philips, Syska, Havells smart devices, Daikin and LG ACs, and many plug and IR controller makers. Setting up routines for lights, fans and ACs is usually straightforward.

Google Home also supports many devices, particularly Android-linked ones and Chromecast. But availability of Google's own speakers in India has been patchier, so check stock before committing.

## Routines and automations

Alexa routines are flexible: schedule lights, trigger actions by voice, motion or time, and chain several devices. Alexa Hunches and location routines work too, with a large catalogue of skills.

Google Home routines are good, especially with phone-based triggers and integrations with Google Calendar. The newer script editor in the app offers advanced control for those who like to tinker.

## Privacy and data

Both assistants listen for a wake word and send requests to the cloud. Both offer controls to review and delete recordings, and mute buttons that cut the microphone. Alexa is tied to your Amazon account and shopping data; Google to your Google account and activity history.

Turn on the physical mute switch at night, review voice history periodically, and avoid placing devices in bedrooms if you are uncomfortable. Disable purchasing by voice or set a PIN, especially with children around.

## Ecosystem fit

If your family uses iPhones, either assistant works through its app, though Apple HomeKit is another route. If you are Android-first and use YouTube Music, Google fits naturally. If you shop on Amazon and use Prime Video, Alexa integrates well, including on Fire TV.

## Music and media

Alexa works with Amazon Music, Spotify, JioSaavn and others. Google works with YouTube Music, Spotify and JioSaavn. Check that your preferred service supports voice play on your device.

## Who should skip a smart assistant

Skip both if you dislike always-listening devices, or if your home Wi-Fi is unreliable. A simple IR remote and timer may serve you better.

## Recommendation

Choose Alexa with a display if you want the widest device support and easy setup in Indian homes. Choose Google Home if your household is Android-first, speaks a regional language that Google covers better, and you can find the hardware in stock. Pick one ecosystem and buy compatible devices.
