## What elderly users really need

Most older people use a tablet for four things: WhatsApp video calls, YouTube and OTT, reading news or bhajans, and photos from family. They do not need power. They need large text, a screen that is easy to see, a loud speaker, a stable app experience and a design that does not change under their feet after updates. A phone-sized interface is the usual cause of frustration, so a tablet of 10 inches or more already solves much of the problem.

## 1. Apple iPad: best for simplicity and long life

The iPad's interface is consistent, and iPadOS 17 and later offer Assistive Access, which swaps the home screen for large icons on a handful of chosen apps such as Messages, Camera, Phone-style FaceTime and Photos. A family member can set it up in minutes. The iPad gets software updates for many years, which matters when it will last a decade. The downsides are the price and a base 64 GB model that fills with photos and WhatsApp media, so consider 256 GB if your parents receive many forwards. Browse [iPad](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=iPad+10th+generation+64GB&tag=adifystore-21).

## 2. Samsung Galaxy Tab A9 Plus: best budget Android

The Galaxy Tab A9 Plus, roughly ₹16,000 to ₹22,000, has an 11-inch screen, decent speakers and an easy mode in One UI that enlarges icons. Samsung's interface is familiar to anyone who has used a Samsung phone. The processor is modest, so heavy multitasking is slow, but for calls and video it is adequate. See [Samsung Galaxy Tab A9 Plus](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=Samsung+Galaxy+Tab+A9+Plus&tag=adifystore-21).

## 3. Samsung Galaxy Tab S9 FE: best step-up Android

At roughly ₹30,000 to ₹40,000, the S9 FE has a brighter, smoother 10.9-inch screen, better speakers and IP68 dust and water resistance, which is genuinely useful if a cup of chai gets spilled on it. It also includes an S Pen, handy for those who prefer to write. Compare [Samsung Galaxy Tab S9 FE](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=Samsung+Galaxy+Tab+S9+FE&tag=adifystore-21).

## 4. Lenovo Tab P12: best for a big, loud screen

The Lenovo Tab P12 has a 12.7-inch display and strong speakers, roughly ₹25,000 to ₹35,000. The larger screen is very comfortable for reading and video calls. It is heavier and rests best on a stand. Look at [Lenovo Tab P12](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=Lenovo+Tab+P12+12.7+inch&tag=adifystore-21).

## Set it up before it arrives

The best gift is a tablet that works the moment it is switched on. Before handing it over, sign in with a family-managed account, install WhatsApp, YouTube and the OTT apps your parents use, set the font size to large, turn on auto-updates at night, and add the family on speed dial. Pin the apps to the home screen. Turn on screen-sharing or remote help apps such as Google Family Link or Apple Screen Time if you need to assist from a distance. Use a [tablet stand for bed or table](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=adjustable+tablet+stand+for+bed&tag=adifystore-21) for long calls.

## Who should skip a tablet

Skip a tablet if your parents are comfortable with a smartphone and mostly want calls, because a phone with a bigger font may suffice. Skip very cheap tablets under ₹10,000, which tend to be slow and poorly supported. Skip tablets for someone with severe hand tremors unless you add a stand and voice features.
