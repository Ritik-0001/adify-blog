## WiFi vs Wired: When Wireless Makes Sense

**Wireless security cameras are ideal when:**
- You rent your home and can't run cables or drill walls
- You need 1–3 cameras for targeted monitoring (front door, main room, parking)
- You want self-installation without a technician
- You're monitoring a temporary location (construction site, vacation home during a stay)

**Wired systems are better when:**
- You need 4+ cameras
- 24/7 continuous recording (cloud costs add up with multiple wireless cameras)
- Maximum reliability (no WiFi dependency)
- Long-term permanent installation

## Essential Accessories for Wireless Cameras

**microSD card:** 32–128GB Class 10 or UHS-I card. Sandisk or Samsung brand recommended for reliability in continuous-write CCTV use.

**WiFi range extender:** TP-Link RE300 or RE200 (₹999–₁,499) if camera placement is at the edge of your WiFi range. Dead zones cause dropped recordings and missed alerts.

**Outdoor mounting bracket:** Most outdoor cameras include a bracket, but an adjustable swivel mount (₹200–₃₀₀) allows precise angle adjustment after installation.

**Recommendation:** Tapo C310 for outdoor wireless, Tapo C210 for indoor monitoring. Both work on the same Tapo app, shareable with family members. For higher resolution indoor monitoring, Xiaomi Mi 2K is worth the slight premium.
