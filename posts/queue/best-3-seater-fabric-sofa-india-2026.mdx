## How to Choose a 3 Seater Sofa

Check the size. A 3 seater is usually 6 to 7 feet wide. Measure your wall and allow walking space.

Look at frame and foam. Solid wood or a strong engineered frame lasts longer, and higher-density foam holds its shape.

Choose the fabric for your lifestyle. Tight weaves and darker colours hide stains, and removable covers make cleaning easier.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Ignoring seat depth.** Deep seats can be uncomfortable for shorter people.

**Choosing fabric by colour alone.** Weave and finish affect durability.

**Overlooking assembly.** Check what is included.

**Skipping reviews.** Customer photos reveal real proportions.

## Real-World Scenarios

**A first flat.** A simple 3 seater from a mainstream brand is a good start, and you can add a side chair later.

**A family room with children.** Choose stain-resistant fabric and a sturdy frame.

**A rented home.** Look for lighter frames that can be moved easily.

## What to Check on the Listing

## Fabric Care and Longevity

Fabric sofas last longer when they are cared for. Vacuum the seat and back weekly to lift dust, rotate loose cushions so they wear evenly and treat spills quickly by blotting rather than rubbing. A fabric protector spray applied when the sofa is new can help resist stains, but test it on a hidden area first.

In dusty cities, a light throw or cover over the seat can extend the life of the upholstery. Keep the sofa away from direct sunlight, which fades fabric, and from radiators or heaters, which dry out foam.

## Sizing Your Space

A 3 seater is typically between 180 and 210 centimetres wide. Leave at least 90 centimetres of walkway, and allow enough space in front for a small centre table. Measure the door and stairwell too, so the sofa can be delivered without being returned.
