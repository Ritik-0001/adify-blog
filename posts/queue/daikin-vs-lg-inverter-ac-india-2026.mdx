Daikin is a specialist in air conditioning and is known for strong cooling and consistent performance in hot conditions. LG's Dual Inverter compressor adjusts speed smoothly and is designed for efficiency and quick cooling.

Both offer 5-star inverter models that save electricity compared with fixed-speed units. In real use, installation quality and room conditions often matter as much as the brand.

## Compressor and Warranty

Both brands typically offer a long compressor warranty on inverter models, often ten years, and a shorter warranty on the product and parts. Read the exact terms on each listing, since they differ by model and seller.

## Features and Smart Control

LG offers AI-based cooling, Wi-Fi control and a range of comfort features on many models. Daikin's mainstream models focus on core performance, with app control on some variants.

If smart features matter, LG has more of them. If you want a simple, dependable AC, Daikin fits.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Ignoring installation quality.** A poor installation reduces cooling.

**Choosing tonnage by price.** An undersized AC works harder and wears faster.

**Not checking copper.** Copper condensers generally last longer.

## Installation and Running Costs

Installation quality affects cooling and the compressor's life. Confirm what is included, the copper pipe length and the outdoor stand. Choose a 5-star inverter if you use the AC many hours a day, set the temperature to a moderate level and clean the filters regularly.

## Warranty and Service

Read the compressor, PCB and product warranty separately, and confirm the service network in your city. Keep the invoice and the installation report safe, since they help with claims.

## What to Check on the Listing

Compare the tonnage, ISEER rating, condenser material and the price for the same star rating. Confirm the compressor, PCB and product warranty, and whether the price includes installation. ## Maintenance and Long-Term Costs

Both brands recommend periodic servicing, including filter cleaning every few weeks in heavy use and a professional service once or twice a year. Skipping maintenance lowers cooling efficiency and raises electricity use. Check the price of annual maintenance contracts, and the availability of spare parts such as PCBs and fan motors.
