## What "Compact" Means for Indian Kitchens

**Standard dishwasher width:** 60cm — often too wide for smaller Indian kitchen layouts.

**Compact/slimline dishwasher width:** 45cm — fits under counters in smaller kitchens while sacrificing some capacity.

**Capacity trade-off:** Compact models typically hold 9-10 place settings versus 12-14 for full-size units — adequate for families of 3-4 but tighter for larger households or frequent entertaining.

Measure your available under-counter space precisely before purchasing — even "compact" 45cm dishwashers require adequate width, depth, and plumbing/electrical access. Many older Indian kitchen designs weren't built with dishwasher installation in mind, so confirming physical fit and utility connection feasibility before purchase prevents costly installation surprises.
