## Why Cartridge Printers Remain So Expensive to Run

Cartridge printers use small, pre-sealed ink cartridges that cost ₹500-₹1,200 each and print only 150-300 pages before needing replacement — the packaging, cartridge mechanism, and lower-volume design all drive up per-page cost compared to bulk ink tank refills.
