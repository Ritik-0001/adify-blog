## How to Choose a Wireless CCTV System

Map your property. Mark gates, doors, blind spots and areas far from the router. Walk with a phone to test signal strength at each planned camera location.

Decide on power. Battery and solar cameras avoid wiring but record on motion, while plugged-in Wi-Fi cameras can record continuously. Weather-resistant ratings such as IP65 or better suit outdoor use.

Think about storage and privacy. MicroSD cards give local storage, and cloud plans add remote backup. Use strong passwords, enable two-factor authentication and keep firmware updated.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Weak Wi-Fi at camera locations.** Test first.

**Ignoring storage.** Confirm what is supported.

**Default passwords.** Change them.

**Pointing cameras at neighbours.** Respect privacy.

## Real-World Scenarios

**A villa with a garden.** Battery or solar cameras cover the garden and gate.

**A villa with a good network.** Wi-Fi cameras and an NVR give continuous recording.

**A villa with blind spots.** Add a mesh Wi-Fi system.

## What to Check on the Listing

Confirm the power method, storage options, weatherproof rating, night vision range and app support. See whether a subscription is needed for key features.

## Quick FAQ

**Are wireless cameras reliable?** They can be, with good signal and power.

**Do they work without internet?** Local recording works, but remote viewing needs internet.

**How long do batteries last?** It varies with activity, from weeks to months.

## Privacy and Security

Change default passwords, enable two-factor authentication and keep firmware updated. Avoid pointing cameras at neighbours' windows or public spaces beyond what is reasonable, and place a notice at the gate if cameras cover the entrance. Review who has access to the app and remove old users.

## Recommendation

For most villas, a mix of Reolink or Ezviz wireless cameras at gates and gardens, plus a strong mesh network, works well. For continuous recording, consider a Wi-Fi NVR kit or a wired PoE system.
