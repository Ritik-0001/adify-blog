## How to Choose for Your Mom

Look at where she spends most of her day. If it is the kitchen, a well-chosen appliance will improve her routine; if it is the living room, entertainment or comfort gifts fit; if she rarely does anything for herself, a personal accessory or a massager gives her permission to relax.

Do not assume she wants a household item. Many mothers feel awkward accepting a personal gift because they think of the family first. Make it clear the gift is for her, with a note that says so.

Check the practical details. Weight, ease of cleaning, service centres and warranty all matter. A heavy appliance she cannot lift or a gadget she cannot operate will disappoint, so keep the choice simple and offer to set it up.

## Quick FAQ

**Is a kitchen appliance a good gift for mom?** It depends on her. If she has mentioned wanting a better mixer or cooker, it is a thoughtful upgrade. If not, she may feel it is a household purchase, so pair it with something purely personal.

**What if she never buys anything for herself?** Choose a gift that is clearly for her, such as a watch, a massager or a Kindle, and say so directly. It helps to add a note that the gift is meant only for her comfort.

**How do I choose the right size of appliance?** Ask about family size and counter space. A 3 litre pressure cooker suits two or three people, while five litres suits larger families. For a mixer, check the number of jars and motor wattage on the listing.

**Should I buy jewellery instead?** Jewellery is very personal and hard to choose online without knowing her taste. A watch or an accessory in a neutral style is a safer online purchase.
