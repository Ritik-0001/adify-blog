## DLP vs. 3LCD for Home Theatre

**DLP (Digital Light Processing):** Single chip with mirror array. High contrast. Vivid, punchy colours. Some users see "rainbow effect" (colour fringing on fast movement) with DLP. Used by BenQ, Optoma, Dangbei.

**3LCD (Three Liquid Crystal Display):** Three separate LCD panels for Red, Green, Blue. No rainbow effect. Accurate colour reproduction. Higher colour brightness relative to white brightness. Used by Epson, Sony.

For Indian home cinema watching Indian content (Bollywood, cricket, OTT), 3LCD projectors like Epson are often preferred for natural skin tone reproduction. For gaming and high-contrast content, DLP is competitive.

A fixed home theatre projector requires a dedicated projection screen (₹5,000–₁₅,000 for 100"–120" screens) or a white-painted wall. Textured walls reduce image quality significantly — invest in either a screen or a painted wall panel.
