## How LG's lineup is organised

LG's range, in broad terms, has three groups. The OLED line (C and G series are the best known globally, B series for entry OLED) uses self-lit pixels. The QNED line adds quantum dots and, on higher models, Mini LED backlighting to an LCD panel. The UHD or UA line is the standard LED 4K at value prices. Series names and numbers change annually, so identify the group and the year.

Prices in India: UHD LED 55 inch around ₹40,000-60,000, QNED 55 to 65 inch around ₹70,000 to 1.5 lakh, OLED 55 to 65 inch from around ₹90,000 to 2 lakh and higher for premium G-type models.

## OLED: where LG leads

LG OLED has been a benchmark for contrast, with true black and a very wide viewing angle, so everyone on the sofa sees the same picture. Gaming features are strong: 120 Hz, four HDMI 2.1 ports on many models, VRR, and low input lag. The Alpha processor handles upscaling well.

The trade-offs: peak brightness is lower than Mini LED in sunny rooms, there is a small risk of burn-in with static content like news tickers if used heavily, and prices are high. For a dim or curtained room, it is the best choice.

## QNED: a brighter alternative

QNED uses LED backlight with quantum dot colour; higher-end versions with Mini LED have more dimming zones. These are brighter than OLED and cost less, making them good for daytime viewing. Contrast is not as deep as OLED, and viewing angles are narrower on some models. Look at higher-numbered QNED models if contrast matters.

## webOS

LG's smart platform, webOS, is fast, with a Magic Remote pointer, a clean home screen and support for the major OTT apps in India. It is generally praised for responsiveness. Check the update policy and the version on the listing. Some people dislike the ads on the home screen, which can be reduced in settings.

## Gaming

LG is a favourite among console and PC gamers, especially with OLED: HDMI 2.1, VRR, G-Sync and FreeSync compatibility, and a Game Optimizer menu. Check which ports support 4K 120 Hz; many LG sets support it on all HDMI ports on higher models. Entry LED models are limited to 60 Hz. OLEDs are thin and wall-mount well but the glass is heavy at 65 inch and above. For delivery and wall mounting, check the lift, the bracket and the VESA pattern. See [lg 77 inch oled tv](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=lg+oled+77+inch+4k+tv&tag=adifystore-21) if you want a larger screen, noting the higher price. For a standard living room, 55 or 65 inch is enough.

## Strengths and trade-offs

Strengths: picture contrast on OLED, excellent gaming features, a smooth interface and wide size choices. Trade-offs: OLED price and brightness, ads in webOS, and entry LED models that lack the processing of higher tiers. Service is widely available in India, though turnaround varies by city.

## Who should skip

Skip OLED if your room is very bright all day or if you leave news channels on for many hours. Skip entry UHD LED if you are buying LG for picture quality, since it lacks the processing and panel of the higher tiers. Skip very large sizes if your viewing distance is short.

## Recommendation

For dark rooms, movies and gaming: LG OLED in 55 or 65 inch. For bright living rooms at a lower price: LG QNED. For basic viewing on a budget: a UHD LED with webOS. Check series, year and ports before buying.
