## Dual Monitor Setup — What You Need

**Two matching monitors:** Matching monitors (same model, same size, same stand height) create a seamless dual setup. Mismatched heights and different bezels create visual discomfort.

**GPU with dual outputs:** Most modern laptops and desktop GPUs support dual monitors. Verify your PC or laptop has two video outputs (two HDMI, or HDMI + DisplayPort, or HDMI + USB-C).

**Desk space:** Two 24" monitors require approximately 130cm of desk width. Two 22" monitors need ~115cm.

**Monitor stand or arm:** The included stands may not align perfectly. A dual monitor arm (₹2,500–₄,000) allows precise height and angle matching.

## Vertical (Portrait) Secondary Monitor

An increasingly popular dual setup among developers: primary in landscape (normal) orientation + secondary rotated 90° to portrait mode for reading long documentation, code files, and PDFs.

For portrait-mode secondary, verify the monitor stand supports pivot rotation. The Dell E2222H (₹8,000–₁₀,000) and Dell E2422H (₹11,000–₁₃,000) include pivot stands. LG and Acer standard stands don't — use a monitor arm or wall mount for rotation.

## Desk Requirements

A proper dual monitor desk setup in India:
- Desk width: minimum 120cm for two 24" monitors
- Desk depth: minimum 60cm to maintain ergonomic viewing distance
- Indian standard office desk (120cm × 60cm) is the minimum workable size

Common Indian study tables (100cm × 50cm) are too small for two 24" monitors — two 21.5" monitors fit more comfortably at this desk size.
