## How to Choose an Auto Clean Chimney

Match the width to your hob: 60 cm for up to four burners, 90 cm for wider hobs. Check suction, with 1200 m3/hr or above a good target for heavy cooking.

Understand how auto clean works. Most heat the oil so it flows into a collector, which you empty every few weeks. The internal parts still need occasional wiping.

Check duct length, installation height and noise. A shorter, straighter duct keeps suction high.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Ignoring the duct.** Long or bent ducts reduce effective suction.

**Choosing size by looks.** A chimney narrower than the hob leaves smoke unchecked.

**Skipping the collector tray.** A full tray reduces performance. Empty it regularly.

## Quick FAQ

**Is auto clean truly maintenance-free?** It reduces effort, but you still need to empty the oil tray and wipe surfaces.

**Ducted or ductless?** Ducted vents outdoors and is more effective.

**How loud are they?** Noise depends on suction and motor, and higher speeds are louder.

## Installation and Upkeep

Ask the brand or seller whether installation is included and whether duct pipe and a stand are part of the price. Mount the chimney at the height the manual recommends, usually 65 to 75 centimetres above the hob, and keep the duct short and straight. Poor installation is the most common reason a good chimney underperforms.

For upkeep, empty the oil collector every couple of weeks in heavy-use kitchens, wipe the outer surface regularly and run the auto-clean cycle as directed. Even a filterless chimney benefits from a periodic wipe of the inside if the manufacturer permits.

## Real-World Scenarios

**A joint family that cooks three meals a day.** Choose 1200 m3/hr or higher and a filterless auto-clean design, and plan to empty the collector every week or two.

**A small kitchen with light cooking.** A 60 cm unit at a moderate price is enough. Prioritise low noise and easy wiping.

**An open kitchen.** Look for a stronger motor, a wider unit and a low-noise design, since smoke and smell reach the living area sooner.

**A rented home.** Check that installation does not require permanent changes, and ask whether a ductless mode is offered if venting is not possible.
