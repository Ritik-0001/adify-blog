## FDM, in one paragraph

Fused deposition modelling melts plastic filament through a nozzle and builds objects layer by layer. It is the cheap, common type, and what almost every beginner printer uses. Resin printers give finer detail but need messy, toxic liquid and washing, so they are a poor first machine. Everything below is FDM.

## Bed levelling: the biggest beginner problem

If the first layer does not stick evenly, the whole print fails. Old printers needed you to adjust four screws with a sheet of paper. Modern beginner machines use sensors that measure the bed and compensate automatically. Look for auto bed levelling, a flexible PEI build plate that releases prints when it cools, and direct-drive extruders that handle flexible filaments. These features matter more than the print volume or the speed figures on the box.

## Enclosure: do you need one?

PLA, the easy plastic, prints fine on an open-frame machine. PETG works too. ABS, ASA and nylon warp and crack without a hot enclosed chamber, and they release fumes that need ventilation. An enclosure also muffles noise and keeps out dust and curious fingers, which helps in a house with kids or pets. If you only want to print toys, tools and brackets, skip it.

## Filament costs in India

A 1 kg spool of PLA typically costs roughly ₹800 to ₹1,500, and a 1 kg spool makes dozens of small objects. PETG and TPU cost more. Buy from reputable brands, since cheap filament can have inconsistent diameter, causing jams. Keep spools in a sealed box with silica gel, because Indian humidity makes filament brittle and bubbly. ## Noise, space and safety

Most FDM printers run around 45 to 55 dB with fans spinning, which is like a loud fridge, and louder at high speed. Do not print in your bedroom. Never leave a printer running unattended overnight without thermal runaway protection, and fit a [smoke detector](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=smoke+detector+for+home&tag=adifystore-21) nearby. Print ABS only with ventilation. Place the printer on a sturdy [printer stand or table](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=heavy+duty+work+table+for+3D+printer&tag=adifystore-21) away from flammables. Keep fingers away from the hot nozzle at 200 degrees or more.

## Who should skip a 3D printer

Skip it if you only need one or two parts, because online printing services in major cities cost less than owning. Skip it if you want zero tinkering: even easy printers need maintenance. Skip it if you have no spare space for a noisy machine.
