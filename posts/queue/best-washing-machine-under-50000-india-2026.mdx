## Features worth paying for

- **Inverter or direct-drive motor:** quieter, more efficient, longer life. A 10 year motor warranty is common.
- **Steam wash:** helpful for allergies, baby clothes and removing odours.
- **AI or sensor wash:** adjusts water and cycle time to the load, saving water.
- **Quick wash:** a 15-30 minute cycle for lightly worn clothes.
- **Spin speed of 1,200-1,400 rpm:** dries clothes better for monsoon months.
- **Stainless steel drum and anti-vibration design:** reduces noise on upper floors.

## Features not worth paying extra for

Wi-Fi is nice but optional. Long lists of fabric-specific modes (like "sports wear") overlap, and you will use three or four of them. Giant capacity labels are less useful than your actual household load. A 7-8 kg machine suits a family of four, and 9-10 kg suits five or more.

## Space, water and power

A 9 kg front loader needs around 60 by 60 cm and clearance for the door. Top loaders need overhead lid room. Check water pressure: some front loaders need a minimum pressure to fill, and a few need a booster pump in a low-pressure supply. An inverter front load draws around 0.5-1 kWh per normal wash, more with a hot cycle.

## An honest trade-off

If you want the most dependable machine for ten years, Bosch's simplicity wins. If you want better features and AI, LG or Samsung are more capable but have more electronics that can fail outside warranty. If water is expensive where you live, front load saves more than a top load, though it costs more upfront.
