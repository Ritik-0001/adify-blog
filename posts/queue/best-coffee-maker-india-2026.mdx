## Indian Coffee Culture Context

**South Indian filter coffee:** Made with dark roast Arabica/Robusta blend (Coorg, Chikmagalur origin preferred). Brewed strong, mixed 1:3 with hot milk.

**North Indian coffee culture:** Americano, cappuccino, and latte are the standard. Morphy Richards Europa or similar espresso machine serves this style.

**Bean sourcing in India:** Corridor Coffee (Bengaluru), Blue Tokai (Delhi/Mumbai/online), and Araku (Araku Valley, Andhra) are excellent Indian specialty coffee options available on Amazon and direct.

**Recommendation:** Morphy Richards Europa for most Indian urban coffee drinkers who want to make café-quality espresso at home. Philips Drip for large families who prefer lighter coffee in volume. Traditional filter for South Indian filter coffee authenticity.
