## India-Specific Considerations

### Why Quick Heal Matters in India

**USB malware risk:** India's printing/scanning culture creates significant USB risk. At neighbourhood printing shops, USB drives are shared between customer computers. Quick Heal's USB auto-scan is designed for this — scans automatically when USB is inserted.

**Phishing attacks:** UPI-related phishing (fake PhonePe, BHIM, bank SMS links) is India's most common attack vector. Quick Heal's Safe Banking and URL reputation system is trained on India-specific phishing domains.

**Customer support:** When something goes wrong (false positive blocks an important file, renewal issues), Hindi phone support resolves issues faster than English-only international support.

### Why Kaspersky's Detection Matters

For users who download from various sources, install software frequently, or receive files from multiple sources (WhatsApp forwards, email attachments), Kaspersky's superior zero-day detection catches threats that Quick Heal misses. The 1.2% detection gap means catching an additional 12 threats per 1,000 — relevant for higher-risk users.

### Why Norton Wins on Features

Norton 360 Deluxe covers 5 devices — family laptops, desktops, and phones — at ₹1,499–₂,₉₉₉ during sales. The unlimited VPN, dark web monitoring, and 10GB cloud backup add genuine security value beyond antivirus. For a family with 3–5 devices, Norton's per-device cost becomes very competitive.
