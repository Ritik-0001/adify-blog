## What 99–130L Refrigerators Hold

A 99–100L single-door refrigerator holds:
- 1–2L milk carton
- 500g yoghurt (2 containers)
- 2–3 days of vegetables (2–3 types)
- 4–6 beverage bottles or cans
- Small freezer compartment for ice, 1kg frozen food

A 130L refrigerator holds approximately 30% more — suitable for people who bulk-buy weekly groceries or store larger quantities.
