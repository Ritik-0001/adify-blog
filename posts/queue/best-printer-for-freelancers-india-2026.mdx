## Freelancer Printing Scenarios

**Contracts and NDAs:** Print the agreement, sign, scan, email back. Needs: printer + flatbed scanner.

**GST invoices:** Monthly or project-based. Mono print, straightforward. Inkjet or laser both work.

**Portfolio prints:** Design and photography freelancers occasionally need colour quality prints for client meetings. Needs: colour inkjet with decent quality.

**Tax documents, TDS certificates, ITR acknowledgements:** Mono print from phone or laptop. Wi-Fi printing from phone is very convenient.

**Quantity:** Most freelancers print 50–150 pages/month — far less than office workers. Running cost at this volume is low regardless of technology.

## Do Freelancers Really Need a Printer?

Honest answer: fewer and fewer. Digital signing tools (Zoho Sign, Adobe Acrobat Sign, DigiLocker) replace most contract printing. Many government processes now accept digital documents.

A printer is still worth owning if you:
- Regularly print physical invoices for clients who require them
- Work with government, courts, or regulated industries requiring physical signatures
- Are a designer or photographer who presents physical prints

If you print fewer than 20 pages per month, a nearby print shop (Kinkos, local shop) may be more cost-effective than owning a printer.
