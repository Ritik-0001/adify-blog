## How to Choose a Laser Printer for Business

Match the volume. Look at the monthly duty cycle and toner yield. A machine with a higher yield toner lowers cost per page, and duplex saves paper.

Look at connectivity and features. Wi-Fi, Ethernet, mobile printing and an automatic document feeder help in shared offices. Consider scan-to-email if you send many documents.

Check toner cost and service. Original toner costs more but ensures quality. Confirm the service centre network and the warranty terms.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Ignoring toner costs.** Check yield and price.

**Buying for colour when you need mono.** Choose what you print.

**Skipping duplex.** It saves paper.

**Placing it in a hot, dusty room.** Keep it clean.

## Real-World Scenarios

**A small office with invoices.** A mono laser MFP with duplex.

**A shared space with several users.** Look for Ethernet and Wi-Fi.

**A business that prints colour.** Consider a colour laser or a business inkjet.

## Quick FAQ

**Is laser better than inkjet?** For text-heavy black-and-white work, yes.

**Do laser printers dry out?** No, toner does not dry.

**How long do they last?** Many last five years or more.

## Toner Costs and Duty Cycle

Toner cartridges come in standard and high-yield versions, and high-yield versions lower the cost per page for busy offices. Check the monthly duty cycle of the printer against your volume, since running a machine near its limit shortens its life. Keep a spare toner on hand so a job does not stall.

## Placement and Maintenance

Laser printers produce a little heat and fine dust, so place them in a ventilated spot and away from where people sit for long hours. Use good-quality paper to avoid jams, and clean the paper path as the manual advises.

## Scanning and Document Workflow

If your office scans invoices and contracts, an automatic document feeder saves time, and scan-to-email or scan-to-folder features reduce manual steps. Check the scan resolution and whether the scanner supports duplex, and consider a separate document scanner if scanning volume is high.
