## RTX 4070 vs. RTX 4060: The Real Difference

The RTX 4070 delivers roughly 25-35% higher raw performance and the same 8GB VRAM as the 4060 — but its stronger core count sustains higher frame rates at 1440p specifically, where the 4060 starts to struggle in the most demanding titles. If your display is 1440p or higher, the RTX 4070 is the meaningfully better investment.
