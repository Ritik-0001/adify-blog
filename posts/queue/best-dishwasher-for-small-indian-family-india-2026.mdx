## How to Choose a Dishwasher for a Small Family

Choose 12 place settings as a sensible minimum for Indian cooking. Large kadhais, pressure cookers and tawas need room, and smaller capacities lead to hand washing the big items anyway.

Check racks and programmes. Adjustable racks help with tall pots, and an intensive cycle helps with turmeric and oil. A half-load option saves water if you run smaller batches.

Confirm installation. A dishwasher needs a water inlet, a drain and a power point, and many brands include installation. Ask about it before you buy.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Skipping rinse aid and salt.** Hard water needs dishwasher salt and rinse aid for good results.

**Overloading the racks.** Blocked spray arms lead to poor cleaning.

**Assuming everything is dishwasher-safe.** Wooden items, some non-stick pans and delicate glass may not be suitable.

## Quick FAQ

**Can a dishwasher handle Indian cookware?** Yes, if you scrape off large residue, arrange items well and use an intensive cycle.

**Does it use less water than hand washing?** Typically yes, though it depends on cycle and habits.

**Do I need a special detergent?** Use detergent designed for dishwashers, plus salt and rinse aid where recommended.

## Running Costs and Ownership

A dishwasher uses electricity, water and consumables such as detergent, salt and rinse aid. Many households find total water use lower than hand washing, and the time saved is the main benefit. Budget for a monthly supply of tablets or powder and salt if you have hard water.

Regular upkeep keeps performance steady: clean the filter weekly, wipe the door seal and run an occasional maintenance cycle. Ask about after-sales service in your city before you buy, since a machine down for weeks is frustrating.

## What to Check on the Listing

Confirm the number of place settings, the programmes offered and whether the price includes delivery and installation. Check the noise rating, the water and energy ratings, and the service network. Read the warranty terms, since motors and control boards are often covered separately.
