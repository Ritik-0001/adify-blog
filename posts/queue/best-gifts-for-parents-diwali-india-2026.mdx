## What NOT to Gift

- **Smartphones:** Unless parents have specifically requested an upgrade, new phones cause more anxiety than joy for older users unfamiliar with the new interface.
- **Smart TVs:** Most households already have a functional TV. The app ecosystem change is disruptive.
- **Fitness trackers:** Well-meaning but often unused by parents who didn't request one.

**Recommendation:** Kent Grand Plus water purifier for parents without a good RO purifier — universally practical and genuinely impactful. For parents who already have a purifier, a BLDC ceiling fan is the best ₹3,000–₅,000 gift that keeps delivering value on every electricity bill.
