## How to Choose a Video Door Phone

Check the monitor and camera. A 7 inch monitor is easy to see, and a wide-angle camera with night vision helps in dim corridors.

Look at door release and extras. A door release lets you unlock an electric strike from inside, and picture memory records visitors when you are away.

Plan wiring. Door phones need cables between the outdoor camera and the indoor monitor, so ask the installer about conduit routes, especially in finished flats.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Ignoring wiring access.** Plan before buying.

**Choosing a narrow camera.** Check field of view.

**Skipping society permission.** Some societies need approval.

**Buying without installer support.** Check who installs.

## Real-World Scenarios

**A family in a 3BHK.** A 7 inch monitor in the living area.

**A parent living alone.** A door phone with door release and a clear monitor.

**A flat with two entrances.** A kit that supports two monitors.

## What to Check on the Listing

Confirm the monitor size, the camera resolution, the night vision, the door release support, the storage, the cable requirements and the warranty. See what is included in the box.

## Installation Notes

Ask the installer to place the camera at about 1.4 metres, avoid direct light behind the visitor and test night vision. Keep the monitor within reach of the main living area, and label cables for later servicing.

## Warranty and Service

Check the warranty on the outdoor unit and the monitor and confirm who handles service. Keep the invoice and the installer's contact details.

## Quick FAQ

**Do door phones need Wi-Fi?** Wired kits do not.

**Can I add a second monitor?** Many kits support it.

**Are they hard to install?** Wiring needs planning.

## Power Cuts and Reliability

Wired door phones usually run on low-voltage power from a small adapter, so they stop working during power cuts unless the supply is backed up. A small UPS or an inverter-backed socket keeps the system alive, and many people place the adapter on the same backup circuit as their router. Ask the installer to plan this, since a door phone that fails during an outage defeats its purpose.
