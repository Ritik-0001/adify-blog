## Combo Unit vs. Separate Washer and Dryer

**Combo unit (single machine, wash then dry in same drum):** Saves space, lower total cost than two separate machines, but dry capacity is typically less than wash capacity (e.g., 9kg wash / 5kg dry) since not all washed items dry in the same cycle.

**Separate washer and dryer:** Faster overall process (can wash next load while previous load dries), full capacity dry cycles, but requires more space and higher total investment.

For most Indian apartments with limited utility space, a combo unit's space efficiency outweighs the capacity and speed trade-offs of separate machines.
