## Matching the Purifier to Your Water Source

**High TDS, hard municipal or borewell water:** RO+UV+UF combination is necessary to handle dissolved solids effectively — don't settle for UV-only in these areas.

**Low TDS, relatively soft municipal water:** A UV-only or UV+UF purifier is sufficient and avoids RO's water wastage and mineral stripping.

**Frequent power cuts in your area:** Check for a storage tank capacity of at least 6-8 litres so purified water remains available during outages.
