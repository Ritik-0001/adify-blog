## How to Choose a PoE CCTV System

Plan the coverage. Walk the property, list entrances, driveways and corners and decide how many cameras you need, with a few spare NVR ports for later.

Size the storage. More cameras and higher resolution use more space, so choose a surveillance-grade drive, such as 2TB to 4TB, and consider motion recording to extend retention.

Think about cabling. PoE uses network cables, with a limit of about 100 metres per run, so plan routes and use quality cable.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Using cheap cable.** Poor cable causes drops.

**Undersizing storage.** Plan for days of footage.

**Skipping passwords.** Change defaults.

**Poor camera placement.** Avoid glare and backlight.

## Real-World Scenarios

**A villa with a garden.** An 8 camera PoE kit with outdoor bullets.

**An apartment with entrance and balcony.** A 4 camera kit.

**A home office or shop.** A PoE kit with a 4TB drive.

## What to Check on the Listing

Confirm the number of cameras, the resolution, the NVR ports and the hard drive included, the night vision, the weather rating and the warranty. See whether cables and installation are included.

## Installation Notes

Use a professional installer for neat cabling, test each camera at night and set the NVR on a UPS to survive power cuts. Keep the NVR in a secure, ventilated place.

## Warranty and Service

Ask about the warranty on cameras, the NVR and the hard drive, and how service is handled. Keep the invoice, the serial numbers and the login details safe.

## Quick FAQ

**Is PoE better than analogue?** It offers a cleaner install and higher resolution.

**Can I view on my phone?** Yes, with the brand's app.

**Do I need a UPS?** It is wise for power cuts.

## Network Safety for Your Cameras

Keep cameras and the NVR on a separate network or VLAN if your router allows it, change default passwords, disable unused remote access features and update firmware regularly. Use strong, unique passwords for the NVR and the phone app, and avoid exposing the recorder directly to the internet. Good habits protect your privacy as much as the best hardware.

## Final Checklist Before You Buy

Before you order, confirm that the product fits your door or wiring, check power and internet needs and what still works during an outage and ask who installs it. Change default passwords on first use, keep a mechanical or manual backup, read the warranty on electronics and keep the invoice and the installer contact details together.
