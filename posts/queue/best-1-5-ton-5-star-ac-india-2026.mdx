## Is 1.5 Ton Right for Your Room?

**Room up to 120 sq ft:** 1 ton is sufficient — 1.5 ton would be oversized, cycling on/off more than necessary.

**Room 120-180 sq ft:** 1.5 ton is the correct size — this covers most Indian bedrooms and small living rooms.

**Room above 180 sq ft, or rooms with high sun exposure/many occupants:** Consider 2 ton instead — an undersized 1.5 ton AC in a large room runs constantly at full capacity, reducing both comfort and efficiency.
