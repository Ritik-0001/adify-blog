## HP Toner Buying Tips India

**Avoid starter cartridges:** HP ships many LaserJet printers with starter cartridges (500–700 pages) — these run out quickly. Budget for a full-yield cartridge in your purchase planning.

**High-yield cartridges:** Always buy the high-yield variant (e.g., W1500X vs W1500A). The per-page cost is 30–50% lower.

**Genuine vs third-party:** Third-party HP LaserJet toner is available from ₹500–₈₀₀. Quality varies — some work well, some damage the drum. If reliability matters, stick to genuine HP toner for the first year and assess.
