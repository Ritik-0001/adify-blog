## How to Choose for a Family of Five

Pick 9 to 10 kg. A family of five generates around 5 kg of laundry a day, so a bigger drum lets you do fewer, fuller loads.

Look at spin speed and motor warranty. A spin of around 1,200 to 1,400 rpm reduces drying time, and a long motor warranty offers peace of mind.

Check features you will use. Steam, hard water programs and quick wash are useful, while many other programs go unused.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Buying 7 kg for a family of five.** You will run too many loads.

**Ignoring installation space.** Check door width and water points.

**Skipping the warranty details.** Read the terms.

**Overloading the drum.** Follow guidelines.

## Real-World Scenarios

**A family with children.** A 9 kg or larger machine with steam.

**A joint family.** A 10 kg machine.

**A flat with limited drying space.** A washer-dryer combo.

## What to Check on the Listing

Confirm the capacity, the spin speed, the motor warranty, the energy rating, the installation terms and the model number. See whether a stand and a water inlet filter are advised.

## Installation and Space

Measure the door and corridor, plan for a level floor and ensure access to a water inlet and drain. Leave space behind for hoses, and check the door swing so that you can load easily.

## Quick FAQ

**Is 9 kg enough for five?** Usually, yes; 10 kg is more comfortable.

**Is steam useful?** It helps with sanitising and wrinkles.

**Is a stand needed?** Check the manual and your floor.

## Running Costs for a Larger Machine

A bigger machine uses more water and power per cycle, but running full loads is more efficient than running several small ones. Use eco programs for everyday clothes, keep hot washes for items that need them and avoid half loads. Over a year, good habits save more than small differences between models.

## Care for Longer Life

Clean the door seal and detergent drawer, use the right amount of detergent and run a hot maintenance cycle from time to time. Leave the door open after use to prevent odours, and avoid overloading, which strains the motor and bearings.

## Warranty and Service

Check the motor warranty, the whole machine warranty and the availability of service in your city. Keep the invoice and installation details, and confirm who handles installation.

## Final Checklist Before You Buy

Before you order, measure the space, the doorway, the lift and the path to the room, check water and drain points or ventilation clearance and confirm whether delivery and installation are included. Compare the warranty on the motor or compressor with the warranty on the whole product, check the energy rating and keep the invoice and installation record.
