## How to Choose for a Family of Five

Pick 9 to 10 kg. A family of five generates around 5 kg of laundry a day, so a bigger drum lets you do fewer, fuller loads.

Look at spin speed and motor warranty. A spin of around 1,200 to 1,400 rpm reduces drying time, and a long motor warranty offers peace of mind.

Check features you will use. Steam, hard water programs and quick wash are useful, while many other programs go unused.

## Sizing Mistakes Specific to Large Families

A 7 kg drum looks like a saving until you count the extra loads each week. Bedsheets, quilts and school uniforms all compete for the same slot, and a joint family of six or more will be happier at 10 kg. Check the door width and the lift to your balcony or utility area before ordering, because a drum this size is a bulky delivery, and confirm whether installation is done by the brand or by the seller.

## Running Costs and Care

A bigger machine uses more water and power per cycle, but full loads are more efficient than several small ones. Use eco programs for everyday clothes and keep hot washes for towels and bedding. Wipe the door seal, clean the detergent drawer and leave the door ajar after use to prevent odours, since a large rubber gasket is where smells start.

## Who Should Skip This

If your household is only two or three people, a 9 to 10 kg front-loader is more drum than you need, and a 7 or 8 kg machine will wash the same clothes with less water and a lower price. Skip it too if your flat has a narrow door or no water inlet and drain point near the planned spot.

## Recommendation

For most families of five, a 9 to 10 kg inverter front-loader from LG, Samsung or Bosch is a strong pick.
