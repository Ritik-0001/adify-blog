## How to Choose a Mattress

Start with your sleeping position. Back sleepers usually prefer medium-firm to firm support; side sleepers need more cushioning at the shoulders and hips; stomach sleepers do best on firmer surfaces.

Check thickness and materials. Six to eight inches is common for foam, and layered construction generally gives better support. Confirm the exact size against your bed frame.

Read the trial period, return policy and warranty. A home trial of 100 nights or more lets you test the mattress properly before you commit.

## Quick FAQ

**Foam or spring?** Foam offers pressure relief and motion isolation; spring is more breathable and bouncy. Hybrid types combine both.

**How long does a mattress last?** Many last seven to ten years with care, though this depends on quality and use.

**Should I buy online?** Yes, if the brand offers a trial period and easy returns, since fit is personal.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Buying by brand alone.** A mattress that suits one sleeper can feel wrong for another. Match firmness to your sleeping position and body weight.

**Ignoring the exact dimensions.** Queen and king sizes vary between brands by a few inches, and Indian bed frames come in many sizes. Measure your frame and check the mattress dimensions on the listing.

**Skipping the trial period.** A home trial lets you test the mattress for weeks. Read the return conditions and how the pickup works.

**Forgetting the base.** A slatted or solid base with proper support keeps the mattress in shape. A sagging base can void warranty terms.

## Making the Most of a Sleep Trial

Give the mattress at least three to four weeks before you judge it. New foam can feel firmer or softer than it will after your body adjusts, and a mattress that feels unusual on night one may suit you fine by week three. Keep the protective wrapper off, use a fitted sheet and sleep in your usual position.

Note what you feel each morning: lower back tightness, shoulder pressure or heat. If the same complaint repeats after a month, use the return option rather than hoping it will improve.
