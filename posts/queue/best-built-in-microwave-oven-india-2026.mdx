## How to Choose a Built-in Microwave

Check the capacity and convection. A 25 to 34 litre cavity suits most families, and convection adds baking and roasting.

Look at the cleaning. Ceramic or easy-clean interiors save time, and a removable turntable helps.

Confirm the cutout and ventilation. Standard widths vary, so check the cabinet dimensions, the depth and the clearance the maker specifies.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Using a countertop model in a closed cabinet.** Only use models rated for built-in use.

**Ignoring ventilation.** Leave the gap the maker requires.

**Choosing the wrong height.** Place it at a comfortable level.

**Skipping the trim kit.** It gives a clean finish.

## Real-World Scenarios

**A modular kitchen.** A built-in convection microwave in a tall unit.

**A baker.** A convection model with a larger cavity.

**A rental home.** A countertop microwave, not a built-in.

## What to Check on the Listing

Confirm the capacity, the convection and grill functions, the cutout dimensions, the power rating and the warranty. See whether a trim kit is included and read reviews on cleaning.

## Using a Built-in Microwave Well

Use microwave-safe containers, cover food to avoid splatter and clean the interior regularly. Do not run it empty, and keep the vents clear.

## Quick FAQ

**Can any microwave be built in?** Only models rated for it.

**Is convection worth it?** Yes if you bake or roast.

**What size is best?** Around 25 to 34 litres for most families.

## Microwave, Grill and Convection Explained

A plain microwave heats food quickly, a grill browns the top and a convection mode bakes with circulated hot air. Combination modes use more than one at a time to save time. If you only reheat food, a simple model is enough, and if you bake or roast, convection is worth the extra cost.

## Fitting and Using It Well

Check the cabinet cutout, the vent clearance and the electrical point before ordering, and ask the carpenter to include a ventilated niche. Use microwave-safe containers, keep the door seals clean and never run the oven empty.

## Warranty and Service Matters

A microwave's magnetron and electronics are the parts most likely to need service. Ask for the length of cover on those parts, whether service is on-site and how easy it is to get spares such as turntables and rollers. Keep the invoice with the model number for any claim.
