## Sizing Your UPS Correctly

**Basic desktop + monitor:** 1100VA is generally sufficient for 8-15 minutes of safe shutdown time.

**Desktop + monitor + additional peripherals:** Consider 1500VA+ for adequate runtime with the additional load.

**Gaming PC with high-power components:** Verify the UPS's wattage rating (not just VA) comfortably exceeds your PC's actual power draw under load.
