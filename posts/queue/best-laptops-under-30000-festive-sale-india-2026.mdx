## What to Watch Out For Under ₹30,000

**8GB vs 4GB RAM:** Some GIF deals on ₹20,000–₂₄,000 laptops have 4GB RAM — avoid. Windows 11 with 4GB is sluggish and not future-proof. 8GB is the minimum; 16GB if available in budget.

**SSD vs HDD:** A ₹24,000 laptop with HDD (spinning disk) will feel slower than a ₹27,000 laptop with SSD. SSD is mandatory for acceptable Windows performance.

**32-bit vs 64-bit Windows 11:** Not common in 2026 but verify. 64-bit is required for modern software.

**Check exact SKU:** "16GB" in the listing title sometimes refers to the monitor resolution (1600×900) not RAM. Read specs carefully.
