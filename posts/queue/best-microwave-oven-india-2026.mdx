## Which Type Do You Need?

**Solo Microwave (₹6,000–₁₂,000):** Heats, defrosts, and cooks (steams, boils). Cannot grill or bake. Right for: reheating leftovers, defrosting meat, cooking rice, making chai. 80% of Indian microwave use is solo-category tasks.

**Grill Microwave (₹8,000–₁₅,000):** Solo + grilling element. Grills bread, chicken, paneer tikka. Cannot bake cakes or pastries. Right for: tandoori-style items at home without needing a full oven.

**Convection Microwave (₹14,000–₂₅,000):** Solo + Grill + Convection heating (circulates hot air like an oven). Can bake cakes, make naan/pizza/cookies. Right for: serious home bakers and those who want to replace a separate OTG.

## Indian Cooking-Specific Tips

**Making chai in microwave:** Use a microwave-safe glass. Heat milk for 90–120 seconds, add tea powder, heat again for 30 seconds.

**Cooking rice:** 1 cup rice + 2 cups water in microwave-safe vessel. 10–12 minutes on high, covered with plastic wrap (leave small gap). Easier than stovetop for small quantities.

**Defrosting meat:** Use the defrost setting (30–40% power) rather than reheating — prevents cooking the outside while inside stays frozen.
