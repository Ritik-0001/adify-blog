## What Makes an Ultrabook

**Weight:** True ultrabooks are under 1.5kg. Under 1.3kg is exceptional. Above 1.5kg is thin-and-light, not ultrabook.

**Battery:** 10 hours minimum. 15+ hours separates leaders from the rest.

**Thickness:** Under 18mm (0.7 inches). The MacBook Air M3 is 11.3mm.

**Build:** Premium materials — aluminium, magnesium alloy, or carbon fibre. No flexing lid, no creaking chassis.

**Display:** QHD+ or better at this price point. OLED preferred.
