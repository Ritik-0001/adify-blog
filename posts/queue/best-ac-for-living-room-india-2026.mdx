## Living Room-Specific Considerations

**Higher baseline capacity:** Living rooms connected to dining areas or open kitchens often need 2 ton even at moderate square footage due to additional connected volume.

**Variable occupancy:** Unlike bedrooms (consistent nightly use) or offices (consistent daytime use), living rooms see empty periods, small-family use, and full-capacity guest gatherings — adaptive cooling capacity genuinely saves electricity here.

**Guest-facing noise tolerance:** Living rooms can tolerate slightly higher noise levels than bedrooms since they're not sleep spaces, opening up more model options.
