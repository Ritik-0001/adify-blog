## What to Verify at the Budget Tier

**Foam density:** Ask for or check reviews mentioning foam quality specifically — this is the component most likely to fail early in budget chairs.

**Warranty length:** A genuine 1-2 year warranty signals more manufacturer confidence than chairs offering only 6 months or less.

**Weight capacity:** Verify the rated capacity comfortably exceeds your body weight with margin — chairs run at their rated limit wear out faster.
