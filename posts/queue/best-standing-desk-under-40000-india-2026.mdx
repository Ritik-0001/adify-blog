## Why Dual-Motor Matters at This Price

**Stability:** Dual motors (one per leg) raise and lower in perfect sync, eliminating the wobble and uneven lifting that single-motor desks with a crossbar mechanism can develop over time.

**Speed:** Dual-motor desks typically transition between sitting and standing height in 15-20 seconds versus 30-40+ seconds for single-motor alternatives.

**Weight capacity:** Dual motors typically support 100-120kg versus 70-80kg for single-motor desks — important for setups with multiple monitors, a monitor arm, and other desk equipment.
