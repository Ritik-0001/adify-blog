## Summer Fan Performance — CMM Matters

**CMM (Cubic Metres per Minute):** The most direct measure of airflow. Higher CMM = more air moved per minute = stronger perceived cooling effect.

Typical CMM ratings:
- Budget standard fan: 160–175 CMM
- Premium standard fan: 190–210 CMM
- Standard BLDC fan: 195–215 CMM
- Premium large fan (1400mm): 225–250 CMM

The difference between 175 CMM and 210 CMM is noticeable in a hot room — the higher CMM fan creates a stronger airflow that improves perceived cooling even without temperature change.

## Summer Fan Tips for India

**Height:** Ceiling fans should be installed 2.4–2.8 metres above floor for maximum airflow efficiency. Lower installation (below 2.3m) reduces effective CMM and can be hazardous.

**Direction:** In summer, fans should run anti-clockwise (when viewed from below) to push air downward — creating the direct downwash that produces cooling effect. Most fans have a reverse switch for winter (clockwise to draw warm air down from ceiling).

**Regulator:** Standard fans with resistor regulators lose 20–30% energy to heat in the regulator at low speeds. Replace with electronic regulators (₹300–₅₀₀) for better efficiency and finer speed control.
