## Where each class sits

An RTX 4060 class card handles modern games at 1080p with high settings, and many at 1440p with upscaling. It supports DLSS upscaling and frame generation, ray tracing at modest levels, and draws only around 115 watts, so most power supplies cope. An RX 7600 class card from AMD offers similar rasterised performance at a lower price, but weaker ray tracing and a less mature upscaling story, using FSR instead.

If ray tracing and DLSS matter, the RTX class is better. If pure value matters, an RX 7600 class card saves a few thousand rupees. Browse [RX 7600 graphics card](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=rx+7600+graphics+card&tag=adifystore-21) listings to compare pricing.

## VRAM: the 8GB question

Most cards in this class carry 8GB of VRAM, which is adequate for 1080p but increasingly tight at high or ultra textures in newer games, and for 1440p. Some variants, such as RTX 4060 Ti 16GB and RX 7600 XT 16GB, offer more memory at a higher price, roughly ₹32,000-48,000.

If you keep a card for four or five years, extra VRAM gives breathing room. If you play mostly esports titles, 8GB is fine. Check [RX 7600 XT 16GB](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=rx+7600+xt+16gb&tag=adifystore-21) if you want more memory on a budget.

## Power supply

An RTX 4060 class card uses around 115 watts and one 8-pin power connector. An RX 7600 draws around 165 watts, also with one 8-pin connector. A decent 550-650 watt power supply from a reputable brand handles either with a mid-range CPU. Do not buy a no-name unit.

Check that the PSU has an 8-pin PCIe connector. Older or very cheap supplies may lack one or have a weak 12V rail. Replace a questionable PSU when you upgrade the GPU, because it is the part that fails when stressed. See [650W 80 plus bronze power supply](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=650w+80+plus+bronze+power+supply&tag=adifystore-21) listings for options.

## Case size and cooling

GPUs come in dual-fan and triple-fan versions, and some are quite long. Measure the maximum GPU length your case allows, and check the card's stated length. Compact cases often support only shorter cards, so a two-fan model is a safer choice.

Airflow also matters. A GPU in a stuffy case throttles. Make sure your case has at least one intake and one exhaust fan.

## Brands and cooling quality

ASUS, MSI, Gigabyte, Zotac, Sapphire (for AMD), PowerColor and XFX all sell cards in India. Rather than chasing a particular model, compare dual versus triple fan, noise level in reviews, warranty length and build. Sapphire's Pulse and Zotac's Twin Edge are common compact options. Prefer a brand with Indian service and a warranty of three years where offered.

Check the seller too. Some cards sold through marketplaces come from grey imports that lack proper warranty.

## CPU pairing

A strong GPU with a weak CPU gets bottlenecked. A recent Ryzen 5 or Core i5 pairs well with this class of card. If you have an older quad-core processor, upgrading the CPU may give more benefit than a new GPU.

## PCIe and motherboard

Most cards use a PCIe x16 slot and are backward compatible. The RTX 4060 uses an x8 interface, which works fine on PCIe 4.0 boards but can lose some performance on older PCIe 3.0 boards in certain games, so check your motherboard generation.

## Monitor match

A 1080p or 1440p monitor matches this class of card. For 4K you need a stronger GPU. Pair with a [1440p 165Hz gaming monitor](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=1440p+165hz+gaming+monitor&tag=adifystore-21) if you want to push into higher resolution.

## When to skip this class

Skip it if you play at 4K, want ray tracing at high settings, or run professional rendering, where a higher-tier card pays back. Skip it also if your PC's CPU, PSU or case limit what you can fit, as an upgrade across several components may be cheaper than a stronger card alone.

## Recommendation

For most gamers at 1080p to 1440p, buy an RTX 4060 class card at roughly ₹28,000-34,000, or an RX 7600 class at roughly ₹24,000-28,000 to save money. Go for 16GB variants only if you plan to keep the card for years or play at higher texture settings. Confirm PSU wattage and connectors, case clearance and warranty before checkout.
