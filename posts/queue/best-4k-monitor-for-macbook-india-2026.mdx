## macOS scaling: the thing most buyers miss

macOS looks sharpest at integer scaling. A 27 inch 4K monitor has around 163 pixels per inch. At the default setting, macOS renders the desktop at a 2560 x 1440 look, which requires non-integer scaling, so text is slightly softer than on a MacBook or a 5K display.

A 24 inch 4K monitor reaches about 185 ppi and scales cleanly at 1920 x 1080 looks, which is sharper but has a smaller workspace. A 32 inch 4K runs about 138 ppi, so text looks larger and less crisp, though many people are fine with it.

Practical rule: 27 inch 4K is a good compromise; for the sharpest text, look at 5K.

## USB-C and power delivery

For a MacBook Air, 65W is enough. For a MacBook Pro, aim for 90W or more. A monitor that charges your laptop, carries video and offers a USB hub with a keyboard and mouse is a single-cable desk. Check the monitor's ports: Thunderbolt monitors add daisy-chaining and high-bandwidth docks.

## Colour and brightness

For photo editing, look for P3 coverage of around 95 percent or more and factory calibration. For general work, sRGB coverage near 100 percent is enough. Brightness of around 350 to 400 nits is fine indoors. HDR on these monitors is mild, so do not pay a premium for HDR claims.

## Refresh rate and gaming

Most Mac-friendly 4K monitors run at 60Hz, which is fine for work. If you also game, look at models with 120Hz or higher, though Mac support depends on the port and cable. See [4K 144Hz monitor USB-C](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=4k+144hz+monitor+usb-c&tag=adifystore-21).

## Cables and docks

Use a certified USB-C or Thunderbolt cable rated for the bandwidth. A cheap cable can limit resolution or charging. A good cable is a small cost relative to the monitor. See [Thunderbolt 4 cable](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=thunderbolt+4+cable+1m&tag=adifystore-21).

## Who should skip a 4K monitor

If you mostly work on a MacBook screen and occasionally connect for presentations, a cheaper 1440p USB-C monitor may suit. If text clarity matters more than anything else, save for 5K.
