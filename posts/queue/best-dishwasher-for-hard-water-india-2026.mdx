## What hard water does to a dishwasher

Hard water contains calcium and magnesium. In a dishwasher it causes:

- White film and spots on glasses and steel.
- Scale on the heating element, which raises power use and can cause failure.
- Clogged spray arm nozzles, which reduce cleaning.
- Weaker detergent performance, so dishes need rewashing.

Indian cities such as Delhi, Jaipur, Hyderabad, Chennai and parts of Bengaluru have moderate to very hard water, and borewell water is often harder than municipal supply.

## How a built-in softener works

Most dishwashers sold in India for hard water use an ion-exchange resin unit. Water passes through the resin, which swaps calcium and magnesium for sodium, before the wash. The resin gets saturated over time and regenerates using dishwasher salt (coarse, special salt) that you pour into a compartment. This is not table salt, which has additives that can clog the system.

Most machines let you set the hardness level, often through a dial or buttons, so you can match it to your water. The higher the hardness, the more salt is used per cycle.

## Features to look for

- **Salt compartment and hardness adjustment:** the key feature.
- **Salt and rinse aid indicator:** reminds you to refill.
- **Stainless steel interior and filter:** resists scale and corrosion.
- **Intensive and high temperature cycles:** hot water helps clean oily dishes, but also scales faster, so softening matters.
- **Self-cleaning filter or an easy-access filter:** you will need to clean it often.
- **Drying options:** hard water leaves spots, so a good rinse aid and a drying boost reduce marks.

## Salt, rinse aid and detergent

Use dishwasher salt in the dedicated compartment, a rinse aid for spot-free drying and a detergent tablet or powder. Many people try to skip the salt, but in hard water the resin stops softening and scale builds up. A 1 kg or 2 kg bag of [dishwasher salt](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=dishwasher+salt+2+kg&tag=adifystore-21) typically lasts weeks to months depending on hardness and use. Pair it with a [dishwasher rinse aid](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=dishwasher+rinse+aid+liquid&tag=adifystore-21) to cut spots.

## If your water is extremely hard

If your TDS is above 500-600 ppm or you see heavy scaling in kettles and taps, a built-in softener alone may struggle. Consider a whole-house softener or a water softener at the dishwasher inlet. A softener system helps the geyser, pipes and the dishwasher together. ## Cycles and care

- Run an empty descaling cycle with a dishwasher cleaner or citric acid every one to two months.
- Clean the filter weekly and rinse spray arms monthly. Use a toothpick to clear nozzle holes.
- Use hot cycles only when needed. Eco cycles at lower temperature reduce scale.
- Wipe the door seal, and leave the door ajar after use to dry.
- Use the hardness setting that matches your supply, and top up salt when the indicator shows.

## An honest trade-off

A built-in softener adds ongoing cost and a small chore of refilling salt, but without it, hard water is the most common cause of dishwasher complaints in India. A machine without a softener may work for a year or two, then show cloudy glasses and weaker cleaning.
