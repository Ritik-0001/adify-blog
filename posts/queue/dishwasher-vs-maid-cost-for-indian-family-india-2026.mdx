## 1. The Upfront Cost of a Dishwasher

Roughly ₹30,000–70,000 for a freestanding model, and more for built-in models. Installation, a water inlet and drain and sometimes a stabiliser add to the cost. It is a one-time spend, but a sizeable one with plumbing attached.

## 2. Running Costs of a Dishwasher

Electricity, water, detergent, rinse aid and salt add up each month. A modern dishwasher often uses less water than hand washing, but you should check the figures for your model. Consumables never stop, so count them in your monthly comparison.

## 3. The Cost of a Maid for Dishes

This is a recurring monthly payment that varies by city and by how many households the helper serves. Maids also do other work, so you cannot assign all the cost to dishes. Availability and reliability can change without notice.

## 4. A Simple Way to Compare

Take the monthly amount you pay for dish help, subtract the monthly running cost of a dishwasher and divide the dishwasher price by the difference. That gives the payback period in months, and it is worth running with your own numbers. The maths ignores time and convenience, so treat it as a floor rather than the whole answer.

## 5. Time, Hygiene and Convenience

A dishwasher runs while you do other things, uses hot water for sanitising and gives you back time, especially for families who entertain or cook large meals. A maid can handle large vessels and pre-wash with experience. You still load and unload the machine yourself.

## 6. Using Both Together

Many Indian homes keep a maid for sweeping and cooking help and add a dishwasher to handle plates, glasses and cutlery. Large vessels can still be washed by hand. The catch is that you carry both costs.

## How to Decide

Start with your numbers. Write down what you pay for dish help, what you would save and how many years you plan to stay in the home.

Think about your cooking. Heavy, oily cooking and large vessels may still need a pre-wash, and lighter households benefit most from a dishwasher.

Consider your space and plumbing. A dishwasher needs a water inlet, a drain and a power point near the sink.

## What the Payback Maths Misses

A dishwasher does not replace everything a maid does, so if you would keep paying for sweeping and cooking help regardless, only the dish portion of that wage counts as a saving. Hard water adds salt and descaling costs, and a machine that breaks outside warranty adds repair bills. Equally, the maths gives no credit for the hours you stop spending at the sink.

## Who Should Skip This

If you are a couple who eats out often or cooks only a little, the machine will run half-empty and take years to pay for itself. Skip it too if your kitchen has no water inlet or drain near the counter and you would need a plumber to reroute pipes, since that cost can stretch the payback past the useful life of the appliance.

## Recommendation

Run the payback calculation with your own numbers. If your family cooks daily and pays a meaningful amount for dish help, a good dishwasher is a sensible investment. Check the plumbing at your sink and the installation terms before you order.
