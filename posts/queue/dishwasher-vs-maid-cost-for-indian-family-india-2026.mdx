## 1. The Upfront Cost of a Dishwasher

Roughly ₹30,000–70,000 for a freestanding model, and more for built-in models. Installation, a water inlet and drain and sometimes a stabiliser add to the cost.

Why it works: a one-time cost. The trade-off is the upfront spend and the need for plumbing.

## 2. Running Costs of a Dishwasher

Electricity, water, detergent, rinse aid and salt add up each month. A modern dishwasher often uses less water than hand washing, but you should check the figures for your model.

Why it works: predictable costs. The trade-off is that consumables are an ongoing expense.

## 3. The Cost of a Maid for Dishes

This is a recurring monthly payment that varies by city and by how many households the helper serves. Maids also do other work, so you cannot assign all the cost to dishes.

Why it works: flexible help for many tasks. The trade-off is dependence and variable availability.

## 4. A Simple Way to Compare

Take the monthly amount you pay for dish help, subtract the monthly running cost of a dishwasher and divide the dishwasher price by the difference. That gives the payback period in months, and it is worth running with your own numbers.

Why it works: personalised maths. The trade-off is that it ignores time and convenience.

## 5. Time, Hygiene and Convenience

A dishwasher runs while you do other things, uses hot water for sanitising and gives you back time, especially for families who entertain or cook large meals. A maid can handle large vessels and pre-wash with experience.

Why it works: time saved and consistent hygiene. The trade-off is that you still load and unload.

## 6. Using Both Together

Many Indian homes keep a maid for sweeping and cooking help and add a dishwasher to handle plates, glasses and cutlery. Large vessels can still be washed by hand.

Why it works: the best of both. The trade-off is that you carry both costs.

## How to Decide

Start with your numbers. Write down what you pay for dish help, what you would save and how many years you plan to stay in the home.

Think about your cooking. Heavy, oily cooking and large vessels may still need a pre-wash, and lighter households benefit most from a dishwasher.

Consider your space and plumbing. A dishwasher needs a water inlet, a drain and a power point near the sink.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Comparing only the purchase price.** Include running costs.

**Expecting to replace all help.** A dishwasher only handles dishes.

**Skipping the plumbing check.** Plan before ordering.

**Ignoring hard water.** Use salt and rinse aid.

## Real-World Scenarios

**A nuclear family of three or four.** A compact or 12 place model.

**A family that entertains often.** A larger model with flexible racks.

**A couple who eat out often.** A maid or hand washing may be enough.

## What to Check on the Listing

Confirm the capacity, the programmes, the water use, the warranty and the installation terms. See whether large Indian vessels fit the racks.
