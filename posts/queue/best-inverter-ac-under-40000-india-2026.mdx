## Why Inverter Technology Matters

Inverter ACs modulate compressor speed continuously to maintain set temperature, rather than the on/off cycling of non-inverter units — this delivers 30-50% lower electricity consumption, quieter operation, and more stable room temperature. At ₹40,000, non-inverter ACs are essentially obsolete for this budget; every serious option should be inverter-based.
