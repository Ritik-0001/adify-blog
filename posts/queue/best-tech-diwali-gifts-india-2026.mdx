## Choosing the Right Tech Gift for the Recipient

**For fitness-conscious recipients:** Smartwatches with genuine health tracking (Amazfit, Noise Pro) resonate more than general electronics.

**For music/audio enthusiasts:** Headphones or earbuds, prioritizing sound quality tier appropriate to your budget.

**For photography-interested recipients:** Consider camera accessories or, at higher budgets, an entry-level mirrorless camera.

**For practical, non-gadget-focused recipients:** Power banks and basic accessories remain safer than niche tech gifts they may not use.
