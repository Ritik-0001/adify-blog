## QLED vs. Standard LED vs. OLED

**Standard LED:** Basic LED backlighting with standard color filters — most affordable but least vivid color and lowest contrast.

**QLED:** Quantum Dot color filter layer over LED backlighting — meaningfully wider, more accurate color and higher brightness than standard LED, at a moderate price premium.

**OLED:** Self-emissive pixels (no backlighting needed) — best possible contrast and black levels, but at significantly higher price for comparable size.

QLED represents genuine value territory for buyers who want a noticeable step up from basic LED TVs without OLED's premium cost.
