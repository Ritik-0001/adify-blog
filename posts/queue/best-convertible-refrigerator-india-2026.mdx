## How Convertible Modes Work

Using app control or physical buttons, you can convert freezer compartments to fridge temperature (or partial cooling) temporarily — useful when:

- **Festival season (Diwali, weddings):** Convert extra freezer space to fridge mode for storing prepared food, sweets, and beverages for guests.
- **Summer months:** Shift balance toward more fridge capacity for cold beverages and fresh produce.
- **Bulk freezer stocking (winter, monsoon prep):** Convert back to maximum freezer capacity for stocking frozen goods.

## Is Convertible Technology Worth the Premium?

Convertible models typically cost 10-15% more than equivalent fixed-configuration refrigerators. For households that host frequently, celebrate major festivals with extended family, or experience meaningfully different seasonal storage needs, this premium delivers genuine practical value. For households with consistent, unchanging storage patterns year-round, the premium may not translate into noticeable everyday benefit.
