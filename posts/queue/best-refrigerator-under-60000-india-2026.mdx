## What the Rs 60,000 band covers

Under Rs 30,000 you mostly buy a basic double-door. Between Rs 40,000 and Rs 60,000 you get convertible freezers, digital inverter compressors, better shelving and sometimes a multi-door layout. Side-by-side units start just above this band, so this is the top end for a regular double-door and the entry for a multi-door.

## Features worth paying for

- **Convertible freezer modes:** a flexible zone for seasonal use, which saves money over buying a bigger fridge.
- **Digital inverter compressor:** quieter and more efficient, and many brands give a 10 year compressor warranty.
- **Star rating:** 3 star is common at this price. A 5 star saves a little on bills but costs more upfront.
- **Vegetable crisper and humidity control:** useful in a vegetarian household with a lot of fresh produce.
- **Toughened glass shelves and spill-proof designs:** handle heavy vessels.
- **Door alarm and child lock:** useful for busy homes.

## Features you can skip

Water dispensers are rare in this band and add complexity. Touch panels and fancy displays do not improve cooling. Overly large capacity is a waste for two or three people, since an under-filled fridge costs more to run.

## Capacity guide

- Two or three people: 250-300 litres.
- Family of four: 300-350 litres.
- Five or more people, or heavy bulk buyers: 350-450 litres.

Measure your space, including 5-8 cm behind the unit for ventilation and the door swing.

## An honest trade-off

A convertible double-door is the practical buy because you can change use through the year. A multi-door gives a layout many people like, with separate compartments, but at this price the capacity is smaller and the service cost may be higher. If you want a large fridge with a water dispenser, you will need to go beyond the band.
