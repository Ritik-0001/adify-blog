## Bore Well Water: What You Need to Know

**Measure your TDS first.** A TDS meter (₹200–₃₀₀) is essential before buying any purifier for bore well water. Results guide your choice:

- **TDS 500–1000 ppm:** Standard RO (Kent Grand Plus, AO Smith Z8) is adequate.
- **TDS 1000–2000 ppm:** High-rejection RO (AO Smith Z9, Kent Supreme) is required.
- **TDS above 2000 ppm:** Bore well water may need industrial pre-treatment before home RO — consult a water treatment professional.

**Other bore well contaminants to test for:**
- Fluoride (common in Rajasthan, Gujarat, Telangana) — RO removes 90–95%.
- Nitrates (common in agricultural areas) — RO removes 60–70%.
- Arsenic (certain parts of West Bengal, Bihar, Punjab) — RO removes 80–90%.
- Iron (causes staining, metallic taste) — RO removes most; iron-specific pre-filter helps.

## Maintenance for Bore Well Purifiers

Bore well water puts more stress on RO membranes than municipal supply. Replace the RO membrane every 12–18 months rather than the standard 24 months. Pre-sediment filters need replacement every 3–4 months if water has visible turbidity. Budget ₹2,000–₃,500/year for consumable filter replacements.
