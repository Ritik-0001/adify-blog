## What Makes a Drone Beginner-Friendly?

**GPS stabilization:** Drone holds position automatically when you release sticks. Without GPS, you must constantly fight wind and drift — much harder for beginners.

**Return-to-home:** When battery is low or signal is lost, drone flies back to launch point automatically. Saves drones from getting lost.

**Obstacle sensing:** Sensors detect and avoid obstacles. More sensors = more crash protection.

**Weight/regulations:** Under 249g means Nano category in India — no pilot license or registration for recreational green zone flying. Less legal complexity for beginners.

**App quality:** A good companion app with real-time maps, airspace warnings, and educational tutorials significantly reduces beginner mistakes.

## Beginner Tips for Indian Drone Pilots

**First flight checklist:**
1. Check Digital Sky app for no-fly zones before flying
2. Fly in open areas away from people and trees for first 10 flights
3. Start in GPS mode (not ATTI/manual mode)
4. Keep drone within visual line of sight at all times
5. Don't fly above 400 feet (120 meters) — DGCA limit

**Weather:** Never fly in rain. Strong wind above 20 km/h makes Mini 3 unstable. Check wind speed in weather app before flying.

**Battery care:** Don't store fully charged or fully discharged. DJI batteries self-discharge to 50% if stored for 10+ days (feature built in).

**Insurance:** DJI Care Refresh covers one crash replacement — worth buying for the first year if you're a beginner.

**Recommendation:** DJI Mini 3 if budget allows — the safety features justify the price by dramatically reducing the chance of a costly crash. Ryze Tello if you just want to learn flight mechanics before committing to a camera drone.
