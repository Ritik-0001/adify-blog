## Why OLED Matters for Gaming Specifically

**Per-pixel black levels:** Unlike IPS/mini-LED which use backlighting (creating light bleed in dark scenes), OLED pixels switch off individually — atmospheric games (horror, cyberpunk aesthetics) look dramatically more immersive.

**Response time:** OLED's near-instant pixel response virtually eliminates motion blur and ghosting in fast-paced games — a genuine competitive advantage in shooters, not just an aesthetic upgrade.

**Burn-in consideration:** Static HUD elements (health bars, minimaps) over very long sessions can theoretically cause temporary or permanent burn-in — modern OLED panels include pixel-shifting and refresh algorithms that largely mitigate this for typical use.
