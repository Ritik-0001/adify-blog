## Features worth paying for at this price

- **Copper condenser and evaporator coils:** longer life, easier repair.
- **5 star rating:** the saving from 3 star to 5 star is real for halls running ten hours a day. For a bedroom used six hours, a 3 star premium model can be the smarter pick.
- **Dust or PM filter:** useful in dusty cities, but the filter needs regular cleaning.
- **Wi-Fi:** convenient, but not a reason to choose a worse cooler.
- **Anti-corrosion coating on the outdoor coil:** valuable within a few kilometres of the coast.
- **Long compressor warranty:** most premium brands offer a 10 year compressor cover, and a PCB warranty beyond the first year is a good sign.

## Features that are marketing

Ionizers, aroma diffusers and flashy display panels add little. "Turbo" modes pull the room down faster but cost more power, so use them briefly. Stabiliser-free operation is useful only if your supply really fluctuates. Also be sceptical of any claim of massive electricity saving without a BEE label to back it.

## An honest trade-off

A Rs 58,000 5 star unit versus a Rs 45,000 3 star unit: the extra Rs 13,000 saves roughly Rs 2,000-3,500 a year at typical use of 8 hours a day over 150 days. It pays back in four to six summers, so it makes more sense in a home you will stay in for years than in a rental.
