## Can a Tablet Replace a Laptop for College?

**Yes, for:** Humanities, reading-heavy subjects, note-taking, and general coursework where a keyboard case and stylus cover most needs.

**No, for:** Engineering, computer science, or any coursework requiring specific desktop software (CAD, IDEs, statistical analysis tools) that doesn't have adequate tablet equivalents.
