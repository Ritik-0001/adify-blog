## What Warranty Claims Actually Cover

**Motor failure:** The motor stops working, moves too slowly, or produces grinding sounds. Under warranty: replacement motor or full desk replacement.

**Frame wobble:** The frame develops structural wobble due to manufacturing defect. Under warranty: frame replacement.

**Control panel failure:** The height memory panel stops saving presets or stops working. Under warranty: replacement control panel.

**Not covered (typically):** Physical damage, water damage, overloading beyond rated capacity, normal wear on tabletop surface.
