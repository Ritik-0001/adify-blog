## IP Ratings for Indian Outdoor Cameras

**IP66:** Dust-tight and protected against powerful water jets. Adequate for most outdoor placements — covered entrance areas, under eaves, parking with roof coverage.

**IP67:** Dust-tight and protected against immersion up to 1 metre for 30 minutes. Recommended for exposed outdoor placements — compound walls, gate pillars, areas with direct rain exposure.

**IP68:** Protected against continuous immersion beyond 1 metre. Used for underwater cameras — not needed for CCTV.

For Indian outdoor CCTV: IP67 minimum for exposed locations. IP66 is adequate under covered areas.

**IK ratings (vandal resistance):** IK10 means resistant to 20-joule impacts. Metal housing cameras with IK10 are preferred for cameras at reachable heights (below 3 metres) in commercial locations.
