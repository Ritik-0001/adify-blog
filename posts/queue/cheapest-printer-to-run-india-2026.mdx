## When Cartridge Printers Make Sense

If you print fewer than 20 pages per month, even the EcoTank or MegaTank may not be the best choice. Very infrequent use means:
- Ink tanks may dry out or clog if not used for months
- You'll never print enough to justify the higher upfront cost

For 1–2 print jobs per month: A cheap cartridge printer or just using a print shop makes more economic sense.

**The tipping point:** If you print 50+ pages per month, ink tank printers save money within 6–12 months. At 200+ pages/month, the Canon G2020 or Epson EcoTank saves thousands of rupees annually.

**Recommendation:** Canon G2020 for the absolute lowest running cost (USB, no Wi-Fi). Canon G3770 if you need Wi-Fi. Epson L3252 if Canon ink availability is a concern in your city.
