## When Built-In Speakers Are Worth It

**Video calls only:** If your monitor is used exclusively for Zoom/Teams and YouTube at low volume, 1W monitor speakers are adequate. Save the external speaker cost.

**Minimal desk space:** If your desk has no space for even small external speakers, built-in is the only option.

**Children's education setup:** A simple single-cable setup (laptop to monitor with HDMI audio) is convenient for children's online class use.
