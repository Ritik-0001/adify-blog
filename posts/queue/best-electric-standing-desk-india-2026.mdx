## Single Motor vs Dual Motor

**Single motor (Flexispot E2, entry-level brands):** One motor drives both legs via a crossbar. Adequate for loads up to 70–80kg. Slight flex at maximum height with heavy setups (2 monitors + desktop PC).

**Dual motor (Flexispot E7, E7 Pro):** Independent motor per leg. More torque, smoother movement, less flex. Handles 120–160kg loads. More stable for monitor arms, standing monitors, and heavy accessories.

**Which to buy:** For laptop + single external monitor setups, single motor is fine. For dual monitor + desktop setups or tall users who regularly go above 100cm, dual motor is worth the premium.

## Electric Desk Setup Checklist

**Cable management:** Use cable raceways or flexible cable trays that move with the desk. Loose cables snag during adjustment.

**Anti-fatigue mat:** Standing for 2+ hours on hard Indian floor tiles causes foot fatigue. Anti-fatigue mat (₹2,000–₄,000) is a recommended companion purchase.

**Monitor arm:** Mounted monitor on an arm keeps the screen at eye level whether sitting or standing. Clamp-on monitor arms (₹2,500–₄,000) are easier to position than stands.

**Programming height presets:** Set 2 positions — one for sitting height, one for standing. Name them mentally so adjustment becomes automatic.

**Recommendation:** Flexispot E2 for most Indian buyers. The E7's dual-motor advantage is meaningful for heavy setups or heavy users — if you don't fit those criteria, save ₹15,000 and invest in a better chair.
