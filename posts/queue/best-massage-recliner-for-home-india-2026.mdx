## Recliner or full massage chair

A massage recliner is a normal recliner with vibration motors or simple rollers built in. Features are modest: a few zones, heat on the lower back, remote control. A full massage chair has long rails for the rollers, air bags for the arms, legs and feet, body scanning and zero-gravity recline. It costs roughly ₹90,000 to ₹5,00,000 and takes up around 1.5 m by 0.9 m, with extra clearance behind to recline.

For a household where the chair is used 15 to 20 minutes a few times a week, a recliner is usually enough.

## 1. Massage recliners with vibration and heat

These cost roughly ₹20,000-40,000 and offer vibration zones in the back, seat and sometimes legs. Heat in the lumbar area is common. They are comfortable as TV chairs and give light relief. ## Features that matter

Rollers: look for adjustable width and track length. A short track misses the lower back. Heat: lumbar heat is common and welcome in winter. Zones and intensity: more levels mean more control. Recline angle: a deep recline lets you nap. Remote: a simple wired or wireless remote is easier than touch screens.

Check the weight limit, usually 110 to 150 kg, and the height range. Short users may find rollers sit too high.

## Space and installation

Measure your room for the fully reclined length. Some recliners need 30 to 60 cm of wall clearance, while full chairs need more. Check the doorway width and lift size for delivery. Place the chair on a hard floor or a mat to prevent marks. [Floor protector mat for furniture](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=furniture+floor+protector+mat&tag=adifystore-21).

## Noise, power and upkeep

Motors hum, and rollers click on some models. Most use around 100 to 200 W. Leather or faux-leather upholstery needs wiping, and sunlight and humidity can crack cheaper covers. Ask about the warranty on motors and rollers, and whether technicians visit your city.

## Safety and who should skip

People with spinal conditions, recent surgery, osteoporosis, pacemakers or pregnancy should consult a doctor first. Children should not use these chairs. If you want therapy for chronic pain, a physiotherapist is a better first step than a chair.

Who should skip a massage recliner: anyone with a small flat, a tight budget, or those who will use it rarely. A good ergonomic chair and a handheld massager may serve better. [Handheld massage gun](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=massage+gun+deep+tissue&tag=adifystore-21).

## Upholstery and the Indian climate

Faux-leather covers on cheaper recliners can peel in humid coastal cities and crack in dry, hot summers. Fabric covers breathe better but stain more easily. If the chair will sit near a window, keep it out of direct sun and wipe it with a slightly damp cloth, never a harsh cleaner.

## Recommendation

For most homes, a shiatsu massage recliner at ₹40,000-60,000 is the sensible pick. Choose a full chair only for heavy daily use and a dedicated corner. Always check dimensions before buying.
