## Why winter and AC rooms get dry

Winter air in north India falls to 20 to 30 percent relative humidity indoors, causing dry skin, cracked lips, blocked noses and static. Air-conditioners also strip moisture, and a bedroom with AC running all night can fall well below 40 percent. A humidifier lifts it to a comfortable 40 to 50 percent.

## 1. Evaporative humidifiers

A fan blows air over a wet wick, evaporating water into the room. The output slows as humidity rises, which makes over-humidifying less likely. Minerals remain in the wick, not in the air. Prices are roughly ₹8,000-20,000, with Philips offering well-known models. The trade-off is fan noise, slower output and wick replacement every few months. [Philips evaporative humidifiers](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=philips+evaporative+humidifier&tag=adifystore-21).

## 2. Ultrasonic humidifiers

A vibrating plate makes a fine mist. They are quiet and have strong output but spray minerals into the air as white dust on furniture, which is common with hard Indian tap water. Look for demineralisation cartridges, and use distilled or filtered water. Larger premium units cost roughly ₹8,000-20,000. [Ultrasonic humidifiers with demineralisation](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=ultrasonic+humidifier+demineralization+cartridge&tag=adifystore-21).

## 3. Warm mist humidifiers

These boil water, which reduces bacteria, and gentle warm mist feels nice in cold weather. But the boiling uses more power and the hot surface is a risk around children. [Warm mist humidifiers](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=warm+mist+humidifier+large+room&tag=adifystore-21).

## 4. Humidifier plus purifier combos

Premium combos from Dyson and others add filtration, but cost much more. See them only if you also want air purification. [Humidifier with purifier](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=humidifier+with+air+purifier+combo&tag=adifystore-21).

## Mineral dust and hard water

In cities with TDS above 300 ppm, an ultrasonic humidifier will dust surfaces white within days. Use filtered, RO-treated or distilled water, or choose an evaporative unit. Fine mineral particles can also irritate lungs, a reason for caution with ultrasonic units and children. [Distilled water for humidifier](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=distilled+water+5+litre&tag=adifystore-21).

## Room size and tank

For a bedroom of 120 to 200 sq ft, a 3 to 4 litre per day output is enough. For 250 to 400 sq ft, look for 6 litres or more per day. A tank of 4 to 6 litres lasts 12 to 24 hours at medium output. Bigger tanks mean fewer refills.

## Cleaning routine

Empty the tank daily and refill with fresh water. Rinse weekly with diluted white vinegar to remove scale. Wipe the base and replace wicks on the maker's schedule. A neglected humidifier can grow bacteria and mould and spread them in the air. If you can't keep to the routine, skip the purchase.

## Using it in AC rooms

Place the humidifier away from the AC outlet and from walls. Keep windows closed. Use a hygrometer to avoid going above 50 percent. Condensation on windows means humidity is too high. [Digital hygrometer for room](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=digital+hygrometer+room+humidity&tag=adifystore-21).

## Noise at night

Evaporative units have a fan that runs constantly, usually 25 to 40 dB on low, comparable to a quiet AC fan. Ultrasonic units are almost silent, which is why light sleepers are tempted by them. If you pick evaporative, test the low speed in your bedroom during the return window.

## Who should skip a humidifier

If you live in a humid coastal city, you do not need one. People with asthma, mould allergy or dust-mite allergy should ask a doctor first. A bowl of water or a damp towel works for small, occasional dryness, but is far less effective.

## Recommendation

For most Indian bedrooms, buy an evaporative humidifier with a humidistat and use filtered water. Only buy ultrasonic if you will use distilled or demineralised water. Clean it daily.
