## Priority Ranking Explained

### 1. Large-Screen TVs (65-inch+) — Buy During GIF

New TV models typically launch ahead of Diwali, pushing manufacturers to clear outgoing-generation inventory aggressively. Combined with Diwali's home-upgrade gifting tradition, this is the single best-aligned big-ticket category for October timing.

### 2. Premium Laptops (₹75,000+) — Buy During GIF

Consistently the deepest absolute rupee discounts of any electronics category, with ₹8,000-₴0,000+ savings on flagship models. No meaningfully better alternative discount window exists elsewhere in the year.

### 3. Refrigerators — Buy During GIF

Strong combination of 15-20% sale discount plus exchange offer value for trading in an aging unit — no significant off-season discount advantage exists for refrigerators the way it does for ACs.

### 4. Gaming Laptops/Equipment — Buy During GIF

Manufacturers frequently clear current-generation gaming hardware ahead of new GPU architecture launches (often announced around this time of year), creating genuine urgency-driven discounting.

### 5. Washing Machines — Buy During GIF (Moderate Priority)

Solid 15-20% discounts without being the absolute best category, but no compelling reason to wait for a different window either.

## Categories to Skip During GIF

### Air Conditioners

February-March pre-summer sales offer meaningfully deeper discounts (25-35% vs. GIF's 15-20%) — wait unless you have immediate cooling need.

### Geysers/Water Heaters

Similar seasonal logic to ACs — better discounts typically appear ahead of winter demand (September-October in South India, but often better deals slightly earlier or through specific off-season promotions rather than the general GIF sale).
