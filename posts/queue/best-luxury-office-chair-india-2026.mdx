## Choosing Between the Three

**Want the most proven, size-tailored fit:** Herman Miller Aeron.

**Want continuously adaptive lumbar support for frequent movement:** Steelcase Leap.

## Recommendation

All three represent genuine luxury-tier engineering worth their price for professionals sitting 8-10+ hours daily over many years — the choice comes down to whether tailored sizing (Aeron), continuous adaptivity (Steelcase), or self-adjusting simplicity (Humanscale) matters most to your specific body and working style.
