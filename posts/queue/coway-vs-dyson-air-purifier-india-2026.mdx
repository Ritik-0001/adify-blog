## Filtration

Coway purifiers typically use a multi-stage system with a pre-filter, activated carbon and a HEPA filter, aimed at removing particles and odours. Dyson purifiers use a sealed HEPA filter and an activated carbon layer, with a design that projects filtered air.

Both can handle fine particles. The bigger differences are airflow, coverage and how the machine behaves in your room.

## Design and Extra Functions

Dyson's design is distinctive, and models such as the TP07 combine a purifier with a fan, so you can use it for cooling in warm months. Coway's units are compact and functional.

If you want a single device for air and cooling, Dyson makes sense. If you only want air cleaning, the extra function may not justify the price.

## Sensors and Smart Features

Dyson models include sensors that report particle and gas levels and offer app control and scheduling. Coway models offer air quality indicators and auto mode, with app control on some.

Check the specific features on each model, since they vary.

## Filter Costs and Maintenance

Filters need replacing on both. Coway filters are generally priced lower than Dyson's, and Dyson filter cost is a significant ongoing expense. Check the live price and availability of replacements before you buy.

## Price and Value

Coway models typically cost less than Dyson for similar room coverage. Dyson's premium is for design, the fan function and brand.

## How to Choose Between Coway and Dyson

Start with room size and coverage, then filter costs. If two models fit, decide whether you want a fan and the design premium. Read recent reviews on noise and service.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Paying for features you do not need.** A fan function only helps if you will use it.

**Ignoring filter costs.** Add the yearly filter cost to your budget.

**Choosing the wrong size.** Match the purifier to the room.

## Quick FAQ

**Which cleans better?** Both clean well when sized correctly. Coway often offers more coverage per rupee.

**Which is quieter?** It depends on model and speed, so check reviews.

**Is Dyson worth the premium?** Only if you value the design and extra functions.

## Real-World Scenarios

**A bedroom in a polluted city.** A compact Coway or similar HEPA unit is enough and costs less to run. Prioritise coverage and filter price.

**A large living room.** Compare the coverage ratings carefully. Coway's larger models and Dyson's bigger units can both work, so check the room size rating.

**A buyer who wants a fan as well.** Dyson's purifier and fan models add cooling for warm months, which can justify the premium if you would otherwise buy a separate fan.

**A design-conscious household.** Dyson's look and app experience are part of the appeal, so decide whether that is worth the extra cost.

## Running Costs and Ownership

Add yearly filter costs to your budget and check replacement availability in India. Keep the purifier clean, place it with clearance and follow the manufacturer's schedule for filter changes. Consider warranty and service coverage in your city.

## Noise and Placement

Noise rises with fan speed on both brands. Check ratings for the lowest and highest speeds, since night-time noise matters in bedrooms. Place the unit away from walls and furniture for best airflow.

## What to Check on the Listing

Compare the CADR, the recommended room size, the filter type and the replacement cost for each model. See whether the warranty covers the motor and the sensor, and check the noise levels at each speed. Read recent buyer feedback for real-world noise and app reliability.

## Smart Features and Everyday Use

Both brands offer app control on certain models, letting you check air quality, schedule runs and monitor filter life. Most people use auto mode and leave the purifier running quietly in the background, so the app matters less than the sensor and filter design. If you like data and control, Dyson's app is detailed, and Coway's is simpler.
