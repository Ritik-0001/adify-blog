## How to Plan for Winter in a New Home

Check wiring first. Geysers, heaters and dryers draw high power, so ask an electrician to confirm points and the load capacity.

Plan installation early. Geyser, purifier and washing machine installers are busiest at the start of winter, so book ahead.

Think about space. Washing machines, dryers and refrigerators need ventilation and water or power points planned in advance.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Using extension cords for heavy appliances.** Use dedicated points.

**Ignoring ventilation.** Dryers and heaters need airflow and safety space.

**Skipping an RCCB.** Earth leakage protection is important with geysers.

**Buying last minute.** Installation queues grow quickly.

## Real-World Scenarios

**A flat in Delhi.** A geyser, an air purifier and a heater.

**A home in Pune.** A geyser and a washer-dryer for monsoon and winter.

**A home in Bengaluru.** A geyser and a heater only for a few cold weeks.

## Quick FAQ

**Do I need an air purifier everywhere?** Most useful in cities with poor winter air.

**Is an oil-filled heater safe?** Generally yes when used as instructed, but keep it away from fabrics.

**Do I need a separate dryer?** Only if drying space is limited.

## Electrical Safety for Winter Appliances

Geysers, heaters and dryers draw high current, so use a dedicated point with the correct wire gauge and an MCB. An earth leakage protection device adds an important safety layer in bathrooms. Never use a damaged cord, and switch heaters off when you leave the room.

## What to Check on the Listing

Look at the wattage, capacity and warranty of each appliance, the star rating where applicable and the installation terms. For heaters, check for tip-over protection and overheat cut-off, and for geysers, check for a safety valve and a thermostat with auto cut-off.

## Saving Electricity in Winter

Use timers on geysers, heat only the room you use, and close doors and windows to hold warmth. An air purifier on low speed is cheaper to run than most people expect, and a well-insulated storage geyser holds heat for hours, so you do not need to keep it on all day.

## Recommendation

Start with a five-star storage geyser and an air purifier, then add a heater and a washer-dryer as needed. Book installation early.
