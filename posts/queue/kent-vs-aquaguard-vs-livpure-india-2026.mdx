## Brand Overview

### Kent
**Founded:** 1999 (Noida). **Parent company:** Kent RO Systems Ltd (founder: Mahesh Gupta, IIT Kanpur alumnus).

Kent popularised RO water purification in India in the early 2000s. The brand has the widest service network nationally — present in 350+ cities with both company service centres and authorised third-party service agents.

Kent's TDS controller (adjustable permeate ratio) is a notable differentiator — you can tune output TDS to 75–200 ppm rather than stripping to near-zero.

Best for: Buyers in tier-2 and tier-3 cities, users who value TDS control, and anyone who might relocate.

### Aquaguard (Eureka Forbes)
**Founded:** 1982 (Mumbai). **Parent company:** Eureka Forbes (now Advent International PE).

Aquaguard pioneered domestic water purification in India — their door-to-door sales force of "Forbes Friends" built brand trust across South India especially. Eureka Forbes maintains the strongest service presence in Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Kerala, and Andhra Pradesh.

Modern Aquaguard models have smart features: IoT connectivity, Alexa integration, and in-tank UV sterilisation (re-UV treatment of stored water every few hours — preventing bacterial regrowth).

Best for: South India buyers, IoT feature enthusiasts, and buyers who want managed service contracts.

### Livpure
**Founded:** 2012 (New Delhi). **Parent company:** SAR Group.

Livpure entered as the value-price challenger. Their purifiers typically cost ₹2,000–₅,000 less than equivalent Kent and Aquaguard models. Quality has improved significantly since 2018.

Weakness: Service network is still less extensive than Kent or Aquaguard in smaller cities.

Best for: Budget buyers in major metros (Delhi-NCR, Mumbai, Bangalore) where Livpure service is reliable.

## Decision Guide: Which Brand for Your Situation

**Delhi-NCR, UP, Bihar, Rajasthan (high TDS, 400–1500 ppm):**
→ Kent Grand Plus or Kent Supreme. High TDS is Kent's strong suit; their RO membranes handle up to 2000 ppm input.

**Bangalore, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kerala:**
→ Aquaguard Marvel NXT or Aquaguard Delight NXT. Eureka Forbes' service density in South India is unmatched.

**Mumbai, Pune (TDS 200–500 ppm, municipal supply):**
→ Either brand works. Kent or Aquaguard based on service proximity to your area.

**Budget under ₹10,000:**
→ Livpure Bolt+ or Livpure Glitz. Better value than budget Kent or Aquaguard models.

**Tier-2 / tier-3 cities:**
→ Kent. More likely to have authorised Kent service in smaller cities.

## Real Complaints Worth Knowing

**Kent:** Membrane replacement (₹800–₁,500) needed every 12–18 months. Some users report aggressive post-warranty service upselling.

**Aquaguard:** Annual maintenance contract cost is higher than competitors. In some cities, service visit scheduling takes longer than advertised.

**Livpure:** Filter kits for older models sometimes out of stock in tier-2 cities. Service quality inconsistent outside major metros.

**Recommendation:** Kent Grand Plus for pan-India reliability. Aquaguard Marvel NXT for South India buyers and smart home users. Livpure Glitz+ for budget-conscious buyers in major metros.
