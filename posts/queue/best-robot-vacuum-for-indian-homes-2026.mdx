## The Indian Home Problem

Most international robot vacuum reviews test on hardwood floors in American apartments. In India:

**Marble and granite** are the dominant flooring. Smooth tiles + grout lines = robot vacuums pick up debris off the tile easily but pack grout lines with dust that only suction can clear. High suction (2500+ Pa) matters more than it does on carpet.

**Dust load is higher.** If you live in a city with ongoing construction (everywhere in India), your floors accumulate fine silica dust that clogs HEPA filters within days, not weeks. Look for robots with washable filters and large dustbins.

**Furniture clearance.** Indian sofas often sit 4–6 inches off the ground. South Indian homes frequently have low divans. The robot's height matters — most are 9–10 cm tall. A robot that can navigate under your furniture cleans more than one that avoids it.

**Monsoon:** August–September, you're tracking in mud, wet leaves, and grit daily. Robots with mop + vacuum combo help during this period, but make sure the mopping attachment is removable when you don't need it.

## What Indian Home Robots Struggle With

**Raised thresholds between rooms** — many Indian homes have 1–2 cm door thresholds (especially bathrooms). Most robot vacuums can handle 2 cm but struggle above that. Check your home's threshold heights before buying.

**Pooja room rugs** — small, light rugs get pushed around or sucked up by the robot. Use rug tape on the underside or set a no-go zone.

**Charging cable management** — robots get tangled in charging cables. Invest in cable clips (₹200) or use a no-go zone around your cable-heavy desk area.

**Socks and small clothes** — robots are very good at picking up and shredding socks. Run the robot with the floor clear of small items.

---

## Do You Need Mopping?

Indian tile floors collect a film of dust-and-humidity that dry vacuuming doesn't remove. If you're on the fence about vacuum-only vs combo: get the combo. The mopping feature on the Roborock and Dreame units is genuine (not just a wet wipe dragging around), and Indian marble floors come out notably cleaner.

The only exception: if you have carpet in most rooms, mopping is irrelevant. But most Indian apartments are fully tiled.
