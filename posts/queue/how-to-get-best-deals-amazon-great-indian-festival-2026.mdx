## The Five-Step Preparation Framework

**Step 1: Pre-shortlist your target products.** Decide specifically what you're buying — brand, model, configuration — rather than deciding during the sale itself when time pressure leads to worse decisions.

**Step 2: Track price history for 2-3 weeks before the sale.** Use Keepa or similar tools to establish a genuine baseline price, so you can verify GIF discounts are real rather than inflated.

**Step 3: Subscribe to Prime if targeting high-demand items.** The 24-48 hour Early Access window matters most for premium laptops, flagship phones, and popular TV sizes that sell out fast.

**Step 4: Prepare your payment method and exchange device in advance.** Confirm your bank card's specific GIF offer terms, and have any trade-in device factory reset and ready for valuation.

**Step 5: Act within the first 24-48 hours for competitive categories.** Flash deals and popular configurations sell out fast — don't wait until day 4 or 5 of the sale for genuinely popular items.

## Common Preparation Mistakes to Avoid

**Waiting to decide what to buy until the sale starts** — this virtually guarantees missing the best flash-deal windows on popular items.

**Not verifying price history** — trusting a displayed "% off" badge without checking whether the "regular price" is genuine.

**Forgetting to check bank offer terms in advance** — some offers require registering or opting in before the sale, not automatically applying at checkout.
