## What to Verify Before Buying

**Output voltage compatibility:** Confirm the UPS's DC output matches your specific router/ONT's power adapter requirements (voltage and connector type vary by device).

**Combined power draw:** If backing up both a router and a separate ONT/modem, verify total combined power draw stays within the UPS's rated capacity.
