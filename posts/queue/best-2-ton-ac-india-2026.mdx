## When You Actually Need 2 Ton

**Room 200-280 sq ft:** Standard 2 ton recommendation.

**Open-plan living/dining rooms:** Even at slightly smaller square footage, open layouts benefit from 2 ton capacity since cooled air disperses across a larger connected volume.

**Rooms with heavy sun exposure (west/south-facing in India):** Consider sizing up to 2 ton even for spaces that would nominally suggest 1.5 ton, since solar heat gain adds substantial cooling load.

**Rooms with 4+ regular occupants:** Body heat from multiple people adds meaningful cooling load that pushes borderline spaces toward needing 2 ton.
