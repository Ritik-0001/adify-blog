## How Mineralizer Technology Works

**True Mineralizer (AO Smith MIN-TECH):** A post-RO cartridge containing calcium and magnesium salts. Purified water passes through the cartridge and dissolves small quantities of minerals to reach 50–150 ppm TDS. The cartridge depletes and requires replacement every 6–12 months (₹800–₁,500/year).

**TDS Controller (Kent's method):** A blend valve (not a mineralizer) mixes a small fraction of unfiltered tap water with RO-purified water. This maintains TDS by blending rather than adding minerals. Advantage: no additional cartridge to replace. Disadvantage: the blended tap water brings back a small fraction of whatever was in the original water (including contaminants).

**Copper Mineralizer (Aquaguard Active Copper):** A copper cartridge that adds trace copper to purified water. Copper vessels for water storage are traditional in Indian households (Ayurvedic practice). Aquaguard's implementation adds scientifically relevant copper levels (below 1 ppm WHO limit).

**Should You Buy a Mineralizer Purifier?**

Mineralizer purifiers are worth the premium if:
- You consume primarily RO-purified water (most of your daily water intake)
- Your diet is low in calcium and magnesium (limited dairy, vegetables)
- You prefer the slightly mineral-enhanced taste of water at 80–120 ppm TDS

They're not necessary if:
- You have a varied diet with adequate mineral sources
- You're buying for a household with a traditional Indian diet (dairy-rich)
