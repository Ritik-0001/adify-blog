## How to Choose a QLED TV

Understand the technology. QLED is an LED TV with a quantum dot layer, so it does not have the per-pixel contrast of OLED. It excels in brightness and colour, which suits bright rooms.

Check local dimming and HDR. Better dimming zones improve contrast, and HDR peak brightness matters for high dynamic range content.

Look at refresh rate and gaming features. A 120Hz panel with VRR suits console gaming.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Assuming QLED equals OLED.** They are different technologies.

**Ignoring dimming zones.** They affect contrast.

**Skipping the sound.** Plan for a soundbar.

**Buying on discount alone.** Check the model.

## What to Check on the Listing

Confirm the panel type, local dimming, refresh rate, HDR formats and warranty. See whether installation and a wall mount are included.

## Quick FAQ

**Is QLED worth it over LED?** For colour and brightness, yes.

**Is QLED as good as OLED?** OLED has deeper blacks, and QLED is brighter.

**How long do they last?** Seven to ten years.

## Real-World Scenarios

**A bright living room with large windows.** QLED's brightness helps the picture hold up in daylight.

**A family that streams a lot.** Look for a platform with all your apps and a remote that suits everyone.

**A sports household.** Check motion handling and refresh rate, since fast action benefits from smooth processing.

**A console gamer on a budget.** Some QLEDs offer 120Hz and VRR, so check the specification.

## Setup and Calibration

Start with the Movie or Filmmaker picture mode, adjust brightness for your room and disable unnecessary processing. Update the software, and enable HDR if your sources support it. If you plan to add a soundbar, connect it through eARC for the best audio.

## QLED vs Mini-LED vs OLED

QLED adds quantum dots to an LED backlight for stronger colour and brightness. Mini-LED uses many tiny backlight zones for better contrast, and can be paired with QLED, which is why some sets are labelled both. OLED lights every pixel individually for the deepest blacks. Under ₹70,000 you are mostly choosing between QLED and Mini-LED QLED, and the Mini-LED versions give noticeably better contrast in dark scenes if you can find one at a fair price.
