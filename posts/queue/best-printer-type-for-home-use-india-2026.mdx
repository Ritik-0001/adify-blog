## The Three Printer Types Explained

### Type 1: Traditional Inkjet (Cartridge)
**Examples:** HP DeskJet 2331, Canon PIXMA E3370, Epson L455 (older cartridge models)
**Price:** ₹3,000–₆,000
**Running cost:** ₹3–₈ per color page, ₹1–₂ per black page
**Best for:** Very low volume (under 10 pages/month) where cartridge cost is acceptable

Traditional inkjet printers are the most common mistake purchase in India. The low upfront price attracts buyers who then discover that ink cartridges cost ₹700–₁,000 per set and print 150–200 pages. Avoid unless you genuinely print very rarely.

### Type 2: Ink Tank Inkjet (EcoTank / MegaTank / Smart Tank)
**Examples:** Epson L3252, Canon G2020, HP Smart Tank 529
**Price:** ₹8,000–₁₅,000
**Running cost:** ₹0.10–₀.20 per color page, ₹0.05–₀.10 per black page
**Best for:** Home users who print 20–300 pages/month

Ink tank printers have a large built-in reservoir filled from ink bottles (not cartridges). Replacement bottles cost ₹500–₇₀₀ and last 4,000–7,000 pages. This is the recommended type for 90% of Indian home buyers.

### Type 3: Laser (Toner-based)
**Examples:** HP LaserJet M141a, Canon LBP6230, Brother HL-L2321D
**Price:** ₹8,000–₁₅,000 (mono), ₹25,000+ (color)
**Running cost:** ₹0.40–₁.20 per black page, ₹5–₁₅ per color page
**Best for:** High-volume black-and-white document printing (invoices, forms, reports)

Laser printers use toner powder instead of ink. Advantages: sharp text, fast printing (20+ ppm), no ink clogging. Limitations: most affordable laser printers are black-and-white only; color laser printers cost ₹25,000+ and have expensive toner.

## Photo Printing at Home

For photo printing specifically, inkjet technology (both cartridge and ink tank) produces better color gradients than laser. However:

- Standard ink-tank printers (Epson L3252, Canon G3020) print photos at adequate quality for framing at 4x6 and 5x7 sizes.
- Dedicated photo printers (Canon PIXMA iP8720, Epson EcoTank ET-8550 at ₹25,000–₄₀,000) use 6+ color ink sets for professional photo quality.

For occasional home photo prints, the standard Epson L3252 is adequate. For professional photo output, use a print shop (most Indian photo studios charge ₹15–₂₅ per print for 4x6 photos, which is cheaper than owning a dedicated photo printer at home volumes).

**Recommendation:** Ink-tank inkjet for the vast majority of Indian homes — the Epson L3252 at ₹8,500–₉,500 is the right choice for students, families, and WFH adults who need color printing, scanning, and copying at sustainable running costs.
