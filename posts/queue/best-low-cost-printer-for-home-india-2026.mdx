## The Real Cost of Home Printing

**Cartridge printer scenario** (e.g., HP DeskJet 2331 at ₹3,499):
- Printer: ₹3,499
- HP 682 cartridge set: ₹750, prints ~150 color pages
- If you print 100 color pages/month: 8 cartridge sets/year = ₹6,000/year
- Year 1 total cost: ₹9,499
- Year 2 total cost: ₹6,000 (just ink)
- 3-year total: ₹21,499

**Ink-tank printer scenario** (Epson L3252 at ₹8,999):
- Printer: ₹8,999
- Ink bottle set: ₹600, prints 4,500 color pages
- If you print 100 color pages/month: ~1.5 bottle sets/year = ₹900/year
- Year 1 total cost: ₹9,899
- Year 2 total cost: ₹900
- 3-year total: ₹11,699

**Saving over 3 years: ₹9,800** — and the Epson costs almost the same in year 1.
