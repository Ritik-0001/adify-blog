Buying the right office chair in India in 2026 comes down to four checks in order of importance: genuine dynamic lumbar support (not just "ergonomic" marketing language), breathable mesh over solid foam, multi-directional adjustable armrests, and a weight capacity comfortably above your actual body weight — getting these four fundamentals right matters more than brand name or overall price.

Most office chair buying mistakes happen because shoppers trust marketing terms like "ergonomic" or "premium" without verifying the specific engineering features that actually deliver comfort and support over years of daily use.

## Check 1: Dynamic (Not Fixed) Lumbar Support

The lumbar pad should move and adjust as you shift position throughout the day — a fixed foam bump marketed as "ergonomic" provides meaningfully less real support than genuine dynamic mechanisms.

## Check 2: Breathable Mesh Over Solid Foam

Mesh distributes weight evenly and allows airflow, preventing the heat buildup and pressure points that solid foam cushions develop over 6-8 hour workdays.

## Check 3: Multi-Directional Adjustable Armrests

Height, width, and depth adjustment (not just height alone) accommodate different desk heights and typing postures correctly — look for "4D" armrests specifically.

## Check 4: Weight Capacity With Margin

Verify the rated capacity comfortably exceeds your body weight — chairs running near their maximum rated limit wear out mechanisms faster than chairs with margin.
