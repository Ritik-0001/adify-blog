## Why the Gap Is So Large

**Cartridge economics:** Small ink cartridges (₹500-₹1,200 each) print only 150-300 pages, and the packaging/mechanism overhead is spread across a small page volume.

**Ink tank economics:** Bulk ink bottles (₹400-₶00) print 6,000-7,500 pages, spreading the cost across a vastly larger volume with minimal packaging overhead per page.

---

## The Only Scenario Where Cartridge Makes Sense

Genuinely minimal printing — under 10-15 pages monthly — where the ink tank's ₹5,000-₸,000 higher upfront cost never gets recovered through per-page savings within a reasonable few years of ownership.

---

## Recommendation

For any household or office printing more than occasionally, ink tank technology from Epson, Canon, or HP delivers dramatically lower total cost than cartridge printers — the specific brand matters less than committing to ink tank technology itself as the fundamental cost-saving decision.
