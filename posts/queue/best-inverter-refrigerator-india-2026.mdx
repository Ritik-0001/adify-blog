## Why Inverter Compressors Matter More for Refrigerators Than You'd Expect

Refrigerators run continuously, every hour of every day, unlike ACs that operate only when needed. This means the efficiency difference between inverter and non-inverter compressors compounds over far more operating hours annually — a refrigerator's inverter advantage often delivers larger absolute electricity savings over its lifetime than an AC's inverter advantage, despite refrigerators drawing less peak power.

**Quieter operation:** Inverter compressors run at variable, generally lower speeds most of the time, producing less audible motor noise than fixed-speed compressors cycling at full power then shutting off repeatedly.

**Compressor longevity:** Continuous modulated operation puts less mechanical stress on the compressor than repeated full-power starts, which is often the highest-wear moment for any motor.
