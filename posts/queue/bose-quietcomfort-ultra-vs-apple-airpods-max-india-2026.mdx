## Weight and comfort

This is the clearest difference. The AirPods Max weigh around 385 grams, mostly because of the stainless steel frame and aluminium cups. The Bose QuietComfort Ultra weigh roughly 250 grams. That 130 gram gap is noticeable after two hours, especially in warm Indian weather where heavier cups feel sweaty. The Bose uses a plush headband and ear cushions designed for long wear, while the AirPods Max clamp firmly and press on the neck when you look down.

## Noise cancelling

Both are among the best in the category. Bose has long been known for strong low-frequency cancellation, which suits flights, trains and the hum of an air conditioner. AirPods Max cancel noise very well, with slightly more natural transparency mode, which helps when talking to a flight attendant or crossing a road. For a commuter on noisy Indian streets, either works; the Bose often holds the edge on steady drone, and the Apple on awareness.

## Sound

AirPods Max deliver a balanced, detailed sound with strong spatial audio when used with Apple devices and head tracking. Bose QC Ultra has a warmer sound with adjustable EQ in its app and Immersive Audio spatial mode. Neither beats audiophile wired headphones, but both sound refined. If you prefer to adjust bass and treble, Bose's EQ is easier to use.

## Ecosystem and connectivity

AirPods Max pair instantly with iPhones, switch automatically among Apple devices and support features such as Find My. On Android, they work as normal Bluetooth headphones but lose most smart features. Bose works with iOS and Android through the Bose app, supports Bluetooth multipoint (connect to two devices at once) and uses a more modern Bluetooth codec set including aptX Adaptive on Android. AirPods Max do not support multipoint across non-Apple devices.

## Battery and charging

Bose quotes up to around 24 hours with noise cancelling on. AirPods Max quote about 20 hours. The Bose also charges via USB-C and ships with a wired cable that works with audio. The AirPods Max USB-C version supports wired listening through a USB-C audio cable sold separately, and older versions need a Lightning to 3.5 mm cable. If you fly, a cable matters for in-flight entertainment.

## Calls

Bose's microphones suppress background noise well for calls. AirPods Max have good microphones and work smoothly with iPhone, though wind noise outdoors can bother some users. Both are fine for work calls from a home office.

## Cables and extras

A spare USB-C audio cable is useful for flights. [Search USB-C to 3.5mm audio cables](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=USB-C+to+3.5mm+audio+cable&tag=adifystore-21) before you travel.

## Case and build

The AirPods Max Smart Case puts the headphones into an ultra-low power mode, but it has been criticised as awkward and not protective of the headband. The Bose case is a more conventional hard shell that fits in a bag. The AirPods Max look luxurious and come in several colours. Bose uses plastic and metal, lighter and arguably less premium to touch.

## Price gap

The AirPods Max cost roughly ₹20,000 to ₹25,000 more than the Bose in India. For that gap you get an Apple badge, premium build and tight Apple device integration, but little else.
