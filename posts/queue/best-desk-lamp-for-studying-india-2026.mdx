## The Science of Study Lighting

**Illuminance:** The recommended illuminance for reading is 300–500 lux at the reading surface. A 9–12W LED desk lamp positioned 40–50cm from the page produces approximately 300–500 lux at the book surface. Ceiling lights from 2–3 metres provide 50–150 lux at desk level — insufficient for extended reading.

**Colour temperature for studying:** 4000–5000K (neutral to cool white) promotes alertness and focus — ideal for active studying. 2700–3000K (warm white) is more relaxing — better for reading before sleep.

**Flicker and the visual system:** Even flicker invisible to conscious perception (100Hz AC frequency) creates subconscious eye muscle fatigue. Flicker-free lamps use DC current to LEDs (or high-frequency PWM above 3,000Hz) — measurably reduce eye fatigue in research settings.

## Lamp Positioning Tips

For maximum eye care benefit:
- Position lamp to the left (right-handers) or right (left-handers) of the work surface — not directly behind
- Lamp head 40–50cm from reading material
- Angle lamp downward at 30–45° from vertical to illuminate the page evenly
- Avoid positioning where reflected light from the page bounces into your eyes
