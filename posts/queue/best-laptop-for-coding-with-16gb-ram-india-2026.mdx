## How to Choose a Coding Laptop

Check CPU and RAM. A recent multi-core processor and 16GB RAM suit most development, and heavy workloads like Android emulators or machine learning benefit from more RAM and a dedicated GPU.

Look at the keyboard and display. You will type and read all day, so a good keyboard and a sharp, bright screen of 14 to 16 inches matter.

Think about battery and weight. A lighter laptop with long battery life is easier to carry, and fast SSD storage of 512GB or more speeds up builds.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Buying 8GB RAM.** It limits multitasking.

**Ignoring the display.** Low-resolution screens tire your eyes.

**Choosing on name only.** Check the exact configuration.

**Skipping warranty checks.** Service reach matters.

## Real-World Scenarios

**A student developer.** A mid-range IdeaPad or Vivobook with 16GB RAM.

**A professional developer.** A ThinkPad or MacBook Air.

**A mobile developer.** A laptop with more RAM and a strong CPU.

## What to Check on the Listing

Confirm the CPU, the RAM and whether it is soldered, the SSD size, the display resolution and brightness, the weight, the battery claim and the warranty. See which ports are included.

## Setting Up for Development

Install your tools through a package manager, keep the OS updated, use version control and back up your projects. Consider a Linux or WSL setup if your workflow needs it.

## Warranty and Service

Compare the warranty and the service reach, and check accidental damage options. Keep the invoice and serial number.

## Quick FAQ

**Is 16GB enough for coding?** Yes for most work.

**Do I need a GPU?** Only for ML, games or video work.

**Windows or Mac?** Depends on your tools and budget.

## What Else Matters for Developers

Beyond RAM, look for a good keyboard with decent key travel, a screen with at least 1920 by 1080 resolution and brightness around 300 nits, and enough ports for peripherals. A webcam and microphone matter for remote work. If you use virtual machines or containers heavily, consider 32GB RAM or a model with upgradeable memory slots.

## Final Checklist Before You Buy

Before you order, check the exact configuration, such as processor, memory, storage and display, because the same model name can come in several variants. Confirm the seller is authorised and the warranty is valid in India, check accessory compatibility and keep the invoice and serial number for service claims.
