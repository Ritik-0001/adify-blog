## Why the Mini 4 Pro Suits Beginners Specifically

**Sub-249g weight class:** Qualifies for DGCA's simplified "Nano" drone category, reducing registration and certification complexity compared to heavier drones.

**Omnidirectional obstacle sensing:** Detects obstacles from all directions and can automatically avoid them — genuinely reduces the crash risk that comes with unfamiliarity.

**Auto-return-to-home:** If you lose visual line of sight or signal connection, the drone automatically returns to its takeoff point — a critical safety net for beginners still learning spatial awareness while flying.

**Beginner flight modes:** Includes simplified flight modes with capped speed and altitude for initial practice sessions.

## Before Your First Flight

**Register with DGCA's Digital Sky platform** even for Nano category drones — verify current requirements as regulations can update.

**Practice in open, obstacle-free areas first** before attempting more complex shots, regardless of the drone's built-in safety features.

**Check local no-fly zone restrictions** (airports, government buildings, certain public spaces) using the Digital Sky app before any flight.
