## What to Prioritise Under ₹8,000

**Must have:**
- Full HD (1920x1080) — don't buy 1366x768 at any price
- HDMI port — VGA-only monitors should be avoided for modern laptops and PCs
- Anti-glare coating — reduces reflections in lit rooms
- Brand warranty — 1–3 years from LG, Dell, BenQ, Acer, HP

**Can compromise on:**
- IPS vs TN panel — TN is acceptable for general use, not ideal for colour work
- Size — 19.5" vs 21.5" is manageable at a desk
- Built-in speakers — external speakers or headphones are better anyway
- Stand adjustability — tilt-only stands are standard at this price
