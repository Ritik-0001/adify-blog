## How to Choose an 8 Camera CCTV System

Plan coverage first. List entrances, gates, driveways, corners and indoor common areas. Assign cameras to cover each with overlap, and consider varifocal or wide-angle lenses where needed.

Size the storage. Eight cameras at 5MP produce a lot of data. A 2TB drive typically stores around two weeks at continuous recording, and 4TB or more is better for longer retention or motion-only recording.

Think about cabling and power. Wired DVR systems need coaxial or power cables to each camera, and PoE systems use one network cable. Plan for a UPS, surge protection and a secure location for the recorder.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Poor placement.** Avoid glare and backlighting.

**Undersized storage.** Increase the drive.

**Default passwords.** Change them.

**Skipping maintenance.** Clean lenses and check recordings.

## Real-World Scenarios

**A villa with a gate and garden.** Cover the gate, driveway, sides and back.

**A shop or small office.** Cover the entrance, counter, stock area and back door.

**A small apartment complex.** Consult a professional for coverage.

## What to Check on the Listing

## Installation Costs and Planning

With eight cameras, cabling is the biggest job, so budget for professional installation and a written scope covering cable, power supplies, conduits and remote viewing setup. A good installer will survey the property, test camera angles before drilling and label each cable.

## Recording Modes and Retention

Continuous recording gives the most complete record but uses the most storage, while motion-triggered recording saves space. Decide how many days of footage you need, calculate storage from the bitrate, and consider a surveillance-grade hard drive designed for continuous writes.

## Cameras Types and Placement

Bullet cameras suit outdoor walls and long-range views, dome cameras suit indoor and ceiling mounts, and PTZ cameras add remote pan and tilt for large areas. Mount cameras at least two and a half metres high, angle them to avoid direct sun and floodlight glare, and use weatherproof enclosures outdoors.
