## How Instant Geysers Work

Traditional storage geysers heat a full 15–25L tank before you can shower. Instant geysers have a small heating chamber (1–3L) and a powerful heating element (3000–4500W) that heats water as it flows through.

**Benefit:** Hot water in 30–60 seconds, no pre-heating wait.

**Limitation:** Water flow rate is limited. Instant geysers provide 1.5–3 litres/minute — adequate for a shower but not for fast bucket filling. They cannot supply 2 outlets simultaneously.

## Instant vs Storage — Which for Indian Bathrooms?

**Choose instant if:**
- Bathroom space is limited
- Only 1 person showers in the morning
- Rental apartment where you can't drill for large bracket
- Need hot water at kitchen sink point

**Choose storage (15–25L) if:**
- Family of 3–4 taking sequential morning showers
- Want hot water ready without waiting when you get up
- Pressure bucket filling (cooking rice, chai) needed

**Recommendation:** Racold Pronto NXT for compact apartments needing shower-point instant hot water. Bajaj Fastflo for kitchen sink only. For families — storage geyser (Bajaj Calenta 15–25L) is better suited.
