## How to Choose a Travel Action Camera

Think about weight and battery. For treks, light weight and long battery life matter, and spare batteries are essential in cold weather.

Look at stabilisation and low light. Smooth footage on rough terrain and clear video at dawn and dusk make travel videos better.

Check waterproofing and protection. Rain, river crossings and dust are common, so look for a good waterproof rating and consider a lens guard.

## On the Trail

Keep the camera in a padded pouch, rinse and dry the seals after water use and avoid leaving it in a hot car or a closed bag in the sun. A lens guard costs little next to the scratch it prevents, and a slow memory card can stop recording mid-clip, so buy a fast rated one. Cold drains batteries quickly, so keep spares in an inner pocket close to your body.

## Storage and Power on Long Trips

Carry two or three spare batteries, a compact charger and a power bank that can charge via USB-C. Use high-speed memory cards of 128 GB or more and keep a spare. Back up footage each night to a laptop or an external drive if you can, and format the card in the camera before each day of shooting.

## Who Should Skip This

If your trips are mostly city breaks and you shoot short clips, your phone will likely cover it without another battery to charge. Casual trekkers who rarely edit video may also leave a 360 camera at home, since reframing every clip takes more time than most people want to spend.

## Choosing Between Flat and 360 Footage

A standard action camera records what you point it at, which makes editing simple and files smaller. A 360 camera records everything and lets you pick the angle afterwards, which is forgiving on a steep trail but heavy on storage and editing time. Think about whether you will really sit down and reframe clips, or whether you want something that is ready to share straight away.

## Recommendation

For most travellers, a GoPro HERO13 Black or a DJI Osmo Action 5 Pro with spare batteries is a strong choice, and an Insta360 model suits those who like reframing.
