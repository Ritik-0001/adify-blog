## Why not just use your phone or a flatbed

Phone scanning apps are fine for a single receipt. A multi-function printer's flatbed copes with one page at a time and is slow for a 40-page contract. A sheet-fed scanner pulls a stack through an automatic document feeder, scanning both sides at once, which is the only practical way to digitise files, tax papers, invoices or a child's school records. It also produces cleaner, straightened pages than a phone photo.

## Features that matter

An automatic document feeder (ADF) holds the stack, 20 to 50 sheets for home models. Duplex means both sides scan in a single pass. Speed is quoted in pages per minute for single-sided and images per minute for both sides, so a "25 ppm, 50 ipm" scanner does 25 sheets a minute duplex. Resolution of 600 dpi is more than enough. OCR software turns scans into searchable PDFs, which is what makes digitising worthwhile.

## OCR software and file organisation

OCR is what makes scanned pages searchable by text. Many scanners bundle basic OCR; higher-end ones use better engines, and you can use Adobe Acrobat, ABBYY FineReader or free tools. Scans in English OCR well; Hindi and other Indian-language accuracy varies, so test with a sample page. Build a folder structure before you start, name files by date and type, and back up to the cloud and a [portable hard drive](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=portable+external+hard+drive+2TB&tag=adifystore-21).

## Paper handling in real life

Scanners jam with staples, torn or folded pages and sticky notes. Remove staples and clips before feeding. Mixed sizes such as receipts and A4 together are tricky. Keep a [carrier sheet](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=scanner+carrier+sheet+document&tag=adifystore-21) for fragile documents, and a [scanner cleaning kit](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=scanner+roller+cleaning+kit&tag=adifystore-21) to wipe the rollers, since dust causes streaks.

## Flatbed versus sheet-fed

If you scan books, photographs or fragile old papers, a flatbed scanner such as an [Epson flatbed photo scanner](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=Epson+Perfection+flatbed+photo+scanner&tag=adifystore-21) is better, since sheet feeders can damage originals. Many offices keep one of each.

## Who should skip a document scanner

Skip it if you scan fewer than ten pages a month, because a phone app is enough. Skip it if your printer's built-in scanner has an ADF and meets your needs. Skip cheap portable pen scanners for any serious project.
