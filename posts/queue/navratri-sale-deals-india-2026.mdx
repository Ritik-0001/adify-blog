Navratri 2026 (September 22 – October 1) is India's first major festive sale window before Diwali — Amazon typically runs a "Navratri Special" sale, Flipkart runs a "Navratri Carnival," and brands like boAt, Noise, and Realme launch festive offers directly. These deals are 10–20% off, smaller than the deeper cuts during Amazon Great Indian Festival (GIF) and Flipkart Big Billion Days (BBD) in October, but they're genuine discounts — not inflated list prices.

The strategic question for buyers is: buy during Navratri at 10–15% off, or wait for GIF/BBD at 20–30% off? The answer depends on the product category, your urgency, and stock availability.

> **Recommendation:** For products under ₹5,000 (earbuds, accessories, small gadgets), buy during Navratri if the deal is good — the absolute savings gap between Navratri and GIF is small (₹200–₅₀₀). For large purchases (refrigerators, laptops, phones), wait for GIF/BBD — the larger absolute savings (₹3,000–₁₂,000) justify the 2–3 week wait. ## What to Wait for GIF/BBD Instead

### Smartphones (₹10,000+ range)

Wait for GIF/BBD. The absolute savings on a ₹15,000–₂₅,000 phone during GIF (₹2,000–₄,000 off) versus Navratri (₹500–₁,500 off) are significant enough to justify the wait.

### Laptops

Absolutely wait. Diwali GIF brings laptop deals that are ₹4,000–₸,000 better than Navratri deals.

### Refrigerators, Washing Machines, ACs

Wait for GIF. Appliance deals during Navratri are typically 10–15% off; GIF brings 20–30% off plus exchange bonuses.

### TVs

Wait for GIF/BBD. New models launch specifically for Diwali — buying a TV during Navratri may mean you get the outgoing model at marginal discount when a newer, same-priced model arrives weeks later.

## How to Spot Real Navratri Deals

**Check price history with Keepa** (Amazon) or Smartprix (cross-platform). A "40% off" on Navratri that's been at that price all September is not a deal.

**Compare platforms.** Amazon and Flipkart run simultaneous festive sales — compare prices on both for the same product.

**Look for bank card discounts.** HDFC and SBI often offer 5% instant discount (lower cap than GIF) on Navratri purchases — stacking this with the sale price gives real value on mid-range purchases.
