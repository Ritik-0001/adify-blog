## What Defines Premium Tier

**Performance-tuned motors:** Higher peak power output delivering genuinely quicker acceleration than mainstream variants of the same scooter line.

**Premium build materials and finish:** Higher-quality plastics, metal accents, and refined paint finishes distinguish flagship trims from base models.

**Advanced connected features:** Larger, more responsive touchscreen displays with onboard navigation, over-the-air software updates, and detailed ride analytics.

**Enhanced suspension and braking:** Premium models typically include upgraded suspension tuning and often disc brakes on both wheels versus drum-disc combinations on lower trims.
