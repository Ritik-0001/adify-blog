## Wired vs. Wireless Budget Headphones

**Wired (₹800–₂,000):** Better sound quality per rupee. No charging. 3.5mm works with any device. Sony, Audio-Technica, and Sennheiser entry models fall here.

**Wireless Bluetooth (₹1,000–₃,000):** Freedom from cables. Battery requirement (charging needed every 10–20 hours). Bluetooth compression slightly reduces audio quality vs. wired at same price. boAt, pTron, and JBL offer competitive options.

For sound quality priority at under ₹2,000: wired headphones win. For convenience priority (wireless, no cable): boAt Rockerz 450 or JBL Tune 510BT.

## Over-Ear vs. On-Ear at Budget

Budget headphones are typically on-ear (earpads rest on the ear, not around it). On-ear is lighter and cheaper to manufacture than over-ear. The downside: on-ear headphones cause more ear pressure discomfort during extended sessions (2+ hours). If you use headphones for 3–4 hour study sessions, look for models specifically described as "over-ear" with larger earcups.
