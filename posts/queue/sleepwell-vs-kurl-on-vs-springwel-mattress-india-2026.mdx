## Brand character at a glance

Sleepwell, from Sheela Foam, is built around polyurethane and memory foam, with ranges sold on firmness and orthopaedic claims. Kurl-on has a long history in rubberised coir and foam; its lineup leans firm, and it also sells spring mattresses. Springwel positions itself on spring-based sleeping surfaces. Brand ownership and ranges can change over time, so check the maker named on each listing.

## Sleepwell: foam-first range

Sleepwell's upper tiers combine memory foam with layered PU foam and gel or contour surfaces, often described as "dual comfort" with a soft side and a firm side. Prices for queen sizes start around ₹15,000 for decent foam and reach ₹40,000-50,000 for thicker memory foam builds. The brand has wide dealer and online availability and generally shorter delivery times, which helps if you want a mattress within a few days.

The trade-off is heat: dense memory foam traps warmth, so in warm cities consider the perforated or hybrid versions. See what the range includes in a [Sleepwell orthopaedic mattress](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=sleepwell+orthopedic+mattress&tag=adifystore-21) search.

## Kurl-on: coir and firm support

Kurl-on has deep roots in coir, and many Indian households still prefer the feel of firm support over soft cushioning. Its rubberised coir mattresses are firm and breathable and tend to cost less than memory foam of the same size, though not all of them reach the ₹15,000 floor for this site. Premium models layer coir with foam or latex, and spring mattresses add a bounce.

Coir is heavier and stiffer, and over years it can become lumpy if not rotated. If you want a firm base with a softer top, look for a coir-plus-foam layer build instead of plain coir. A [Kurl-on mattress](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=kurlon+mattress+queen+size&tag=adifystore-21) search shows what is on offer in your size.

## Springwel: spring-based surfaces

Springwel's identity is spring mattresses: bonnell and pocket-spring units with foam top layers, aimed at buyers who prefer a bouncier, more airy mattress. Spring builds ventilate better than foam, but cheaper bonnell ones transmit motion and may squeak as they age. Look for pocket coils if you share the bed, and reinforced edges if you sit on the side often. Listings can vary by seller, so be sure the brand is being sold or shipped by an authorised channel.

For a spring comparison across brands, a [pocket spring mattress queen size](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=pocket+spring+mattress+queen+size&tag=adifystore-21) search is more useful than looking at a single maker.

## Trial and warranty differences

The big retail brands generally advertise warranties on paper, commonly around 5 to 10 years, but fewer offer the 100-night in-home trial that direct-to-consumer brands do. That matters: if you are buying mostly on description, a trial is the only way to correct a wrong firmness choice. If a store-bought mattress does not come with a trial, make sure the retailer allows a reasonable exchange window and ask what the warranty covers, since sagging under about 25 mm is often excluded.

Keep the invoice, warranty card and a photo of the mattress label; claims need them. Also check that the warranty is valid with the mattress used on a proper slatted or solid base, a condition many brands attach.

## Which brand suits which sleeper

Pick Sleepwell if you want a soft-to-medium feel, have shoulder or hip pressure, and sleep in an air-conditioned room. Pick Kurl-on if you grew up on a firm bed and want firm back support at a modest price. Pick Springwel if you sleep hot, prefer bounce over sink, or want a spring mattress with easy turnover.

If you are a side sleeper, memory foam is the safer first try. If you have a lower-back preference for firmness, coir-and-foam or firm spring builds are better. For couples, pocket springs reduce motion transfer, so a [Sleepwell Kurlon spring mattress](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=spring+mattress+king+size+branded&tag=adifystore-21) in king size is worth comparing.

## When none of these is the best answer

If you want a long-lasting natural latex bed, specialist latex ranges and some direct-to-consumer brands may offer more in the ₹25,000-50,000 band. If you want the longest free trial, Wakefit, Sleepyhead and The Sleep Company usually advertise about 100 nights. And if you are budget-bound under about ₹15,000, you may be better with a good coir-foam mattress or a topper over your existing bed. ## Recommendation

For a foam, memory-foam-led mattress that is easy to find and replace: Sleepwell. For a firm Indian-style mattress with coir support: Kurl-on. For spring sleepers: Springwel, preferably pocket coil. Whichever brand you choose, confirm the exact layers, the warranty terms and the return window before you pay.
