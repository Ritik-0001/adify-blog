## Why to Avoid Generic Android Boxes

Generic "X96," "H96," "MXQ Pro" Android TV boxes sold under ₹1,500–₂,000 have significant issues:

- Android 7 or 8 — many OTT apps (Netflix, Hotstar) require Android 9+
- No Widevine L1 certification — means no HD streaming on Netflix (you get SD quality only)
- No regular security or app updates
- Inconsistent app compatibility
- Often have unofficial modified Android builds

**Important:** Netflix requires Widevine L1 DRM for HD/FHD streaming. Generic boxes typically have Widevine L3, giving you only 480p Netflix even with a FHD TV.
