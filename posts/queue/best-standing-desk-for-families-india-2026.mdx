## Why Families Need a Different Standing Desk

**Multiple heights:** The ergonomic desk height for a 5'2" adult is approximately 70cm seated, 100cm standing. For a 5'10" adult: 76cm seated, 112cm standing. A fixed desk at 75cm is too high for shorter users, too low for taller users.

**Children's ergonomics:** Children's ergonomic desk height: typically 55–65cm for primary school age (8–12 years), 60–70cm for secondary (12–16 years). A fixed 75cm adult desk forces children to hunch, creating poor posture habits.

**Shared space efficiency:** Urban Indian homes rarely have space for multiple dedicated desks. A shared adjustable desk that serves the whole family eliminates this constraint.

## Family Standing Desk Tips

**Anti-fatigue mat:** A standing mat (₹1,500–₃,000) is essential for family members who stand for 30+ minutes. Standing on hard Indian marble or tile floors causes leg and back fatigue that makes standing desk use short-lived.

**Cable management:** With multiple family members using the same desk, cable tangle becomes an issue. Cable trays (₹500–₁,000) mounted under the desk top keep charging cables organized.

**Monitor arm:** If multiple family members use the same monitor, a monitor arm (₹2,500–₄,000) allows height and angle adjustment without a riser — more flexible than a fixed riser.

**Recommendation:** Flexispot E1 for families genuinely committed to ergonomic use — the memory presets and one-touch adjustment make it practical to switch between users. Fixed wooden study table with monitor riser for families where budget is constrained — a good monitor riser improves ergonomics significantly even without desk height adjustment.
