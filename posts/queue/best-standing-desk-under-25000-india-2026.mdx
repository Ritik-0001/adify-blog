## Setup and Assembly

Electric standing desks require assembly — typically 45–90 minutes with one person. The main frame, legs, and motor assembly is manageable with included tools. Tabletop attachment is straightforward. Cable routing through the included cable management spine takes additional time.

Most Amazon-sold Flexispot desks don't include assembly services — budget ₹500–₁,000 for Urban Clap or local assembly if you prefer not to self-assemble.
