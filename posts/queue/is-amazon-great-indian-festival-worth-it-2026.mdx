## When GIF Is Genuinely Worth It

**You have a planned purchase and the timing aligns:** If you were already going to buy a laptop, TV, or appliance in the next few months, waiting for GIF and buying during the sale is straightforwardly the right financial decision.

**The category sees genuinely deep discounts:** Laptops, TVs, and premium electronics see 15-25%+ off — meaningful enough to justify timing your planned purchase around the sale.

**You've verified the discount is real:** Checking price history confirms you're not paying the same amount you'd pay any other time of year under a misleading "sale" label.

---

## When GIF Isn't Worth It

**You're buying something you didn't need just because it's discounted:** A 40% off badge on a product you weren't planning to buy is still 100% unnecessary spending.

**The specific product's discount is minor or fake:** Some listings inflate "regular price" before the sale — verify with Keepa or similar tools before assuming a genuine deal.

**You're financing a discretionary purchase you can't comfortably afford**, even with no-cost EMI — spreading payments doesn't make an unnecessary purchase necessary.

---

## Recommendation

Treat GIF as a timing optimization for purchases you were already planning, not as a reason to generate new purchase decisions — build your shortlist based on genuine need in the weeks before the sale, then let GIF determine when you buy, not whether you buy.
