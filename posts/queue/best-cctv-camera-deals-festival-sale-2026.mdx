## What to Check Before Buying

**Indoor vs outdoor rating:** Wi-Fi cameras built for indoor use typically aren't weatherproof — confirm the IP rating if you need gate or balcony coverage.

**Local vs cloud storage costs.** Some brands include a microSD slot for free local storage; others push a paid cloud subscription for recorded footage — factor this into the real ongoing cost.

**Number of cameras needed.** Most homes need 2-3 cameras (entrance, living area, and one more) rather than a single unit, so budget for the total setup, not just one camera's price.
