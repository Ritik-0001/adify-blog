## What Makes a Good Photo Printer

**Colour ink count:** Standard printers use 4 colours (CMYK). Photo printers use 6 or more (adding light cyan, light magenta, sometimes grey) — produces smoother skin tones, more accurate colours, and better gradients.

**Resolution:** 4800×1200 dpi or higher for photo printing. Documents can look fine at 600 dpi — photos need more.

**Paper compatibility:** Must handle glossy photo paper, matte photo paper, and fine art paper. Feed from multiple trays.

**Borderless printing:** Photos without white borders need borderless support.
