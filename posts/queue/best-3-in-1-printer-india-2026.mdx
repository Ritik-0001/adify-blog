## 3-in-1 Printer Feature Checklist

**Print:** Color + black and white. Minimum 1200 DPI for sharp text. 5760 DPI for photos.

**Scan:** Flatbed scanner captures A4 documents, photos, certificates. Resolution: 600 DPI minimum. 1200 DPI for archiving photos.

**Copy:** Standalone copying without a computer. Useful for quickly duplicating forms, notes, and documents.

**ADF (Automatic Document Feeder):** Higher-end models add ADF — feeds multiple pages into the scanner automatically. Essential for scanning multi-page contracts or books. Not available under ₹13,000.

## 3-in-1 vs Separate Devices

Some home offices wonder whether to buy a dedicated printer + dedicated scanner. The answer for Indian home use is almost always the 3-in-1:

- 3-in-1 printers from Epson/Canon/HP scan at quality adequate for 95% of home needs
- Separate flatbed scanners cost ₹4,000–₹8,000 for no quality advantage at home volumes
- Space: one machine vs two

Only buy a standalone dedicated scanner if you're digitising large photo archives (500+ photos) or scanning professional artwork — dedicated scanners at ₹8,000+ offer better colour accuracy for those specific tasks.

**Recommendation:** Epson L3252 for most homes — the best balance of price, ink economy, WiFi connectivity, and scan/copy capability under ₹10,000. HP Smart Tank 615 if ADF (multi-page scanning) is important for your workflow.
