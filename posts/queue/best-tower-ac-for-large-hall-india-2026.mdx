## How to Choose a Tower AC for a Hall

Start with the room size and layout. A hall of 300 to 450 square feet may need 2 to 3 ton of cooling, depending on sun exposure, height and the number of people.

Check airflow and placement. A tower AC throws air higher and farther, so place it where air can circulate and avoid obstructions.

Plan installation and power. Tower units need space, a drain and a strong electrical supply, so involve an installer and an electrician.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Underestimating the load.** Large halls with glass need more capacity.

**Ignoring power supply.** Check the wiring.

**Blocking airflow.** Keep space around the unit.

**Skipping professional installation.** Use a certified technician.

## Real-World Scenarios

**A large living hall.** A 2 ton tower or two splits.

**A small showroom.** A tower AC with a clear airflow path.

**A function hall.** Cassette or ducted units, planned professionally.

## What to Check on the Listing

Confirm the capacity, the star rating, the airflow, the power requirement, the warranty and the installation terms. See whether the model is currently in stock and who services it.

## Splits Versus a Single Tower

Two smaller splits cost about the same as one tower and give redundancy, since one keeps working if the other fails. A tower suits spaces where wall mounting is not possible, while splits are often neater and quieter.

## Quick FAQ

**Is a tower AC better than a split?** For very large spaces, it can be, but splits are often more flexible.

**Do they need a stabiliser?** Check the manual and local voltage.

**Are they noisy?** Models vary, so check.

## Electricity and Power Planning

Large ACs draw significant current, so check the rated power of the unit and the capacity of your wiring and MCB. A dedicated line is usually advised, and a voltage stabiliser may be needed if your area has fluctuations. Use an electrician to check the load before ordering.

## Warranty and Service

Ask about warranty on the compressor and the whole unit, and whether the brand offers an annual maintenance contract for large systems. For commercial or large-hall use, service response time matters, so check availability in your city before buying.
