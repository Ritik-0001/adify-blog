## How to Choose a 12 Place Dishwasher

Check the racks and layout. Adjustable upper racks and a deep lower rack help with tall pots, and a cutlery tray or basket suits Indian cutlery use.

Look at programmes and water use. An intensive cycle for oily cookware, an eco cycle and a half-load option add flexibility.

Check installation, hard-water support and warranty. Ask whether installation is included and whether the machine has a built-in softener or needs salt.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Ignoring water hardness.** Hard water leaves spots and scale. Use salt and rinse aid as the manual advises.

**Not planning space.** Freestanding units need space for door opening and hoses.

**Loading incorrectly.** Face dirty surfaces toward the spray arms and avoid nesting items.

## Quick FAQ

**Is 12 place enough for four people?** Yes, for most families.

**Can I wash a pressure cooker?** Usually the pot, yes. Check gaskets and valves for care instructions.

**How long does a cycle take?** It varies by programme, from under an hour to a few hours.

## Loading Tips for Indian Cookware

Scrape off thick residue but skip heavy pre-rinsing. Place large kadhais and pots on the lower rack facing the spray arm, and keep plates and bowls on their designated slots. Tall items such as pressure cooker bases work best at the sides, and small items such as spoons belong in the cutlery basket.

Avoid loading wooden utensils, aluminium items that may discolour, and non-stick pans unless the maker says they are dishwasher safe. Stack in a way that lets water reach every surface.

## Running Costs and Ownership

Budget for detergent, rinse aid and salt, and clean the filter weekly. Hard water raises maintenance needs, and a model with a softener helps. Confirm the warranty and the service network in your city before you buy.

## What to Check on the Listing

Look at the number of racks, the rack adjustability, the programmes offered and the noise rating in decibels. Confirm the water and energy ratings, the warranty on the motor and the control board, and whether installation is free. Read recent reviews for hard-water performance and service response.
