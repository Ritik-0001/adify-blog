## Laser vs Inkjet for Documents — Why Laser Wins

**Text sharpness:** Laser toner fuses to paper at a molecular level. Inkjet ink sits on top of paper and can bleed slightly, especially on low-quality paper. For official documents and contracts, laser text is visibly crisper.

**Speed:** HP LaserJet M141w prints 20 pages per minute. Epson L3252 prints 10 pages per minute. For a 20-page document: laser = 1 minute, inkjet = 2 minutes.

**Consistency:** Inkjet nozzles clog if the printer is unused for weeks — common in India where printing is sporadic. Laser printers are unaffected by inactivity.

**Paper type tolerance:** Laser printers handle poor-quality paper (common in India) better than inkjet. Ink smearing on rough or low-GSM paper is an inkjet problem.
