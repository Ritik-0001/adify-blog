## Brand Focus

Elica is an Italian brand known for range hoods and chimneys, and it focuses heavily on filterless technology and suction performance. Faber is also of Italian origin and is known for a wide range of kitchen appliances, with chimneys designed to suit modern kitchens.

Both have manufacturing and service presence in India. The difference is emphasis: Elica leans towards performance and filterless designs, while Faber leans towards style and features.

## Suction and Performance

Both brands offer chimneys with suction ranging from about 1000 to 1500 m3/hr in popular models. For heavy Indian cooking, aim for 1200 or more from either brand.

Actual performance also depends on duct length, installation height and hob size. A well-installed chimney from either brand will outperform a poorly installed one from the other.

## Auto-Clean and Maintenance

Both offer auto-clean models that collect oil in a tray. Elica is widely associated with filterless designs, which avoid mesh filters altogether. Faber offers auto-clean and baffle options, and some models add motion or gesture control.

Check what each model uses. Filterless designs are easier to maintain, while baffle filters need periodic cleaning.

## Design and Controls

Faber often offers slim, curved-glass or angled designs with touch panels and gesture control. Elica also offers touch controls and a range of finishes, with a more functional look on many models.

Pick the design that fits your kitchen, and check the controls on the listing.

## Price and Service

Prices are similar for comparable models, roughly ₹15,000–25,000 for 60 cm auto-clean chimneys. Sale periods change prices, so compare the same suction and features.

Both have service networks in major cities. Check for a service centre in your area and confirm the warranty on the motor and the product.

## How to Choose Between Elica and Faber

Start with suction, width and auto-clean type. Then compare design, controls and price. If two models match, choose based on service in your city and recent feedback for that model.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Choosing by brand alone.** Model-level specifications matter more.

**Ignoring installation.** A poor installation reduces suction. Confirm what is included.

**Overlooking noise.** Higher suction can be louder, so check reviews.

## Quick FAQ

**Which is better for heavy frying?** Either, as long as suction is 1200 m3/hr or above and installation is good.

**Which is quieter?** It depends on the model. Check reviews for noise.

**Which lasts longer?** Both are well regarded. Look at the motor warranty.

## Real-World Scenarios

**A family that fries and cooks heavily every day.** Prioritise suction of 1200 m3/hr or more and a filterless auto-clean design. Elica is often the first brand to check for this profile.

**A modern kitchen where looks matter.** Look at Faber's slim and curved-glass options with touch or gesture controls, and check the noise level on reviews.

**A budget-conscious buyer.** Compare entry-level auto-clean models from both, and consider a slightly smaller suction if your cooking is light.

**A wide hob or open kitchen.** Choose a 90 cm chimney from either brand and make sure the suction and installation height match your hob.

## Installation and Upkeep

Confirm installation terms, duct length and mounting height before you order. Empty the oil collector regularly and wipe the surface after heavy cooking. A well-installed chimney from either brand will perform better than a poorly installed one.

## What to Check on the Listing

Check the motor warranty, whether installation and a duct kit are included, and whether the model is filterless or uses baffle filters. Recent buyer reviews often show real-world noise and auto-clean reliability better than the specification sheet.
