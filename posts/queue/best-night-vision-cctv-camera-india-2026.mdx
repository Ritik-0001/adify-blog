## Night Vision Technology in CCTV Cameras

**Standard IR (black and white):** Most cameras switch to black-and-white night mode using IR LED illumination when ambient light drops. Range typically 20–30 metres for basic cameras, 50–80 metres for better ones.

**Colour night vision (Starlight/Full-colour):** Uses a larger, more sensitive image sensor that captures colour even in near-dark conditions. Provides significantly more useful detail (recognise clothing colour, vehicle colour). Requires some ambient light — not functional in complete darkness without additional LEDs.

**Smart IR:** IR intensity adjusts based on distance to object — prevents overexposure (white washout) of close objects while maintaining visibility at range.
