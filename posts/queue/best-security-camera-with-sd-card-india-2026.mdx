## SD Card Storage vs Cloud Storage vs NVR

**SD Card (local):**
- Cost: One-time SD card purchase (₹400–₁,500)
- Storage: 7–60 days depending on card size and recording mode
- Risk: Card can fail, camera can be stolen (taking footage with it)
- Best for: Low-cost standalone installations

**Cloud Storage:**
- Cost: ₹200–₅₀₀/month/camera subscription
- Storage: 7–30 days in cloud
- Benefit: Footage survives if camera is stolen or damaged
- Best for: High-security areas where evidence preservation is critical

**NVR (Network Video Recorder):**
- Cost: ₹6,000–₂₀,000 NVR + 1–4TB HDD
- Storage: 15–60 days depending on camera count and HDD size
- Best for: Multi-camera installations (4+ cameras)

## MicroSD Card Recommendation for Security Cameras

Use a **high-endurance microSD card** specifically rated for security cameras — standard cards fail early under the constant write/rewrite cycles of security recording.

**Recommended:**
- Samsung PRO Endurance 128GB (₹800–₁,000) — rated for 43,800 hours of recording
- WD Purple microSD 128GB (₹700–₉₀₀) — purpose-made for surveillance

Standard cards (Sandisk Ultra, basic Kingston) can work but may fail within 6–12 months of continuous use.

**Recommendation:** TP-Link Tapo C320WS with Samsung PRO Endurance 128GB card — best outdoor SD card camera with no subscription cost. For wired multi-camera setups, use Hikvision with NVR but add SD card backup in each camera for redundancy.
