## How to Choose a Kitchen Chimney

Match the width to your hob: 60 cm for two to four burners, 90 cm for wider hobs. Check the suction in cubic metres per hour, since heavy Indian cooking works better with 1200 or more.

Choose the filter type. Filterless auto-clean chimneys are easier to maintain than baffle or mesh filters, which need regular manual cleaning.

Check installation height, duct length and noise level. Confirm the warranty and service coverage.

## Quick FAQ

**Is a chimney necessary?** For Indian cooking with frying and tadka, it helps reduce smoke and oil deposits on walls and cabinets.

**How often do I clean it?** Auto-clean models need periodic oil collector cleaning, while filter types need cleaning every few weeks.

**Ducted or ductless?** Ducted models vent outside and are more effective. Ductless recirculate air and are used where ducting is not possible.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Choosing suction that is too low.** Light cooking may be fine with a modest chimney, but heavy frying and tadka need at least 1200 m3/hr to clear smoke and oil. Under-powered units struggle and leave residue on walls.

**Ignoring duct length.** A long or bent duct reduces effective suction. Ask the installer to keep the duct as short and straight as possible.

**Mounting at the wrong height.** Most brands recommend a specific distance between the hob and the chimney. Too low is a safety risk, and too high reduces performance.

**Forgetting maintenance.** Even auto-clean models need the oil collector emptied and the surface wiped regularly.

## Installation and Timing

Book installation as soon as the chimney is delivered. Festive weeks are busy for technicians, and a chimney left in its box will not help your Diwali cooking. Confirm whether the seller or the brand handles installation and whether the duct pipe is included.

Clear the wall and check electrical points before the technician arrives. It also helps to deep-clean the kitchen walls first so that a fresh chimney starts on a clean surface.

## Keeping It Clean After Diwali

Festive cooking is the hardest test for a chimney. After the busy days, empty the oil collector, wipe the outer surface and run any auto-clean cycle the manual recommends. Doing this after heavy cooking prevents oil from hardening, which makes later cleaning far easier.
