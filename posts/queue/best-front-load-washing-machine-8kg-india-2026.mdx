## How to Choose an 8 kg Front Load

An 8 kg drum suits four people comfortably and handles bedding and jeans better than 7 kg. If your family is larger or you wash heavy items often, consider 9 kg.

Look at the motor and warranty. A direct drive or inverter motor with a multi-year or ten-year warranty is a good sign, and it is worth reading what the warranty covers.

Check water pressure, floor strength and installation. A front loader needs a level base and a nearby inlet, and hard water calls for a model with suitable features.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Choosing on price alone.** A cheaper machine with a short motor warranty or weak service can cost more in the long run.

**Overloading the drum.** Filling to the brim leads to poor cleaning and strain on the motor. Leave space for clothes to move.

**Neglecting maintenance.** Clean the drum, door seal and detergent drawer regularly to prevent odours.

## Quick FAQ

**Is 8 kg too big for two people?** It works, but you may pay more than you need. A 7 kg model may be enough for a couple.

**Do I need steam?** Steam helps with allergens and creases, but it is optional. Many users rarely use it.

**How much water does it use?** Front loaders generally use less water than top loaders, though it varies by cycle.

## Running Costs and Maintenance

Front loaders are efficient, but they reward regular care. Wipe the door seal after use, leave the door slightly open to dry the drum and run a tub clean cycle monthly. Use the recommended amount of detergent, since too much causes residue and odours over time.
