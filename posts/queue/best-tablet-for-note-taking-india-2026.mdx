## 1. Samsung Galaxy Tab S9 FE

Roughly ₹30,000–40,000. A value tablet with an included S Pen, a good display, water resistance and strong notes apps.

Having the pen in the box makes the headline price honest, and Samsung Notes handles handwriting and PDFs well. The chip is slower than in the pricier tablets, which only matters if you also edit video or play heavy games.

## 2. Samsung Galaxy Tab S10 Series

Roughly ₹50,000–90,000. Higher-end Galaxy tablets offer brighter displays, faster chips and an included S Pen, suited to heavy note takers and creators.

The brighter, larger screens help anyone writing in sunlit rooms or annotating big documents. For plain note taking, the cheaper S9 FE usually covers the need.

## 3. Apple iPad

Roughly ₹30,000–40,000 plus the Apple Pencil. The standard iPad is a popular note-taking tablet with excellent apps such as GoodNotes and Notability.

Apps built for the iPad are polished, with handwriting search and tidy notebook management. Adding the Pencil pushes the real spend up noticeably, so price the two together.

## 4. Apple iPad Air

Roughly ₹55,000–80,000 plus the Apple Pencil. The iPad Air has a better display, a faster chip and support for newer Pencil options, for heavier use.

The laminated display feels closer to writing on paper, and the extra performance helps with large PDFs and multitasking. It is a premium to pay if you only need basic notes.

## How to Choose a Note-Taking Tablet

Check the stylus. Latency, palm rejection and tilt support differ, and whether the stylus is included changes the real price.

Look at the screen. A matte or paper-like film makes writing feel better, and an 11 inch display is a good balance between portability and space.

Think about apps and storage. GoodNotes, Notability and OneNote are popular, and 128GB is enough for most notes while 256GB helps if you store PDFs and recordings.

## Taking Better Digital Notes

Use a consistent notebook structure, tag pages, back up notes to the cloud and use handwriting search. A matte film and a comfortable stylus grip make long writing sessions easier.

## Accessories That Improve Writing

A paper-like screen protector adds friction for a pen-on-paper feel, a folio case with a stand helps at a desk, and spare stylus tips are worth having. Check whether the stylus attaches magnetically and charges on the tablet, since losing or forgetting to charge a pen is a common annoyance. Back up notes to the cloud regularly.

## Who Should Skip This

If you take only a few lines of notes in meetings and prefer typing, a stylus tablet is an expensive way to do it, and a notebook and pen cost far less. Anyone who finds handwriting on glass slippery and unnatural should try one in a store first before buying. Heavy lecture-takers who need real paper feel may be happier with an e-ink device.

## Recommendation

For most students, the Galaxy Tab S9 FE with its S Pen or the iPad with an Apple Pencil is a strong note-taking choice, and the iPad Air or Tab S10 suits heavier users. Compare specifications and warranty on each listing.
