## How to Choose a Vlogging Kit

Start with how you film. If you mostly shoot yourself walking and talking, a compact fixed-lens camera is easy, and if you want lens options, an interchangeable-lens body is more flexible.

Prioritise audio. A good microphone costs less than a camera body and does more for quality, so include it in your budget.

Think about battery and storage. Carry spare batteries and fast memory cards, since vlogging drains both.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Ignoring audio.** Bad sound loses viewers.

**Overspending on the body.** Budget for a mic and a grip.

**Skipping spare batteries.** Carry extras.

**Shooting against bright backlight.** Face the light.

## Setting Up Your First Vlog

Set the camera to the right frame rate, use a clip-on or on-camera microphone, face a window for soft light and check the frame before recording. Record a short test and listen with headphones.

## Warranty and Service

Check that the warranty is valid in India and that service is available. Keep the invoice and serial numbers.

## Quick FAQ

**Is a phone enough to start?** Yes, but a camera gives better quality and control.

**Which mic first?** A clip-on or wireless mic.

**Do I need a gimbal?** Not to start.

## Editing and Workflow

Plan how you will edit before you buy. Check that your laptop or phone can handle the camera's video format, shoot at a resolution you can comfortably edit and back up footage every day. Simple editors on phones and laptops are enough to start, and good sound and framing matter more than fancy effects.
