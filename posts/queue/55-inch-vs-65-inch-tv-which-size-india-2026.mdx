## 1. 55 Inch TVs

Roughly ₹35,000–80,000. A 55 inch 4K TV is the most common size in Indian homes, offering a large picture without dominating a mid-sized room, with the widest range of models.

Why it works: balance of size, price and choice. The trade-off is that a big room can feel under-screened.

## 2. 65 Inch TVs

Roughly ₹55,000–1,50,000. A 65 inch TV gives a noticeably more immersive picture for movies, sport and gaming and has become far more affordable over the years.

## 4. Viewing Distance and Room Size

A rough rule for 4K TVs is to sit about 1.2 to 1.6 times the diagonal away. For 55 inch, that is about 5.5 to 7.3 feet, and for 65 inch about 6.5 to 8.7 feet. If your sofa is farther than 9 feet, a 65 inch or larger is easier to enjoy.

Why it works: a quick way to choose. The trade-off is that preferences vary.

## How to Choose Between 55 and 65 Inch

Measure your seating distance and wall width. Use the distance rule and make sure the TV and stand fit the space, including any furniture around it.

Think about what you watch. Movies, sports and gaming benefit from a larger screen, while casual TV and news are comfortable on 55 inch.

Check your budget honestly. If the 65 inch means a weaker panel, go for a good 55 inch instead.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Choosing size alone.** Panel quality matters.

**Ignoring the wall and stand.** Check the weight and width.

**Sitting too close to a large TV.** Check the distance.

**Skipping sound.** Add a soundbar.

## Real-World Scenarios

**A medium living room with a sofa 6 to 7 feet away.** A 55 inch TV.

**A larger hall with seating 8 feet away.** A 65 inch TV.

**A bedroom.** A 43 or 50 inch TV may be better.

## What to Check on the Listing

Confirm the screen size, the panel type, the resolution, the refresh rate, the HDMI ports, the warranty and the dimensions with stand. See whether wall mounting is included.

## Warranty and Service

Compare warranty on the panel and the whole TV, and check service availability in your city. Keep the invoice and serial number.

## Quick FAQ

**Is 65 inch too big for a flat?** Not if your sofa is 8 feet or more away.

**Is 55 inch enough?** For most rooms, yes.

**Does a bigger TV use more power?** Slightly, depending on the model.

## Final Checklist Before You Buy

Before you order, measure the room, the seating distance and the wall or stand space, check the HDMI ports and supported formats and confirm what is in the box. Read the warranty on the panel or the light source, plan mounting and cable routes and keep the invoice and serial number for any service claim.
