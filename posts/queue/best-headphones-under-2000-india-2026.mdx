## By Use Case

**Online classes and lectures:** Sony MDR-ZX310 or Sennheiser HD 206 — vocal clarity over bass.

**Music (Bollywood, EDM, hip-hop):** boAt Rockerz 450 — V-shaped bass-boosted sound suits these genres.

**Calls and WFH meetings:** Any model with a built-in mic. boAt Rockerz 450 and JBL Tune 510BT both have adequate call mics. Sony ZX310 is wired-only with no mic.

**Long sessions (2+ hours):** Sennheiser HD 206 — lighter clamping force and balanced sound reduces listening fatigue.
