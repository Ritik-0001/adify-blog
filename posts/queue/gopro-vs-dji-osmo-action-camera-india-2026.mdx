Roughly ₹30,000–50,000. GoPro's HERO13 Black and earlier HERO12 and HERO11 models are widely used for sports and travel, with a large mount and accessory ecosystem. The price is higher, and its front-and-rear screen layout gives you a single main display.

## How to Choose Between GoPro and DJI

Start with how you will use it. For sports and mounting variety, GoPro is a strong choice, and for low-light and value, DJI is attractive.

Compare bundles. Look at what is included, such as batteries, mounts and cases, and compare total prices rather than the base price.

Check support. Confirm warranty and service before buying, especially if you travel or shoot in rough conditions.

## Settings for Different Activities

For biking and sports, use a higher frame rate such as 60 fps for smooth motion and enable the strongest stabilisation. For travel scenes, 4K at 30 fps balances quality and file size. Use a lens guard on trails, buy fast rated memory cards and test a short clip before heading out so that you are not surprised by exposure or sound.

## Which Fits Which Rider or Traveller

A biker or trekker who wants a chest or helmet mount will be well served by either. A night-time traveller shooting streets and festivals may lean toward a DJI model for its low-light reputation, while a creator who loves accessories such as poles, floating grips and cages will find more choice around GoPro. Pick on the situation you actually shoot in, not on brand loyalty.

## Who Should Skip This

If you mostly shoot calm family videos and travel clips, a recent phone with good stabilisation can do much the same without a second device to charge. Skip a top-tier model too if you only need casual footage, since an older generation of either brand often records very similar video for less.

## Audio, Voice Control and Everyday Handling

Action cameras have tiny microphones, so wind noise is common on a bike or a trail. Look at whether a model accepts an external microphone or a wireless one, and test voice control in noisy places, because it often fails when you need it most. Also check how easily you can swap the battery with gloves on, since fiddly doors are frustrating in the cold.

## Recommendation

If you want the biggest ecosystem, shortlist GoPro. If you want strong low-light video and value, shortlist DJI. Compare bundles, warranty and availability on each listing.
