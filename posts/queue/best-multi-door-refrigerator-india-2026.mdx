## Why Multi-Door Design Matters

**Dedicated compartments:** Separate zones for vegetables, dairy, meats, and beverages maintain appropriate humidity and temperature for each category, extending freshness compared to mixed general storage.

**Reduced cold air loss:** Multiple smaller doors mean opening one compartment (e.g., just the beverage drawer) doesn't expose your entire fresh food storage to warm air, unlike single large-door designs.

**Organizational clarity:** Families report significantly less food waste when storage is clearly zoned — items don't get lost or forgotten in a single large compartment.
