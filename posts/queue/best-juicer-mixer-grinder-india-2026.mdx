## JMG vs Standalone Juicer

**JMG advantages:**
- One appliance replaces two
- Lower total cost (₹5,000–₇,000 vs ₹5,000 mixer + ₹3,000 juicer)
- Less storage space

**JMG disadvantages:**
- Centrifugal juicer in JMGs is smaller capacity than standalone
- Need to swap attachments when switching between juicing and grinding
- Juicer drum is smaller (less juice per load)

**For serious juicing (1L+ daily):** A dedicated centrifugal or cold-press juicer is better. **For occasional juice (2–3 times/week):** JMG is the practical choice.

## Cold Press vs Centrifugal Juicer JMG

JMG combo units use centrifugal juicers (high-speed spinning blade). Cold press (slow juicer) extracts more juice, retains more nutrients, and handles leafy greens better — but cold press JMG combinations don't exist at reasonable prices.

If cold-pressed juice quality matters, buy a standalone cold press juicer (₹8,000–₁₅,000) alongside your mixer grinder separately.
