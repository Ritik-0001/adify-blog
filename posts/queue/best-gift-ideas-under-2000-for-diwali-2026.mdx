## How to Choose a Gift Under ₹2,000

Match the gift to how the person lives. A student or young professional will use earbuds and a smartwatch every day; a parent may prefer a grooming item or something for the home; a colleague is usually best served by a neutral, consumable gift such as chocolates.

Watch for compatibility. Earbuds and smartwatches work with almost every phone, but some smartwatch features, such as call answering, depend on the phone and app. If you do not know what phone the person uses, read the listing carefully or choose a non-tech option.

Warranty matters at this price point. Buy from the brand's official store or an Amazon-fulfilled listing so that a faulty piece can be replaced. A cheap price from an unknown seller is not worth the risk of an unusable gift on Diwali morning.

## Quick FAQ

**Are budget earbuds and smartwatches a good gift?** Yes, if you buy from a known brand and set expectations. They cover the essentials such as Bluetooth calls, music and step tracking, though sound quality and sensors are basic compared with premium models.

**Should I gift tech or a consumable?** For close family and friends, tech that they will use daily is more memorable. For colleagues and neighbours, chocolates or dry fruits avoid any mismatch in taste or phone compatibility.

**Can I return a gift if it does not suit?** Most Amazon listings allow returns within a set window, but the terms vary by category and seller. Check the return policy on the listing before you buy, and keep the original packaging until Diwali is over.
