## Mistake 1: Trusting Inflated Discount Badges

Some sellers artificially raise the listed "regular price" shortly before the sale, making the sale price look like a bigger discount than it actually represents. **Fix:** Always check price history (Keepa or similar) before trusting a displayed percentage discount.

## Mistake 2: Impulse-Buying Outside Your Actual Needs

A steep discount on something you weren't planning to buy is still unnecessary spending — the "% off" framing creates urgency that overrides normal purchase deliberation. **Fix:** Shop from a pre-built list of genuine needs, not from browsing category pages.

## Mistake 3: Ignoring Exchange Offer Value

Many buyers skip the exchange offer step entirely, missing ₹3,000-₹15,000 in additional credit for an old working device. **Fix:** Check exchange eligibility for your specific old device before finalizing any purchase in an eligible category.

## Mistake 4: Waiting Too Long on Fast-Selling Items

Premium laptops, flagship phones, and popular TV configurations frequently sell out within the first day or two of the sale. **Fix:** Decide your target purchase in advance and act within the first 24-48 hours for competitive categories.

## Mistake 5: Not Comparing Across Platforms

Amazon and Flipkart run simultaneous sales, and pricing can differ meaningfully for the same product. **Fix:** A quick cross-platform check for high-value purchases takes seconds and can reveal better pricing elsewhere.

## Recommendation

Avoiding these five mistakes requires roughly 15-20 minutes of preparation — checking price history, building a genuine want/need list, confirming exchange eligibility, and deciding your purchase timing — a small time investment relative to the hundreds or thousands of rupees each mistake typically costs.
