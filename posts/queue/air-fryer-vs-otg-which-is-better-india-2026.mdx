## How they cook differently

An air fryer is essentially a small, powerful convection oven. A compact chamber and a strong fan push hot air around the food at high speed, so it crisps quickly. An OTG (oven toaster griller) uses top and bottom heating rods, and the better ones add a convection fan. It has a larger cavity and slower, gentler heat.

The same principle underlies both, but the air fryer concentrates it, which is why it crisps faster and preheats in a minute or two.

## Speed

Air fryers preheat quickly, often in under three minutes, and cook samosas, tikkis, French fries or chicken pieces in 10 to 20 minutes. OTGs take 10 to 15 minutes to preheat and longer to cook, but can handle multiple items at once.

For a quick evening snack, air fryer wins. For a Sunday bake with several trays, OTG is better.

## Capacity

A typical air fryer holds 3 to 6 litres of food, enough for 2 to 4 people in batches. OTGs range from 10 to 60 litres, with 25 to 45 litres suitable for a family and 60 litres for large trays. If you cook for guests, you may need to run an air fryer in several batches.

## Baking

OTGs are better for cakes, cookies, breads and casseroles because they have space for standard tins and give steady heat. Air fryers can bake small cakes and muffins, but the fan can dry the surface or blow batter, and tin size is limited.

If baking is a serious goal, an OTG with convection and rotisserie options gives more control. Search [OTG for baking cakes](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=otg+for+baking+cakes&tag=adifystore-21).

## Power use

Air fryers typically draw 1,400 to 2,000 watts but run for a short time. OTGs draw similar or higher wattage, around 1,500 to 2,200, and run longer, with a long preheat. For small meals, an air fryer usually uses less electricity in total, while a full tray in an OTG can be efficient per portion.

## Cleaning

Air fryer baskets are non-stick and easy to wash, but each batch needs cleaning. OTGs need tray cleaning and occasional interior wiping, and grease splatter on rods can smoke over time. Using foil or liners helps with both.

## Healthy cooking

An air fryer uses little oil, a spoonful or less, to crisp foods, which suits people cutting oil. OTGs can also cook with little oil, but results are less crisp for fried-style foods.

## Space

Both take counter space. An air fryer is compact, around the size of a coffee machine, while a 30 to 45 litre OTG is larger. ## Who should pick an air fryer

- Singles, couples or small families
- People who want crisp snacks in minutes
- Kitchens with limited counter space
- Cooks who reheat frequently

## Who should pick an OTG

- Home bakers making cakes, cookies and bread
- Large families cooking in trays
- People who want to roast whole chicken or large vegetables
- Buyers who want grill and rotisserie functions

## Who should skip both

If you only cook on the stove and rarely bake or fry, neither will earn counter space. And if you already have a microwave with convection, an air fryer may be redundant.

## Recommendation

For daily snacks and small meals, buy an air fryer. For baking and large trays, buy an OTG. If you have space and budget, many households end up with both, using the air fryer on weekdays and the OTG on weekends.
