## Paper Cost — Often Overlooked

Paper at ₹300/ream (500 sheets) = ₹0.60/sheet. At 300 pages/month, paper costs ₹2,160/year — regardless of printer type. Use automatic duplex to halve this.

**With auto duplex:** ₹1,080/year on paper vs ₹2,160 without. Over 5 years: ₹5,400 saving.

## When Laser Makes Financial Sense

For businesses printing exclusively mono at very high volume (2,000+ pages/month), high-yield laser toner can approach EcoTank running costs while offering faster print speeds.

**Brother TN-2480 (3,000 pages, ₹4,500)** at 2,000 mono pages/month: ₹3,000/month on toner. EcoTank black at 2,000 pages: ₹140/month. EcoTank still wins dramatically on running cost.

Laser wins only on speed (30+ ppm vs 10 ppm inkjet) and duty cycle (suitable for much higher monthly volumes).
