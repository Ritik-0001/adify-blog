## Why Brightness Matters More at 75 Inches

Larger screens in typical living room settings (versus dedicated dark home theatre rooms) need to overcome more ambient light across a bigger surface area to maintain punchy, high-contrast image quality — Mini-LED and QLED technologies' higher peak brightness capability generally serves large-screen living room use better than OLED, whose peak brightness (even with brightness-boosting technologies) still trails premium Mini-LED options.

## Viewing Distance Considerations

At 75 inches, optimal viewing distance is roughly 8-12 feet for 4K content — verify your living room layout accommodates this distance for the resolution benefits to be fully apparent; sitting too close emphasizes pixel structure, while sitting too far wastes the large screen's visual impact advantage.
