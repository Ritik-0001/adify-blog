## What Changes Under ₹20,000

Compared to the Flexispot E2 (₹22,000+):

**Lower capacity:** Most sub-₹20,000 electric desks handle 60–80kg maximum load vs 70–100kg for mid-range.

**Less height range:** Minimum sitting height may be 73cm instead of 71cm — minor, but affects very short users.

**Motor noise:** Budget motors are 5–10dB noisier during adjustment.

**Frame material:** Thinner steel gauge, slightly more flex at maximum height.

**Desktop:** Usually MDF or cheaper composite; some premium desks use full solid wood or thicker MDF.

For users who don't have dual monitors or heavy accessories (just a laptop and external keyboard), these trade-offs are acceptable.

**Price:** ₹10,000–₁₅,000
