## How to Choose a 55 Inch TV

Check the viewing distance. A 55-inch TV suits a seating distance of about 7 to 10 feet. Closer than that, and a smaller screen may feel better.

Look at panel and processor. LED is common, QLED adds colour and brightness, and OLED offers the best contrast. Processor quality affects motion, upscaling and app speed.

Check ports and audio. Look for at least three HDMI ports, one with ARC or eARC, and consider a soundbar since slim TVs have small speakers.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Choosing by brand alone.** Check panel and features.

**Ignoring refresh rate for gaming.** Look for 120Hz if you play consoles.

**Skipping the sound.** Plan for a soundbar.

**Forgetting wall mount.** Check installation terms.

## What to Check on the Listing

Confirm the panel type, resolution, refresh rate, HDR formats, HDMI ports and warranty. See whether wall mounting and installation are included.

## Quick FAQ

**Is 55 inches the right size?** For most living rooms, yes.

**Do I need HDR?** It improves contrast on supported content, and most 4K TVs support basic HDR.

**How long do TVs last?** Seven to ten years is common.

## Setting Up for the Best Picture

Move the TV out of the default vivid mode. Choose a cinema, movie or filmmaker mode for films and standard for everyday viewing, and turn off aggressive motion smoothing unless you like it. Place the TV at eye level, and reduce glare from windows with curtains.

If you use a set-top box or a streaming stick, connect it through HDMI and enable the game or PC mode only for consoles. Update the TV's software after setup to get the latest fixes.
