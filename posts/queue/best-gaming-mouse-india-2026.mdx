## Gaming Mouse Specifications Explained

**DPI (Dots Per Inch):** Sensitivity. Higher DPI = cursor moves more per physical mouse movement. Most gamers use 400–1600 DPI regardless of the mouse's maximum. Any mouse above 6,000 DPI is sufficient.

**Polling Rate (Hz):** How often the mouse reports position to the PC. 1000Hz = position update every 1ms. Standard mice use 125Hz (8ms latency). Gaming mice at 1000Hz feel noticeably more responsive.

**Optical vs. Laser:** Optical sensors (used in G102, DeathAdder) track better on most surfaces. Laser sensors over-track and produce micro-jitter. Buy optical for gaming.

**Shape:** Ergonomic (right-hand only, like DeathAdder) vs. Ambidextrous (works both hands, like G102). If you're right-handed, ergonomic shapes are generally more comfortable for long sessions.
