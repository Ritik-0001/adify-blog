## Panel technology: the real difference

Samsung makes many VA panels, known for deep contrast and curves, plus QD-OLED panels for premium gaming and some IPS panels in its ViewFinity line. VA gives rich blacks but narrower viewing angles.

LG leans on IPS, Nano IPS and WOLED. IPS gives wide viewing angles and accurate colour, and LG's OLED gaming monitors are strong competitors to Samsung's QD-OLED.

Neither is universally better; it depends on whether you favour contrast and curves or viewing angles and colour accuracy.

## Design and curves

Samsung offers more curved monitors, such as the Odyssey G5 and G7, with aggressive curves of 1000R or 1800R for immersion. The Odyssey Neo G9 is a huge super-ultrawide.

LG focuses on flat panels with slim bezels and clean designs, and offers curved ultrawides too. LG's stands tend to be more adjustable in higher-end models.

## Smart monitors versus standard

Samsung's Smart Monitor line, such as the M7 and M8 series, adds a Tizen smart platform with streaming apps, a remote and wireless features, so you can watch Netflix without a computer. LG has webOS on some monitors and TVs, but fewer such models.

Smart monitors suit small rooms and students. They add cost and sometimes sluggish menus.

## Stand and build

LG's mid and high-end stands usually adjust for height, tilt and pivot, a practical ergonomic benefit. Samsung's stands are often simpler, with tilt only on cheaper models, though higher-end lines are better. Either brand pairs with a VESA arm if the monitor supports it. ## Colour accuracy and HDR

LG's IPS and Nano IPS typically provide better out-of-box colour. Samsung's VA monitors offer deep contrast but less colour consistency off-angle. HDR on mid-range monitors from both is mostly marketing; OLED and Mini-LED models deliver true HDR.

## Warranty and service

Both brands offer one to three years of warranty depending on the model, serviced through authorised centres. Check the terms for your model and pixel policy, and keep the invoice.

## Who should skip which

Skip Samsung VA models if you do colour-critical work or sit off-axis. Skip LG if you want a curved, immersive gaming display at a lower price, or built-in smart apps.

## Recommendation

Choose Samsung for curved gaming, QD-OLED and smart monitor convenience. Choose LG for IPS colour, ergonomics and ultrawide variety. Compare the specific model's panel, stand and warranty before you buy, since both brands span good and basic products.
