## The three technologies

Shiatsu uses rotating nodes or rollers that knead the sole and calf. Air compression inflates and deflates airbags around the leg, squeezing in waves. Heat warms the foot and calf using a small heating element. Good units combine all three, but each adds cost and parts that can fail.

## Comfort and sizing

The most common complaint is a poor fit. Measure your calf circumference at the widest point and compare it with the listed maximum. For men with larger calves or users in thick pants, the airbags may not close. Check the shoe size range as well, with many models fitting up to UK size 10 or 11.

Pressure levels should be adjustable. A unit with only one strong setting will be unpleasant for many users.

## Heat: useful but not for everyone

Heat helps stiff feet and cold winter evenings. It should be gentle, around 40 to 50 degrees Celsius, and should switch off automatically. People with diabetes or reduced sensation in their feet may not feel overheating, so they should avoid heated models without medical advice.

## Noise, power and use

Most units run at about 40 to 60 dB, similar to a quiet fan. Sessions often auto-stop after 15 to 20 minutes. Power draw is low, around 50 to 100 W. Plan a 15 minute session while watching TV rather than expecting a spa. Keep the unit on a stable floor.

## Safety notes

Do not use compression on legs with suspected blood clots, deep vein thrombosis, open wounds, skin infection or severe swelling without a doctor's approval. People with pacemakers, pregnancy or neuropathy should ask before using. Stop if you feel pain, numbness or tingling. ## Who should skip a leg massager

If your problem is persistent pain or swelling in one leg, see a doctor instead of buying a device. If you have a modest budget, a foot roller ball and a warm water soak at home do part of the job for far less. If you want full body relief, a massage chair or recliner is a better home fit.

## Using it without overdoing it

Limit sessions to the maker's timer, usually 15 to 20 minutes, and use a medium setting for the first week. Leave a gap of a few hours between sessions. Strong shiatsu pressure on a tender arch can leave a bruised feeling the next morning, which is a sign to dial it down.

## Recommendation

Pick a foot and calf unit with adjustable intensity, optional heat and a calf size that fits you, from a brand with a clear warranty. Check safety conditions with your doctor if you have any circulation concerns.
