## Price Tier Breakdown

**₹12,000-₹15,000 (Manual crank):** Height-adjustable via hand crank, no motor. Cheapest entry but requires manual effort for every height change.

**₹18,000-₴5,000 (Single-motor):** Motorized but with a single motor and crossbar mechanism — functional but prone to wobble and slower transitions over time.

**₹28,000-₸5,000 (Entry dual-motor):** RoyalOak, Flexispot E5 — genuine dual-motor stability at accessible pricing, 100kg capacity.

**₹35,000-₴0,000+ (Premium dual-motor):** Featherlite Rise, Flexispot E7 Pro — enhanced features like programmable presets, higher weight capacity, extended height range.

---

## What Actually Changes as Price Increases

**Motor configuration:** Single to dual-motor is the single biggest quality jump, affecting stability, speed, and weight capacity.

**Weight capacity:** Increases from 70-80kg (single-motor) to 100-120kg (dual-motor) as price rises.

**Height range:** Premium desks often offer extended ranges accommodating both very tall and very short users.

**Programmable presets:** More common and more numerous (up to 4 saved heights) at premium tiers.

---

## Recommendation

Budget toward the ₹30,000-₸5,000 dual-motor tier if possible — this range consistently delivers the stability and reliability that separates a standing desk lasting years from one developing wobble or motor issues within the first year of daily use.
