## Why 24 Inches Changes Everything

Most students and WFH workers who switch from a 19.5" monitor to a 24" report this as the most noticeable upgrade they've made to their setup. The reasons:

**Side-by-side windows:** Two browser windows open side-by-side become genuinely readable on 24" at 1080p. On 19.5", each window is too narrow to be comfortable.

**Video lectures:** Full-screen video with room for a note-taking app beside it — possible on 24", cramped on 21.5" or smaller.

**Text size:** 1080p on 24" hits the sweet spot for text size without scaling — text is large enough to read comfortably at arm's length.

## 24-inch vs 27-inch Under ₹12,000

At ₹12,000, 27-inch monitors start appearing — but almost all are 1080p TN panels. A 27-inch 1080p monitor has lower pixel density than a 24-inch 1080p, making text slightly softer. For productivity and reading, a 24" IPS 1080p is a better choice than a 27" TN 1080p at the same price.
