## How BLDC Technology Works

Standard fan: AC motor runs at full speed, resistor regulator wastes current as heat at lower speeds. Total consumption: 70–80W at full speed.

BLDC fan: Brushless DC motor with electronic inverter. The inverter converts AC to DC and varies voltage to control speed — no resistor waste. At full speed: 28–35W. At low speed: 8–12W.

The permanent magnet rotor in BLDC motors also has no commutator wear — longer motor life than brush-based motors (15–20 year BLDC motor life vs. 8–12 years for standard motors).

## Installation Note

BLDC ceiling fans require correct wiring — live, neutral, and earth (three-wire installation). Older Indian homes with two-wire (live + neutral only, no earth/ground) may need an electrician to add earthing. BLDC fan electronics are sensitive to voltage fluctuations — a voltage stabilizer (₹1,000–₁,500) is recommended in areas with frequent power fluctuations above ±10% of 230V.
