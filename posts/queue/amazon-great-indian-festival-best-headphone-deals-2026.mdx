## Choosing Between Premium and Budget During the Sale

**Choose premium (Sony/Bose)** if you use headphones for daily commuting, flights, or long work sessions where noise cancellation quality genuinely changes your day-to-day experience.

**Choose budget (boAt/JBL/Noise)** if you primarily need headphones for casual listening or calls at home — the marginal quality difference matters far less in that use case.

**Don't skip checking earbuds versus over-ears for your specific need.** Over-ears typically offer better battery life and noise cancellation; earbuds win on portability and workout use.
