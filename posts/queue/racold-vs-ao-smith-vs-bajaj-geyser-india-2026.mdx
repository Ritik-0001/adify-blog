Roughly ₹15,000–35,000 for premium ranges. Racold is a long-standing storage geyser brand known for tanks with protective coatings and a range from compact to large capacities. Its premium lines add smart or digital features, and they cost more than mainstream rivals.

## How to Choose Between Racold, AO Smith and Bajaj

Start with capacity, then the star rating. A five-star 25 litre storage geyser is the safest starting point for a family of four.

Check the tank and safety details. Look for glass-lined or stainless steel tanks, multiple safety valves and a thermostat with auto cut-off. The warranty terms on the tank often say more than the brand name.

Compare service. Look at the presence of authorised service centres in your city and the installation arrangement, since a geyser needs correct earthing and a pressure valve.

## What Trips Up Geyser Buyers

Choosing on brand name alone is the usual slip, since capacity, star rating and warranty can differ more between two models of one brand than between brands. Poor plumbing can void a warranty, so let the brand's installer do the work. Ask when and how the magnesium anode is replaced, because that part decides how long the tank lasts in hard water.

## Who Should Skip the Premium Brands

If you are furnishing a rental, or the geyser serves one guest bathroom that is used a few times a week, paying for a premium tank warranty is hard to justify. A simpler Bajaj storage unit with a decent warranty covers that need. Premium Racold and AO Smith lines pay off in a home you will live in for many years, especially with hard water.

## Recommendation

If you want the best tank and warranty for the bathroom, shortlist Racold and AO Smith. If you want value and wide service, shortlist Bajaj. Compare capacity, star rating and warranty on the listing.
