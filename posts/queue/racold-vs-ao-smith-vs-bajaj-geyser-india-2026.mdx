## 1. Racold

Roughly ₹10,000–35,000 across ranges. Racold is a long-standing storage geyser brand known for tanks with protective coatings and a range from compact to large capacities. Its premium lines add smart or digital features.

Why it works: tank focus and broad capacity range. The trade-off is that premium lines cost more than mainstream rivals.

## 2. AO Smith

Roughly ₹10,000–40,000. AO Smith is a global water heater brand with a strong presence in storage geysers, and many of its models highlight tank protection and safety features. Its range includes higher-end options for larger homes.

Why it works: a reputation for build and safety features. The trade-off is that pricing is generally on the higher side.

## How to Choose Between Racold, AO Smith and Bajaj

Start with capacity, then the star rating. A five-star 25 litre storage geyser is the safest starting point for a family of four.

Check the tank and safety details. Look for glass-lined or stainless steel tanks, multiple safety valves and a thermostat with auto cut-off. The warranty terms on the tank often say more than the brand name.

Compare service. Look at the presence of authorised service centres in your city and the installation arrangement, since a geyser needs correct earthing and a pressure valve.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Buying on brand alone.** Compare capacity, rating and warranty.

**Ignoring installation.** Poor plumbing can void a warranty.

**Paying for smart features you will not use.** A simple thermostat is enough for many homes.

**Not checking the anode.** Ask when and how it is replaced.

## Real-World Scenarios

**A budget-minded family.** A Bajaj storage geyser with a good warranty.
