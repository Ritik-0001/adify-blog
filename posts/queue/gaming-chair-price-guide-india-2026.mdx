## Price Tier Breakdown

**₹6,000-₴,000 (Entry):** Basic recline, 2D or fixed armrests, lower-density foam prone to sagging within 6-12 months of daily use.

**₹10,000-₹15,000 (Budget-mid):** 4D armrests appear at this tier, better foam density, steel frames with 120-130kg capacity — Cellbell C100 range.

**₹15,000-₴0,000 (Mid-premium):** Higher-density cold-cure foam, established brands (Green Soul, DXRacer), better long-term durability.

**₹20,000-₸0,000+ (Premium):** International brands, premium materials, extended warranty periods, maximum build quality.

## What Actually Changes as Price Increases

**Foam density and type:** Higher tiers use cold-cure foam that resists sagging far longer than the standard foam in budget models.

**Armrest adjustability:** 2D (height + width) at budget tiers, 4D (adding depth + angle) becomes standard from ₹10,000+.

**Frame material:** Steel frames appear from the budget-mid tier onward; entry-level chairs sometimes use weaker composite materials.

**Warranty length:** Increases from 6 months at entry level to 2-5 years at premium tiers, reflecting manufacturer confidence in longevity.
