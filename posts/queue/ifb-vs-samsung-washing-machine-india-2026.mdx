## What to Check on the Listing

Confirm the capacity, the spin speed, the motor warranty, the energy rating, the installation terms and the model number. See whether a stand is recommended.

## Installation and Care

Place the machine on a level floor, remove transit bolts and leave the door slightly open after use. Clean the filter and the detergent drawer every month, and run an occasional hot cleaning cycle.

## Quick FAQ

**Which is better for hard water?** Look for hard water programs and good service.

**Which has more features?** Samsung generally.

**Which is easier to service?** Both are widely available.

## Drum Design and Fabric Care

Drum design affects how gently clothes are handled. Look for descriptions of drum patterns, cushioned wash action and low-vibration balancing, and read reviews about how well the machine treats delicates. Washing delicate items in a mesh bag protects them regardless of the brand.

## Water and Electricity Use

Check the energy label and the water use per cycle. Front-loaders generally save water, and heated programs raise power use. If your supply is limited, choose a model with a good eco program and avoid unnecessary hot washes.

## Final Checklist Before You Buy

Before you order, measure the space, the doorway, the lift and the path to the room, check water and drain points or ventilation clearance and confirm whether delivery and installation are included. Compare the warranty on the motor or compressor with the warranty on the whole product, check the energy rating and keep the invoice and installation record.
