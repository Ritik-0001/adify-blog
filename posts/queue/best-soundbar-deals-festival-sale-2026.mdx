## Choosing Between 2.1 and Dolby Atmos Systems

**Choose a 2.1 setup (soundbar + subwoofer)** if your priority is clearer dialogue and stronger bass than TV speakers, without needing full surround immersion.

**Choose a Dolby Atmos system** if you watch a lot of movies or shows with immersive sound mixes and have a living room large enough to benefit from the height-channel effect.

**Match the soundbar size to your TV, not just your budget.** A soundbar noticeably narrower or wider than your TV looks and sounds mismatched in a living room setup.
