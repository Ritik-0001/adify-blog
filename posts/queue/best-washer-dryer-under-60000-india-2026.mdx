## How to Choose a Washer Dryer

Understand the capacity gap. A combo that washes 9 kg may dry only 5 or 6 kg, so a full wash usually needs to be split before drying. Check the dry capacity, not just the wash size.

Consider time and electricity. Drying in a combo takes longer than a separate dryer and uses more power. If you have a balcony or terrace, a washer-only machine plus air drying is often cheaper.

Check installation and space. Washer dryers need proper drainage, ventilation and a level floor. Confirm that your bathroom or utility area can accommodate the size.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Expecting the dryer to match a standalone dryer.** Combos dry smaller loads more slowly. Plan cycles accordingly.

**Skipping lint and drain maintenance.** Clean filters and the drain regularly to keep performance.

**Ignoring noise and vibration.** A stable base and level floor reduce noise.

## Quick FAQ

**Is a washer dryer worth it?** For flats without drying space, yes. Otherwise a washer and air drying is often enough.

**Can I dry everything?** Delicate fabrics and items with special care labels may need air drying.

**How long does drying take?** It varies by load and settings, and often takes a few hours.

## Real-World Use of a Washer Dryer

Most owners run a wash, then dry half the load at a time, and hang bulky items to finish. Setting realistic expectations avoids disappointment: a combo is a space-saving convenience, not a replacement for a full-size dryer. Items like towels and t-shirts dry well, while delicate fabrics, wool and items with special care labels should be air dried.

Plan for ventilation. Drying produces warm, moist air, so a bathroom or utility area with an exhaust or open window helps. Keep the lint filter and drain clean to maintain performance.

## Power, Water and Timing

Drying uses far more electricity than washing, so a wash and dry cycle can cost several times as much as a wash alone. Plan around off-peak hours if your tariff allows it, and use the timer if the model offers one. Expect a full wash-and-dry cycle to take a few hours, and check the water pressure and drain requirements before installation.
