## Electric Kettle Safety for Indian Homes

**Auto shut-off is non-negotiable:** Indian cooking involves multitasking — a kettle without auto shut-off left unattended on a counter is a fire risk. All listed models have auto shut-off.

**Stainless steel interior:** Plastic-interior kettles can leach BPA, particularly in the first few months of use. Stainless steel is safer and more durable.

**Indian voltage:** All listed kettles are 220V/50Hz compatible.

**Descaling:** Hard water in Indian cities (Delhi, Bengaluru) causes limescale buildup inside kettles. Descale every 2 months with a solution of 1 tablespoon white vinegar + 500ml water, boil once, rinse.

**Recommendation:** Philips HD9303 for most Indian buyers — proven durability and safety features that justify the small premium over Bajaj. Morphy Richards Voyager if you drink multiple types of tea and want temperature precision.
