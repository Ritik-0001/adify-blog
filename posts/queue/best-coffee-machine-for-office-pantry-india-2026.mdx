## Start with cups per day

Estimate honestly. A team of 10 might drink 20 to 30 coffees a day, with a morning rush that matters most. A home machine brewing a cup in 30 to 60 seconds creates a queue at 9:30 am, while it can cope with a steady trickle all day.

- Up to 8 people: a premium home bean-to-cup can work.
- 8 to 20 people: look at prosumer or small commercial machines with bigger hoppers and tanks.
- Above 20: a commercial bean-to-cup or vending-style machine on a plumbed water line is more realistic.

## Bean hopper and water tank

A hopper of 250 to 300 grams suits home use, but an office will empty it in a day. Each espresso uses roughly 8 to 10 grams of beans, so 300 g is about 30 cups. Larger hoppers of 500 g or more, and tanks of 2 litres or above, reduce refill frequency. Some commercial machines connect directly to a water line, which removes tank refills entirely. ## Milk options

Office staff love lattes and cappuccinos, but milk is where hygiene and mess happen. Choose carefully.

### 1. Automatic milk carafe

Convenient but needs daily rinsing and a weekly deep clean. Left uncleaned in an office fridge, it quickly goes sour.

### 2. Manual steam wand

Flexible but needs someone with skill, which is rare in a pantry.

### 3. Black coffee only plus separate milk

For teams who drink mostly black or Americano, skip the milk system entirely and offer a small [milk frother](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=electric+milk+frother&tag=adifystore-21) beside the machine.

## Cleaning and ownership

Every office machine needs an owner. Without a person who empties the drip tray, grounds container and runs rinse cycles, the machine will smell and clog within weeks. Assign one person, and schedule weekly cleaning and monthly descaling. Use tablets designed for the machine. Search [coffee machine cleaning tablets](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=coffee+machine+cleaning+tablets&tag=adifystore-21).

Buy filtered water or use an RO line, because hard water scale is the main cause of breakdowns in Indian offices.

## Durability and service

Home machines are rated for limited daily volume, and office use can void warranties if the seller considers it commercial. Check the warranty wording. For heavier use, commercial brands that serve cafes may be a better match, although they cost more and may need an installer. Ask about on-site service and spare parts availability in your city.

## Capsule and pod machines

For small teams that value zero cleaning, capsule machines are simple, but cost per cup is much higher at office volumes, and they create plastic or aluminium waste. They suit a boardroom, not a pantry for 20. See [capsule coffee machine office](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=capsule+coffee+machine+office&tag=adifystore-21).

## Features that help in a pantry

- Two-cup output for faster service
- Programmable user buttons for black, white and strong
- Removable brew group for easy cleaning
- A drip tray that is deep enough to avoid daily overflow
- Auto-rinse on startup and shutdown

## Who should skip a bean-to-cup

If your office has fewer than four coffee drinkers, a simple filter machine or a few capsule units may be enough. And if your team drinks mostly instant coffee and tea, a premium machine will sit idle.

## Recommendation

Size the machine to your busiest 30-minute window, not to the daily average. For small teams, a premium home machine from De'Longhi or Philips with a large tank can be enough; for larger teams, a commercial bean-to-cup on a water line is the safer long-term buy.
