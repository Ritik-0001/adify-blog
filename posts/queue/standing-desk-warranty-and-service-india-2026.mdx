## Typical Warranty Terms by Component

**Motor:** Usually the longest coverage, 2-5 years depending on brand — this reflects manufacturer confidence in the most failure-prone mechanical component.

**Frame:** Often 5-10 years, since structural steel components rarely fail under normal use.

**Desktop surface:** Typically 1-2 years, covering manufacturing defects in the laminate or wood surface.

**Electronics (control panel, height display):** Usually matches motor warranty terms, 2-5 years.

## What Voids Warranty Coverage

**Exceeding the rated weight capacity** consistently can void motor warranty claims, since this causes the failure the manufacturer didn't design for.

**Unauthorized repair attempts** before contacting the manufacturer typically void remaining coverage.
