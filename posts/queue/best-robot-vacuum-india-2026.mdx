## India-Specific Robot Vacuum Considerations

**Threshold height:** Most Indian apartments have bathroom-to-hall floor transitions, bedroom doorway thresholds, and TV unit ledges. Ensure robot can handle 1.5–2cm transitions — most modern robots can.

**Charging dock placement:** Robot needs unobstructed 50cm space around dock. Place in a corner of the living room where it can reach most floor area.

**Obstacle avoidance:** Indian homes with footwear near doors, cable clutter, and scattered toys need better obstacle detection. Premium robots (Ecovacs Deebot T30) have LIDAR + camera obstacle avoidance.

**Daily scheduling:** Set for 7–8 AM when house is empty. Robot completes in 45–70 minutes for standard 2-BHK. Returns to dock automatically.

**Recommendation:** Xiaomi E12 for most Indian families entering robot vacuum ownership — good suction, LiDAR mapping, and reliable Diwali discounts make it the best starting point. Ecovacs N10 Plus for those who want mopping and convenience of auto-empty.
