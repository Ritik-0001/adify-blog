## Why Flights Are the Best Use Case for Premium ANC

Airplane cabin noise is:
- Consistent frequency profile (turbofan engine at 200–800Hz peak)
- Continuous (not intermittent like street traffic)
- Predictable (ANC processors can model it accurately)

This makes flights the ideal environment for ANC headphones. Budget ANC headphones that struggle with varied noise environments work noticeably better in airplane cabins. Premium ANC headphones reduce engine noise to a near-inaudible whisper.

Flight duration also matters: Indian domestic routes (IndiGo Delhi-Mumbai, 2 hours) benefit from ANC. Long-haul routes (Air India Delhi-London, 9 hours; Delhi-New York, 14+ hours) make premium ANC essentially mandatory for comfortable travel.

## Flight ANC Tips for Indian Travellers

1. **Enable maximum ANC** before boarding: The XM5 and QC45 auto-detect flight mode, but manually confirming maximum ANC in the app ensures optimal performance.

2. **Charge the night before**: Full charge before departure eliminates mid-flight battery anxiety.

3. **Airline adapter**: IndiGo's economy in-flight entertainment uses 3.5mm mono jack (not stereo). Bring a mono-to-stereo adapter (₹100–₹200 on Amazon) if using wired connection with airline screens.

4. **Pressure equalisation**: During descent, remove headphones momentarily if you feel ear pressure — ANC combined with cabin pressure change can amplify discomfort.

**Recommendation:** Sony WH-1000XM5 for frequent flyers — the investment pays back quickly in comfort on long routes. Sony WH-1000XM4 during sales for budget-conscious travellers.
