## What these filters actually do

Most ACs recirculate room air: they pull warm air in through the indoor unit, pass it over a cold coil and push it back. A standard mesh filter catches coarse dust and hair. An "advanced" filter adds a finer layer, such as a PM 2.5 filter, a HEPA-type or electrostatic filter, designed to catch smaller particles on that pass.

In practice, the airflow through an AC is lower than that of a dedicated purifier, and the filter is thin to avoid choking the airflow. So you may see less fine dust settling in a room that has the AC running, but you should not expect hospital-grade air. Brand claims about percentage of PM 2.5 removed are measured under test-chamber conditions, so read them as best-case numbers.

## Ionizers and plasma: be sceptical

Ionizer and plasma functions release charged particles to clump pollutants. Some produce small amounts of ozone as a by-product, which is why many people prefer to skip them. If you do have an ionizer, keep it optional, and avoid running it in a closed bedroom all night.

## AC vs air purifier

A dedicated purifier has a motor and a deep HEPA filter built to move several hundred cubic metres of air an hour. An AC filter handles a fraction of that. If you have a baby, a person with asthma or you live near construction, buy a purifier for the bedroom in addition to the AC. The AC filter then cuts the dust load, and the purifier does the real cleaning. For general city dust, the AC filter alone may be enough.

Also remember the AC filter must be cleaned. A clogged filter lowers cooling, raises power use and stops filtering. Plan to wash the mesh every two to four weeks in summer and have the coil serviced annually.

## What to verify on the listing

1. The filter's exact name, because "anti-dust filter", "PM 2.5 filter" and "HEPA" are different claims.
2. Whether the filter is washable, replaceable, or has a replacement cost. Some replaceable filters cost a few hundred to a couple of thousand rupees.
3. Whether the cleaning cycle is automatic. Self-clean modes dry the coil, which reduces odour, but they are not a deep clean.
4. Whether the model has a dust sensor and an air quality indicator, which helps you see when it needs servicing.

## Who should not pay extra

If you live on a high floor in a low-dust area and run the AC at night, a standard filter plus regular cleaning does the job.
