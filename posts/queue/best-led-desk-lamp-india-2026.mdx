## Key LED Desk Lamp Features for Studying

**Flicker-free:** The most important eye-care specification. Cheap LED lamps flicker at 100Hz (synchronized with AC current) in a way invisible to the naked eye but registered by the brain, causing fatigue and headaches over hours. Flicker-free lamps modulate LED current above 1,000Hz. Look for "flicker-free" explicitly stated in specs.

**Colour temperature:**
- 2700–3000K: Warm white — relaxed, comfortable for casual reading
- 4000K: Neutral white — balanced, recommended for general study
- 5000–6500K: Cool daylight — alert, best for focused work and coding

**Adjustable brightness:** Multiple brightness levels (typically 3–5 modes) allow matching lamp brightness to ambient room light — prevents the jarring contrast between a bright screen and dark surroundings.

**CRI (Colour Rendering Index):** CRI 80+ reproduces colours accurately. Important for design work, art, and craft tasks. Less critical for reading text.
