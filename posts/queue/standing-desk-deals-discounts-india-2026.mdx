## When Standing Desk Prices Drop in India

### Amazon Great Indian Festival (October)
Amazon India's largest annual sale event. Standing desks from Flexispot and Green Soul participate. Historical discounts: 20–30% off list price. The E1 Electric (usually ₹18,000–₂₂,000) drops to ₹13,000–₁₆,000 range in past GIF events.

**When:** Usually early-to-mid October. Check starts and Prime Early Access (24–48 hours before public sale).

### Flipkart Big Billion Days (October)
Simultaneous with GIF. Featherlite, Wakefit, and local Indian brands discount more on Flipkart. If the standing desk you want is on both platforms, compare prices — GIF and BBD run simultaneously.

### Amazon Republic Day Sale (January)
Moderate discounts. 10–15% off standard prices. Good for buyers who can't wait until October.

### Brand-Direct Sales (Flexispot India)
Flexispot's India website runs occasional independent discounts (New Year, Diwali, Independence Day) that are sometimes deeper than Amazon — worth checking directly at flexispot.in during sale periods.

## Price Tracking Tips

**Amazon price history:** Use browser extensions like Keepa (India-compatible) or CamelCamelCamel to view historical price data for specific standing desk products on Amazon India. Avoids buying at inflated pre-sale list prices.

**Set price alerts:** Amazon's app allows setting deal notifications on specific products. Flipkart has watchlist alerts. Set both for the specific standing desk model you want.

**List price inflation warning:** Some sellers artificially inflate list prices before sales to make discounts appear larger. Keepa's price history reveals whether a "30% discount" is genuine or manufactured from an inflated base.
