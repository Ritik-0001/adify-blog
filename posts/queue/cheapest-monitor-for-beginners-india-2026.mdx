## What "Cheapest Worth Buying" Means

The floor for a monitor worth owning in 2026:
- **Full HD (1920x1080):** Non-negotiable. 768p resolution in 2026 is unusable for reading text at desk distance.
- **HDMI port:** Modern laptops and PCs output HDMI. VGA-only requires a ₹300–₅₀₀ adapter.
- **Brand warranty:** 1–3 years from LG, Dell, BenQ, Acer, HP — covers defects.
- **Anti-glare:** Reduces reflections in lit rooms.

No-name monitors at ₹3,000–₄,000 often fail all four criteria.

## Avoiding Common Beginner Mistakes

**Don't buy 768p:** "Full HD" on the label can be misleading — verify the resolution is 1920x1080, not 1366x768 or 1280x720.

**Don't buy VGA-only:** Your laptop likely has HDMI or USB-C, not VGA. Check your laptop's output ports before buying.

**Don't buy no-name brands:** Monitors from brands like "Zebronics", "Foxin", or generic OEMs at ₹3,000–₄,000 have poor panel quality, no genuine warranty, and no service support.

**Recommendation:** LG 20MK400H at ₹5,500–₆,500 — the IPS panel and Reader Mode make it meaningfully better than the Acer V206HQL for the same or slightly higher price. For the tightest budgets, Acer V206HQL is the minimum acceptable starting point.
