## Option 2: Sit-Stand Desktop Converter (₹4,500–₆,500)

**What you get:** A platform that sits on your existing desk. Raises monitor, keyboard, and mouse to standing height. Lower the platform to sit.

**Who it's for:** Buyers who already have a desk and want to add standing capability for the lowest cost. No desk replacement needed.

**Height range:** Most converters offer a fixed standing height (approximately 40cm above desk surface). You push up to stand, lower to sit — not infinitely adjustable.

**Limitation:** The converter occupies desk surface. Some large converters (50cm × 60cm) consume most of a small desk.

Recommended: Zebronics or AmazonBasics sit-stand converter (₹5,000–₆,500) with dual monitor capability and keyboard tray.

## Option 3: Fixed-Height Standing Desk (₹4,000–₇,000)

A desk permanently built at standing height (100–110cm). You use a bar stool or high chair to sit. No adjustment mechanism.

Best for single-user setups where you know your ergonomic standing height and don't need to adjust. Drawback: if your standing height preference changes or another person uses the desk, the fixed height doesn't adapt.
