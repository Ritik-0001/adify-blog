## Rice Cooker Capacity Guide

| Capacity | Serves | Best For |
|----------|--------|---------|
| 0.6–0.8L | 1–2 people | Hostel students, singles |
| 1.0L | 1–3 people | Couples, small families |
| 1.5–1.8L | 3–4 people | Small families |
| 2.2–2.8L | 5–6 people | Larger families |

Capacity refers to cooked rice output (uncooked rice expands 2–2.5x when cooked). A 1-litre cooker produces 1 litre of cooked rice from ~400g of uncooked rice.
