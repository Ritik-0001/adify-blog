## How Solar Security Cameras Work

Solar security cameras have two key components:

**Battery:** Rechargeable lithium battery (typically 5,000–10,000 mAh) stores energy for overnight and cloudy-day operation.

**Solar panel:** 3W–6W panel that charges the battery during daylight. In India's 5–8 hours of daily sunlight, even a 3W panel keeps most cameras charged without issue.

**Motion-activated recording:** These cameras don't record continuously — they activate on motion detection. This dramatically reduces battery drain and storage consumption. Continuous recording is not practical on battery/solar cameras.

## Solar Camera Placement in India

**South-facing panel:** Solar panels in India's northern hemisphere must face south to maximize sunlight exposure. Angle the panel at approximately your city's latitude (Mumbai 19°, Delhi 28°, Bengaluru 12°).

**Avoid shade:** Partial shading dramatically reduces solar panel output. Keep panel clear of tree branches and building overhangs.

**Wi-Fi reach:** Solar cameras still need Wi-Fi. For remote locations (farm perimeter, boundary wall), a Wi-Fi range extender may be needed. 4G-connected solar cameras are available but significantly more expensive (₹8,000–₁₅,000) and require a SIM card.

**Best use cases in India:** Farm gates, agricultural land, construction site, boundary wall, parking area, holiday home.
