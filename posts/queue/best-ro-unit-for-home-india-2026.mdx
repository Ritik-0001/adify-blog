## Why RO (Not Just UV) Matters for Hard Water

RO membrane filtration physically removes dissolved salts and heavy metals that UV purification cannot address — UV kills biological contaminants but does nothing for dissolved solids, making it inadequate alone for genuinely hard or high-TDS water sources.
