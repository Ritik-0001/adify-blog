## GIF 2026 Laptop Buying Strategy

**1. Bank card cashback adds 5–10% more:** SBI card holders typically get 10% instant discount (up to ₹1,500) and SBI credit card EMI offers. ICICI and Axis Bank also participate. Check which bank has the best offer before the sale starts.

**2. EMI no-cost offers:** Most laptops above ₹30,000 have no-cost EMI options during GIF — 6–24 months at 0% interest. The total cost is the same as outright purchase, but spreads payment.

**3. Early Bird vs Day 2–3:** The first 6–12 hours of GIF see the deepest discounts on premium items. Laptops often sell out in popular configurations. Have your payment method ready and checkout quickly.

**4. Compare with Flipkart Big Billion Days:** Flipkart's equivalent sale runs simultaneously (same October dates). Compare final price (after cashback) on both platforms before buying.

**5. Add-on accessories at GIF price:** Laptop bags, mice, keyboards, and external SSDs also get GIF discounts. Bundle purchases save on shipping and you're already buying.

## What Not to Buy at GIF

- **8GB RAM laptops:** Some models are discounted heavily but 8GB RAM is a future performance limitation. The saving isn't worth the long-term compromise.
- **Previous generation chips:** Some GIF deals clear 11th/10th gen Intel stock. Avoid — current gen 13th gen Intel or Ryzen 7000 series is meaningfully better.
- **Unknown brands:** Stick to HP, Dell, Lenovo, ASUS, Acer, Apple — brands with service networks in India.

**Recommendation:** MacBook Air M2 16GB if budget allows. Lenovo IdeaPad Slim 5 Ryzen 7 for the best value under ₹60,000 after GIF discount.
