## How to Choose a Bedroom Wardrobe

Measure the room and the wall. Allow clearance for doors to swing open, or choose sliding doors if space is tight. Check the height against the ceiling and any fans or lights.

Look at materials and build. Thicker engineered boards and strong hinges last longer. Steel wardrobes resist moisture and pests but are heavier.

Check the interior layout. Hanging space, shelves, drawers and a locker should match how you store clothes.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Ignoring door swing.** Check clearance.

**Buying thin boards.** They sag and warp.

**Overlooking assembly.** Plan for the brand's technician.

**Ignoring humidity.** Choose suitable materials for damp climates.

## What to Check on the Listing

Confirm the dimensions, board thickness, hinge type, weight capacity and warranty. See whether assembly is included.

## Quick FAQ

**Sliding or hinged doors?** Sliding saves space, and hinged doors give full access.

**Steel or wood?** Steel is durable, and wood looks warmer.

**How long do wardrobes last?** Often ten years or more with good care.

## Planning the Interior

Think about what you store. Long hanging space suits sarees, coats and dresses; short hanging suits shirts and trousers. Add drawers for innerwear and accessories, shelves for folded clothes and a locker for documents and valuables. A good layout uses vertical space well and keeps daily items at eye level.

Check the weight the shelves and rods can take. Thin shelves sag under heavy folded clothes, and cheap rods bend. Ask about adjustable shelves so you can change the layout later.

## Delivery and Assembly

Wardrobes are heavy and bulky. Measure the door, lift and staircase before ordering. Many brands send a technician for assembly, and it is worth confirming this before you pay. Assemble on a level floor, and fix taller wardrobes to the wall if the manual recommends it, especially in homes with children.

## Sliding vs Hinged Doors

Hinged doors give full access to the interior and are simple and reliable, but they need clearance in front of the wardrobe. Sliding doors save floor space, which suits narrow bedrooms, but you can only open half the width at a time, and the tracks need occasional cleaning. If your room is tight, sliding doors are usually the smarter choice, and if you want full visibility of your clothes, choose hinged.

## Moisture and Pest Care

Humid cities benefit from small silica gel packs or moisture absorbers inside the wardrobe, and neem leaves or natural repellents can help against pests. Avoid overstuffing, since airflow keeps clothes fresher. Clean the interior occasionally and keep the wardrobe a few centimetres away from damp walls.
