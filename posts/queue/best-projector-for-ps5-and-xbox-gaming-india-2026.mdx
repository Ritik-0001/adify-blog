## Input lag: the number to find

Input lag is the delay between pressing a button and seeing the result. Under about 20 ms feels responsive for most players; under 10 ms suits competitive shooters. Many home-cinema projectors have 40 to 100 ms in standard mode and only reach lower numbers in a game mode, which often reduces image processing. Look for a stated figure for the mode and resolution you will use, for example "16 ms at 4K 60Hz" or "8 ms at 1080p 120Hz". If the listing gives no number, assume it is not low.

## 4K 60Hz versus 1080p 120Hz

The PS5 and Xbox Series X can output 4K at 120Hz in supported games, but a projector must accept it on HDMI 2.1 and show it. Many "4K" projectors accept a 4K signal over HDMI 2.0 at 60Hz only, and many of the pixel-shifting models display 4K through a smaller chip. A few gaming projectors offer 1080p at 120Hz or higher at very low lag, which suits fast shooters better than a sharper but slower 4K picture. Choose by game type: story games prefer 4K HDR, competitive games prefer high refresh.

## HDMI claims worth checking

Be sceptical of listings that say "supports 4K" without specifying HDMI version. Check for HDMI 2.0 or 2.1, HDCP 2.2, and whether VRR (variable refresh rate) is supported, as few projectors support it. If you use the PS5's HDR, the projector needs HDR10 support, but remember that HDR on a projector is dimmer than on a TV.

## Brightness and room

For a room you can darken, 1,500 to 2,500 ANSI lumens is enough for a 100 inch picture. If you play with lights on or in a bright flat, aim higher, 3,000 and above, or reduce the screen size. Do not trust "LED lumens" or "supported lumens" numbers; seek ANSI lumens. [Search 3000 lumen projectors](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=3000+ANSI+lumens+projector&tag=adifystore-21).

## Practical pitfalls

- A projector's fan noise can interfere with quiet moments in single-player games. Look for a noise rating around 30 dB in eco mode.
- Wall colour affects the picture. A white or light grey wall is best.
- Keystone correction digitally distorts the image and adds delay; place the projector square to the screen.
- Sound from the projector is weak. Plan for a soundbar or headphones with the console.
- Latency can differ between HDMI inputs. Connect the console to the input specified for game mode.

## Who should skip a projector for gaming

If you play competitive online shooters seriously, a 120Hz TV or monitor will feel better than any projector at this price. If your room is bright, a TV also gives better HDR. [See 4K 120Hz TVs](https://www.amazon.in/s?k=4K+120Hz+gaming+TV&tag=adifystore-21).

## Recommendation

For cinematic single-player gaming on a big screen in a dark room, choose a native or pixel-shifted 4K projector with a stated game-mode lag near 16 ms. For fast shooters, choose a model that offers 1080p at 120Hz. Check HDMI version, ANSI lumens, lag figures and your room's dimming before ordering.
