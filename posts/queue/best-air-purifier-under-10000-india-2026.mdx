## Coverage vs Budget Trade-off

Under ₹10,000, coverage is limited to 20–30m². This means:

**Works for:** Bedroom (10–15m²), nursery, home office room, small living area in 1-BHK.

**Doesn't work for:** Open-plan living areas (30m²+), entire house coverage, high-ceiling rooms.

For a 2-BHK family wanting whole-house protection: buy one budget unit for the bedroom (where you spend 8 hours) and save up for a larger model for common areas.

**Recommendation:** Xiaomi Air Purifier 3C for most buyers under ₹10,000 — highest CADR in this budget means maximum particle removal for bedroom use. If you need gas/odour filtration (near highway, kitchen odours), choose Honeywell Air Touch U2 with its activated carbon layer.
