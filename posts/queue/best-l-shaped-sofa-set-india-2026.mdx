## How to Choose an L Shaped Sofa

Measure your room. Allow at least a metre of walking space around the sofa, and make sure the chaise or return side fits the wall you have in mind. Decide whether the chaise sits on the left or right, since many sofas are sold in one orientation.

Check the frame and the seat foam. A solid wood or strong engineered frame lasts longer, and high-density foam holds its shape. Ask about foam density in kilograms per cubic metre if the listing gives it.

Consider the fabric. Tight weaves and darker colours hide stains, and removable covers make cleaning easier. In humid coastal cities, breathable fabric is more comfortable than leatherette.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Buying on photos alone.** Colours and proportions look different in real rooms. Read reviews with customer photos.

**Ignoring seat depth.** A very deep seat can be uncomfortable for shorter people.

**Skipping the delivery plan.** Measure doors, lifts and staircases.

**Overlooking assembly.** Check whether assembly is included.

## Real-World Scenarios

**A small living room.** Choose a compact L shape with slim arms, and consider a modular set you can rearrange.

**A family with children.** Prefer stain-resistant fabric, sturdy frames and washable covers.

**A home with pets.** Look for tight weaves that resist claws and hair, and avoid delicate fabrics.

## What to Check on the Listing

Confirm overall dimensions, seat height and depth, frame material, foam density and warranty. See whether covers are removable and whether the price includes delivery and assembly.

## Quick FAQ

**How many people does an L shape seat?** Usually five or six, depending on the design.

**Fabric or leatherette?** Fabric is breathable and comfortable in Indian summers, and leatherette is easier to wipe.

**How long do sofas last?** Often eight to ten years or more with good frames and care.
