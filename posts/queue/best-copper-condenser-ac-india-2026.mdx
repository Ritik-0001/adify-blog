## Copper vs. Aluminum Condensers: The Real Difference

**Heat conductivity:** Copper conducts heat approximately 60% better than aluminum — this translates to more efficient heat exchange and better real-world cooling performance for the same rated capacity.

**Corrosion resistance:** Copper resists corrosion from humidity, salt air, and general environmental exposure significantly better than aluminum, which can develop pinhole leaks (refrigerant leaks) within 3-5 years in harsh conditions.

**Repairability:** Copper coils can be repaired (brazed) if damaged; aluminum coils typically require full replacement, making copper condensers cheaper to maintain over the AC's lifetime despite higher upfront material cost.

**The "hybrid" trap:** Some budget ACs use copper-aluminum hybrid condensers (copper tubes with aluminum fins, or vice versa) — always check for "100% copper" specification, not just "copper" which can be misleading marketing for hybrid designs.
