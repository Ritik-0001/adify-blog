## What NOT to Buy During Diwali Sale

**ACs:** The best AC deals are in February–March (pre-summer buying season when dealers clear stock). Diwali AC discounts are modest — 10–15% vs. 25–35% in March.

**Geysers/Water Heaters:** Best deals in September–October off-season in South India, but February–March before peak geyser season everywhere else. Diwali deals are average.

**Microwave Ovens:** Microwave deals are flat year-round — no meaningful Diwali premium. Buy when you need one.
