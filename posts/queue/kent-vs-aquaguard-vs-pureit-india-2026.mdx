## Filtration Quality

**Kent:** Multi-stage RO+UV+UF with patented TDS Controller. Water retains essential minerals. ISI-marked. Most comprehensive purification available at its price point.

**Aquaguard:** Multi-stage RO+UV+MTDS. Good filtration quality. e-boiling+ technology adds additional UV sterilisation step in some models. Comparable to Kent in purification outcomes.

**Pureit:** HUL (Hindustan Unilever) technology. Advanced RO membrane. Eco Water Saver model recovers 60% water vs industry average 25–40%. Purification quality matches Kent and Aquaguard.

**Winner: Tie between Kent and Pureit** — Kent for TDS control, Pureit for water efficiency.

---

## Service Network

**Kent:** Service network is good in metros and Tier 1 cities. AMC (Annual Maintenance Contract) available. Some gaps in Tier 3 cities and rural areas.

**Aquaguard:** Strongest service network across India — 1,500+ service centres including Tier 3 cities and smaller towns. If you live outside a major metro, Aquaguard's technician will come faster.

**Pureit:** Available through HUL distribution network. Filter cartridges available at local general stores (not just Amazon/Flipkart). Service network decent but smaller than Aquaguard's.

**Winner: Aquaguard** — unmatched pan-India reach for service.
