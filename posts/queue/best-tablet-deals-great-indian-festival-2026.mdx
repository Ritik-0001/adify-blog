## Questions to Answer Before Buying

**Will you use a stylus regularly?** If yes, prioritize iPad or Galaxy Tab S9 FE — both have mature stylus ecosystems, unlike most budget Android tablets.

**Is this a shared household device or a personal one?** Shared tablets benefit more from larger screens and longer battery life than from premium build quality.

**Do you already own phones or laptops in one ecosystem?** Staying within Apple or Samsung's ecosystem improves file sharing and continuity features meaningfully.
