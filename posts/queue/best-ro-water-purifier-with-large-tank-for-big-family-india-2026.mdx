## How to Choose a Large Tank RO

Match tank size to usage. A big family may need 10 to 15 litres of storage, with a purification rate of around 15 to 25 litres per hour or more.

Check technology for your water. RO handles high TDS, UV and UF protect against microbes, and TDS control keeps some minerals in.

Look at service and water waste. RO systems reject water, so check recovery and use reject water for cleaning where possible.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Choosing a big tank without checking space.** Measure the wall.

**Ignoring TDS.** Test water first.

**Skipping service details.** Ask about AMC and filters.

**Leaving water in the tank for days.** Use regularly.

## Real-World Scenarios

**A joint family of eight.** A 12 to 15 litre RO plus UV purifier.

**A family with a low-pressure supply.** A model with a booster pump.

**A home with high TDS.** A purifier with a strong pre-filter.

## What to Check on the Listing

Confirm the tank size, the purification rate, the technologies, the TDS control, the warranty and the installation terms. See whether the seller offers free installation and a service plan.

## Installation and Maintenance

Mount the unit at a comfortable height near a power point and the water supply, and plan for drainage of reject water. Service filters on schedule and clean the tank periodically.

## Warranty and Service

Compare warranty on the whole unit and key parts, and check service in your city. Keep the invoice and installation records.

## Quick FAQ

**Is a larger tank better?** For big families, yes.

**Does it waste more water?** Not because of the tank, but RO rejects water.

**Is UV needed?** It adds microbial protection.

## Daily Capacity Versus Tank Size

Tank size is only part of the story, because the purification rate decides how fast the tank refills. A family that uses a lot of water in the morning needs a tank that holds enough for peak demand and a purifier that refills it by evening. Ask for both the tank capacity and the litres per hour rating, and compare them to your daily use.

## Final Checklist Before You Buy

Before you order, test your water TDS, check the tank size and purification rate against your daily use and confirm the technology suits your water source. Ask for the annual service or AMC terms in writing, check that filters and membranes are available in your city and keep the invoice and installation record.
