## Why Ink Tank Printers Suit Student Households Best

**Unpredictable volume:** School printing spikes around project deadlines and exam seasons — ink tank printers absorb these spikes without a sudden ink cost shock the way cartridge printers do.

**Color for projects:** Charts, diagrams, and presentation covers benefit from reliable color printing at low per-page cost.

**Fewer "out of ink" emergencies:** Ink tanks hold significantly more volume than cartridges, reducing the likelihood of running dry right before an assignment deadline.

**Enable Wi-Fi printing from all household devices** so each child can print directly from their own laptop or tablet without file-transfer hassle.
