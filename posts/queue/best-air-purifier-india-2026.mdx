## Understanding CADR for India

CADR (Clean Air Delivery Rate, measured in m³/hour) is the key specification for matching purifier to room size.

**Rule:** CADR should be at least 2/3 of room volume for hourly air changes adequate for Indian pollution levels.

For a 15m² bedroom (ceiling height 2.7m = 40.5m³): minimum CADR ~27 m³/hour.

For a 30m² living room (81m³): minimum CADR ~54 m³/hour.

Most Indian buyers underestimate room coverage needs and buy undersized purifiers.

## Air Purifier Placement for Indian Homes

**Bedroom priority:** Sleep 7–8 hours in the bedroom — this is where time-weighted exposure reduction matters most. Put your best purifier in the bedroom.

**Avoid corners:** Place in centre of room or where airflow moves across breathing zone. Corners create dead zones.

**Run continuously:** Turning off for hours defeats the purpose. Noise concern? Sleep mode on most models drops to 20–30dB.

**Pre-filter cleaning:** Most HEPA purifiers have a washable pre-filter for dust/pet hair — clean monthly to extend HEPA life.
