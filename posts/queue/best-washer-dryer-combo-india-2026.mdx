## Heat Pump vs. Condenser vs. Vented Drying

**Heat pump drying:** Most energy-efficient and gentlest on fabrics — recirculates and reheats air using a refrigerant cycle rather than direct heating elements. Longer dry cycles but lowest running cost and fabric wear.

**Condenser drying:** Moderate efficiency, no external venting needed — condenses moisture into a removable water tank you empty periodically.

**Vented drying:** Least common in Indian combo units — requires external venting installation, generally less practical for apartment settings.

For Indian apartment living, heat pump or condenser technology (both ventless) are the practical choices — always verify which technology a combo unit uses before purchasing.
