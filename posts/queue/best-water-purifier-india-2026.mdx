## Understanding Purification Technologies

**RO (Reverse Osmosis):** Forces water through a semipermeable membrane. Removes dissolved solids (TDS), heavy metals (lead, arsenic, fluoride), and most bacteria. Requires electricity. Produces some reject water (wastewater). Essential for high-TDS water.

**UV (Ultraviolet):** UV light kills bacteria and viruses by damaging their DNA. Does NOT remove dissolved solids or heavy metals. Works only on clear water. Effective for low-TDS municipal water.

**UF (Ultrafiltration):** Physical membrane filtration. Removes bacteria and suspended particles without electricity. Does not remove dissolved solids.

**TDS Controller:** Blends some unfiltered water back with RO-purified water to maintain healthy mineral levels (ideal: 150–300 TDS).

## Water Type to Purifier Type Guide

| Water Source | TDS Range | Recommended Type |
|--------------|-----------|-----------------|
| Municipal tap | 100–500 | RO+UV or UV only |
| Municipal tap (lead/chlorine issues) | Any | RO+UV+UF |
| Borewell | 500–2,000+ | RO+UV (mandatory) |
| Tanker water | Variable | RO+UV+UF |
| River/surface water | Variable | RO+UV+UF |

**Test your TDS:** Buy a TDS meter (₹200–₄₀₀) to test your tap water. Above 300 TDS, RO is recommended. Below 200 TDS from clean municipal source, UV alone may suffice.
