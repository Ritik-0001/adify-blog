## Which Technology Do You Need?

**Test your water TDS first:** TDS meters are available for ₹200–₃₀₀. Insert in a glass of tap water. The reading determines your purifier type.

- TDS under 200 ppm: UV purifier sufficient. RO is optional (wastes water unnecessarily).
- TDS 200–500 ppm: RO+UV recommended. Hard water, minerals and dissolved solids need removal.
- TDS above 500 ppm: RO+UV+UF essential. Very hard water, common in North and Central India.

**Technology types:**
- **RO (Reverse Osmosis):** Removes dissolved salts, heavy metals, TDS. Also removes bacteria and viruses. Wastes approximately 3L of water for every 1L purified at standard TDS.
- **UV (Ultraviolet):** Kills bacteria and viruses. Does NOT reduce TDS or remove dissolved minerals. Best for low-TDS water.
- **UF (Ultrafiltration):** Physical membrane filter. Removes bacteria, cysts, and suspended particles. Works without electricity. Does not reduce TDS.
