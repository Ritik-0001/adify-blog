## How to Size an AC for 150 sq ft

Start with the room area. As a rough guide, up to about 100 to 120 square feet suits 1 ton, around 120 to 180 square feet suits 1.5 ton and larger rooms need 2 ton.

Adjust for conditions. Top-floor rooms, west-facing walls, large windows, a tall ceiling or a kitchen next door raise the load, and a more efficient 1.5 ton with a good inverter handles this better than a smaller unit.

Choose the star rating. A 5-star model costs more upfront and typically uses less electricity, so the savings depend on how many hours you run it each year.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Buying too small.** An undersized AC runs constantly and wears out sooner.

**Buying too large.** An oversized unit may cool unevenly and waste energy.

**Ignoring installation.** Copper piping and mounting charges add up.

**Skipping servicing.** Clean filters improve efficiency.

## What to Check on the Listing

Confirm the capacity, the star rating, the compressor and PCB warranty, the condenser coil material, the installation terms and the model number. See whether basic installation is included.

## Running It Efficiently

Set the thermostat to around 24 to 26 degrees, use the sleep or eco mode, keep doors and windows closed and clean the filters monthly. A ceiling fan on low speed helps spread cool air and lets you use a slightly higher setting.

## Quick FAQ

**Is 1.5 ton enough for 150 sq ft?** Usually, yes.

**3-star or 5-star?** 5-star costs more but uses less power with heavy use.

**Does an inverter AC save power?** Yes compared with fixed-speed models.

## Installation Details That Affect Cost

Basic installation often covers a short length of copper piping, a drain pipe and mounting, while extra piping, an outdoor stand or wall cutting costs more. Ask what is included, and get the installer to confirm the position of the indoor unit, the outdoor unit and the drain before work starts. A neat installation reduces noise and leaks.

## Warranty and Service

Compare the warranty on the compressor, the PCB and the whole unit, because the compressor is the most expensive part. Ask whether the brand has service centres in your city and how quickly technicians usually respond in peak summer.
