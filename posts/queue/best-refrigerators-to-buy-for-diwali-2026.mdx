## How to Choose a Refrigerator

Pick capacity by family size: about 200 to 250 litres for three people, 250 to 350 for four to five, and larger for joint families. Double-door frost-free models are the standard choice in most homes.

Look at the compressor type and warranty. Inverter compressors adjust cooling to demand, which generally reduces electricity use.

Measure your space, the doorway and the route to the kitchen. Confirm the delivery and installation policy before you order.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Buying the largest fridge you can afford.** An oversized fridge uses more power and takes up space. Match capacity to your family size and shopping habits.

**Not measuring the doorway.** Side-by-sides and large double-doors have been returned because they could not pass through a door or lift. Measure before you order.

**Ignoring ventilation.** Refrigerators need clearance at the back and sides to release heat. A tight niche can reduce efficiency and shorten life.

**Focusing only on the star rating.** Also look at the compressor type, warranty and shelf layout, since these affect daily use.

## Quick FAQ

**Is an inverter compressor worth it?** Inverter compressors adjust speed to demand and are generally quieter and more efficient, which is why most current double-door models use them.

**How many stars should I look for?** A 3-star fridge is common and priced sensibly. A 4- or 5-star model can save more electricity over time, but check whether the extra cost is worth it for your usage.

**Single-door or double-door?** Single-door suits one or two people. Double-door is better for families because the freezer is separate and stays cold when you open the fridge.

**What is a convertible freezer?** Some models let you turn part of the freezer into fridge space, which is handy during festivals when you need extra room for food.

## Buying Tips Before Diwali

Delivery and installation slots fill up quickly in the weeks before a major festival, so order early if you want the fridge in place before guests arrive. Ask the seller whether the old fridge can be exchanged or removed, and clear the space and power point in advance.

Give the new fridge time to settle. Most brands recommend leaving a refrigerator upright for a few hours after delivery before switching it on, and then letting it cool fully before loading food. Follow the manual rather than rushing.
