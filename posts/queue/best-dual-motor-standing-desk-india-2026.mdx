## Why Dual-Motor Is Worth Prioritizing

**Perfect synchronization:** Both legs raise and lower in exact tandem, eliminating the tilting that single-motor crossbar mechanisms develop over years of use.

**Faster transitions:** 15-20 seconds versus 30-40 seconds for single-motor desks — meaningful if you switch positions multiple times daily.

**Higher weight capacity:** 100-120kg versus 70-80kg, important for multi-monitor setups with monitor arms and additional equipment.
