## Hair Dryer Tips for Indian Hair

**Don't dry completely wet hair on high heat:** Towel-blot to 60–70% dry first. High heat on soaking wet hair causes cuticle damage.

**Keep 15–20cm distance:** Hold dryer 15–20cm from hair while moving constantly. Stationary heat on one section burns hair.

**Use concentrator nozzle:** For straight styles, concentrator directs airflow along hair shaft for smoother results. Diffuser (bowl attachment) for curly/wavy styles.

**Cool shot:** After styling, use cool shot (blue button on most dryers) to set the style. Cold air closes the hair cuticle, adding shine and holding the style longer.
