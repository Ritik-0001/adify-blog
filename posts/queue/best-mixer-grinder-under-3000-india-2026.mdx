## What to Expect Under ₹3,000

**Works well for:**
- Blending smoothies and shakes
- Grinding small quantities of dry spices
- Making chutneys (small jar)
- Light wet grinding (small quantities)

**Struggles with:**
- Large-batch batter grinding (500W overheats)
- Hard spices like dried red chillies in bulk
- Continuous operation beyond 3–4 minutes

For daily South Indian cooking with significant wet grinding needs (idli, dosa batter), budget for ₹5,000+ where 750W motors handle sustained loads.
