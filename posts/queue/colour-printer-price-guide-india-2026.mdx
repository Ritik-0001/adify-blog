Colour printer prices in India in 2026 range from ₹4,000 for basic cartridge models to ₹12,000+ for full-featured ink tank all-in-ones — this price guide breaks down what you actually get at each tier, and why the middle-to-upper range delivers dramatically better long-term value despite requiring a higher upfront investment.

Printer pricing in India follows a clear pattern: the cheapest machines shift their real cost into ongoing ink expenses, while slightly pricier ink tank models front-load the cost into the machine itself and then cost almost nothing to run.

## Price Tier Breakdown

**₹3,500-₴,500 (Budget cartridge):** HP DeskJet, Canon PIXMA MG-series. Low upfront cost, but ₹3-₸ per color page — expensive to actually use.

**₹8,000-₹10,000 (Entry ink tank):** HP Ink Tank 315. Genuine ink tank economics but print-only, no scan/copy.

**₹9,000-₹12,000 (Full-featured ink tank):** Epson L3252 EcoTank, Canon PIXMA G3010. The sweet spot — ink tank cost, Wi-Fi, all-in-one functionality.

**₹15,000-₴5,000 (Business/high-volume ink tank):** Epson L6290 and similar. Larger paper trays, automatic duplex printing, higher-volume tanks for small offices.

---

## Why the Middle Tier Wins

At ₹10,000-₹12,000, you get everything that matters for home and small-business use — genuine ink economics, wireless printing, and multi-function capability — without paying for business-grade features (large paper trays, network printing for multiple users) that most households don't need.

---

## When to Consider the Budget Tier

Only for genuinely occasional color printing — a handful of pages per month where the cartridge cost never accumulates meaningfully, and the ₹6,000+ savings on the machine itself outweighs the higher per-page cost.

---

## Recommendation

Budget toward the ₹10,000-₹12,000 tier for the best overall value in color printing — this range consistently delivers the technology (ink tank) that actually determines your total cost of ownership, rather than just the lowest upfront sticker price.
