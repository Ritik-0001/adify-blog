## How to Build a Starter Kit

Pick one ecosystem. Choose between Alexa and Google Home, and buy devices that clearly support it, to avoid juggling several apps.

Start with high-value items. A hub, a few plugs and bulbs and one security device give the most benefit for the least money.

Plan your Wi-Fi. A good router or mesh system matters, since many smart devices rely on a stable connection throughout the flat.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Mixing too many brands.** Compatibility headaches follow.

**Ignoring Wi-Fi.** Weak signal breaks routines.

**Skipping security basics.** Change default passwords.

**Overbuying.** Add devices as you find needs.

## Real-World Scenarios

**A couple in a 2BHK.** Hub, plugs, bulbs and a smart lock.

**A family with kids.** A door phone, cameras and a few sensors.

**A work-from-home setup.** Smart lighting and plugs for scheduling.

## What to Check on the Listing

Confirm the supported assistant, the connectivity type, the power rating for plugs, the app, the warranty and the privacy features. See what happens if Wi-Fi or power goes down.

## Router and Network Planning

A dual-band or mesh Wi-Fi system with good coverage keeps devices connected, and a UPS for the router helps during power cuts. Place the router centrally and keep smart devices on a stable network.

## Warranty and Service

Check the warranty and support for each device, and keep invoices and account details in one place. Update firmware regularly and review app permissions.

## Quick FAQ

**Do I need a hub?** Many devices work through a smart speaker.

**Is it secure?** Use strong passwords and two-step verification.

**Can I add devices later?** Yes, within your ecosystem.

## A Sensible Staged Budget

Spend the first part of your budget on the hub and a handful of plugs and bulbs, the second on security, such as a lock or a door phone, and the third on cameras and sensors. Live with each stage for a few weeks and see what you actually use. This avoids buying gadgets that sit unused and keeps your routines simple enough for the whole family.
