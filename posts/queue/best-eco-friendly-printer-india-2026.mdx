## Printer Waste in India — The Problem

Standard cartridge inkjet printers require a new cartridge every 150–300 pages. Each cartridge is a single-use plastic unit — approximately 50–100g of plastic waste per cartridge. A household printing 200 pages/month generates 8–16 cartridges per year — 400–800g of plastic waste annually.

EcoTank ink bottles: Compact, mostly paper packaging. One set prints 4,500–7,500 pages. Dramatically less waste per page.

Laser toner cartridges: Larger plastic units (200–500g) but print 1,000–3,000 pages per cartridge — better plastic efficiency than cartridge inkjet.

## Practical Tips for Greener Printing in India

**Use both sides:** Set your default print settings to duplex. Most print dialogs have this option.

**Print preview:** Check before printing — avoid printing errors that waste paper.

**Digital first:** Scan documents to PDF rather than printing for filing.

**Recycled paper:** Available on Amazon India (JK Copier, Bilt) — slightly higher cost but reduces virgin paper demand.

**Toner cartridge recycling:** HP and Canon have recycling programs. Ask at authorized service centers in major cities.

**Recommendation:** Epson EcoTank L3252 for home use — the cartridge waste reduction alone is the biggest environmental improvement most households can make to their printing habits. Upgrade to L6490 for offices where automatic duplex paper savings compound the benefit.
