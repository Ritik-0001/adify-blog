## Colour Accuracy at Under ₹15,000 — What's Achievable

**sRGB coverage:** Most IPS monitors in this range cover 92–96% of the sRGB colour space. For web/social video delivery (YouTube, Instagram, Reels), 95%+ sRGB is adequate.

**Delta E:** Colour accuracy is measured in Delta E (dE) — lower is better. Factory-calibrated professional monitors achieve dE under 2. Budget IPS monitors typically run dE 3–6 out of the box, which produces visible colour inaccuracy but is workable at this budget with monitoring profiles.

**No factory calibration:** No monitor under ₹15,000 comes factory-calibrated. If you have a colorimeter (Datacolor Spyder X Pro at ₹9,000–₁₂,000), calibrate the display for the most accurate results.
