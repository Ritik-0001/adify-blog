## How to Choose Premium ANC Headphones

Prioritise comfort. You will wear them for hours, so weight, clamp force and ear cup size matter. Try to check whether the ear cups fit around your ears.

Look at noise cancelling and calls. Adaptive ANC that adjusts to the environment, plus good microphones, matters for commuting and calls.

Check battery and connectivity. Thirty hours or more of battery and multipoint connection, which lets you switch between two devices, add convenience.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Buying on brand alone.** Fit varies.

**Ignoring the case.** Some cases are bulky.

**Expecting silence.** ANC reduces steady noise, not everything.

**Skipping the warranty check.** Buy from authorised sellers.

## Sound Quality and Codecs

Most premium headphones sound very good, and differences come down to tuning. Sony offers LDAC on supported Android phones for higher-bitrate wireless audio, while iPhones use AAC. If you care about the sound, use the app's equaliser to shape it to taste, and spend a week listening before you judge.

## Battery, Charging and Travel

Look at battery life with noise cancelling on, quick-charge performance and the case. Frequent flyers will appreciate a case that folds compactly and a 3.5 mm cable for in-flight systems. USB-C charging means one cable can serve your phone and headphones.

## Fit, Cushions and Long Wear

Ear cup size and cushion material decide comfort more than the brand name. Protein leather cushions seal well but can feel warm in Indian summers, while fabric cushions breathe better. Check that replacement cushions are sold locally, since they wear after a couple of years of daily use.
