## How to Choose for a Small Flat

Prioritise noise. Look for a cushioned deck, a smooth motor and reviews that mention low noise, and use a mat. Run during hours that suit your neighbours.

Check the folded size. Compare the folded dimensions and weight with your storage space, and make sure the machine fits through doors and lifts.

Choose the right motor. A continuous motor of around 1.5 to 2 HP suits jogging in a small flat, while heavier running needs more.

## Living With Neighbours

Check your society's rules and choose running hours that are considerate, such as mid-morning or early evening rather than late at night or very early in the morning. Walking is much quieter than running, and a heavier rubber mat under the machine reduces vibration through the floor. On an upper floor, place the treadmill near a load-bearing wall or an outside wall where the floor has good support.

## Mats and Floor Protection

A dense rubber mat under the treadmill does two jobs: it softens footfall noise before it reaches the slab and it protects tile or wood from the frame and the rear rollers. Pick one a little larger than the base so that the wheels never sit on bare floor. Check the floor under the mat for hollow spots, because a treadmill placed over a hollow patch of tiling will sound louder than the machine really is.

## Storage and Moving

Choose a model with transport wheels so that you can roll it into a corner after your session. Fold it carefully and lock the fold, and store it away from damp spots such as balcony doors during the monsoon. Wipe the console and belt with a dry cloth, and cover the machine if it sits near an open window.

## Who Should Skip a Treadmill Altogether

In a studio flat or a ground-floor room with no spare corner, a treadmill that must be unfolded every day soon becomes a coat rack. If you mainly want daily steps, a walking pad that slides under the bed is easier to live with, and an evening walk outside costs nothing. Buy a folding treadmill only if you will run on it indoors and have a corner where it can stay folded without blocking a door.

## Recommendation

For most small flats, a compact folding treadmill from PowerMax or Cult.sport with a mat is a sensible pick. If you only walk, choose a walking pad.
