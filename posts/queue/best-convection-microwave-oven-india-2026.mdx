## How to Choose a Convection Microwave

Pick the capacity. Twenty-five to 28 litres suits two to four people, and 30 litres or more suits larger families or big bakes. Check the turntable and tray size against your favourite dishes.

Understand the modes. Convection heats the whole cavity like an oven, grill adds top heat for browning, and microwave reheats quickly. Combination modes mix them for faster cooking. Look for a rack and a convection tray.

Look at cleaning. A ceramic or stain-resistant cavity saves effort, and a removable tray helps.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Using metal in microwave mode.** Use only in convection or as the manual allows.

**Skipping preheat.** Baking needs a preheated cavity.

**Overloading.** Leave space for air circulation.

**Ignoring counter clearance.** Leave space for ventilation.

## Real-World Scenarios

**A family that bakes cakes on weekends.** A 28 to 30 litre convection model is enough for standard cakes, and a rack helps with multiple trays.

**A household that reheats and cooks daily.** Look for a convenient interface and strong auto menus.

**A small kitchen.** Check dimensions carefully, since convection models are bulkier.

## What to Check on the Listing

Confirm the capacity, the turntable size, the modes, the cavity material and the warranty. See whether accessories such as a rack and a convection tray are included.

## Quick FAQ

**Can I bake in a convection microwave?** Yes, in convection mode.

**Is convection better than an OTG?** OTGs are larger and cheaper for heavy baking, and convection microwaves combine functions.

**How long do they last?** Eight to ten years.

## Convection Microwave vs OTG vs Air Fryer

A convection microwave gives you three appliances in one: it reheats, grills and bakes. An OTG is larger and cheaper for serious baking, but it cannot reheat food quickly. An air fryer is faster for snacks and small portions, but has limited capacity. If you have one slot on your counter and cook a variety of dishes, a convection microwave is the most flexible.
