## How to Choose for Your Dad

Think about the routine he already has. If he reads the newspaper each morning, a Kindle with large font suits him; if he checks his blood pressure, a monitor supports that habit; if he wears the same watch every day, a new one can be a treat.

Avoid gadgets that need constant updates or complicated apps. Simple controls, clear displays and reliable service centres matter more than the latest features.

Finally, consider the message. Many fathers do not ask for anything, so a gift that says you noticed how they live carries more weight than an expensive one. Add a short note and spend a little time with him when you hand it over.

## Quick FAQ

**What if my dad says he does not need anything?** Many fathers say that. Choose something that supports a routine he already has, such as a watch, a health monitor or a reading device, and frame it as something you would like him to enjoy.

**Is a smartwatch a good gift for an older father?** It can be, if he is curious about health tracking and comfortable with a phone. If not, a classic analog watch is far less likely to be left in a drawer.

**Are health devices a sensitive gift?** They can be, so present them as a convenience rather than a worry. An Omron monitor is widely used and often welcomed as a practical tool.

**Do I need to set the gift up?** For anything electronic, yes. Ten minutes of setup and a quick demonstration make the difference between a gift that is used and one that stays in the box.
