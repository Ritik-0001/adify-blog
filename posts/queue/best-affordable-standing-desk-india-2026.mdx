## The Budget Standing Desk Options in India

### Option 1: Sit-Stand Desktop Converter (₹4,000–₈,000)

A converter sits on top of your existing desk and raises a platform (for your monitor, keyboard, and mouse) to standing height. You push it up to stand, lower it to sit — no desk replacement needed.

**Best for:** People who already have a desk they're happy with and want to add standing capability for under ₹8,000.

**Limitation:** Most converters are fixed-height at standing position — you raise them to stand, lower them to sit. No infinite adjustment.

**Best for:** Buyers who want a complete standing desk at the lowest price. Manual cranking is acceptable if height changes are infrequent.

### Option 3: Adjustable Leg Frames for DIY Desk (₹6,000–₁₀,000)

Leg frames only — you source or build your own tabletop (a wooden board from a lumber shop costs ₹800–₁,500 for 140cm × 70cm). Adjustable leg height (telescoping, not motorized) allows manual height setting per user.

**Best for:** Buyers who want to custom-size their desk surface or already have a tabletop.

---

### Option 4: Fixed-Height Standing Desk (₹4,000–₇,000)

A desk fixed at standing height (approximately 100–110cm) with no adjustment. You use a high stool to sit. Simple, inexpensive, but inflexible.

**Best for:** Buyers who primarily want to stand (e.g., creative professionals who paint, draw, or do physical work at a desk) without sitting adjustment needs.

## The Real Cost of Cheap Standing

Anti-fatigue mat (₹1,500–₃,000) is not optional — it's essential. Standing on Indian stone or tile floors without cushioning causes foot pain and back fatigue that makes standing desk use unsustainable. Budget ₹1,500–₂,000 for a mat alongside any standing desk purchase.
