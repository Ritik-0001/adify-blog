## What Defines Premium Tier

**Flagship processing:** The most sophisticated upscaling, motion handling, and HDR tone-mapping algorithms each brand offers, often exclusive to top-tier models.

**Panel innovation:** Whether OLED evo brightness enhancement, Samsung's QD-OLED hybrid technology, or Sony's most advanced Mini-LED implementation — premium tier gets each brand's newest panel technology first.

**Audio integration:** Premium TVs increasingly include genuinely capable built-in audio systems (Sony's Acoustic Multi-Audio, LG's improved down-firing speakers) reducing dependence on separate soundbars.

**Design refinement:** Slimmer profiles, better cable management, and premium materials distinguish flagship models from mid-range alternatives.
