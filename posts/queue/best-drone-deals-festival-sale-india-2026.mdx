## What First-Time Buyers Should Know

**Sub-250g drones (DJI Neo, Mini series)** face lighter registration requirements under Indian drone rules, making them the practical starting point for hobbyists.

**Register on the Digital Sky platform before your first flight.** Even lightweight drones generally require basic registration in India — check current DGCA rules for your specific model before flying.

**Obstacle avoidance matters more than resolution for beginners.** A drone that won't crash into a tree on your first flight is more valuable early on than marginally sharper 4K footage.
