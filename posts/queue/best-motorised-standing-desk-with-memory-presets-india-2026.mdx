## How to Choose a Desk With Memory Presets

Check the number of presets. Three or four is enough for most people, but if several people share the desk, choose one that stores more positions.

Look at the controller. A clear display, a responsive keypad and a reliable memory function matter. Some controllers also include a reminder to stand.

Consider the frame. Presets are convenient, but the frame's stability at standing height decides whether you will actually enjoy using it.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Assuming all presets are equal.** Check how many and how they work.

**Ignoring stability.** Read reviews on wobble.

**Forgetting the desktop.** Confirm what is included.

**Not setting your heights.** Save your positions on day one.

## What to Check on the Listing

Confirm the number of presets, the weight capacity, the height range, the motor type and the warranty. See whether assembly instructions and tools are included.

## Quick FAQ

**Do presets matter?** Yes. They make switching quick, which helps you use the desk more.

**How many presets do I need?** Three or four suit most users.

**Are they noisy?** Motors make some noise. Check the decibel rating.

## Using Presets Well

Set a seated height and a standing height on the first day, then add a third preset for a comfortable perch if you use a stool. Alternate positions every thirty to sixty minutes rather than standing all day. Some controllers include reminders to change position, which helps build the habit.

Recalibrate if the desk shows an error code after a power cut, and keep the controller firmware or reset steps from the manual handy.

## Setup Tips

Assemble on a soft surface to avoid scratching the top, tighten the bolts in stages and check for level. Keep cables loose enough for the full travel. Test the collision protection if the desk has it, and keep children and pets clear when the desk is moving.
