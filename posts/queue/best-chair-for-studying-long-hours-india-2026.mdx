## Why Gaming Chairs Aren't Ideal for Study

Gaming chairs prioritize deep recline (140-165 degrees) for breaks between gaming sessions — this design encourages a semi-reclined posture that isn't ideal for sustained reading, writing, or screen-focused study work requiring an upright, engaged position.

---

## What Matters Most for Study Chairs

**Sustained upright support:** Dynamic lumbar support that holds proper posture through hours of forward-leaning study work.

**Breathable material:** Long sessions without movement (unlike gaming's more active engagement) make heat buildup a bigger comfort issue — breathable mesh matters significantly here.

**Appropriate height for desk work:** Verify the chair's height range matches your study desk properly for correct ergonomic alignment.
