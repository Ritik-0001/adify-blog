## Is Cloud Storage Worth It in India?

**Yes, worth it if:**
- Camera is at entrance or shop where theft of the camera itself is possible
- You travel frequently and need reliable remote access without relying on home internet being up
- Insurance requires cloud-stored footage for claims

**No, save money with SD card if:**
- Camera monitors a low-theft-risk internal area
- You're cost-conscious and check footage regularly

**Hybrid recommendation:** Use local SD card for day-to-day review + cloud subscription for the 1–2 most critical cameras (entrance, main door). This keeps cloud subscription costs under ₹300–₄₀₀/month while protecting the most important footage.
