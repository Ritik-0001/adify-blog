## Fitness Tracker vs Smartwatch for Health Monitoring

**Fitness tracker wins on:**
- Battery life (7–21 days vs 5–7 days for smartwatch)
- Health data accuracy (Fitbit and Garmin are clinically validated)
- Slim, comfortable design for 24/7 wear (important for sleep tracking)

**Smartwatch wins on:**
- Bluetooth calling from wrist
- Full notification mirroring
- Display size and brightness

For pure health tracking: choose a fitness tracker. For all-round daily utility: choose a smartwatch.

**Recommendation:** Mi Band 8 Pro for most budget-conscious buyers or gift-givers under ₹5,000. Fitbit Inspire 3 for health-serious recipients who will engage with the Fitbit app and Premium features.
