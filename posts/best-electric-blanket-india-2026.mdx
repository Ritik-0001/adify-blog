## Electric Blanket Safety

**Always use auto shut-off:** Don't use electric blankets without auto shut-off — overheating risk while sleeping.

**Don't fold while heated:** Folded heated blanket concentrates heat — fire risk. Use flat on bed.

**Not for infants or elderly with reduced sensation:** They cannot feel if the blanket gets too hot. Use lowest setting and limit to pre-warming the bed.

**Storage:** Store flat or loosely rolled, not tightly folded — bends heating wires.

**Running cost:** 150W electric blanket × 8 hours = 1.2 units × ₹7 = ₹8.40/night. A 2000W room heater for 3 hours = 6 units × ₹7 = ₹42/night. Electric blanket is 5× cheaper for personal warmth.

**Recommendation:** Orpat OEB 5 for most Indian buyers — the auto shut-off and 5 settings make it safe for overnight use. Use for pre-warming the bed (30 minutes before sleep) and low-heat overnight for maximum comfort at minimum cost.
