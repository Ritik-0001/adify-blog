## The Dead Zone Problem

A single AC unit's cold airflow weakens with distance — in rooms beyond roughly 25-30 feet from the indoor unit, or around corners in L-shaped spaces, you'll notice warm "dead zones" regardless of how high you set the tonnage. Adding more tonnage to a single unit doesn't solve this — it just makes the near zone colder while the far zone stays warm. Beyond 300 sq ft or in non-rectangular rooms, splitting into two properly placed units is the only real solution.

**Recommendation:** Daikin 2 Ton for standard large rectangular rooms up to 280 sq ft. Voltas 2.5 Ton for slightly larger single rooms. LG Dual Split for genuinely large or open-plan spaces where even coverage matters more than upfront cost savings.
