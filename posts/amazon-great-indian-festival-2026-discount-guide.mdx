## How to Calculate Your Real Discount

**Step 1: Note the sale price** displayed against the regular MRP or recent selling price (verify with Keepa, not the inflated MRP).

**Step 2: Add bank card instant discount.** Most premium cards offer 10% instant discount, typically capped at ₹1,000-₴,000 depending on card tier and category — apply this to the sale price, not the MRP.

**Step 3: Add exchange credit** if you have an eligible old device — this can range from ₹2,000 for a basic accessory trade-in to ₹15,000+ for a premium laptop or smartphone in good condition.

**Step 4: Compare the final effective price** against what you'd pay at any other time of year, not just against the MRP — this is your actual savings figure.

## Common Discount-Calculation Mistakes

**Comparing against an inflated "regular price":** Some listings show an artificially high strikethrough MRP to make the sale discount look larger than it actually is against recent real pricing.

**Forgetting EMI processing fees:** No-cost EMI removes interest but some cards still apply a small processing fee — check the fine print before assuming it's entirely free financing.

**Not checking exchange eligibility before shopping:** Confirm your specific old device qualifies for exchange credit on your target product category before building your budget around that expected credit.
