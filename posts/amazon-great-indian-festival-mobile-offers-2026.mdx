## Maximizing Exchange Value

**Factory reset and back up before valuation:** Most exchange programs require this step, and a clean, functional device scores higher in automated condition assessment.

**Document screen and body condition honestly:** Overstating condition during the online estimate often results in a lower final offer once the device is physically inspected at delivery/pickup.

**Compare against independent buyback services:** Cashify and similar services sometimes offer more than Amazon's exchange program for premium devices in excellent condition — a quick comparison before committing is worthwhile.
