### Option 3: Fixed Standing Desk

A desk built to a fixed standing height (90–110cm). No adjustment — you always stand or use a bar stool. Simplest solution but inflexible.

Some users in home offices use a DIY solution: raise an existing IKEA or local furniture desk with bed risers (₹500–₁,500) to standing height. Practical but doesn't allow returning to seated height easily.

## Do You Actually Need a Standing Desk?

Standing desks improve health outcomes primarily by **breaking up prolonged sitting** — not by replacing sitting with constant standing. The recommended ratio: 1 hour standing for every 2–3 hours sitting.

If you can take 2–3 stand breaks per day (even just standing for 30 minutes), you get most of the benefit. A ₹5,000 converter achieves this effectively.

**Recommendation:** Desk converter for users with an existing desk who want the standing option without buying new furniture. Manual crank desk for users setting up a new workspace from scratch on a budget.
