A complete Amazon Great Indian Festival 2026 shopping checklist covers three phases: pre-sale preparation (wishlist, price tracking, bank card verification), during-sale execution (timing, coupon stacking, exchange documentation), and post-purchase steps (return window awareness, warranty registration) — following all three phases delivers meaningfully better outcomes than treating the sale as a single browsing session.

Most shoppers only think about the "during-sale" phase, but the preparation and follow-up steps are where genuine additional savings and protection against buyer's remorse actually happen.

## Pre-Sale Checklist (1-2 Weeks Before)

- [ ] Build a genuine wishlist of products you actually need
- [ ] Install Keepa or similar price-tracking tool on shortlisted items
- [ ] Confirm your credit/debit card's specific GIF bank offer terms and cap
- [ ] Get an independent exchange value estimate (Cashify) for any trade-in device
- [ ] Factory reset and back up any device you plan to exchange
- [ ] Subscribe to Prime if targeting high-demand, fast-selling categories

## During-Sale Checklist (Sale Days)

- [ ] Check your wishlist items first, within the sale's opening hours
- [ ] Verify each "% off" badge against your tracked price history
- [ ] Apply any clippable on-page coupons before adding to cart
- [ ] Compare high-value purchases against Flipkart's simultaneous sale
- [ ] Confirm the specific product listing matches the exact spec/variant you researched
- [ ] Complete exchange valuation and trade-in during checkout if applicable

## Post-Purchase Checklist

- [ ] Note the return/replacement window for each purchase (varies by category)
- [ ] Register warranty with the manufacturer directly, not just relying on the Amazon order record
- [ ] Keep the invoice accessible for any future warranty claims
- [ ] Set a reminder to inspect large appliances immediately upon delivery for shipping damage

## Recommendation

Print or save this checklist and work through each phase deliberately — the pre-sale phase in particular determines most of your total savings, since price tracking and bank card verification can't be done effectively once the sale has already started and time pressure sets in.
