## How to Choose a Sofa Set

Measure your room and the doorway before you shop. A sofa that does not fit through the door or blocks a walkway is a costly mistake.

Focus on the frame and cushions. A solid wood or strong engineered frame lasts longer, and high-density foam holds its shape. Check fabric durability, colour and how easy it is to clean.

Read the warranty and delivery details. Ask whether assembly is included and how returns work for a large item.

## Quick FAQ

**Fabric or leatherette?** Fabric is breathable and comfortable in Indian summers; leatherette is easier to wipe but can feel warm and may crack with age.

**How much should I spend?** For a sofa you keep for years, mid-range models from known brands often offer the best balance between cost and durability.

**Is an L-shape better than a 3-seater?** An L-shape gives more seating and a lounging corner but needs more space. A 3-seater is simpler for smaller rooms.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Buying on photos alone.** Colours and proportions can look different in a real living room. Read reviews with customer photos and check the dimensions against your space.

**Ignoring seat depth.** A very deep sofa looks plush but can be uncomfortable for shorter people, and a shallow one can feel cramped. Check seat depth and back height in the listing.

**Choosing fabric without thinking about children and pets.** Light colours and delicate weaves stain easily. Consider a darker, tighter weave or a washable cover.

**Skipping the delivery plan.** Large furniture needs a clear path. Measure doors, lifts and staircases, and ask whether assembly is included.
