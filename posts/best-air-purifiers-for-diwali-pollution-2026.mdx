## How to Choose an Air Purifier

Match the purifier to the room. Check the clean air delivery rate and the recommended room size on the listing. A unit rated for a bedroom will struggle in a large hall.

Look for a true HEPA filter and, if you have odours or gases to worry about, an activated carbon layer. Check how easy filters are to buy and what they cost.

Place the purifier away from walls, keep doors and windows closed while it runs, and clean or replace filters on schedule.

## Quick FAQ

**Can an air purifier remove cracker smoke completely?** It reduces fine particles indoors but does not remove all pollutants. Keep windows closed during peak smoke hours.

**How many hours a day should I run it?** Many people run it continuously during high-pollution days and at lower speeds otherwise. Follow the manufacturer's guidance.

**Are filters expensive?** Replacement costs vary by brand and should be included in your budget for the year.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Buying a purifier that is too small.** A unit rated for a small bedroom will not clear a large hall. Check the recommended room area and the clean air delivery rate before you decide.

**Ignoring filter costs.** Replacement filters can cost a significant amount each year. Look at the price and the expected life of the filter before buying.

**Keeping windows open.** A purifier works best in a closed room. If you keep windows open during heavy smoke, the machine has to fight a constant flow of polluted air.

**Placing it in a corner.** Airflow matters. Keep the purifier a little away from walls and furniture so it can draw air in and push clean air out.
