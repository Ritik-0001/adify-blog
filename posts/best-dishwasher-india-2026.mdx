## Indian Dishwasher Concerns — Addressed

**"Dishwashers don't clean Indian curries":** Modern 70°C cycles with high-pressure jets clean turmeric, tamarind, and ghee effectively. Use the Auto Intensive or 70°C programme for heavily soiled Indian utensils.

**"Steel utensils will get damaged":** High-grade 304 stainless steel is dishwasher safe. Most Indian kadais, plates, and tiffins survive dishwashers. Avoid aluminium, copper, and non-stick with damaged coatings.

**"Hard water will leave stains":** Use dishwasher salt + rinse aid combination. Salt softens water, rinse aid prevents spotting. India's hard water requires consistent salt replenishment.

**"Indian households have too many dishes":** A 12-place setting handles the typical family's daily utensils — plates, bowls, glasses, cutlery, a few cooking vessels.
