## Beginner Flying Tips for India

**Fly in open areas:** Parks, open fields, and farmland are ideal for learning. Avoid trees, power lines, and crowds.

**Check wind conditions:** Drones fly poorly in wind above 20–25 km/h. The DJI app shows wind speed — land if wind increases above comfortable levels.

**Morning is best:** Wind is typically lightest in early morning (7–9am). Best visibility for learning flight paths.

**Practice hovering first:** Before moving, practice letting the drone hover in place. GPS positioning means you can release sticks and the drone stays put — use this to build confidence.

**DGCA nano rules apply:** Even sub-250g drones cannot be flown over crowds, near airports, or above 60m without permission. Learn rules before flying.

**Recommendation:** DJI Mini 2 SE (₹30,000–₃₅,000) for the most accessible beginner drone — learn without risking an expensive crash. DJI Mini 3 (₹40,000–₄₅,000) if 4K video matters from day one and budget allows.
