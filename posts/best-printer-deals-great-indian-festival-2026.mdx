## How to Estimate Your Actual Printing Volume

**Under 20-30 pages a month:** A basic inkjet is genuinely cheaper overall — the ink tank premium won't pay back before you'd replace the printer anyway.

**30-150 pages a month:** This is the ink tank sweet spot — the Smart Tank or EcoTank models recover their higher upfront cost within 12-18 months.

**150+ pages a month, mostly text:** A monochrome laser printer becomes the cheaper option per page, despite the higher sticker price.
