## How to Plan for Winter in a New Home

Check wiring first. Geysers, heaters and dryers draw high power, so ask an electrician to confirm points and the load capacity.

Plan installation early. Geyser, purifier and washing machine installers are busiest at the start of winter, so book ahead.

Think about space. Washing machines, dryers and refrigerators need ventilation and water or power points planned in advance.

## Electrical Safety for Winter Appliances

Geysers, heaters and dryers draw high current, so use a dedicated point with the correct wire gauge and an MCB rather than an extension board. An earth leakage protection device adds an important safety layer in bathrooms. Switch heaters off when you leave the room and keep them clear of curtains and bedding.

## Cutting the Winter Electricity Bill

Use timers on geysers, heat only the room you use, and close doors and windows to hold warmth. An air purifier on low speed is cheaper to run than most people expect, and a well-insulated storage geyser holds heat for hours, so you do not need to keep it on all day.

## Who Should Skip the Heater and Dryer

If you live somewhere with only a few cold weeks, such as much of the south, a separate room heater and a washer-dryer are poor value. A geyser and a clothes rack in a sunny spot will carry you through, and you can borrow warmth from a blanket for the rare chilly night. Buy the extras only if your winters genuinely demand them, and revisit the decision after your first January in the new home, when you will know which rooms stay cold and which stay comfortable.

## Who Should Skip Some of This List

If you live in a warm coastal city, a room heater and an air purifier may sit unused for most of the year, so buy them only if you have already felt the need. A washer-dryer is a poor value in a home with a sunny balcony. A geyser, on the other hand, is useful almost everywhere, so if the budget is tight, start there and add the rest only when your first winter in the home shows what you really miss.

## Recommendation

Start with a five-star storage geyser and an air purifier, then add a heater and a washer-dryer as needed. Book installation early.
