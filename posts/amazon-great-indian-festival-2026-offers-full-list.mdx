## Category-by-Category Offer Summary

**Laptops:** 15-25% off across most brands, with premium models (MacBook, gaming laptops with RTX 4070+) seeing the highest absolute savings — often ₹10,000-₴0,000 on a single purchase.

**Smartphones:** Flagship and premium mid-range phones (₹30,000-₸0,000) see 10-20% off, plus exchange bonuses that can add another ₹5,000-₹15,000 in credit for a working old phone.

**TVs:** Large-format 55-inch+ 4K and OLED TVs see some of the steepest percentage cuts (20-25%) as manufacturers clear stock ahead of new-model launches.

**Air Conditioners:** Moderate 15-20% discounts — genuinely good, but not as deep as February-March's pre-summer sale window.

**Refrigerators and Washing Machines:** 15-20% off, frequently bundled with exchange offers for an old working unit.

**Premium electronics (cameras, drones, gaming gear):** 10-20% off, varying by brand and specific model generation.

## Bank and Payment Offers to Stack

**Instant bank discount:** HDFC, SBI, ICICI, and Axis cards typically offer 10% instant discount, capped between ₹1,000-₴,000 depending on the card tier and category.

**No-cost EMI:** Interest normally charged on EMI is discounted from the product price upfront — effectively 0% interest financing on eligible cards for purchases above a minimum threshold.

**Exchange offers:** Trading in an old device (phone, laptop, TV, appliance) adds credit toward the new purchase — amount depends on the old device's brand, age, and working condition.

**Amazon Pay balance cashback:** Occasional additional cashback promotions for paying via Amazon Pay balance on select categories.

## How to Prepare

**Before the sale starts:** Add target products to your Wishlist and track price history with a tool like Keepa to confirm discounts are genuine, not inflated list prices.

**During the sale:** Prime members typically get 24-48 hours of early access before the general public — worth subscribing if you're targeting popular, fast-selling items.

**Documentation for exchange:** Have your old device's brand, model, and condition details ready before starting the exchange valuation process.

## Recommendation

Prioritize research and price-tracking for big-ticket categories (laptops, TVs, premium appliances) where absolute rupee savings are highest, and treat smaller accessory discounts as bonus rather than the main event — the category-level percentage discount matters less than the rupee amount you actually save on the specific product you need.
