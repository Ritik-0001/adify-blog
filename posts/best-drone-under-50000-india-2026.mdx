## DGCA Rules Reminder

Both DJI Mini 2 SE and Mini 3 are under 250g — DGCA nano category. Nano drones are exempt from UIN registration requirement under current rules. However, operational restrictions still apply:
- No flying over dense populated areas
- No flying near airports (within 5km)
- Maximum 60m altitude without permission
- Fly only in legal zones (use DGCA's Digital Sky platform to verify)
