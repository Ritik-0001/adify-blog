## Choosing the Right Star Rating and Capacity

**5-star rating** pays back its higher upfront cost within 2-3 cooling seasons if you run the AC daily for several hours — the right choice for bedrooms and living rooms in regular use.

**3-star rating** is reasonable for occasional-use rooms or guest bedrooms where the AC runs infrequently enough that the efficiency premium doesn't pay back as quickly.

**1.5-ton capacity** suits rooms up to roughly 150 sq ft; larger rooms or rooms with heavy sun exposure should size up to 2-ton units.
