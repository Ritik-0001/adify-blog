## How to Choose a Gift Under ₹10,000

At this level, a gift should feel like an upgrade to someone's daily life. Ask what they use most: if it is a phone and earphones, audio gear is a natural fit; if it is the kitchen, an appliance will earn more goodwill than any gadget.

Compare features that matter rather than headline specifications. For headphones, look at comfort and battery life as much as noise cancelling. For a Kindle, screen lighting and storage are enough. For an air fryer, capacity and ease of cleaning decide whether it gets used.

Buy from trusted sellers and check the warranty. Products in this range are often eligible for brand support, and an authorised listing makes claims much simpler. Look at the return window as well, since a gift that does not fit can be swapped without awkwardness.
