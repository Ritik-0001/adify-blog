## What Pet Owners Need from a Camera

**Pet detection:** Distinguishes pet movement from other motion (fan, shadows) to reduce meaningless alerts. AI-based detection in premium cameras.

**2-way audio:** Talk to your dog through the camera — many dogs respond to their owner's voice and calm down with verbal reassurance.

**Colour night vision:** Pets are active at dawn and dusk. Colour footage in low light is significantly more useful than black-and-white IR footage.

**Pan-tilt:** Pets move around — a fixed camera may miss your dog that moved from the bed to the kitchen. Pan-tilt lets you follow them from the app.

## Placement Tips for Pet Cameras

**Dogs:** Place at pet's face height (30–60cm for most breeds) for clear eye-level footage. Dogs respond better to seeing familiar faces at their level in 2-way audio.

**Cats:** High placement (above 1.5m) covers most cat territory — cats use vertical space more than dogs.

**Avoid direct sunlight:** Backlit footage makes pet details hard to see. Place camera so the light source is behind the camera.

**Separation anxiety:** For dogs with separation anxiety, 2-way audio with your voice can significantly reduce distress — veterinarians commonly recommend this alongside behavioural therapy.
