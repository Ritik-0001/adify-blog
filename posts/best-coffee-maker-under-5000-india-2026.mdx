## Coffee Brewing Options Under ₹5000

**AeroPress (₹3,500–₄,500):** Manual pressure-based brewing. Produces clean, smooth, concentrated coffee with extremely low acidity. Used by World Barista Champions. Fastest cleanup of any manual method — just pop out the puck.

**Moka Pot Stainless (₹1,500–₃,000):** Stovetop espresso. 3-cup or 6-cup versions. Best for South Indian filter kaapi style or espresso-strength coffee. Long-lasting (decades with care).

**Drip Coffee Maker (₹3,000–₅,000):** Automatic — add water and ground coffee, press button. 10–12 cup capacity for households or offices. Familiar drip filter style.

**Manual Pour Over (₹1,500–₃,000):** V60 or Chemex-style. Produces very clean, bright coffee highlighting single-origin flavors. Requires more skill and attention than AeroPress.
