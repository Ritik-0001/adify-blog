## Front-Load vs Top-Load at GIF: Which to Choose

**Front-load (₹30,000–₄₅,000 at GIF):**
- 25–40% less water usage per wash
- Better fabric care (tumble action gentler than agitator)
- Can be stacked with dryer (space saving in small apartments)
- Quieter operation
- Higher upfront cost

**Top-load automatic (₹20,000–₂₈,000 at GIF):**
- More water usage but faster wash cycle
- Can add clothes mid-wash
- Lower cost
- Easier loading/unloading (no bending)

**Semi-automatic (₹8,000–₁₂,000 at GIF):**
- Lowest cost
- Manual water filling and shifting between tub and dryer
- Suitable for areas with intermittent water supply (manual water control is advantage)
