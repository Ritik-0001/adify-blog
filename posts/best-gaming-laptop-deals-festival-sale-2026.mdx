## Choosing the Right GPU Tier

**RTX 4050:** Comfortable 1080p gaming at high settings for most current titles — the right choice if budget is the primary constraint.

**RTX 4060:** The sweet-spot tier for smooth 1080p high-refresh-rate gaming and light 1440p, covering the widest range of buyers.

**RTX 4070:** Worth the premium if you specifically want 1440p gaming or plan to use the laptop for creative rendering work alongside gaming.
