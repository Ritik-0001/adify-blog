## Winter AQI Requirements vs Standard Purchase

Most air purifiers are reviewed and sold for "typical" AQI 100–200 conditions. North Indian winter peak requires:

**CADR:** Minimum 400 m³/hour for a 200 sq ft living room during AQI 300–400. Standard bedroom (150 sq ft) needs 300+ CADR.

**HEPA H13 vs H11:** H13 captures 99.97% of PM0.3 (includes ultra-fine particles). H11 captures only 95%. In winter smog, the difference matters — use only H13.

**Run time:** During November–January in Delhi, run on medium-high continuously. Auto mode may not be sufficient — PM2.5 sensor on purifiers often reads below ambient because they measure the air that's already been partially filtered.

## Winter Air Purifier Strategy

**Bedroom first:** Protect 8 hours of sleep. High CADR in bedroom = significant daily exposure reduction.

**Seal windows:** During AQI 300+, keep bedroom window and door shut while purifier runs. Check seal quality — poorly sealed windows negate the purifier's work.

**Pre-filter washing:** During winter, pre-filters fill with dust in 2–3 weeks. Wash and dry fortnightly.

**Second unit:** For 3-BHK families, a second purifier in the living room provides protection during evening family time (peak indoor gathering time).
