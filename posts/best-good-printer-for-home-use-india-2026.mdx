## What "Good" Actually Means for a Home Printer

**Print quality consistency:** Sharp text and reasonably accurate color across hundreds of pages without streaking or fading — ink tank printers generally excel here since ink flow is more consistent than cartridge systems.

**Low jam frequency:** Reliable paper feed mechanism that handles standard A4 paper without frequent misfeeds.

**Minimal maintenance friction:** Refilling an ink bottle every few months is far less disruptive than replacing cartridges every few weeks under regular use.
