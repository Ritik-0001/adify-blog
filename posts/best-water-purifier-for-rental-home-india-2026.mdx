## Rental-Friendly Purifier Options

### Option 1: Gravity-Based Purifier (₹1,200–₃,500)

No electricity. No wall mounting. No plumbing. Fill manually from tap. Purification by gravity through UF membrane.

**Best for:** Municipal water (TDS under 200 ppm). Does not reduce TDS or remove dissolved minerals. Cannot handle hard or borewell water.

**Models:** HUL Pureit Classic G2 (₹1,500–₂,000), Tata Swach (₹1,200–₁,800), Kent Gold (₹2,500–₃,500).

### Option 2: Pitcher Purifier (₹800–₁,500)

Smallest and cheapest option. Fill-and-pour pitcher format. Activated carbon + ceramic filter. Works without electricity or installation.

**Best for:** Single persons, hostel students, short-term rentals. Limited storage (2–3L). Slowest filtration (drip rate).

Brita Classic (₹1,500–₂,500) — international brand with ceramic and carbon filter. Filter replacement every 2 months.

### Option 3: Countertop RO Purifier (₹8,000–₁₂,000)

Stands on the kitchen counter. Connects to tap with a diverter valve (clips onto faucet without permanent modification). Provides full RO purification without wall drilling. Can be taken when you move.

**Best for:** Tenants in hard water areas who cannot install wall-mounted RO but need TDS reduction.

**Models:** The Prestige Tattva Countertop RO (₹8,000–₁₀,000) — countertop design, tap clip connection, no drilling.

### Option 4: Tabletop Electric UV Purifier (₹5,000–₈,000)

Stands on counter. Connects to a standard water supply tap. Provides UV purification without wall mounting. No drilling.

**Best for:** Municipal water where TDS is acceptable but bacteria is the concern.

## Moving Consideration

Gravity and countertop purifiers are portable — pack them when you move to your next rental. Wall-mounted purifiers (Kent, Aquaguard standard models) are difficult to relocate — plumbing disconnection, possible damage to walls.
