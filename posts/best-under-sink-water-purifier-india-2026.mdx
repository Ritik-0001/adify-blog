## Under-Sink vs Wall-Mounted vs Countertop Purifiers

**Countertop (most common in India):** Sits on the counter, connects to tap. Easy installation, easy to move. Recommended for most homes. Examples: Kent Grand Plus, Aquaguard Marvel.

**Wall-mounted:** Mounted on the kitchen wall. Saves counter space. Requires drilling into wall. Slightly more complex installation. Examples: Kent Supreme Extra, Aquaguard Delight NXT.

**Under-sink / Under-counter:** Fits entirely inside kitchen cabinet. Requires dedicated faucet hole drilled in countertop. Most complex installation but cleanest aesthetic. Examples: A.O. Smith Z8, Kent Excell.

Under-sink makes most sense for:
- Modular kitchens with premium countertops (granite, quartz) where a unit on the counter looks out of place
- Homes where all counter space is used for cooking appliances
- Premium apartments where kitchen aesthetics are a priority

## Installation Considerations

Before buying an under-sink purifier for an Indian home:

1. **Counter drilling:** Under-sink models require a hole drilled in the countertop for the dedicated faucet. Granite and quartz counters require a professional diamond drill — verify the installer has the right equipment.

2. **Cabinet size:** Measure your under-sink cabinet interior (height × width × depth). Most under-sink purifiers are 35–45cm tall and 25–35cm wide. Verify the unit fits before purchasing.

3. **Waste water outlet:** Under-sink RO units need a drain line for reject water. The drain typically connects to the sink's P-trap. Verify your plumber can access this.

4. **Electricity:** Under-sink purifiers need a nearby power socket in the cabinet. Plan for this during kitchen renovation if applicable.
