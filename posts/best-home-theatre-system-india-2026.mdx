## Connectivity Guide

**HDMI ARC:** Single cable connects soundbar to TV and passes audio + remote control. Most modern TVs (2020+) have HDMI ARC port. Recommended connection.

**HDMI eARC:** Upgraded version — supports uncompressed Dolby Atmos. Check if your TV has eARC port for full Atmos quality.

**Bluetooth:** For music streaming from phone when TV is off.

**Optical:** Alternative if TV doesn't have ARC — older TVs often have optical audio out.

**Recommendation:** Sony HT-S400 for most Indian families wanting their first soundbar upgrade — 330W, wireless subwoofer, and Sony reliability at an accessible price. Yamaha YAS-209 for serious audio quality with Dolby Atmos.
