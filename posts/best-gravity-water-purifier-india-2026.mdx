## When to Choose a Gravity Purifier

**Gravity purifier is right if:**
- You rent and cannot drill walls or modify plumbing
- You live in a hostel, PG, or temporary accommodation
- Your municipal tap water TDS is under 200 ppm
- You want to spend under ₹3,000

**Gravity purifier is NOT sufficient if:**
- Your water comes from a bore well or open well
- Your TDS is above 200 ppm
- You're in a hard water area (North India, Rajasthan, Gujarat)
- Your water has a metallic or sulfurous taste (indicates dissolved minerals UV/UF cannot remove)
