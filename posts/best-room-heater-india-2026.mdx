## Heater Type Guide for India

**Oil-Filled Radiator (OFR):** Oil heats inside sealed fins. Fin surface warms by convection — gentle, even heat. Surface temperature low (warm to touch, not burn risk). Silent. Takes 15–20 minutes to reach full output. Best for overnight bedroom use, elderly people, and children.

**Fan Heater:** Electric heating element + fan blows hot air. Fastest heating (3–5 minutes). Loud. Dries air significantly — causes dry throat, skin, and nasal passages. Best for quick bathroom warming or temporary short-duration heating.

**Halogen/Infrared Heater:** Radiant heat — warms objects and people in line-of-sight, not the air. Instant warmth (1 second). High-power consumption. Tipping-over fire risk if not stabilised. Not safe for children's rooms.
