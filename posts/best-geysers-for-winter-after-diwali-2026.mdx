## How to Choose a Geyser

Pick capacity by household size: 10 to 15 litres for one or two people, 15 to 25 litres for a family, and larger for multiple bathrooms. Storage geysers heat a tank in advance, while instant geysers heat as water flows.

Check the star rating for energy use, the tank material, and safety features such as a thermostat and a pressure release valve.

Confirm installation and space. A storage geyser needs a strong wall and correct plumbing.

## Quick FAQ

**Storage or instant?** Storage suits larger households and multiple taps. Instant suits kitchens and small spaces.

**How many stars do I need?** Higher stars mean less standby loss, which matters if you use the geyser daily.

**How long does a geyser last?** Many last several years with proper use and a good anode or coating, but water quality plays a role.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Buying too small a tank.** A geyser that runs out of hot water halfway through the morning routine is frustrating. Match the capacity to the number of people and how many use hot water back to back.

**Ignoring hard water.** Hard water can build scale on the heating element and shorten life. Look for models with a good tank coating, and consider a descaler or water softener if your area has hard water.

**Skipping the pressure and plumbing check.** Storage geysers need proper inlet and outlet pipes and a pressure release valve. Instant models have minimum pressure requirements.

**Forgetting safety.** Always use a proper earthing connection and a suitable plug, and do not attempt the installation yourself.

## When to Buy

Geyser demand rises as winter approaches, and installation slots get tight. Ordering a few weeks earlier gives you time to arrange the technician and avoid cold-water mornings. Check the warranty on the tank and on the heating element separately, since they are often different.

## Running Costs and Efficiency

A 2000 watt storage geyser heating a 25 litre tank draws roughly one unit of electricity for every 30 minutes it is heating, so a daily cycle adds up over winter. Insulation quality, star rating and a good thermostat reduce standby losses, so a higher-rated geyser can pay back its extra cost. Switch the geyser off when not needed, and use a timer if the model allows.

## Recommendation
