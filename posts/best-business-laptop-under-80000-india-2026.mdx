## What Makes a Laptop "Business-Grade"

**Durability certification:** MIL-STD-810H testing simulates drops, temperature extremes, dust, and vibration — a genuine indicator of build quality beyond marketing claims.

**Security features:** Fingerprint/facial recognition, TPM 2.0 chip (for BitLocker encryption), physical camera shutters, and IT-manageable BIOS settings matter for enterprise deployment.

**Keyboard quality:** Business users type for hours daily — keyboard travel and feel genuinely affects long-term comfort and typing speed in ways gaming or creator laptops often don't prioritize.

**Port selection:** Business laptops typically retain more legacy ports (Ethernet, full-size HDMI, multiple USB-A) that enterprise environments still rely on.
