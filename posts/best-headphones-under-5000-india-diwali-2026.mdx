## Over-Ear vs TWS Earbuds for Diwali Gifting

**Give headphones when the recipient:**
- Uses music for focus work or study sessions
- Commutes on metro/train and needs longer wear
- Values sound quality over portability
- Already has earbuds and wants a home/desk option

**Give earbuds when the recipient:**
- Is active (running, gym) and needs in-ear fit
- Travels frequently and values portability
- Is under 25 and prefers the earbuds form factor
