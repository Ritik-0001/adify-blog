## What Matters Most for Bedroom ACs

**Indoor unit noise (dB rating):** Look for sub-20dB ratings — this is the difference between a barely audible hum and a noticeable drone that disrupts sleep quality over months of use.

**Sleep mode:** A good sleep mode gradually raises temperature by 1-2°C over several hours (mimicking natural body temperature drop during sleep) rather than holding one fixed temperature all night — reduces the "too cold by 4am" problem.

**Timer/schedule functions:** Auto-off timers and morning warm-up schedules matter more for bedroom use than for living rooms.
