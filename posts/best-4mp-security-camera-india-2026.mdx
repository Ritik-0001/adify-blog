## Why 4MP Over 2MP?

**2MP (Full HD 1080p):**
- 1920×1440 pixels
- Face identifiable at 2–3 metres
- License plate readable at 5–8 metres (with good placement)

**4MP (2K):**
- 2560×1440 pixels — 2× more detail than 2MP
- Face identifiable at 4–6 metres
- License plate readable at 10–15 metres
- Price premium over 2MP: ₹500–₁,500 per camera

For most Indian homes and small businesses, the identification range improvement alone justifies the 4MP upgrade.

## 4MP vs 5MP vs 8MP — Is Higher Always Better?

**4MP:** Ideal for most homes and small businesses. Good resolution without requiring expensive high-throughput NVRs.

**5MP (3K):** Moderate upgrade — about 25% more resolution than 4MP. Small practical difference in most deployments.

**8MP (4K):** Significant resolution jump — excellent for wide areas and license plate recognition at distance. Requires 4K-capable NVR and more storage (double vs 4MP).

For most Indian residential and small commercial installations, 4MP is the sweet spot — adequate detail without the storage and NVR cost of 8MP.
