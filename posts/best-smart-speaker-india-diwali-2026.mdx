## Smart Speaker Setup for Indian Parents

**First-day commands to teach:**
- "Alexa, play bhajans" / "Alexa, play Kishore Kumar"
- "Alexa, what's today's news?"
- "Alexa, set alarm for 6 AM"
- "Alexa, what's the temperature?"
- "Alexa, call [family member name]" (requires Alexa app setup)

**Alexa calling:** Echo speakers support free Alexa-to-Alexa voice calls — great for elderly parents to call family members. Setup once, they can call hands-free forever.

**Hindi music:** JioSaavn and Gaana integration has the most comprehensive Bollywood and regional music. Link the account in the Alexa app before gifting.

**Recommendation:** Echo Dot 5th Gen for Diwali gifting to parents and in-laws — Hindi Alexa is particularly natural for older users, and Amazon's festive discount makes it the most cost-effective smart speaker gift at under ₹2,500 during sale.
