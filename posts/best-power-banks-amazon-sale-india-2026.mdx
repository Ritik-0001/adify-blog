## Choosing the Right Capacity

**10000mAh:** Charges a modern smartphone (4500mAh battery) approximately 2–2.5 times. Compact enough for a shirt pocket or small bag. Best for daily carry.

**20000mAh:** Charges a smartphone 4–5 times, or charges a tablet once + smartphone once. Larger and heavier — best for travel, not daily carry.

**27000mAh+:** Approaches laptop charging territory. Anker's Gananchor 737 (140W) can recharge a MacBook Air; excellent for frequent travellers without power access.

## Fast Charging Speed Matters in India

**18W / 22.5W:** Standard fast charging. Recharges a 4500mAh phone in 90–120 minutes.

**45W / 65W:** Fast charging. Recharges modern phones (Samsung S24, Pixel 8) in 45–60 minutes.

**100W+:** Laptop charging territory. Required for charging MacBooks and modern gaming laptops from a power bank.

For India's daily commuter, 22.5W in a 10000mAh power bank is adequate. For travellers who want to charge tablets and laptops, 65W+ 20000mAh is the target.

**BIS certification:** Check for BIS (Bureau of Indian Standards) mark — required for legally imported/manufactured power banks in India. Non-BIS power banks may be cheaper but lack safety certification.

**No-cost EMI:** Power banks under ₹2,000 typically don't offer EMI. Buy outright.
