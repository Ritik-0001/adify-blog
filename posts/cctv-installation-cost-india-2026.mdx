## Labour Cost Factors in India

Labour costs vary significantly by city:

**Metro cities (Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru):** ₹500–₈₀₀ per camera for installation (cable routing included).

**Tier 2 cities:** ₹300–₅₀₀ per camera.

**Tier 3 / rural:** Often bundled — ₹1,500–₂,500 for complete 4-camera job.

**Conduit/concealed wiring:** Add ₹40–₆₀/metre if cable needs to be hidden in walls (versus surface-mounted along walls). Concealed wiring can add ₹3,000–₅,000 to a 4-camera home job.

---

## Recurring Costs

**Hard disk replacement:** CCTV HDDs last 3–5 years with 24/7 operation. Replacement cost: ₹2,000–₄,000.

**Annual maintenance contract (AMC):** Professional systems often have AMC at ₹1,500–₃,000/year covering cleaning, cable inspection, and minor repairs.

**Cloud storage subscription:** ₹99–₂₀₀/month per camera if using cloud backup.

---

## DIY vs Professional Installation

**DIY (buying kit from Amazon, self-install):** Saves ₹2,000–₆,000 in labour. Requires drilling, cable routing, and DVR configuration. Feasible for renters wanting simple plug-in cameras or homeowners with basic DIY skills.

**Professional installation:** Recommended for multi-camera systems with concealed wiring. Professional installers configure remote viewing, optimise camera angles, and handle warranty claims. Most CP Plus and Hikvision dealers offer installation as part of the purchase.
