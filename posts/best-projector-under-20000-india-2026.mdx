## What ₹12,000–₂₀,000 Gets in a Projector

**Resolution:** FHD 1080p is achievable under ₹20,000 from Chinese brands (Wanbo, YABER, Kodak). 720p was the standard at this price 2 years ago; 1080p is now the baseline.

**Brightness:** 200–500 ANSI lumens is the realistic range. For comparison, a 70-inch TV in a bright room provides equivalent to 1,000–2,000 lux at the viewing position. Budget projectors at 400 lumens can't compete with ambient daylight.

**Smart OS:** Android TV or Google TV built-in means Netflix, YouTube, Hotstar available natively. Without smart OS, you need a Fire Stick or Chromecast dongle.

**Noise:** Budget projectors produce 35–45dB noise (fan cooling). In quiet Indian bedrooms this is noticeable but not disturbing for movie watching.

## Honest Use Case Assessment

Budget projectors under ₹20,000 are suitable for:
- Movie nights in a completely dark bedroom (blackout curtains, lights off)
- Occasional entertainment (not daily primary screen)
- Presentations in a dimmed conference room

They are not suitable for:
- Replacing a TV in a normally lit living room
- Gaming (input lag is high on most budget projectors)
- Daytime viewing
