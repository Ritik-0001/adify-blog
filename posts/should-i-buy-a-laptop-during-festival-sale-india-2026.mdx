## When It Makes Sense to Buy Now

**Your current laptop is genuinely failing** — slow boot times, battery barely holding charge, or repeated crashes are all signs that waiting for a "better" discount later isn't worth the daily frustration.

**You already know your target specs.** If you've researched RAM, storage, and processor needs for your work (coding, editing, gaming, or basic browsing), the sale is simply the right time to execute that decision.

**You need a laptop for a fixed deadline** — a new job, semester, or project start — where delaying the purchase has a real cost beyond just the price of the laptop.

---

## When You Should Wait Instead

**You haven't decided between categories yet** (ultrabook vs gaming vs 2-in-1) — buying under sale pressure before this decision is made often leads to returns or regret.

**A specific next-generation chipset or model is confirmed for release within weeks.** If a meaningfully better version of your target laptop is about to launch, the outgoing model's discount may not offset missing the upgrade.

**Your current laptop still works fine.** Sale pricing is a reason to buy sooner rather than later within your existing plan — it's not a reason to replace a perfectly functional machine.

---

## How to Avoid Overpaying Under Sale Pressure

**Fix your budget and target specs before opening the app**, so flashy "limited time" banners on unrelated models don't pull you toward an unplanned purchase.

**Check price history on your shortlisted model.** A genuine 15% discount on a fair base price beats a "40% off" badge on an inflated one.

---

## Recommendation

Buy now if your laptop need is real and your specs are already decided — this sale window reliably delivers the year's best pricing on mid-to-premium laptops. Wait only if you're still deciding what you actually need, since no discount fixes buying the wrong laptop.
