## Brand-by-Brand Analysis

### Daikin — Best Overall Reliability

**Best for:** Buyers who want the lowest hassle over 10+ years and don't mind paying a premium.

Daikin is Japan's #1 AC manufacturer and produces its own compressors (most competitors buy compressors from suppliers). This vertical integration means Daikin has better quality control over the most critical AC component.

Daikin India produces ACs locally in the Neemrana, Rajasthan plant — models are specifically tuned for Indian temperature ranges (40–52°C ambient) and power supply characteristics.

**Service network:** Strong in metros and major cities. Tier 2/3 city service can be slower.

**Weaknesses:** Premium pricing (10–20% more than Panasonic/Voltas for equivalent specs). Service network is good in cities but less comprehensive than Voltas in very small towns.

### LG — Best Technology and Efficiency

**Best for:** Tech-forward buyers who want Wi-Fi control, convertible modes, and the latest energy efficiency innovations.

LG's Dual Inverter compressor technology claims 10–15% better efficiency than standard inverter compressors. The 4-in-1 / 5-in-1 convertible feature lets you reduce AC capacity during mild weather (saving electricity) and boost it on peak summer days.

ThinQ app control is among the best AC apps — well-maintained, reliable, and works with Google Home and Amazon Alexa.

**Service network:** Excellent in metros and major cities. Pan-India service is good but concentrated in urban areas.

### Panasonic — Best Value

**Best for:** Buyers who want reliable performance at the best price-to-quality ratio.

PM 2.5 filtration is standard across most Panasonic models — useful for Delhi, NCR, and other high-pollution cities.

**Service network:** Good, similar density to LG and Daikin in cities.
