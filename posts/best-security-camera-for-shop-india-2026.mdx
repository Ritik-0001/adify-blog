## Shop Camera Placement Guide

**Critical positions for a typical kirana/retail shop:**

**Camera 1 — Entrance/exit:** Faces the door to capture every person entering and leaving. Critical for identifying suspects in theft incidents.

**Camera 2 — Cash counter:** Covers the POS/billing area. Monitors employee cash handling and customer interactions.

**Camera 3 — High-value merchandise area:** Focused on the section with most valuable or most stolen items.

**Camera 4 — Wide-angle overview:** Ceiling-mounted covering the entire shop floor — bird's-eye view of all activity.

## DVR vs NVR vs Wi-Fi Cameras for Shops

**DVR + AHD/TVI cameras:** Wired coaxial cable connection. Most reliable for continuous 24/7 recording. Requires professional installation. Best for permanent shop setups.

**NVR + IP cameras:** Higher quality (easier to run cat5e cable alongside existing LAN). Higher cost per camera but better resolution.

**Wi-Fi cameras only (no DVR):** Easier setup, but Wi-Fi is less reliable for 24/7 recording, and cameras with only SD card storage lose footage if card fills up or camera is stolen. Not recommended for commercial shops.

## Legal Requirements for Shop CCTV in India

- No specific law mandates CCTV in retail shops, but many State governments recommend it for high-value establishments
- Insurance companies may require CCTV footage for theft claims — verify with your insurer
- Data retention: Police may request 30-day recordings for investigations
- Signage: Recommended to display "CCTV in operation" notices
