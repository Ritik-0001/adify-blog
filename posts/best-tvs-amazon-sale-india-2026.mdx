## OTT Platform Performance: Why Display Technology Matters

India's top OTT content is on Netflix, Prime Video, Hotstar, and Sony LIV. Netflix's 4K HDR content looks dramatically different on OLED vs LCD:

- **OLED:** Per-pixel lighting gives true HDR — bright highlights and true blacks simultaneously. Streaming content intended for HDR (most Netflix originals, Prime Video content) looks as intended.
- **LED LCD (even premium):** Backlight blooming around bright objects, elevated blacks. HDR certification on budget LEDs is marketing.

For OTT-heavy households (Netflix, Prime Video, Hotstar daily), the OLED difference is immediately visible and lasting.
