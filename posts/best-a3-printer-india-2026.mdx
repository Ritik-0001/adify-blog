## A3 vs A3+ — What's the Difference?

**A3:** 297mm × 420mm — standard large format sheet size.

**A3+:** Slightly larger (329mm × 483mm) — allows borderless A3 printing with bleed. Required for proper borderless A3 photo and design prints.

Most A3 printers sold in India are technically A3+ — confirm in specifications.

## Who Needs an A3 Printer in India

**Architecture, interior design:** A3 floor plans, elevations, section drawings for client presentations.

**Engineering:** Circuit diagrams, mechanical drawings, piping and instrumentation diagrams (P&IDs).

**Printing businesses:** Visiting cards, letterheads, brochures — many print on A3 then cut down.

**Schools and coaching institutes:** A3 study materials, notice boards, hall charts.

**Recommendation:** Epson EcoTank L15150 for office A3 colour printing with low running cost. Canon TC-20 or HP DesignJet for technical CAD/drawing output. Avoid basic A3 inkjet printers from no-name brands — A3 alignment and consistent quality requires reliable engineering.
