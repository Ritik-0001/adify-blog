## What Architects Specifically Need

**BIM performance (Revit):** Large building models with hundreds of linked families need substantial RAM (32GB minimum) and a certified GPU to avoid viewport lag and crashes during coordination work.

**Rendering capability:** Lumion, Enscape, and V-Ray for SketchUp/Revit all benefit from strong GPU VRAM for real-time and final renders used in client presentations.

**Display color accuracy:** Presenting material studies, elevations, and renders to clients demands a display that shows colors as they'll actually appear — inaccurate displays lead to client miscommunication about material choices.

**Portability:** Site visits and client meetings mean architects carry laptops more than typical office workers — weight matters more here than for a desk-bound engineer.
