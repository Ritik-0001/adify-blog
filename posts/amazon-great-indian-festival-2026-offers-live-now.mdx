## Where the Live Discounts Are Strongest

**Smartphones:** Flagship and near-flagship models (iPhone, Galaxy S-series, OnePlus) typically see ₹5,000-₹12,000 off at launch of the sale, with additional bank card discounts stacking on top.

**Laptops:** Premium ultrabooks and gaming laptops carry the largest absolute rupee cuts of any category — often ₹10,000-₹20,000 off models priced above ₹70,000.

**Televisions:** 55-inch and 65-inch 4K and QLED models see aggressive early discounting as brands clear outgoing-generation stock ahead of new launches.

**Large appliances:** Refrigerators, washing machines, and air conditioners combine sale discounts with exchange offers, often the single best time of year to replace an aging unit.

**Small electronics and accessories:** Smaller percentage discounts individually, but frequently bundled with no-cost EMI and card cashback that improves the effective price.

## How to Shop the Live Sale Efficiently

**Have your shortlist ready before you open the app.** The sale surfaces hundreds of "deal of the day" cards, and browsing without a target list burns time and often leads to impulse buys outside your budget.

**Check your bank's instant discount eligibility in advance.** Cards from partner banks typically unlock 10% instant discounts capped at a fixed rupee amount — knowing this ahead of time changes which card you should use at checkout.

**Track price history before trusting the displayed discount.** A "40% off" badge means little if the base price was inflated a few weeks earlier — tools like Keepa show genuine historical pricing on Amazon.

**Set alerts for out-of-stock items you want.** High-demand SKUs sell out and restock throughout the sale window, so a single "unavailable" message doesn't mean the deal is gone for good.
