## Where Genuine Coupons Actually Appear

**On-page clippable coupons:** A checkbox near the price showing an additional discount (e.g., "Apply ₹500 coupon") — click to activate before adding to cart.

**Bank offer sections:** Listed separately from coupons, these show your eligible card discounts directly on the product page.

**Amazon Pay ICICI and other co-branded cards:** Sometimes carry additional cashback specifically for Amazon Pay balance payments.

## Avoiding Coupon Code Scams

**Be skeptical of third-party "promo code" lists:** Sites claiming to have exclusive Amazon India discount codes are almost always outdated or entirely fake — Amazon India's sale discounts don't typically work this way.

**Never enter payment details on external "coupon" sites:** Legitimate Amazon coupons only ever apply on amazon.in directly, never through a third-party portal requesting your card information.

**Check coupon expiry and eligibility carefully:** On-page coupons sometimes have minimum purchase requirements or are limited to first-time buyers of that specific product category.
