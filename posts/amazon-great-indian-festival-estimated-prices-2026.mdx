## Estimated Discount Ranges by Category

**Smartphones (flagship and mid-range):** ₹3,000-₹12,000 off, generally 8-15% of list price, with bank offers adding another 5-10%.

**Laptops (premium and gaming):** ₹10,000-₹20,000 off on machines priced above ₹70,000, roughly 12-18% of list price.

**Televisions (55-65 inch):** 15-25% off, with the steepest cuts on outgoing-generation flagship models being replaced by new launches.

**Large appliances:** 15-25% off list price, often improved further by exchange bonuses of ₹2,000-₹8,000 depending on the old unit's condition.

**Audio (headphones, soundbars):** 20-40% off, among the highest percentage discounts of any category due to healthier margins.

**Furniture and home office:** 10-20% off, typically paired with no-cost EMI rather than steep percentage cuts.

## Why Estimates Can Differ From Actual Pricing

**Inventory levels vary by SKU.** A model nearing end-of-life discounts more aggressively than one still in high demand.

**Competitive pressure changes week to week.** If a rival platform runs a matching sale simultaneously, individual sellers may price more aggressively than historical averages suggest.

**New product launches shift discounting on older models.** A flagship phone or laptop launching close to the sale date often pushes deeper discounts on its immediate predecessor.
