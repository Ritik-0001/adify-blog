## How Exchange Valuation Works

**Brand and model:** Premium brands (Apple, Samsung, Sony) typically retain higher exchange value than budget brands, even at comparable age and condition.

**Age:** Exchange value depreciates significantly year-over-year — a 1-year-old device retains substantially more value than a 3-4-year-old equivalent.

**Working condition:** Fully functional devices with no visible damage receive maximum eligible credit; damaged screens, non-functional buttons, or battery issues reduce valuation significantly.

**Category-specific factors:** For laptops, RAM/storage configuration affects value. For TVs, screen size and technology (OLED vs. LED) matter. For refrigerators, capacity and inverter compressor presence affect valuation.

## Maximizing Exchange Value by Category

### Laptops

Back up and factory-reset your device before evaluation (required for most programs). Include original charger if possible — some programs offer slightly higher credit for complete accessory sets.

### TVs

Note exact screen size and confirm it powers on and displays properly — even minor visible defects (dead pixels, minor screen damage) can reduce valuation substantially.

### Refrigerators and Washing Machines

Ensure the unit is clean and all doors/functions operate normally during the valuation assessment — appliance exchange programs typically require a working-condition verification step.

## Exchange vs. Independent Resale: Which Is Better?

**Choose Amazon exchange if:** Convenience matters most — the credit applies instantly toward your new purchase without separate listing, negotiation, or buyer coordination effort.

**Choose independent resale (OLX, Cashify, local dealers) if:** You have time to list and negotiate, and your device is in excellent condition — independent resale sometimes yields 10-20% more than manufacturer exchange programs, particularly for premium devices in great condition.

**Quick comparison tip:** Get a quick independent resale estimate (via Cashify or similar) before committing to Amazon's exchange offer — if the difference is minor, exchange convenience wins; if it's substantial, the extra effort of independent resale may be worthwhile.
