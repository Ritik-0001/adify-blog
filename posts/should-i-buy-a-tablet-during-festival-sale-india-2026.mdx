## When a Tablet Is Genuinely Worth Buying

**You take handwritten notes regularly** — students and professionals who actually use a stylus for note-taking get consistent daily value from an iPad or Galaxy Tab S9 FE.

**You read extensively** — e-books, PDFs, or documents — where a larger, comfortable screen genuinely improves the experience over a phone.

**You have a specific creative hobby** — sketching, journaling, or light photo editing — that benefits from a bigger canvas and stylus support.

**Your household needs a shared streaming or kids' device**, separate from personal phones and the main TV.

---

## When You Should Skip It This Sale

**You're buying "because it's discounted," without a specific planned use.** This is the single most common reason tablets end up unused within a few months.

**You already have a laptop and a large-screen phone that cover your needs.** A tablet adds real value mainly when it fills a genuine gap between those two devices, not when it duplicates them.

**You're unsure whether you'll actually use a stylus.** Buying the pricier stylus-equipped model "just in case" often means paying for a feature that goes unused.

---

## Recommendation

Be honest about whether you have a specific, recurring use case before buying — if you do, this sale is a good time to act on it; if the honest answer is "it seems like a nice-to-have," skip it this year and put the budget toward something you'll use daily instead.
